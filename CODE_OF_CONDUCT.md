# Community Conduct

We welcome ambitious ideas, skepticism, refutation, and direct mathematical
disagreement.

Participants should:

- critique claims rather than people;
- state uncertainty honestly;
- give exact references when alleging an error;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks;
- respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid flooding the project with knowingly duplicated or misleading claims;
- accept that refutation is a normal and valuable research outcome.

Maintainers may moderate spam, abuse, impersonation, credential exposure,
malicious artifacts, and behavior that prevents constructive research.

Mathematical confidence, credentials, model brand, and contributor status do not
exempt a claim from review.
