## Main consolidated results

### Exact block and cylinder system

The H block dynamics is reduced to

\[
p_{i+1}=\frac{3^{2r_i+1}}{2^{3r_i+2}}p_i+1
\]

with exact residue conditions. Every finite word has one arithmetic-progression
cylinder. The direct 2-adic ghost congruence and recursive suffix construction
are equivalent.

### Carry and ghost formulation

Least representatives are nondecreasing and satisfy an explicit carry update.
A positive ordinary integer realizes an infinite itinerary iff the finite
representatives eventually stabilize, equivalently iff all late carries vanish.

### Toll-Euler identity

The exact identities

\[
p_L/M_L=p_0+\sum_{k\le L}1/M_k
\]

and

\[
p_0+\sum_{k\le L}1/M_k
=p_0\prod_{j\le L}(1-1/p_j)^{-1}
\]

are recorded with proofs.

### Infinite-survivor harmonic theorem

The set of states surviving `h` exact steps has density

\[
\frac1{12}(2/7)^h.
\]

A growing-depth cylinder count gives

\[
A_\infty(X)\ll X e^{-c\sqrt{\log X}},
\]

hence the infinite-survivor set has finite harmonic sum.

### Automatic real escape

Every nonperiodic infinite exact orbit visits distinct infinite survivors, so
the toll-Euler product is bounded. Therefore

\[
\sum 1/M_k<\infty,
\qquad M_k\to\infty,
\qquad K_k\to\infty.
\]

This reduces the infinite obstruction to a one-sided nonperiodic positive path,
or equivalently a positive ordinary-integer 2-adic ghost with real-escaping
capital.

### Bounded-representative rewrite system

For each bound `B`, the prefix-expanding exact tree with `Pi(w)<=B` is finitely
branching, with successors generated by finitely many explicit carries. The
remaining LX problem is equivalent to termination of all these bounded trees.
This is a concrete certificate/search interface.

## Important corrections

The final ghost congruence does enforce every intermediate exact condition.
Reducing it modulo each prefix power of 2 forces the needed intermediate

safe is superseded.

### Contracting-cylinder descent not promoted

The proposed signed-displacement theorem passed exhaustive finite checks, but
the supplied induction's mixed-sign carry/half-plane step was not proved. The
packet therefore records it as `C-9501`, not a theorem. Abstract affine tuples

word-specific carry lemma is genuinely necessary.

simplified graph taxonomy in one thread omitted them. The forward-orbit
statements used here do not require that taxonomy.

predecessor was not proved. It is not used as an admitted dependency.
