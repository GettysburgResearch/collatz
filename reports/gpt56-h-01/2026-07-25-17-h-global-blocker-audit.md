## Main conclusion

For any fixed prefix-closed H architecture, let `S_L` be the positive ordinary
seeds legal for `L` blocks and let `m_L=min S_L`.  Then

```text
one ordinary infinite seed exists
    iff (m_L) is bounded
    iff (m_L) eventually stabilizes.
```

If every finite depth is compatible but no ordinary seed exists, then

```text
m_L -> infinity.
```

This is the exact inference repeatedly missing from the positive program.  The
compactness supplied by `Z_2` extracts a completion, not an ordinary integer.
Ordinary extraction requires an eventual boundary condition: the canonical
least roots must stop changing, equivalently the newly appended carry blocks
must eventually vanish.

## Native countermodel inside H

For every constant letter `r>=3`, the itinerary

```text
r,r,r,...
```

has all of the properties usually advertised as evidence for a divergent
ordinary orbit:

- every finite prefix has infinitely many positive exact starts;
- every positive finite shadow strictly expands;
- every prefix multiplier is greater than one and tends to infinity;
- the infinite compatible `2`-adic path exists and is explicit.

Nevertheless the unique infinite ghost is

```text
-2^(3r+2)/(3^(2r+1)-2^(3r+2)),
```

which is a negative nonintegral rational in `Z_2`.  For `r=3` it is `-2048/139`.
The finite least roots therefore tend to infinity.

This proves, within the native H system, that

```text
finite compatibility
+ an exact infinite p-adic path
+ supercritical expansion/refund
+ conditional growth
```

does not imply one positive ordinary root.

## Why the target is weaker than Collatz

For the full H language, proving `m_L -> infinity` proves termination only for
the residue-restricted H subsystem.  It does not prove full Collatz.  For a
strict H architecture, it eliminates an even narrower class.

If `m_L` is bounded, it stabilizes at one explicit `p`; then

```text
n=(p-4)/3,
N=8n+1
```

reconstruct an infinite H orbit and its shortcut-Collatz lift.  After exact
replay, this is a genuine counterexample candidate.
