  *past the point where the previous family had proved it could not go* — L-9920 had shown its
  own ceiling was m = 207, and L-9927 escapes it by adding information (S-closure) rather than

  the previous run, with the survivors named explicitly (27 packets). This overtakes the

  lengths (L-9927.8). Recording *why* a family caps is now this packet's most reliable output.

  unreviewed PROPOSED claims across four PRs received their first independent pass.

## Failed approaches, corrections, and retractions (README §17.4, §17.12)

**Coordinator errors caught by sub-agents this run** (all flagged in-file by the agent that
caught them; the standing "your derivation is the authority" instruction is what converts these
into documented corrections instead of propagated defects):

1. **Misrouted assignment.** The T-9925 redirect was sent to v26 — *T-9924's verifier* — instead
   of p19. v26 **correctly declined**, citing README §3's identity rules and its own file scope,
   reaffirmed its existing PASS, and relayed salvage pointers for the intended recipient, which
   were forwarded to p19 and used. The protocol caught a coordinator error the coordinator did
   not catch itself.
2. **Two wrong claims in the T-9925 briefing.** The target classification is by **s mod 3**, not
   by the parity of s; and mod 9 does **not** fail for even a — even-a constants are {0, 6} mod 9
   by the sign rule, so there is no parity asymmetry anywhere. The even-row **mod-7 hunt the
   briefing commissioned is provably fruitless**: a full-block kill would need 2^a ≡ 3 (mod 7),
   and powers of 2 mod 7 cycle through {1, 2, 4} only.
3. **The L-9927 briefing missed its own answer.** The sketch proposed a (3/2)^{f(j)} sorted
   staircase that was both unproven and unnecessary, and **omitted the mechanism that actually
   worked** (the mod-3 element sieve). Three further items were corrected: "run length at x_min
   is constrained by L-9912's statistics" is misleading (run length 1 is consistent with
   everything in-repo; the correct positional fact is a slab lemma by value, not orbit
   position); the proposed near-cycle calibration is uninformative; and the bounded-vs-growing
   dichotomy was too coarse — outcomes (a), (b) and (c) all held simultaneously.
4. **Three framing errors in the L-9926 briefing:** M is not multiplicative in the shifted
   coordinate; the pure-calculus legality modulus is 3^β only, not 2^α3^β; and L-9919.8's
   per-node positivity thresholds are redundant (the true witness is θ = −1, not 5/3).

  never been read by anyone but their authors. One reviewer, calibrated on our own proved

  of the standing correct-and-flag instruction is not that it prevents coordinator error; it is
  that it converts coordinator error into documented, attributed corrections at no extra cost.
- **Resumption across a model switch worked.** Two verifiers were killed mid-review by session
  limits and resumed from transcript, one of them onto a different model, both completing their
  reviews with their prior computations intact.
