**Exit rules.** Given x+1=2^m a with a odd and m≥1, the exact first m shortcut steps lead to 3^m a−1. If R=3^m a≡1 mod 4, then Y=R/3≡3 mod 4. If R≡3 mod 4, the previous comparison at 2x+1 yields its merger with x, while F(x)=(R−1)/2≡1 mod 6 is the next odd run entry after m+1 physical clocks. This uses x>1 since 1 is good; at x=2n+1 the strict smaller-multiple premise already fails.
