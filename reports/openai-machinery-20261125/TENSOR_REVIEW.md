* **Growth.** For fixed d let r=floor((log₉k)/2). The largest period is O_d(sqrt(k)); the least burst budget is bounded below by c k/r for either S=k+floor(k/2) or S=2k and large k. Each factor in the product is at least c_d sqrt(k)/r. Its logarithm is r((log k)/2−log r+O_d(1)), giving Ω_d((log k)²). This conclusion holds for every sufficiently large k, with a freely selected, k-dependent endpoint. It does not keep an ordinary endpoint fixed while k increases. For fixed r and prescribed d, implied constants may depend on both r and d.
* **Terminal in the basin of 1.** The order of 4 modulo 3^(m+1) is 3^m because v₃(4^(3^i)−1)=i+1. Thus (4^n−1)/3 covers all residues modulo 3^m, and adding multiples of 3^m to n gives unbounded positive odd representatives in the same class. Each has U(y)=1.
