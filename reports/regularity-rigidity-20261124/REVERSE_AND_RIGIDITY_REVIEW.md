## 1. Physical killing versus skeleton killing

The state is \((k,c)\) where \(k=p_Y-p_X\) and \(Y=5\,3^k X+c\). A root is the joint state \((0,0)\). Force the first source bit to one and continue with unrestricted independent fair source bits, even after a first subsequent physical root. At physical time 1 the state is \((0,-2)\). Let \(h\) be the first strictly positive physical root. Set \(K_i=k_{1+3i}\) and let \(\sigma\) be the first \(i\ge0\) at which the sampled **full state** is a root.

PR 146, f431078244e868dc699fad011044f0d5e7f76437, research/upper-bit-mergers/FIVE_RETURN_CLOCK_AND_TERMINALS.md, section “The first-return tail”, bounds the expected stopped \(W\ge |k|\) by a finite \(D_z\), including an exit at \(|k|\ge A\). For a finite horizon \(j\), choose \(A>|K_0|+3j\). The strip cannot be exited by that horizon, so \(\mathbb E|K_{j\wedge\sigma}|\le D_z\), uniformly in \(j\). The source gets this using bounded positive drift on a finite core, nonpositive drift outside, and a uniformly positive chance of hitting the root at a skeleton time after a core visit.

I also inspected its upstream three-step variance and drift estimates in PR 171, 2bd243b3e05eaa5543adeb2894637b053c9662ca, research/adaptive-closure-20261121/AFFINE_SECTION_RECURRENCE.md, (S4), (S6), (S8)–(S11), and its finite-core control input in PR 146, 66970775e4bdc945a68308e37a8eb028ebde42d2, research/upper-bit-mergers/FIVE_RETURN_IN_EVERY_CLASS.md §1. An odd carry gives a fair signed jump; from any state at most two prescribed bits suffice to make a clock active, with probability at least one quarter. Martingale orthogonality gives the three-step variance bound including paths with later cancellation. Core-to-root control may use free source zeros after a hit to align it to a multiple of three. This is a legitimate event in the unrestricted chain; it is not an assertion about which physical clock first hits the root.

For \(a=L+3\), let \(H(u)=u^2\) on \([-a,a]\), with \(H(u)=2a|u|-a^2\) off that interval. Before \(\sigma\), convexity and the martingale property give nonnegative conditional drift. If \(|K_i|\le L\), every possible three-clock endpoint is in the quadratic region, so the conditional drift is at least \(1/4\), including a step whose endpoint is the root. Since \(0\le H(u)\le2a|u|\), finite telescoping gives

\[
 \mathbb E\sum_{i<j\wedge\sigma}{\bf1}_{|K_i|\le L}\le8(L+3)D_z.
\]

Pass to \(j\to\infty\) by monotone convergence. For \(1\le t<h\), take \(i=\lfloor(t-1)/3\rfloor\). Every sampled clock through \(1+3i\) is before \(h\), hence \(i<\sigma\); the level changes at most two between \(1+3i\) and \(t\). Each \(i\) receives at most three charges. Thus (G) holds with \(24(L+5)D_z\). A physical root strictly between sample clocks only removes times from the physical occupation. Applying the unkilled variance bound to the process already killed at that intervening root would be unjustified; the pathwise envelope above avoids doing so.

## 2. Suffixes, clipping at the kth one, and Tao's published bound

A marked block has \(e\) consumed source bits, starts odd, includes the first positive physical root at \(h\le e\), and continues through the zeros before the next odd bit. The following odd sentinel is excluded from the word. A legal word together with its sentinel costs \(2^{-e}\) under a prescribed initial odd. The fresh padding length \(b=e-h\) has probability \(2^{-j-1}\) at \(j\ge0\) and mean one. Each branch \(F_w(z)=(3^p z+C_w)/2^e\) maps ternary units to units and contracts by \(3^{-p}\le1/3\). The outermost-first iid composition thus has a unique stationary law \(\nu\) and \(k\) blocks determine the first \(k\) ternary digits irrespective of the tail. Pushing Haar density forward multiplies it by \(2^{-e}3^p\) on the branch image.

Fix a chronological suffix of \(l\) bits. At a proposed start \(t=e-l\ge1\), if \(t<h\), the signed level must reach zero by \(t+l\), hence \(|k_t|\le l\). Membership in this permitted starting event is determined by the past before bit \(t\). If \(t\ge h\), the past must have only zeros between \(h\) and \(t-1\). The expected number of those possible starting times, including \(t=e\) as an upper bound, is \(\mathbb E(b+1)=2\). Exposing the next \(l\) prescribed bits costs \(2^{-l}\) in either case. At time zero the odd conditioning gives at most \(2^{1-l}\). Summing (G) proves (S1), \(C(l+1)2^{-l}\). In particular the exposure does not condition first on the value of \(e\).

Put a target prefix of length \(l\) of the infinite reverse stream back into chronological order. Let \(j\in[1,l]\) count the portion read from its earliest block, allowing a whole block. After that portion, the remaining \(l-j\) bits must form complete blocks. Starting at their known marked root, scan for the first joint root and consume zeros up to the next odd; use an appended odd sentinel at the final boundary. For each fixed \(j\) this determines at most one parse, including the number of later complete blocks. The probability of their specified words is \(2^{-(l-j)}\). The preceding independent block has the specified suffix with probability at most \(C(j+1)2^{-j}\). Sum over \(j\) to obtain (S2), \(C(l+1)^2 2^{-l}\). Neither finite mean renewal length nor reverse independence is needed.

If \(A_i\) is the position, starting from one, of the \(i\)th reverse one, the affine expansion gives \(Q=\sum_{i=1}^k3^{i-1}2^{-A_i}\pmod {3^k}\). Under independent fair bits the successive spacings are independent positive geometric variables of mean two. This is exactly Tao's iid offset \(S_k\) in equation (1.29). On \(A_k\le4k\), the stopped cylinders are disjoint and (S2) compares each to fair input with factor at most \(C(4k+1)^2\). For \(A_k>4k\), sum (S2) over all strings of length \(4k\) containing fewer than \(k\) ones and use the binomial Chernoff bound. Hence uniformly for residue sets \(E\),

\[
 \nu_k(E)\le C(k+1)^2\Pr(S_k\in E)+Ce^{-ck}.
\]

Tao, *Almost all orbits of the Collatz map attain almost bounded values*, Forum of Mathematics, Pi 10 (2022), e12, Prop. 1.14, equations (1.26)–(1.27), gives summed absolute difference \(O_A(m^{-A})\) between the entire iid law and its depth-\(m\) coarse marginal spread uniformly on each depth-\(k\) fiber, for every \(1\le m\le k\). Every atom of that spread measure is at most \(3^{m-k}\). Thus \(\Pr(S_k\in E)\le |E|3^{m-k}+C_A m^{-A}\). This imports no pointwise conditional equidistribution at a supplied coarse residue. [Published source](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2022.8)

For \(m=\lfloor k^\delta\rfloor,\ 0<\delta<1\), sets of size at most \(3^{k-2m}\) have \(\nu_k\) mass \(O_{B,\delta}(k^{-B})\) for every \(B>0\), taking \(A>(B+2)/\delta\). There are at most \(3^{k-2m}\) cells heavier than \(3^{-k+2m}\). For cells lighter than \(3^{-k-m}\), the total mass is at most \(3^{-m}\). Both tails are summable. Borel–Cantelli and a countable intersection over rational \(\delta\) give \(-\log_3\nu(Q+3^k\mathbb Z_3)=k+O_{Q,\delta}(k^\delta)\) almost surely for every \(\delta>0\). Discard the heavy cells in the entropy sum with \(B=2\) to obtain the entropy deficit \(O_\delta(k^\delta)\).

For support the source 49 follows \(49,74,37,56,28,14,7\) and 245 follows \(245,368,184,92,46,23,35\). Clock six is the first joint root, with two odds on each arm. The padded map is \((9x+7)/2^e,\ e\ge6\). The order of 2 modulo 27 is 18, so every ternary unit has an inverse unit along one such branch with \(6\le e\le23\). Iterating \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) inverse choices gives a lower cylinder mass at least \(2^{-23\lceil k/2\rceil}\) at any unit, including any specified ordinary unit. This exponent is weaker than the typical one. Nonatomicity assigns zero mass to the countable set of ordinary integers. The first unsupported inference toward PT would be to assert a useful pointwise lower exponent, with suitable ordinary height, on a particular failed ordinary pair.

## 3. Shell and polynomial phase audit

PR 146, f431078244e868dc699fad011044f0d5e7f76437, research/upper-bit-mergers/THREE_RAY_UPPER.md and TRIPLING_VARIATION.md, supplies the ordinary nonmeeting and variation estimates. Unlike exact invariant labels imply nonmeeting. At \(x=R2^t\), rotation by \(\theta=\log_2 3\bmod1\) replaces \(x\) by \(3x/2^j,\ j=1,2\). The integer roundings differ by at most a bounded number of adjacent steps, giving the shell \(L^1\) error \(\epsilon_R=(\log R)^{-1/2}\). Doubling uses a one-step rounding. Under the stated exponent \(\kappa\), a Dirichlet approximant has \(L^{1/\kappa}\ll q\le L\) and the \(q\)-point rotation grid has interval discrepancy \(O(1/q)\). The rotation substitution costs \(O(q\epsilon_R)\). Taking \(L\asymp\epsilon_R^{-\kappa/(\kappa+1)}\) proves the claimed primitive \(\delta_R\ll\epsilon_R^{1/(\kappa+1)}\). The bounded shell Jacobian and dyadic summation preserve the full cutoff estimate.

For linear Fourier cancellation, Abel summation handles \(|\alpha|\ge\sqrt{\epsilon_R}\). The smaller frequencies incur \(O(|\alpha|+R^{-1})\) in replacing unit cells by the shell integral. Write \(\lambda=R\alpha\) and \(A=1/(2(\kappa+1))\). Below \(\epsilon_R^{-A}\) the test's variation gives \(O((1+|\lambda|)\delta_R)\). Above that scale, distinct wrapped rotated tests have mutual frequency separation \(\gg|\lambda|q^{-\kappa}\) on at most three subintervals. Integration by parts and Cauchy–Schwarz give \(O(q\epsilon_R+q^{-1/2}+q^{\kappa/2}/\sqrt{|\lambda|})\). The source's \(q\asymp\epsilon_R^{-A/(2\kappa)}\) yields exponent \(1/[16\kappa(\kappa+1)]\) in powers of \(\log X\). Parseval gives the stated cyclic \(U^2\) consequence with the same centering.

For degree \(d\) the floor stays in the shell integral. On a wrapping piece write the dilations \(c_i=A_i/D,c_j=A_j/D\) with distinct positive \(A_i,A_j\) and \(D\le e^{O(Q)}\). For \(x=Dm+u,\ 0\le u<D\), the floor arguments are exactly \(A_i m+b_i,A_jm+b_j\) with \(|b_i|+|b_j|=O(D)\). If the normalized inner product exceeds \(1/Q\), end blocks and freezing its slowly varying weight cost \(O(D/R)=o(1/Q)\). Averaging over \(u\) and the at most three pieces leaves a weighted sum \(\gg R/(QD)\). Partial summation yields an unweighted subinterval of length \(L\gg R/(QD)\) with normalized correlation \(\gg1/Q\).

Green–Tao, *The quantitative behaviour of polynomial orbits on nilmanifolds*, Ann. of Math. 175 (2012), Prop. 4.3, gives one integer multiplier \(h\le Q^{O_d(1)}\) controlling all nonconstant binomial coefficients at scales \(Q^{O_d(1)}L^{-s}\). A rotated real part of \(e(x)\) is a fixed Lipschitz torus test of zero mean. Conversion to monomials costs a fixed factorial. Undoing the shift \(m_0=O(R/D)\) multiplies degree-\(s\) errors by \(O_d((1+|m_0|)^{s-l})\) at degree \(l\); the result is \(e^{O_d(Q)}R^{-l}\) at every \(l\ge1\). The triangular coefficient map has diagonal \(A_i^l-A_j^l\ne0\) and integer off-diagonal entries \(e^{O_d(Q)}\). Descending substitution gives the claimed simultaneous rational approximation. [Primary paper, Proposition 4.3](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v175-n2-p02-p.pdf)

The rational polynomial phase has period its common denominator \(q\). Replacing it by its mean on full periods loses at most \(q\) points per adjacent color change plus slow phase error \(O_d(qH/R)\) where \(q,H\le e^{C_dQ}\). The slow remainder costs \(O_d((1+H)\delta_R)\) by primitive discrepancy. A sufficiently small \(Q=\lfloor c_d\log\log R\rfloor\) pays all errors. If no pair is resonant, the averaged translates instead have squared norm \(O(1/Q)\). This proves (PW) with coefficients allowed to vary with \(X\). No inverse theorem on nonabelian nilmanifolds is used.

## 4. Explicit coloring

Taking the first convergent denominator at least \(k_i^2\) and, if even, the next one produces an odd \(k_{i+1}\) with polynomial upper bound using the stated irrationality measure twice. Each transition cosine has odd frequency, so their convex combination changes sign under a half-turn. It is a nonzero trigonometric polynomial with at most \(2k_{i+1}\) zeros. For the small-ball argument, resample the slow coordinate independently **within the same fast period**. Upon averaging over periods it is uniform on the full circle and independent of the fast phase. Its displacement changes the sum by at most \(O(k_i/k_{i+1})\). Conditioning on the lower-amplitude cosine and integrating the other arcsine law gives the square-root bound, including the pure-wave endpoints.

This proves \(O(J^{-1/2})\) rotation and depth-change errors. For valuation \(v\le J/2\), the odd-part shell is \(J-v\) at the same mantissa. Telescoping costs \(O(v/\sqrt J)\); progression counts carry weights \(2^{-v}\), and \(O(J^K)\) boundary errors for each of \(O(J)\) progressions are negligible. Higher valuations cost \(O(2^{J/2})\). This proves the claimed adjacent, tripling and odd-edge error counts. Uniform interval equidistribution under the irrational rotation and the bounded shell Jacobian give both half natural densities. Finitely many dyadic rays change \(O_S(\log X)\) labels through \(X\); modified values acting as shortcut outputs may be counted up to \(2X\), with the same bound.

The countermodel leaves open the original exact ordinary graph. Neither this review nor the stationary-measure and spectral conclusions provide a classification of all ordinary inputs. This review is written mathematics, not a Lean formalization of the probability or exponential-sum arguments.
