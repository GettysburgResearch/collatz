## Varying digit automata: VERIFIED conditional on the shell estimates

For a recurrent S-state digit chain, a path from a maximal stationary
atom shows pi_min>=B^(-(S-1))/S. The independent product chain restricted
to equal cyclic phases is irreducible, with stationary measure
d pi_x pi_y and minimum positive edge flow at least S^(-2)B^(-2S).
Successive return excursions give E_a T_b<=1/(mu_a P_ab) on any positive
edge; the argument does not assume an excursion length independent of
its first step. A path of at most S^2 product edges reaches the diagonal.
Thus the meeting time is at most S^4 B^(2S) in expectation. Coupling and
Markov's inequality supply the required uniform phase mixing bound.

Apply it to aligned digit blocks with suffix length proportional to
log log X. The retained prefix phase is constant on at most
O(B^L log log X) ordinary intervals. The already pinned variation and
primitive discrepancy estimates pay the two replacement errors.
The condition S^4B^(2S)=o(log log X) makes all terms vanish. This is an
obstruction to low-state approximation, not an assertion that an
appropriate approximator exists.
