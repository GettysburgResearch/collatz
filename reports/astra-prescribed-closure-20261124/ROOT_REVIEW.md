The displayed square-root recurrence forgets the initial carry on the remaining linear time interval. Counting the original prescribed residue divides by $2^L$, while the induction counts bad children only in their actual single depth-$k$ residue, dividing by $2^k$. These factors cancel. All inequalities have asymptotic slack. The paired agent independently reconstructed these steps in `paired/REVIEW_HEAD.md`.

The simultaneous ternary-word assertion uses only $\exp(O((\log d)^2))$ choices. The sparse pointwise criterion uses the separate premise M and pinned directed-cut and head-series reductions. Its harmonic argument charges discarded **parents**, not the harmonic weight of the sparse child set. It therefore makes no claim of a divergent harmonic mass on that child set.
