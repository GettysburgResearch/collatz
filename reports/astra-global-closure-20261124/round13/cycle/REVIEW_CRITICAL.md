**R1.** For odd numerator and odd denominator $d$ prime to 3, agreement with the ternary prefix makes $3^{-j}(2^{S_j}a-d\,2^{S_j}\beta_j)$ an integer. Its recurrence preserves oddness. On a finite distinct positive signed segment the prefix-product ceiling bounds each positive numerator. On its negative tail the absolute-value recurrence contributes $\prod_i(1-d/(3|a_i|))^{-1}$. Terms below $d$ cost at most $(3d)^d$ by distinctness and integrality. Larger terms have log cost at most $d/(2|a_i|)$, and integration of the distinct-path count $N(X)\le10X^{39/40}$ gives reciprocal sum at most $400 d^{-1/40}$. Reversing a finite segment preserves distinctness for this use of the forward count. That count follows from the unique binary class of a short low-clock gap word and injectivity of time advance on a single path for the high-clock complement. At a positive-to-negative transition, $aA_{l-1}\ge1$ and $|a_l|\le d/3$, bounding the new ratio $|a_l|/A_l$. This verifies the finite distinct-path bound for both signs.

Each suffix of an infinite compatible path has a fixed finite multiplier ceiling $1/A_l$, so each suffix must repeat. Determinism of the forward odd map first puts its initial vertex on a cycle. Applying this to *every* suffix forces all inverse choices onto that same cycle. A positive signed cycle has full multiplier greater than one; a negative cycle has full multiplier less than one. Thus one negative lap and its prefix budgets suffice. For even denominator, after $v_2(d)$ steps every fixed-denominator numerator contains the full dyadic divisor, and another step gives an odd numerator after normalization. A positive even numerator with odd denominator must begin with inverse gap one: a resulting negative value would be larger than $-1/3$, incompatible with membership in a negative odd cycle. These arguments allow the positive even-denominator examples at the source. They identify the rational trace, not the forward fate of arbitrary positive sources.

**S1–S2.** The periodic approximation is valid in $\mathbb Q_3$ since the repeated block multiplier $3^p/2^A$ has norm $3^{-p}$, irrespective of its real size. The unreduced denominator $X-Y=2^s(2^A-3^p)$ is a ternary unit. The forms at infinity and 2 are coordinates; the ternary third form $z(X-Y)-N$ has coefficient $-1$ on $N$. They are fixed and independent at each place conditional on algebraicity of this one $z$. The archimedean and finite norms of $X,Y$ cancel exactly, leaving at most $|N|3^{-m}$. The common integer divisor is dyadic since $X$ is a power of two. Its removal does not alter the three-place product, and the reduced $Y$ remains a positive multiple of $3^p$. The prefix estimates only bound combined clocks at $r$ and $r+p$; no separate criticality of the period is needed. With an $o(j)$ deficit all prefix and translated-prefix deficits used are uniformly $o(r+p)$, also when $r$ stays bounded. The strict S1 inequality or the S2 repetition gain consequently gives a fixed positive power saving relative to the primitive height. On a repeated subspace, dividing its relation by $X$ and passing to the ternary limit gives $u+t z=0$; $t=0$ would force $u=v=0$. Hence algebraicity implies rationality. R1 gives the asserted transcendence for non-eventually-periodic scripts.

**S3 and the explicit coordinate.** Among the first $\lfloor Cn\rfloor+1$ length-$n$ factors at the low-complexity scales two coincide. With separation $d$, the full $d+n$ segment has period $d$ even for overlapping copies. For $d<n/2$ the choice $d\lfloor n/(2d)\rfloor$ lies in $[n/4,n/2]$ and is also a period, giving the required bounded preperiod-to-period ratio and repeat gain. For $d\ge n/2$ the original period works because $d\le Cn$.

Take $S_j=\lfloor j\log_2 3\rfloor$. Irrationality of $\log_2 3$ makes all nonempty prefixes strictly critical; the natural-log deficit is in $(0,\ln2)$. The gap alphabet is $\{1,2\}$. With $\omega=\log_2 3-1$, a length-$n$ factor depends on a circle coordinate $t$ only through $\lfloor t+j\omega\rfloor$ for $0\le j\le n$. The $n+1$ boundary points $-j\omega\bmod1$ yield at most $n+1$ half-open coding intervals, including endpoints under the floor convention. The long-run gap mean is the irrational $\log_2 3$, excluding eventual periodicity. Thus S3 proves that $\sum_{j\ge1}3^{j-1}2^{-S_j}$ in $\mathbb Z_3$ is transcendental over $\mathbb Q$.

At each finite depth the residue cylinder contains arbitrarily large odd ordinary endpoints. These varying witnesses give no single ordinary endpoint with that entire script.
