## Why this change is needed

The ordinary frontier reduction still lacks a merger for every surviving source. Fixed template constructions, partial cycle-denominator cancellation, and averaged inverse counts do not discharge that pointwise obligation. Round twelve adds restrictions on each of these routes and records the exact next missing inference.

**Collatz remains OPEN.** The new global results have written proofs and reconstructed reviews. Lean verifies the named finite kernels and the existing conditional consumer, not all analytic results.

- **Cycle factor obstruction.** In the bounded least-tripling-disagreement branch, every sufficiently large ordinary candidate must contain a noncyclotomic irreducible factor whose degree after removing exponent dilation is at least a constant times p(log log p)^3/(log p)^5. For every fixed epsilon > 0, factors of compressed degree above p^(1-epsilon) must collectively absorb a divisor of 2^h-3^p of size 3^(p-o(p)). The short-relation resultant argument accounts for the full modulus, multiplicities and prime powers. It uses published Dobrowolski and Wu–Wang bounds at the stated steps. A generic irreducible high-degree factor remains unresolved.
- **Finite tower merger classes.** Every fixed positive tower has finite same-clock augmented merger classes, including periodic and nonperiodic reference cases. For an arbitrary nonperiodic reference and any fixed bound on the difference between the two clocks, only finitely many tower starts can merge with it. This ordinary bounded-lag conclusion needs no odd-count premise or external finite convergence range.
- **Two-arm finite-template obstruction.** Every fixed finite bank of parity words with a bounded number of powered blocks per arm misses infinitely many exponents in the ordinary lower-cone problem. Both clocks, all repetition counts and the target's initial halvings are unbounded. The written proof uses Evertse–Schlickewei–Schmidt and the finite-class theorem.
- **Original-source trace.** Under the explicitly source-qualified external renewal floor F, escape forces thin one-sided source intervals and a positive inverse-distance moment at each selected actual terminal, including prescribed ternary unit classes. An unconditional finite word count caps the extra ternary depth of any all-terminal floor. Actual-path phase discrepancy gives moving-arc lower bounds after both endpoint cutoffs. No contradictory upper estimate is established.

The cycle controls include repeated-prime cancellation 7675=5^2*307 and an all-depth rational basin example with both rank orders. These are carefully separated from ordinary positive-integer cycles.
