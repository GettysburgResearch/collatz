## Binary substitution theorem and overlap

The primitive case uses #190 at `ba58227c5a047ff3859a148a5fdaac4fd1451e4c`. For triangular incidence, the nonconstant fixed word has infinitely many occurrences of each letter. Its marked-letter series has polynomial coefficients in the slower coordinate when expanded at each fixed fast-coordinate degree. In the mixed-dominant and Jordan cases, linear algebra over the slow rational-function field cancels quadratically many fast coefficients for linear fast degree. For fixed auxiliary polynomial, the first surviving coefficient is eventually nonzero and costs only slow-coordinate valuation. All higher fast coefficients remain integral at the moving point. The slow-coordinate height is negligible only **after fixing the auxiliary degree**. In the pure-image-dominant case the two valuations are comparable, and the exact rational transverse ratio either takes distinct values of subdominant height or restricts the orbit to a fixed one-parameter subtorus.

In the static-coordinate case #194 supplies a separate argument. The counts of ones before the zero of index $ak+r$ equal the preceding count at $k$ plus $bk+e_r$. Normalization gives a digit-multiplicative sequence with $f_{ak+r}=d_r f_k$ and positive real $d_r$. Algebraicity of its generating function implies rationality by the constant term of a monic minimal polynomial and the order of a rational function under pullback by $z\mapsto z^a$. A nonzero order modulo the minimal-polynomial degree would persist on all distinct depth-$j$ preimages, impossible for a rational divisor. Rationality gives a finite exponential-polynomial expression. The relation $f_{ak}=f_k$ permutes its distinct bases under $a$-th powers. The bases are roots of unity, and the coefficient polynomials are constant upon cycling the permutation. Finite range, tested at repeated nonzero digits, forces every $d_r=1$, contradicting aperiodicity. The ensuing auxiliary-polynomial argument is evaluated in the 2-adic norm. The real normalization only establishes formal transcendence.

The nonidentity hypothesis matters because the identity fixes every word. In fact the difference between «fixed word» and «generated from a growing prolongable letter» here is a finite prefix. An aperiodic binary word uses both letters. If none of its occurring letters has a longer image, the substitution acts positionwise and must fix both. Otherwise locate the first letter with an expanding image. All preceding images are singletons fixed in their positions. The expanding image starts in that letter, hence its iterates grow and generate the remaining fixed tail. Removing or prepending a finite physical word applies an invertible rational affine map and preserves transcendence.

GS2 should not be presented as an exclusive or historically first result. Both approaches still require a structural hypothesis to exclude an ordinary trajectory, and neither treats all binary codings of larger substitution alphabets.

## Carry entropy and ordinary occupation

For $0<x<1/2$, the carry weights $f(0)=1$, $f(c)=Dx^c$ for $c>0$ have total $D=(1-x)/(1-2x)$. A positive source carry thus costs at most $f(c)$ after summing its allowed next carries. At zero only $0,\dots,b-1$ are allowed. These two cases give the pointwise operator majorant and the fixed-word partition bound. With $x=u^2$, the specified quintic enforces $1+Dx=1/u$, so the coefficients of twice the carry sum and of the canonical digit excess cancel into the actual orbit's mean budget. This yields $H(G)\le H(B)+d m\log 3+C_q$ without replacing the actual law.
