A clean temporary build with Lean 4.34.1 rebuilt the vendored PR206 `TernaryItinerary` and all four modules with `autoImplicit=false` and warnings as errors. A separate importing module requested `#print axioms` for every public theorem. All 37 passed. Their combined transitive axiom set was `propext`, `Quot.sound`, and `Classical.choice`. The numerical polynomial check used none. The in-source logs cover 35 of the 37. The other two are the barrier helpers `advance_coherent` and `after_word`. The five sources contain no project axiom, proof placeholder, or native decision shortcut. This review covers the four lane modules below. The coordinator's final proposal has its own build receipt.
