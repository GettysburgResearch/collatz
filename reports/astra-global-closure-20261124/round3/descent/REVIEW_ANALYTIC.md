For a fixed list of actual gaps, expansion of \(2^a q'=3q+1\) composes a positive carry \(C_{ij}\) with adjacent carry 1. A common divisor of the two odd endpoints divides the carry, with no assumption that the source endpoints are coprime to 3. Coverage at position \(j\) is equivalent to \(q_j\) dividing the product of the other states. At a prime dividing \(q_j\), truncating each competing exponent at its exponent in \(q_j\) preserves the required lower bound. Thus \(q_j\) also divides the product of the gcds, and hence the product of incident carries \(P_j\). For fixed gaps and \(j\) the map \(x\mapsto q_j\) is injective, since the nonzero input slope in the composed equation cancels; for \(j=0\) it is the identity. Hence there are at most \(\tau(P_j)\) sources. Summing is a union bound, so overlap creates no lower-bound issue.

For \(x\le X\), multiplying \(2^a q'\le4q\) along the one trajectory bounds the final total exponent by \(\lfloor\log_2X\rfloor+2(t-1)\). Choosing \(t-1\) strictly increasing cumulative exponents counts precisely the possible lists in that cap before ordinary admissibility.
