## 3. Pointwise cycle rank

**VERIFIED as a written deduction with qualified external input.**
The primary Lagarias Lemma 2.2(1) was opened and checked in the
arXiv:math/0512006v4 paper. It yields the stated explicit lower bound on
\(|L\log2-k\log3|\). The original minimum bound follows from the
positive product telescope.

At an actual cut, reduction of the center denominator gives the lower
bound from the endpoint difference, not merely the unreduced clock gap.
For different cuts, cancellation against a denominator is bounded
by the difference of their short clocks. Cancellation against a
numerator is bounded by the two cross-carry congruences. The brackets
are nonzero: the minimum numerator is odd and not divisible by three;
an all-even intermediate arc would otherwise return internally to the
minimum and violate primitivity.

For a putative multiplicative relation, choosing the largest absolute
exponent bounds each denominator valuation by the sum of the other
absolute valuations. Truncating each summand at that denominator
valuation is legitimate. Summing over primes yields the explicit
inequality (14), and substitution of the minimum bound gives (15).
The comparison of constants at \(t=\lfloor\sqrt A/2\rfloor\) is strict.
