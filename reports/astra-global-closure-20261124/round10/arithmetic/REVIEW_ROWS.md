* **Block floor.** With $m=\max(1,\lceil256d^2/n\rceil)$, one has $|d|/m\le\sqrt{n/m}/16$. The jitter at each intermediate cut is at most $\sqrt h/16$, so consecutive cut differences, including the floor error, lie strictly within the available central window. Blockwise floors are uniform over the actual supplied endpoint. Given a full word, the ordered block lengths and block clocks recover the jitter vector, and forward determinism identifies one source per word at fixed depth and terminal. The free choices give a factor of order $(\sqrt h)^{m-1}$ against the $m$ local factors of order $h^{-1/2}$. Composing chronological offsets with multipliers at most $1/2$ preserves $[0,2F_0]$.
* **Moving-barrier upper.** At a timely first entry below $Y$, the preceding values are all at least $Y$ and the landing $z$ is in $[Y/2,Y)$. For fixed $(t,p,z)$ the multiplicative distortion ranges over an interval of relative width $O(H/Y)$. Restriction to the original octave leaves $O(1+HX/Y)$ possible original integers. Summing over $O(H^2)$ clocks and odd counts and $O(Yv)$ eligible landings pays a polynomial in $H$ at each stage, uniformly for $Y\ge M$. Binomial grid probabilities concern full starting residue grids. They are transferred with this preimage bound, not assumed at an entrance selected by the path. Increasing $D$ ensures the good-grid downward gain crosses the next stage and makes first-passage times timely inductively. The sum of the square roots of stage lengths is $O(\sqrt{\ln X})$. The drift coefficient in natural time is $\ln2-(\ln3)/2=\ln(4/3)/2$. Thus the truncated exceptional threshold and the constant $\mu$ are consistent.
* **Escaping rows.** On an actual suffix the excess of the prefix is $2i-A_i$, and $2^{2b-A_i}/3^{b-i}=G_b q_i/(aD_i)$. The first gap at $a$ supplies exactly the advertised minimum relative carry. An eventual nonperiodic endpoint forces all vertices in its supplied prefix to be distinct. The reciprocal bound supplies a fixed finite gate set independently of $b$. The first arrival to the escaping ray from each such gate is unique. A counted source therefore reaches a fixed small gate by clock $2b-i+O_a(1)$, which conflicts with the ordinary-source first-passage lower bound off a set of size $O(q_iG_b e^{-c i^2/b})$ in the stated moderate range. Distinct ray points give disjoint terminal rows in the all-terminal sum. The independent endpoint-truncation condition for the other statistic is explicitly retained.
* **Periodic rows.** The first-hit prefix has bounded distortion. After the first hit it is followed by a whole number of laps. Thus $r(-\ln\lambda)=k\ln2+O_q(1)$ on the shell. The coefficient of the early first-hit gap is positive since $A/(-\ln\lambda)>2/\ln(4/3)$ is equivalent to $A<2p$. This follows strictly for the nontrivial cycle since all odd vertices exceed one. The lower construction permits an off-cycle unit predecessor with arbitrary sufficiently large allowed exponent in a residue class modulo six. Multiplying the preperiod factor by the single-gap residual and $\lambda^r$ places the sources inside both shell boundaries; $\lambda>2^{-100}$ and both auxiliary factors are at least $1/2$. For $q=1$, $A=2p$ makes this clock-displacement argument degenerate and only the reported lower conclusion follows.
