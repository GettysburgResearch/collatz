# Independent review of the ordered-gap entropy argument

**VERIFIED as written for the stated bounded least-disagreement branch and its cited strip input.** This review covers the mathematical argument in the round-ten arithmetic README at local SHA-256 1e1ba3ef971fa35d0e210abf35cded8cb6ae6a74ec19845391c922b1e8da3665. It does not assert that another branch reaches this cycle. [Reviewed manuscript](../../../../research/astra-global-closure-20261124/round10/arithmetic/README.md)

1. Starting with the quarter-grid normalized order of round nine, the lift \(Z_{r+p}=2Z_r,\ c_{r+p}=2c_r\) turns the wrap into \(Z_{r+h}=3Z_r+c_r\) at every nonnegative rank. Subtract the lifted equations at ranks \(r\) and \(r+t\) *before* reducing mod \(p\). This supplies the factor \(2c_{r+t-p}\) at the input cut and takes the same output factor outside the entire gap. The block of \(t\) cut ranks is counted exactly once per rank per back-shift, giving at most \(Lt\) affected time windows.
2. At \(r+t\ge p\) the approximation error for \(Z_{r+t}\) can be \(4p/3\); the error for \(Z_r\) is at most \(2p/3\). This gives \(2p\) for every gap on the base period. The continuous geometric proxy increases by at least \(4(\ln2)^2X/p^2>X/p^2\) per rank. A common gap value therefore appears at most \(1+4p^3/X\) times. This argument does not identify a wrapped gap with a base-period difference.
3. For identical lifted gap words, \(3^a\mid d_a\) from the past and \(2^{B_+}\mid d_a\) from the future. These moduli are coprime. The floor discrepancy of the forward wrap sum is less than one, and \(h/p>\log_2 3\). Thus \(3^a2^{B_+}\ge 3^L/2>10X/p\), larger than the possible absolute difference of two positive gaps each at most \(10X/p\). All \(p\) middle indices are represented since a fixed cyclic rank translation is a bijection.
4. If \(E_t\le p/83\), double counting gives average at most \(L/83\) mismatches per window. At least \(p/83\) have at most \(L/82\). Uniformly \(L=O(\log p)\), and \(t\le p/(\log p)^2\) makes the discarded cut windows at most \(O(p/\log p)\). The remaining positive fraction closes the entropy count. The \(L+1\) bound for the \(b\) word follows from at most one threshold per partial sum as the initial fractional phase traverses the unit interval.
