## The new PR197 composition

PR197 now regenerates many smaller *actual* discordant heads from one original discordant source, and gives an incidence estimate without exponential grade multiplicity. The [scoped reconstruction](head/LATEST_197.md) combines it with W2 from our head theorem. At a fixed target band \(B=d/4\), original sources with at least \(\eta B\) certified clocks admitting an opposite-phase-detected smaller head have total normalized mass

\[
 O_A(d^{-A})+O_\eta(\sqrt{\log d}\,d^{-s}),\qquad s>1.
\]

This is summable over original rows. After that discard, every remaining discordant source regenerates linearly many distinct smaller discordant heads without the tested opposite-phase witnesses. The counting keeps the original source and treats all four possible directed/terminating-phase choices by a finite union.

This is a useful composition, but it does not contract the unresolved mass. The incidence inequality still permits a profile proportional to \(j^{-1/2}\) concentrated on undetected heads. The smaller centers produced at separated physical clocks can lie exponentially farther apart than the tested stretched-exponential windows. Alternating terminating-clock phases therefore do not force a local opposite-phase witness. The full new upstream analytic package remains source-qualified here; only this stated consequence and its interfaces were reconstructed.
