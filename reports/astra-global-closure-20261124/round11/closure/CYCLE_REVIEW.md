**Denominator and orientation.** At rank zero, multiplying the rank polynomial by $X$ moves the old coefficient of rank one to rank zero and sends the old coefficient of rank zero to twice the value at the final rank. This is exactly $S$ when the coefficients are stored in descending powers. A monic polynomial has a unique remainder over a commutative ring, including rings with zero divisors. Opposite signed errors and determinant one give $q_2v_1+q_1v_2=p$. In the quotient, the monomials for those offsets consequently multiply to 2 at the stated powers. The difference of this equality and 1 gives an exact unit-ideal identity with no division. Raising to degree $m+n-1$ makes every monomial divisible by $(X^{v_1}-1)^m$ or $(X^{v_2}-1)^n$. Their simultaneous annihilation of the entire cleared array therefore recovers divisibility by the whole $D$. No factorization or separability premise enters. The $D=1$ case is immediate.

For one filter, the input recurrence still holds after applying the difference because $S$ commutes with $S^h-3$. At $3^L$ every $2^{b_r}$ and $D$ is invertible. A transported error in the initial value acquires $3^L$ in $L$ ordinary rank updates, giving the correct residue at each successive target. If all centered representatives are at most $B$ in absolute value, an edge defect has size at most $4B+3B+J_*$, below its modulus. Its vanishing makes the centered array an exact rational solution to the same recurrence. A full lap has unequal multipliers $2^h$ and $3^p$, so the solution is unique. Comparing filtered values from different ranks requires no injection of the ranks into pairs of filter values.

**Bounds and separations.** For $qh>ap$ unroll from $r$ and divide by $2^a$ to find $S^vZ$. For $qh<ap$ unroll from $r+v$ and solve instead for the starting value, dividing by $3^q$ with the opposite error sign. In the latter case the extra index $+v$ is essential. The bound using $p-1+mv+jh$ generously covers both orientations and all shifts in the first $m$ differences. Expanding $(S^v-I)^m-(\lambda-1)^m$ gives precisely the indicated $2^j E$ factors.

With $q_j\le Q<q_{j+1}$ and $q_{j+1}\le q_j^5$ eventually, both convergents $j-1,j$ have homogeneous error at most $1/q_j\le Q^{-1/5}$. Each error is at least a constant times $Q^{-4.5}$. The perturbation $Q/p$ is smaller, preserving *both* signs uniformly along the permitted sequence $h/p$. The separate integer form at $(p,h)$ gives $X_*\le p^6$ eventually. For $m=\lceil36\sqrt{\ln p}\rceil$ the log of $\eta^m X_*$ is at most $(6-36/5)\ln p+O(\sqrt{\ln p})$. The same choice gives $mv/p=o(1)$. The remaining geometric factors in $E$ are uniformly bounded, leaving $E=O_C(q)$. The resulting ternary precision and difference order are both $O(\sqrt{\ln p})$. Each letter support consists of the same past window translated by a multiple of $q$ in time. The number of specified letters, length of one window and total span have the distinct bounds stated in the packet.

The input concerns a homogeneous form in $\log 2,\log 3$. An alternative published statement is Theorem 2.1 of Fan, Queffélec and Queffélec, which gives the Diophantine exponent 4.117 for $\log 2/\log 3$. For $a$ comparable to $q$, multiplying $|a\log 2/\log 3-q|$ by $\log 3/\log 2$ transfers the bound to the reciprocal normalization. Thus exponent 4.5 suffices here. **Wide rational controls.** At the three overridden ranks only $u$ lies in the free interval for sufficiently large $p$. Its predecessor is a wrap, the final edge is a nonwrap, and both internal transitions keep their bit. Thus all four affected edges remain legal regardless of the other bits. The override changes centered partial sums on every full or partial lap by $O(1)$. The pinned proof's uniform $o(p)$ deviations survive, while consecutive nominal spacing is $\gg p$ for the upper convergents. The same comparison works at the two virtual endpoints. The rotation visits every rank once, and the physical labels advance by $-p$ modulo $h$ at both kinds of step. The table of actual ternary mantissas is therefore sorted with distinct ranks. The claimed width follows at $Ru$ and the two previous wraps at rank zero give $16s\equiv7\pmod9$. The first forward valuation is one and gives $s\equiv3\pmod4$.

For each nonzero shift each free starting bit has mismatch probability one half. Given previously revealed values, switching a remaining bit changes at most two summands. The bounded-difference tail is exponential in $p$, uniformly over the $p-1$ shifts. A positive fraction of patterns also retain the discrepancy event. Distinct successful patterns have distinct rational minima because the unique trajectory and its strict dyadic ordering recover all the labels. Coprimality of $p,h$ excludes shorter periods. The exponentially many minima exceed the polynomial count of possible positive integer minima, which proves an ordinary-denominator obstruction for the constructed *rational* examples, with no assertion about other integral words. **Fresh checks.** I recompiled the seven public inventories in LocalDenominator with pinned Lean 4.34.1 and warning-as-error, and reran the supplied integer checker. Both outputs were byte-identical to the stored records. I also ran an independent composite-modulus control with $p=7,h=13,D=6005=5\cdot1201$. It reconstructed each rotated numerator separately by multiplying the temporal affine recurrences, took differences by repeated signed vector subtraction with the doubled seam, and decoded residues with actual maximum filtered magnitudes. The two offsets and powers were $(4,2),(1,3)$. All $3^7$ words over $\{1,2,3\}$ were tested at multipliers $1,5,1201,6005$, for 8,748 cases. Of these, 4,372 failed both filters, 1,751 passed the first filter only, and 2,625 passed both. Every simultaneous result agreed with direct divisibility by 6005.
