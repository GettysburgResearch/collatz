## Re-evaluation and outcome

The attempted universal composition fails at a concrete distinction. Tower
exponents, actual upper-certificate odd counts, and evolving source heights
are different resources. A source-bounded return can introduce a new
existential good upper arm without a bound on that arm's certificate cost.
Simultaneous coverage at many bases leaves the supplied exceptional base
unresolved. The new S-unit argument proves that bounded-complexity repair
catalogues have a genuine limitation even when all their repetition clocks
are unbounded.

The cycle argument replaces selected-factor or one-way mismatch tests with
an exact local characterization of the complete denominator for each
supplied candidate. It does not prove a failing local window for every
nontrivial admissible word. Its rational controls show that the two rotation
orders and all-shift mismatch conditions alone still permit substantial
width.

The trace estimate proves a quantitative zero range on the original integer
statistic using an externally verified finite floor. Its missing extension
is the small-threshold range forced by a possible escape. The recurrence is
on one actual path, and no new independent law is assigned after a return.

The final Lean proposal records these checked finite implications and retains
the exact open universal merger premise. This pass does not provide a
complete proof of Collatz.
