**Fixed ordinary target.** In (D) and (F) the probability of a varying residue set is bounded *above*. The lower estimate from full support, $\nu(q+3^k\mathbb Z_3)\ge2^{-23\lceil k/2\rceil}$, applies even at a specified target but gives only a weak ternary-cylinder mass. Every ordinary integer belongs to a countable set of stationary measure zero. The sharper almost-everywhere exponent cannot be evaluated there by this argument. Even a lower bound on a ternary cylinder would still need transport to the actual positive, height-truncated, full-clock inverse sum for the same target. The Tao proposition concerns the independent offset law, not the conditional return of a prescribed ordinary source. [Tao, Proposition 1.14 and equations (1.22), (1.26)–(1.29)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.03562)

**Directed parent and child.** The present head count involves the product of two specified label indicators at $x$ and $\lfloor x/81\rfloor$ on the same original input, with a patch, an orientation and a terminating-time phase. A test against a single additive character, an automaton or a macroscopic interval does not bound this product. Even the two terminating phases are exchanged by a shortcut step, whereas (AUT) and (FOU) concern invariant labels. The unphased bad/terminating indicator is invariant, but the displayed estimates still supply no joint estimate along this deterministic pair. The row scale $X=81^j$ also turns the stated logarithmic saving into $O(j^{-c})$ with $c\le1/32$ for the displayed Fourier exponent; this rate alone does not imply summability even for the single-label observable it controls. The signed head bound and its reduction to one directed nonnegative series therefore retain the same unproved convergence condition.
