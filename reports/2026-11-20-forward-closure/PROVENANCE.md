## Primary literature

- [Paper 150, version 2](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/150):
  Lyapunov Weaver, *Critical barriers and memory costs in Collatz inverse
  dynamics*, 15 November 2026. Its critical all-word potential and source
  stopping covers are precedents. The new rational slope $8/5$ and the
  subprobability calculation make the local Haar-tail proof self-contained.
  The critical stationary-source theorem is not imported.
- [Paper 127, version 1](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/127):
  Lyapunov Weaver, *Stationary inverse laws, optimal window limits, and slow
  terminating Collatz families*. Its all-Borel uniqueness assertion UQ(6)
  is the external dependency retained by PR #147.
- [Gehér, arXiv:1401.5927v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.5927):
  *Asymptotic behaviour and cyclic properties of weighted shifts on directed
  trees*, Proposition 3.3. This is a direct antecedent of the general
  rank-one generation-block formula.
- [Gehér, arXiv:1407.1278](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.1278):
  *Positive operators arising asymptotically from contractions*, Theorem 1.
  This supplies the established general compactness dichotomy.

No broad novelty claim is made for weighted-shift theory, rational periodic
shadows, or source-cylinder stopping covers. The narrow additions are the
stopping-defect classification and diagonal obstruction, the quantified
ordinary forward defect and its audited logical boundary, simultaneous
binary/ternary positive rational countermodels, and the arithmetic
identification of the operator limit and finite-tail norms.
