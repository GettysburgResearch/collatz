The escape packet now proves, without external analytic premises, that
every positive ordinary orbit is bounded if and only if normalized
complete finite first-arrival rows vanish uniformly at large odd targets.
The proof starts with elementary parity counting on actual simple
physical trajectories, obtains a uniform reciprocal occupation tail,
and then a positive lower bound on every actual first-arrival mark.
The row-vanishing assertion remains open. Boundedness still allows
nontrivial finite cycles.

The merger packet formalizes the classification of finite positive affine
families by their common prime-to-six slope part, with full marked window
and first-arrival guarantees. The basic written classification is credited
to PR146. New native ratio-five theorems put actual x and 5x into the
same prescribed first-arrival clock and odd grade at y and 5y, in every
sufficiently large relative window. The grade is uniformly bounded and
the common clock has constant additive overhead above the halving bound.
Arbitrary ternary source classes and the actual binary class 49 modulo
64 are retained in the final wrapper; arbitrary binary classes are not
claimed.

The minimum-ancestor theorem supplies arbitrarily high odd-unit minima
in the inverse basin of each supplied ternary-unit target, including 1.
It proves all-ancestor size and charge bounds and identifies an obstruction
to uniform inverse-contraction proposals. The new folded-cycle module
formalizes finite signed-cut algebra; its infinite Farey and Diophantine
consequences have a separate written proof and external-input boundary.
