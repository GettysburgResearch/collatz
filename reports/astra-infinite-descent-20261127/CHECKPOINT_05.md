## 1. Exponential denominators through six odd-valuation mismatches

For a primitive positive rational cycle of odd period `p`, with complete
reduced denominator `q`, if any proper rotation of its actual odd-division
exponent word differs in at most six positions, then

`q > 2^(p/4096)` for every sufficiently large `p`.

No bound on rotation order, individual valuations, clock error, or
physical spread is assumed. In full shortcut period `h`, the same class
has lower limiting `log_2(q)/h` greater than `1/21600`. Thus only finitely
many primitive positive integer cycles belong to this class. The onset
is ineffective, and those possible exceptions have not been excluded.
Cycles with at least seven mismatches at every proper rotation are not
settled by this theorem.

The [written proof](../../research/astra-infinite-descent-20261127/cycles/ROTATION_DENOMINATORS.md)
retains Evertse's theorem and the classical minimum bound as external
inputs. Lean proves the physical shifted-charge and higher-start carry
bounds, the 29 exact matrix leaves covering all 25 geometries, and the
real interpolation step. It does not formalize the external Diophantine
theorem or the entire infinite argument. A separate evaluator reconstructs
the certificates independently of their producer, and native cycles
check the chain identities and their nonvanishing subsums.

A second [written spectral theorem](../../research/astra-infinite-descent-20261127/cycles/SPECTRAL_ROTATIONS.md)
handles every proper odd-valuation rotation when the minimum is at least
`h^(2-o(1))` and the ratio of maximum to minimum odd height is at most
`2+h^(-1+o(1))`. It gives the uniform lower relation
`s(lag) * log_2(q)/h >= 1-o(1)`; constructed positive rational families
attain a limiting product of four at a specified proper lag. Sharpness
of the lower constant one remains open. The physical regime is an
explicit hypothesis, and the examples have denominator greater than one.

## 3. Controlled inverse clocks and a formal mass obstruction

with each gap at most six and `6m <= 13d+48` at each requested grade `d`.
The large potential has 1,062,882 local cases checked by exact arithmetic.
Its physical lift and induction are formal under that finite premise.
Combining it with the ordinary pattern router supplies every sufficiently
large exact grade and every prescribed clock above slope `13/6`, with
the constants depending on the fixed finite pattern and source modulus.
This does not also prescribe an arbitrary real source window.

A separate 243-entry table and its 729 inequalities are entirely checked
in Lean. At every positive odd target prime to three they imply total capped inverse multiplier
mass at least `0.052*(3/4)^d`. At the nonperiodic target five, distinct
ordinary first-hit sources turn this into a positive-row lower bound.
It rules out polynomial times `(2/3)^d` for that raw positive replacement
of the signed statistic. It does not rule out vanishing at a slower rate,
and it does not refute the desired bound for the original signed `Q*`.

## 4. The original signed statistic becomes a finite exact target

The [finite original-rate theorem](../../research/astra-infinite-descent-20261127/escape/FINITE_ORIGINAL_RATE.md)
uses exactly the original all-visit signed comparisons. Its finite
rational statistic `V_j` retains odd targets
`1 < y <= ceil((3/2)^j)` and total gap clocks at most `8(j+1)` and satisfies

`|Q*_j - V_j| <= (12j+18)*(2/3)^j`.

Consequently the required polynomial-times-`(2/3)^j` subsequence condition
is equivalent for `Q*` and these finite statistics. The condition itself
is unproved. The earlier unconditional written dyadic mean bound
`sum_{D <= j < 2D} Q*_j = O(sqrt(D))` is much weaker.

The narrow marked component is zero at every positive odd target greater
than one through grade **92,274,687**. This combines the formal `NarrowBand`
transfer with a separately replayed finite convergence prefix through
`2^22`. It is a range for one component, not a proof of the full signed
bound or an integer verification record claim.

## 5. Universal statements still missing

The packet formally proves that full Collatz is equivalent to arbitrarily
small explicit finite reciprocal sums over surviving shortcut prefixes
with the inherited `5j <= 8q_j` gate. The universal small-mass estimate
remains open. Two other kernel theorems characterize boundedness of all
ordinary orbits by uniform vanishing of complete first-arrival rows,
indexed either by target height or by odd grade with reciprocal-source
damping. Those uniform limits remain open; boundedness alone would still
leave nontrivial cycles.

None of the finite source families, denominator restrictions, dense
inverse constructions, or approximate trace tables supplies these
missing universal assertions.
