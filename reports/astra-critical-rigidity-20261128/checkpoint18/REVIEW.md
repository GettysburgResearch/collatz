## Moving width and denominator

[Polylogarithmic near-octave residence](../../../research/astra-critical-rigidity-20261128/cycles/POLYLOGARITHMIC_NEAR_OCTAVE_RESIDENCE.md)
sets \(x=\log\log M\), \(\kappa=\mu_0-1\). Under
\[
 \eta\ll x^{-\alpha},\quad
 \log(2Q)\ll(\log M)x^\beta,\quad
 \alpha>\kappa,\quad0\le\beta<\alpha/\kappa-1,
\]
every distinct actual odd run in \([M,2^{1+\eta}M]\) has
\(T\le(\log M)^{3+o(1)}\).
For ordinary integers or polynomially bounded \(Q\), every
\(\alpha>4.116201\) is available from the classical linear-form bound;
the separately audited logarithm input allows every \(\alpha>2\).

The uniform inverse generalized Vandermonde bound is the key new estimate.
Schur positivity and homogeneity bound the full alternant and every cofactor.
The exact ratio of specializations at one is
\[
 \frac{(d-1)!}{\prod_{r\ne i}|\ell_r-\ell_i|}
 \le\binom{d-1}{i-1}.
\]
An inverse entry therefore costs an exponential in dimension times the
inverse product of node gaps, without a hidden growing-dimension constant.

For hypothetical \(T=(\log M)^{3+\theta}\), choose
\(L=\lfloor x^u\rfloor\), \(1+\beta<u<\alpha/\kappa'\), and
\(h\asymp\log M/\log T\), where \(\kappa'>\kappa\).
The largest prime used is \(m=O(\log M)\).

contains \(K\gg T/m^2\) labels. Every dilation stays inside one hundredth
of the common path. The full dyadic horizon is
\(O(\log M\,x^\beta)=o(T)\).
A nonzero integer collision coefficient would be at most
\(\exp(-\log M+2h\kappa'\log T+O(h+\log T+\log h))<1\).
The exact \(B_h\) code then gives \(\log K\le(1+o(1))x\), contradicting
\(\log K\ge(1+\theta+o(1))x\).

## Actual sparse high targets and finite high traces

[Sparse high targets](../../../research/astra-critical-rigidity-20261128/ordinary/SPARSE_HIGH_TARGETS.md)
first proves, for every \(\kappa<1/2\),
\[
 \Pr(\exists t\le\tau(U_X):T^tU_X\in E)
 \ll_\kappa (|E|/Y)^\kappa+o(1)
\]
uniformly over actual \(E\subset[Y,2Y]\), with \(X,Y\to\infty\)
independently. The following sharper note reaches the endpoint.

A true odd word and target determine at most one source. The reversed
valuation word supplies its necessary ternary target residue, and the
source weight is bounded by the exact word probability times \(3^k/Y\).
Folding the mask onto a finite ternary partition gives a bounded function
\(f\), of Haar mean \(|E|/Y\), with conditional means \(f_k\).
The real drift \(s_k-\gamma k\) has positive mean and uniformly bounded
occupation moments in every fixed-width interval. Reversal is used only
in the iid representation of the finite word count.

The last true odd state handles even targets, with geometric summation
over their lifted scales. Original uniform first-passage transport and
the stopped-height theorem handle arbitrarily large source/target
ratios. Initial even strips and small sources are treated separately.
No relative growth of \(X,Y\) is imposed.

For every \(o(Y)\) mask the whole visited subset converges in total
variation to the mixture of true eventual-component traces.
Maximizing over masks of size \(k\) selects the \(k\) largest divergent
component densities. With \(k\to\infty\), \(k=o(Y)\), it detects total
divergent mass. Its vanishing permits nontrivial cycles.

[Finite high orbit intersections](../../../research/astra-critical-rigidity-20261128/ordinary/FINITE_HIGH_ORBIT_INTERSECTIONS.md)
proves that \(k\) independently sampled finite true paths, with cutoffs
\(X_i\) and arbitrary deadlines \(H_i\), share a vertex of \([Y,2Y]\)
with probability
\[
 \sum_{C\ {\rm divergent}}d_C^k
 \prod_i\Phi\left(
 \frac{H_i-a_C(Y)-\mu\log X_i}{\sigma\sqrt{\log X_i}}
 \right)+o(1).
\]
The error is uniform over deadlines; the visit dates need not agree.

The original bound for every orbit in every located interval is essential:
conditioning on another independent path gives a deterministic uniformly
sparse mask. This removes common preminimum vertices even for varying
sources. Retained minimum states in one component share an actual tail;
their first high-vertex dates differ by fixed offsets. The joint
minimum-clock law and proper finite truncation yield the displayed
product. The original recurrence bounds
\(a_C(Y)=O_\theta(Y^\theta)\) for every
\(\theta>H_2(\log_3 2)\).

## Sharp physical square-root modulus and attribution

[Sharp sparse target exponent](../../../research/astra-critical-rigidity-20261128/ordinary/SHARP_SPARSE_TARGET_EXPONENT.md)

\[
 \mathcal H(\delta)\asymp\sqrt\delta.
\]
Here \(\mathcal H\) is the joint large-cutoff upper envelope of the
worst preminimum hitting probability over masks of at most \(\delta Y\)

explicit envelope; some other cutoff pairs have negligible probability.
