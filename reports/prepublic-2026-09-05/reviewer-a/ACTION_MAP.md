## Exact open obligations to retain

1. **Fixed-floor exceptional mass:** prove an upper count below the inverse exponent, or the critical little-o/sublogarithmic variant, for actual eternal survivors at one certified floor. Native parity-root/minimum sparsity does not control basin fibers.
2. **Survivor-specific pullback:** instantiate a quantitative gain for the dynamically surviving endpoint set. The all-subset positive saving is already refuted.
3. **Uniform Green mass:** bound the explicit `G_K` uniformly in K. The finite source tail grows with K at fixed cutoff. The fixed exponential moment imposes a stronger stopping-rate burden than qualitative convergence.
4. **Transported distribution:** control the actual ensemble, paying the discrepancy after every reuse. Fresh-shell averages and the signed telescoping identity alone do not supply this.
5. **Complete physical merging:** supply a proved total rule choosing finite forward and inverse clocks and a genuinely lower-rank ordinary source. Forward motion is necessary: 121 has no cheaper ancestor at any inverse depth. Finite inverse-radius termination is not a total cover.
6. **Resident SC*/FC*/cycles:** existing open statuses survive this review. None of the imported density statements or local merging families removes these obligations by implication without its own missing hypothesis.

Do not count overlapping finite covers additively: the 37 minimum-rank tiles, 425 homogeneous rules, 263 clock-defect rules, three radius-two progressions, and AS7 families were compiled for different populations and can overlap.
