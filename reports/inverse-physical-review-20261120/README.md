## Independent direct verification of the published strategy

[`verify_physical.cpp`](verify_physical.cpp) was written independently. It
imports no synthesis code or precomputed transition table and performs no
Bellman iteration. It computes every physical transition directly from its
ordinary residue and checks the **published policy bits**, not merely the
existence of some valid action.

It was built with `g++ -O3 -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -Werror` and run on
the full int32 table with parameters `17 25 12 --policy POLICY.bits`.
The command exited zero. It checked:

* all **86,093,442** unit rows, including every possible next ternary digit
  for each legal odd physical action;
* all **43,046,721** reconstruction identities
  `W(r)=12+W(2r)` for `r==1 mod 3`;
* every terminal inequality `12*a_final(r)-W(r)<=12`, with exact maximum 12;
* the complete doubling cycle to extract the selected inverse exponents,
  obtaining maximum **10**;
* all **258,280,326** collapsed odd-gap lift inequalities, checked directly
  rather than inferred from telescoping.

The maximum credit is 133. There are 22,726,538 selected odd physical rows.
Both the minimum selected physical slack and the minimum collapsed slack
are zero. [Full scalar receipt and action counts](physical_25_12.json).

For the terminal inequality there is also a short independent proof. In
class two the final exponent is 1 and the potential is nonnegative. In
class one the final exponent is 2 and the exact even-action identity gives
`W(r)>=12`. Thus the terminal payment minus current credit is at most 12
in both classes. The total numerator is consequently
`25t+133+12-25=25t+120`, not the weaker bound obtained by discarding all
terminal credit.
