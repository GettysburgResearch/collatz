## New results

- `L-9895` proves opposite tropical determinant geometry at the two
  distinguished primes.  Every adjacent Casoratian is controlled by the
  reverse permutation at `2` and the identity permutation at `3`.  The
  canonical cofactor vector therefore has projective height at least
  `max(2^(S_2),3^(S_3))`; both exponents are quadratic whenever the width is
  a positive fraction of the top order.
- `R-9805` closes the tempting next inference negatively.  The evaluated
  numerator and denominator are controlled by the same endpoint cofactor at
  each prime.  Their reduced ratio has `v_2=0` and the fixed value
  `v_3=4(1+9(S+m))`, independent of order and width.  Thus the quadratic
  cofactor cost is genuine but invisible in the final ratio at `2` and `3`.
- `L-9896` proves that the full delayed one-hot obstruction has relative
  ordinary natural density one in every suffix-exposure progression.  Each
  fixed delay has density `64^(-(m+1))`; delays spaced by `m+1` are exactly
  independent.  For every fixed `s`, depths with at least `s` lower
  block-zero competitors have relative density one, including separately in
  all eight phases outside `n=1 mod36`.
- `L-9897` proves a qualitative fresh-prime theorem for exact H bridges.
  Reused prime exponents pay an exact multiplicative-order valuation tax,
  every transition pays through its next room or its forward primitive
  factor, and no fixed finite prime support can occur infinitely often on a
  nonperiodic exact chain.  Globally new primes therefore appear infinitely
  often with divergent unweighted logarithmic mass.
- `L-9898` constructs the requested cap seam graph.  A triple is one of 1024
  five-symbol states and has 64 candidate successors.  At scales `m=12,13`,
  the first seam has no edge modulo `2048`; words still survive modulo `1024`,
  so that separating modulus is minimal.  This is an exact two-scale
  obstruction, not an all-late-scale theorem.

## Failed or closed routes

- The linearly wide adjacent-order cofactor vector cannot be projectively
  small, but its two defining prime factors cancel under evaluation.  A
  reduced-height proof must use the real place, other primes, or a global
  evaluation determinant.
- The positive-Haar-measure avoidance left by the selected family in
  `L-9892` does not survive the full lift family.  Avoidance now has Haar and
  natural density zero, although infinitely many exceptional ordinary depths
  are not excluded.
- A fixed finite alphabet of bridge-core primes cannot support a nonperiodic
  H chain.  The surviving escape is sparse arrival of new primes, compatible
  with the exponentially discounted budget.
- Two empty cap scales do not establish eventual emptiness.  The full
  canonical odd-radix carry is not closed on the 1024 low-bit seam states.

## Organizational improvement ideas

the missing induction variable.  Density statements should distinguish the
formal profinite parameter, finite periodic patterns, and valid ordinary

wave eleven's strongest formulas from becoming false global conclusions.
