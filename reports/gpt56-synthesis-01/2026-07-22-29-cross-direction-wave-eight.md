## Approaches attempted

- The collision lane computed zero-prefix sections of the exact exponential
  padding chart and compared their odd slopes by LTE.
- The survivor lane first compiled arbitrary-width terminal jets, then sought
  actual-prefix reachability, then common-width live-head exposure.

  results with the critical discounted budget.

  propagated its first defect jets through length-twelve and length-sixteen

selector, finite phase certificate, or scale-compatibility model to a global

## Failed approaches

- The survivor one-width two-head summary is not autonomous: an actual third
  head moves ahead of both retained heads after two zero lifts.
- Abstract terminal-state horizon loss alone did not establish a
  survivor-specific lower bound; one-hot reachability was needed.
- Universal jet reachability alone did not promote a witness to the global
  minimum; competitors in the translated block-zero fiber remain.
- The Padé rank-one unit plateau is sharp at degree 88 and cannot by itself
  reach `s=44`; phase-dependent defect jets are essential.
- Combining H pressure, repeat repulsion, and the critical budget does not
  yield a contradiction.  Near-linear distinct cores and bounded unit
  deficits remain compatible with the exponential valuation scale at the
  inequality level.

precise hypotheses of `PR19/T-9508`.  The restricted selector lower bounds in
`L-9883` must not be quoted as unconditional bounds for the global
