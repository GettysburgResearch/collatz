## Results

### Finite collision and padding compilers

- `L-9808` gives an exact geometric polynomial
  `P_(m,L)=-V_m sum_(j<L)(-2^e V_m)^j` for the inverse bulk. Its error has
  exact valuation `Le`, its minimal depth for `Q` bits is `ceil(Q/e)`, and it
  obeys an append-only law.
- `L-9809` applies the imported Mahler 2-adic Hermite--Lindemann theorem to
  show that the normalized bulk limit is transcendental. Its binary word is
  not eventually periodic and has factor complexity at least `n+1`.
- `L-9810` shows that an algebraic exponential counter isometry cannot map a
  rational 2-adic address to that transcendental target. Hence the completed
  address is nonrational and its finite prefix word is nonperiodic.
- `L-9811` transports the finite compiler through any 2-adic isometry with no
  loss of precision. It also proves the exact scale odometer
  `v_2(s_(m+1)-s_m)=e_m-1`.
- `L-9817` expresses the address increment through a finite 2-adic logarithm
  and gives explicit truncation budgets for all four physical padding charts.
- `L-9820` resolves the last one-bit interface: the old boundary bit is one
  ordinary quotient parity of `V_m` and the already-known address prefix.
  The next stable bit is its complement. No inverse-permutation oracle or
  higher target precision is needed.

The finite transition problem is therefore reduced to realizing ordinary
modular exponentiation and one exact quotient parity inside the physical
residual grammar. Transcendence rules out eventual periodicity and an
autonomous no-input finite generator, but it does **not** rule out
2-automaticity; Christol's criterion leaves that question open.

### Finite survivor renewal and pairwise rigidity

- `L-9812` proves that the finite minimum `M_K` remains unchanged at the next
  depth exactly when its endpoint `m_K` is congruent to `0` or `1 mod 64`.
  The simultaneous base-`5184` extension digit vanishes under the same legal
  endpoint condition.
- `L-9815` defines the exact cyclic successor gap `D_K(A)` between surviving
  residue classes. It gives monotonicity, plateau, and renewal laws for these
  gaps. An internal exact replay extends the stored minimum-root word to depth
  47, obtains a legal zero endpoint at that depth, and therefore proves the
  depth-48 plateau for that replay.
- `L-9819` proves that a common aligned directive block of length `r` forces
  `r < delta s + log_64(d+1)` for two survivor starts separated by `d`.
  It also turns bounded aligned Hamming distance into an exponential lower
  bound on `d`.
- `L-9818` identifies a sufficient correlation mechanism: a chart-exact
  padded-cylinder router whose common suffix merges the two states. Bounded
  reset delay plus bounded ordinary addition-carry avalanche already forces
  an exponential successor gap with leading exponent `1/(2+delta)`.

This isolates the exact missing theorem. Single-word complexity is
insufficient; one needs either bounded pair merging for the minimum and its
successor, or an equivalent direct cross-correlation statement.

### Genuine H crossing families and the return obstruction

- `L-9813` inserts the ordered H offset sum into the first-crossing inequality
  and proves strict displacement for several infinite positive-density
  families. These are actual H words, not abstract affine tuples.
- `L-9814` classifies fixed-suffix phase windows. The two suffixes `30` and
  `10` cover the whole multiplier interval `(1,4/3)`, with a uniform strict
  margin; no single suffix can do so, making the compiler cardinality-optimal.
- `R-9803` proves why this local compiler does not automatically iterate.
  Natural post-crossing renormalization retains an unbounded 2-adic tail
  coordinate, and the rational tail addresses encountered by the compiled
  outputs are infinitely many. Finite phase-only, bounded-carry, and
  fixed-address closures are therefore impossible.

The H frontier has moved from “find one strict suffix” to a true skew-product
return problem on multiplier phase and an unbounded tail coordinate.

### Padé cyclotomic cancellation

- `L-9816` proves the exact diagonal common-factor order and exact local
  period-four factors.
- Finite q-Lucas block factorization yields growing cyclotomic multiplicity
  `floor(D/(2L_d))` for every eligible root order, including both even and odd
  sectors when the block length is four.
- The full automatic factor product is quantified asymptotically for arbitrary
  starting height. At the strongest available divisor amplification, the best
  resulting exponent is approximately `0.997872`, still below one.
- Homogenization is performed before specialization, and every remaining
  prime-to-81 arithmetic gcd is supported by the cofactor resultant.

Thus automatic cyclotomic cancellation alone cannot close period four. The
only remaining possibilities are exceptional residual blocks,
noncyclotomic polynomial factors, or arithmetic specialization gcds.

### Automata audit

The regular-sanctuary packet on PR #12 contains finite canonical binary
acceptors, not the padded base-64 letter-to-letter directive router required
by `L-9818`. Its first-hit automaton `H_d` does have a genuine sharp
`(d+3)`-suffix synchronization property at the transition-skeleton level,
and collapsing its two tail states gives a reset sink. That quotient loses
the Moore colors, its delay grows with the safety horizon, and recurrent
products such as parity have no reset word. This is useful negative
information: generic regularity cannot silently be substituted for the
needed pairwise chart transduction.

## Candidate counterexamples

## Failed approaches and negative information

- Transcendence and nonperiodicity do not imply nonautomaticity.
- A finite inverse permutation is an exact specification, not yet a physical
  local rewrite implementation.
- Finite survivor minima can plateau; finite renewal does not imply infinitely
  many renewals.
- Single-word directive complexity gives no aligned correlation with the next
  surviving word.
- A small ordinary successor gap can still create one long base-64 carry
  avalanche.
- A regular safety acceptor need not synchronize, and a transition reset may
  destroy output semantics.
- The optimal H suffix cover cannot close in multiplier phase alone; the tail
  coordinate cannot remain in finitely many ordinary charts.
- Large automatic Padé factors still leave a positive quadratic-size deficit.
