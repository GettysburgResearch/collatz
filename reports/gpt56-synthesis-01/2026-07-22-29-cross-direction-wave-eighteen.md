### `T-9820` -- the base cell and symbol offset share one carry

Write the base-cell correction as `r_(0,0)=WJ+V`, let
`u_m=(PN_2)^(-1) mod 2^(L_m)`, and define the exact scaled two-transition word

```text
Z_m=(64PV+N_1Y_0+b_b)/T_2.
```

The sum that previously looked like two independent pieces factors exactly:

```text
s_(m,c)=[J_m+delta_(m,c)]_(2^L)
       =[u_m H_(m,c)]_(2^L),
H_(m,c)=T_3 p_0-b_c-N_2 Z_m.
```

Thus there is only one inverse defect.  At the next scale, if
`h=v_2(H_(m+1,c))`, multiplication by the connector coefficient erases
exactly `h` of the `Delta_m=2849*2^(m-8)-1` potential carry bits, up to the
full width.  An odd coefficient preserves all of them isometrically.

Parity makes the conclusion uniform across the four third-symbol lanes:
according to the parity of `Z_m`, exactly one or exactly three coefficients
are odd.  On every odd lane the universal compatible-lift fiber still fills
all 64 top blocks, each `2^(Delta_m-6)` times.  Adjoining the base cell
therefore does not close a universal bounded transition.  This is not an
actual branching theorem: the distinguished source may correlate `Z_(m+1)`
with its computable carry, and the decoded room lane need not be one of the
odd lanes.

### `T-9821` -- the ten-phase combined-moment system has full rank

For the native periodic tail, combine the residue phases before constructing
approximants.  Its moments are

```text
u_N=R^(N(N-1)/2) Z^N P_W(X lambda^N).
```

Every minor `det(u_(s_i+t_j))` has a phase-expanded generalized-Vandermonde
formula whose real terms share one sign.  At `p=2` the unique tropical term is
the reverse permutation with phase zero in every row; at `p=3` it is the
identity permutation with phase `r-1` in every row.  This proves every square
combined-moment minor nonzero and gives exact endpoint valuations for
arbitrary row and column orders.

The canonical Hankel system therefore has a unique denominator `B_n`.  Its
Cramer coefficients and every bordered error determinant have exact local
valuations.  Most importantly,

```text
v_2(B_n(1)H(1)-A_n(1))
 =6{2 zeta n+27Sr n(n-1)/2}
 =81Sr n^2+O(n).
```

Thus the prescribed ten-phase sum has no hidden cancellation in its first
evaluated error and pays no ten-phase allocation penalty.  This still does
not prove irrationality: raw determinants have cubic global height.  The
remaining target is the explicit normalized estimate

```text

 <=(81Sr-eta)n^2+o(n^2)
```

on an infinite nonproportional subsequence.  Ordinary Vandermonde content is
visible but insufficient; additional common-factor/content cancellation in
the normalized alternants is required.

### `T-9822` -- every positive `4/5` survivor oscillates at every scale

For a hypothetical positive PR #35 chart survivor put `M_n=A_n+2`, so

```text
epsilon_n=M_n mod 4,
4M_(n+1)=5M_n-epsilon_n.
```

Both symbols occur infinitely often.  If `h<h'` are consecutive occurrences
of `s`, direct transport through the intervening opposite-symbol run gives

```text
v_2(5M_h-4+3s)=2(h'-h).
```

Consequently, with `alpha=log_4 5`, every same-symbol gap obeys

```text
h'-h < (alpha-1)h+log_4(5M_0).
```

Each symbol occurs in every corresponding affine multiplicative shell, and
so does a phase switch.  For the symbol counts `N_s(Y)` and switch count
`S(Y)`,

```text
liminf N_s(Y)/log Y >= 1/log(alpha),
liminf S(Y)/log Y   >= 1/log(alpha)
                         = 6.70013449289...,
limsup r_k^(1/k) <= alpha
```

for ordered switch positions `r_k`.  This turns the completion-height target
into a native grammar test: subcritical minority-symbol or switch counts, or
super-`alpha` switch growth, exclude survival.

The complementary finite-language theorem is equally important.  Every
binary word of length `L` is the initial chart word of infinitely many
positive roots, in the explicit residue class

```text
M_0 = 5^(-L) D_L mod 4^L.
```

No fixed forbidden-block census can provide the needed upper bound.  A
successful grammar must transport one-root residue/carry coherence through
unbounded depth.
