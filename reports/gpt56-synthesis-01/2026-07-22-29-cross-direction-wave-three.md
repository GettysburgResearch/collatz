## Starting question

The first two waves produced exact finite compilers, cylinder laws, and
completion obstructions. The third wave asked whether the remaining proposed
finite-state shortcuts survive once their hidden state is written exactly.
The answer was repeatedly useful even when negative: the inverse-cylinder
router, H return compiler, component-coloring kernel, survivor inverse limit,
and Padé exceptional sector all admit substantially sharper state geometry
than their source packets exposed.

The common discipline was:

1. derive the exact causal or inverse-limit state before positing reset;
2. distinguish ambient symbolic branching from the physically selected path;
3. count the entire automatic or squarefree sector before extrapolating its
   asymptotic effect;
4. turn failed shortcuts into reusable boundary theorems.

## Parallel proof program

- The adelic/successor lane derived the inverse base-64 carry transduction,
  settled ordinary borrow, and then identified all survivor-pair differences
  as a signed ternary full shift.
- The collision/padding lane coupled the finite bulk counter to the actual
  outgoing residual and isolated the exact one-bit state missing from a
  bulk-only physical lift.
- The H lane solved the renormalized real phase, affine fiber, and 2-adic tail
  shifts of the `10/30` suffix compiler, then intersected the abstract tail
  tree with exact physical cylinders and analyzed ordinary stabilization.
- The Padé lane normalized exceptional residual orders, completed the
  reduced-prime and antisymmetric sectors, counted prime-power support, and
  classified the small-remainder odd-prime-power range before deriving an
  exact characteristic-two descent for the dyadic augmentation order.
- The integrating and adelic lanes reconstructed invariant-component
  periodic rigidity, reduced binary automaticity to translated ternary
  states, and closed every fixed-width translated cone.
- The integrating lane identified the survivor inverse limit as an exact
  metric full shift and kept its countable ordinary intersection separate
  from its fractal geometry.

All formulas were rederived by the integrating agent. Small exact searches
were used only to audit proved recurrences and multiplicities.

## Results

### Exact inverse-cylinder and successor-pair geometry

- `R-9804` constructs the exact input-digit/carry transduction. At each stage
  there are two legal base-64 input digits, the exact reachable carry set has
  size `2^n`, and the zero-input carry map is injective. Two carries agree for
  `r` zero outputs exactly when they are congruent modulo `64^r`. The legal
  fixed pair `0,1` therefore rules out every globally chart-exact bounded zero
  reset router.
- `L-9830` separates ordinary addition borrow from chart propagation. Once
  the base-64 borrow for a finite ordinary gap has settled, the carry
  difference is exactly a signed chart iterate with digits in `{-1,0,1}`.
  Its base-64 valuation is exactly the length of the later common directive
  block. An explicit signed numerator gives a general valuation bound, but
  lexicographic successor minimality has not yet bounded its cancellation.
- `L-9834` closes the ambient pair state space. At depth `n`, the `2^n`
  survivor cylinders have exactly `3^n` differences. The unique signed word
  for a difference has representation multiplicity `2^z`, where `z` is its
  number of zero digits, so the additive energy is exactly `6^n`. Every
  valuation stratum and its ordered-pair weight are explicit; in the limit the
  difference set is a ternary full shift of dimension `log_64(3)` and Haar
  measure zero.
- `L-9847` turns each signed-word multiplicity into an exact common-translation
  fiber. If one representation is a cyclic successor, every other translation
  must avoid the two open intervals `(0,g)` and `(Q-g,Q)` relative to it;
  endpoints are correctly allowed. Repeated successor arcs of that word have
  disjoint interiors, giving `s_n(eta)g(eta)<=Q`.

This replaces an unspecified pair-correlation problem by one precise order
problem: control the pointed largest gaps of the exact translation fibers.
Global pair probabilities and representation multiplicity alone do not
control the `2^n` cyclic successor edges.

### Collision/padding residual interface

`L-9852` closes the first local bulk-to-physical interface. If the padding
address is compiled from a bulk word `V` through precision `H`, it routes the
actual outgoing tail `h` exactly on the cylinder
`h congruent V modulo 2^H`. On that cylinder the next physical address bit is
the bulk quotient bit XOR `qbit_H(h-V)`. The same law is verified after
clearing the physical chart denominator: the two ordinary numerators differ,
after the exact power-of-two divisor, by the odd multiple
`3^G(h-V)/2^H`.

For the odd-affine zipper tail `h(y)=psi+Ny`, compatibility selects a unique
class of `y modulo 2^H`. Its two successive lifts in the quotient coordinate
have the same lower physical prefix but opposite next padding bits. This is a
sharp obstruction to every bulk-only next-bit rule, while the XOR formula is
a positive realization once one residual-defect bit is exposed. Propagating
that cylinder through the zipper and routing the remaining `K-H` connector
block remain open.

### H phase, tail, physicality, and nonordinary stabilization

- `L-9822` shows that deleting the common terminal zero from the two completed
  suffixes exposes an irrational circle rotation on `(1,81/64]`; its branch
  coding is Sturmian with complexity `n+1` and exact frequencies. The actual
  completed suffix remains contracting, so this rotation is renormalized
  symbolic dynamics rather than a physical repeated crossing.
- `L-9824` identifies the real affine skew product. In the normalized fiber
  coordinate, each step adds `1/(4R')`; the drift is uniformly positive with
  an exact asymptotic average. Fiber differences are preserved and the
  pointwise Lyapunov exponent is zero, ruling out a hidden contracting graph.
- `L-9827` derives the exact 2-adic tail maps for suffixes `10` and `30`.
  They are respectively `128`- and `8192`-to-one inverse shifts and expand
  within-cylinder 2-adic distances rather than contracting them.
- `L-9829` composes those shifts along every Sturmian phase orbit. The consumed
  precision is `K_n=kappa_* n+O(1)` with universal discrepancy below six, and
  the abstract preimage degree is `2^(K_n)`.
- `L-9831` clears the odd denominators in that abstract tree. One mixed-radix
  congruence selects exactly one tuple of interface blocks, and exact cylinder
  concatenation proves that this tuple also passes the separate real interval
  condition. Thus physicality leaves one nested path, not exponentially many.
- `L-9833` decides the ordinary-section question for this architecture. The
  nested input is ordinary nonnegative exactly if its interface blocks are
  eventually zero. But a zero block maps `72+128j` to `46+81j` or
  `4608+8192j` to `3691+6561j`, strictly lowering a positive integral
  endpoint. Eventual zero blocks would create an infinite descent. Therefore
  every infinite `10/30` schedule has a nonordinary 2-adic seed.
- `L-9835` quantifies that descent. The two zero-block slopes equal `3/4`
  times the telescoping phase multiplier, so every zero run is logarithmic in
  its starting endpoint. Along the physical Sturmian carry stream there are
  at least logarithmically many nonzero blocks through depth `n`. Exact zero
  paths of all finite lengths show the logarithmic endpoint scale is sharp;
  unbounded nonzero resets still prevent a positive-density conclusion.
- `L-9837` resolves the local reset bookkeeping. A future length-`L` all-zero
  cylinder is reached exactly when the present ordinary quotient matches one
  internal dyadic carry modulo the future denominator. Reachability forces
  that residue below the finite quotient range and becomes an equality test
  once the future modulus exceeds the current odd coordinate. Long cylinders
  are therefore cofinally excluded after every fixed reset, but the resulting
  threshold remains linear in the stage.

The present H suffix architecture is therefore ruled out as an ordinary
infinite return, even though every finite exact concatenation exists. A future
return theorem must change the suffix architecture or add a genuinely new
state-reset mechanism.

`L-9840` makes that design lesson general. For any finite exact H suffix
family, every zero interface strictly lowers every positive endpoint exactly
when each suffix satisfies `U_z>=V_z` and `A_z>Y_z`. Such a family has no
ordinary nonnegative nested seed under any infinite schedule. The exact
complementary quotient table identifies the necessary escape hatch: a viable
replacement must recurrently use a suffix violating one inequality at a
reachable non-descent quotient.

`L-9841` then supplies a constructive escape. The full completed family `r0`
has explicit canonical data; every `r>=5` expands every zero-interface
endpoint. The three suffixes `{30,60,70}` form a closed raw multiplier system:
`30` crosses from expansion to contraction, while `60` or `70` returns from
contraction to expansion. The two-step phase map is an irrational rotation on
`(1,9/8]`, with exact branch frequencies. This is the first finite raw return
architecture in the packet, but its affine offset and 2-adic carry fibers
remain live, so it is not yet an ordinary seed or orbit.

`L-9843` solves the real affine part of that new return. In the normalized
coordinate `w=q/R`, each two-step return adds a uniformly positive cocycle.
Its every-orbit mean is explicit, while fiber derivatives telescope to
`R_n/R_0`, giving zero Lyapunov exponent and preserved fiber differences.
There is no bounded or integrable invariant graph, bounded fiber orbit, or
cycle. The only plausible closure coordinate left is therefore the exact
2-adic carry fiber, not the raw real phase or affine offset.

`L-9845` solves that inverse 2-adic fiber. The `3060` and `3070` inverse maps
contract by exactly `2^-35` and `2^-38`, so every phase itinerary selects one
endpoint, always of valuation nine. Their offsets first differ at bit 31,
giving an exact recursive decoder modulo `2^32` and a strongly separated
full-shift attractor of dimension about `0.0274133130`. The fixed-slope
Sturmian slice has only `n+1` depth-`n` cylinders and therefore Hausdorff
dimension zero. By itself this leaves ordinary realization open: recursive
address aperiodicity alone does not prove 2-adic irrationality.

`L-9846` gives the necessary signature of any ordinary intersection: its
macro endpoints are positive integers of valuation nine, grow between the
explicit linear cocycle bounds with `N_n/(nR_n)->bar(phi)`, and their two
modulo-`2^32` residues form the aperiodic Sturmian branch word. This remains a
conditional signature; `L-9850` shows that it cannot be realized here.

`L-9850` closes the ordinary section of the raw-return graph. Its residue
decoder makes hypothetical integer endpoints pairwise distinct, while exact
drift and triangular phase equidistribution force `C_* y T+o(T)` of them below
`yT`, with `C_*=16/[7(1+1/mu_3)]>1` exactly because `59049>57344`. Distinct
positive integers allow at most `yT+O(1)`, a contradiction. Thus the graph has
no ordinary integer; the argument does not rely on an aperiodic-address
irrationality converse.

`L-9851` abstracts the packing mechanism. For a continuous positive phase
scale over a compact uniquely ergodic base, eventual positive integers of
multiplicity at most `M` and normalized growth `N_n/(nR_n)->m` must satisfy
`m^(-1) integral R^(-1)dnu<=M`. The proof derives triangular product
equidistribution on proportional time blocks. The raw phase seam is handled
separately by the Weyl argument already present in `L-9850`.

### Invariant components and automaticity

- `L-9823` proves elementary periodic rigidity for finite component
  colorings: factors two and three can be stripped from a putative period,
  and the remaining affine actions generate translation by one. Hence every
  eventually periodic coloring is constant. Pólya--Carlson then gives a unit-
  circle natural boundary for every nonconstant integer coloring, excluding
  rational, algebraic, D-finite, Presburger, semilinear, and unary-regular
  descriptions.
- `L-9825` shows that binary automaticity makes the central ternary dilates
  `{s_(3^e n)}` finite. An exact binary-section table explains why translated
  ternary states, not the central spine, are the missing Cobham hypothesis.
- `L-9836` closes every fixed-width translated cone. The central spine and
  the binary kernel share one finite section-closed family; applying a fixed
  affine multiplier preserves a uniform finite automaton. Every coheight-`k`
  ternary cone therefore has at most `3^k K` states and combined binary kernel
  at most `2*3^k K`. Its entire profile is eventually periodic in the scale
  variable. The full ternary kernel is the increasing union of these cones,
  so only unbounded-coheight state creation remains uncontrolled at this stage.
- `L-9838` replaces the crude `3^k` offset family by one exact horizontal
  cycle per 3-adic valuation stratum. Multiplication by `2^(-1)` is transitive
  on that stratum, the associated binary word is balanced with exact least
  period, and it gives an explicit self-section address. Every absolute
  primitive state already lies in the original finite binary kernel; a
  positive-valuation kernel is its horizontal cycle plus exits into that fixed
  kernel. Full ternary-kernel finiteness is therefore equivalent to finiteness
  of one representative family over widths and nontrivial central states.
- `L-9842` computes the off-cycle decorations and their width refinement.
  Every escape lies in one finite ternary-closed subset of the original binary
  kernel and is therefore eventually periodic. The three lifts of each old
  position are its three ternary sections in an explicit arithmetic
  permutation, so decoration-count vectors obey one fixed incidence matrix.
  Its incidence matrix deliberately forgets cyclic order.
- `L-9844` resolves that ordering datum. The first-order congruence
  `2^(L_k)=1+3^k mod 3^(k+1)` makes the three lift digits one affine `F_3`
  orbit, the canonical correction is exactly one minus the staying bit, and
  a three-state top-digit transducer generates every ordered permutation.
  It leaves equality of the resulting infinite roots open.
- `L-9849` resolves that equality problem by adjoining the finite
  section-closed core `F=K_2(s) union B`. Every horizontal cycle is wholly
  absorbed or wholly disjoint from `F`; off the core, the unique outside child
  recovers the staying bit from the root itself. Least-period rigidity then
  makes width intrinsic, while padded binary digits prove that the ordered
  decoration word is a complete pointed-root invariant. Full ternary-kernel
  finiteness is exactly eventual absorption. By Cobham and `L-9823`, any
  nonconstant coloring must have infinitely many unabsorbed widths.

The automatic-coloring question remains open, but its state-space frontier is
now exact. A power-of-two automatic sequence shows that central finiteness and
even density-one satisfaction of the odd identity cannot make the cone bounds
uniform; the full component identity must be used at its sparse hard indices.

### Padé exceptional residuals

- The expanded `L-9816` normalizes every exceptional order by
  `U=T^g`, gives an all-order residual divisibility criterion, completely
  classifies reduced odd-prime residual zeros and multiplicity, and bounds
  their total degree by `O(n log n)`. One copy of every antisymmetric composite
  residual is also only linear. Together with the automatic factor sector,
  these classes leave a positive quadratic deficit.
- `L-9828` gives the exact coefficient-block criterion for reduced
  prime-power orders. Even granting one exceptional copy at every eligible
  squarefree prime-power support point contributes only `O_G(D^(3/2))`
  degree. Explicit valuation caps show why multiplicity-weighted support is a
  separate problem and why valuation bounds alone can still be quadratic.
- `L-9832` completely classifies odd reduced `p^k` in the small-remainder
  range `s<p`: a zero occurs exactly through the known complement factor
  `p^k|C_(g,s)`, and that factor is simple. Every non-antisymmetric zero and
  every higher multiplicity is therefore confined to `p<=s<p^k` (plus the
  separate power-of-two sector).
- `L-9839` finds the missing cancellation inside the power-of-two residual
  budget. Forced lower dyadic factors consume exactly half of the diagonal
  2-adic valuation, so the target multiplicity is at most `s/2`. An exact
  dyadic induction gives the sharp total envelope `D^2/27` per reduced chain;
  at `G=71` its specialization budget is `266.743319 n^2+o(n^2)`, below the
  remaining deficit. The exact even-macro jet calculation also shows why the
  last transfer is not formal: the first excess derivative is the derivative
  of a sheared residual, multiplied by the common first nonzero macro jet,
  plus the sum of the next macro jets. Residual multiplicity alone therefore
  does not give a triangular excess-gcd bound; a genuine moment
  noncancellation (or an equivalent transverse-jet inequality) is still
  required.
- `L-9848` turns dyadic target nonvanishing into a one-variable augmentation
  problem. Its subset form and exact doubling convolution stay inside one
  2-adic parameter family, while the already-forced diagonal and lower
  factors consume exactly `s/2+A(s)` augmentation degrees. A target zero
  would require another `L/2`. Complete characteristic-two group-ring
  identities give augmentation orders `6` and `14`, excluding every target
  in the first two actual nonzero remainder layers `s=4,8`. The proposed
  all-layer order formula, and even the weaker uniform slack bound needed for
  `s>=12`, remain conjectural.

The remaining Padé risk is now sharply localized: small primes relative to
the residual remainder, non-antisymmetric composite orders, noncyclotomic
polynomial factors, dyadic augmentation slack from `s=12` onward, and
arithmetic specialization gcds.

## Explicit non-results

- No nontrivial infinite orbit, divergent ordinary seed, sanctuary, cycle, or
  `K-####` candidate is claimed.
- A full symbolic shift in a completion is not one ordinary marked seed.
- Haar-null or low-dimensional completion geometry does not exclude a
  countable ordinary intersection.
- The zero-dimensional fiber and aperiodic address in `L-9845` alone do not
  exclude ordinary points. `L-9850` does exclude them for this exact graph by
  adding real drift and integer packing, but does not cover other H returns.
- The abstract inequality in `L-9851` is necessary, not sufficient, and does
  not supply the arithmetic endpoint multiplicity required in applications.
- `L-9852` realizes one residual-aware padding bit and refutes only the naive
  bulk-only lift. It does not construct the residual-aware local grammar or
  route the remaining `K-H` connector block.
- Exact global pair statistics do not control cyclic successor adjacency.
- The translation-fiber isolation and equal-arc packing in `L-9847` are
  necessary, not sufficient; outside-fiber survivor points can still obstruct
  an arc, and no uniform pointed maximum-gap estimate is proved.
- The renormalized H rotation deletes a physical zero and is not a forward
  repeated-crossing orbit.
- The unique physical H carry tuple is finite-stage consistency; its nested
  limit is now proved nonordinary for this suffix family.
- Logarithmically many nonzero H blocks do not imply positive density.
- Finiteness of every fixed-width ternary cone is not uniform finiteness of
  their union.
- A three-state compiler for the exact ordered escape lift does not imply that
  the resulting unbounded family of pointed horizontal roots is finite.
- `L-9849` proves that unabsorbed widths really do give new root cycles, but it
  does not decide absorption or construct a nonconstant automatic coloring.
- Squarefree prime-power support and valuation-weighted multiplicity are
  deliberately different Padé estimates.
- The exact `L-9848` doubling descent and its `s=4,8` base identities do not
  prove the conjectural all-layer augmentation order or all-layer dyadic
  target nonvanishing. They also do not prove `L-9839`'s residual-to-full-gcd
  transfer for any surviving target zero.
