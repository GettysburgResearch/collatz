## New results

- `L-9891` proves that every finite adjacent equal-allocation error
  Casoratian is nonzero. A canonical scalar coupling of `q` neighboring
  orders cancels exactly `q-1` additional blocks, its evaluated error and
  denominator valuations are exact, and no scalar combination has higher
  formal order. Even with zero cofactor-height cost, the unchanged-height
  period-four model needs `q>0.07114166n`; actual reduced height remains open.
- `L-9892` proves unbounded exposed global rank outside all predecessor
  phases. For any suffix, rank target, and common-prefix target, infinitely
  many depths outside `n=1 mod36` have that many distinct lower competitors
  at once, with exact values `64^(n-h_i)` and zero terminal blocks. The
  selected refinements are thin in the 2-adic phase parameterization; their
  Haar-measure complement is not an ordinary-depth density theorem.
- `L-9893` resolves the whole 252-cell cap middle into 84 canonical triple
  seams. Every inter-triple quotient vanishes. The physical terminal/head
  mismatch language has at most 4096 values, so it cannot witness the
  exponential generic Hensel sections from `L-9888`. The remaining middle
  question is an explicit 84-layer finite overlap graph.
- `L-9894` gives the first exact constraint between successive H bridge cores:
  `8^R 4^b Y - 9^R 3^a X = 9^R - 8^R`. Shared prime powers force
  multiplicative-order divisibility of `R`; a second integral sign law links
  core growth to renewal capital and adjacent gap change; both valuation
  excesses share one discounted budget. The central CRT construction is only
  algebraic and need not complete either outer bridge.

## Failed or closed routes

- A fixed-width adjacent Pade determinant cannot obtain a quadratic gain by
  cancellation or normalized valuation alone. Growing width moves the problem
  to reduced cofactor height.
- Merging a lower survivor into a later point of the one-hot trajectory creates
  no phases beyond the predecessor classifier. Delayed disjoint words are the
  new obstruction, but the selected family does not cover every exposure.
- The unrestricted Hensel state count is not dynamically reached by the cap
  bridge. The physical language is finite per scale, although not uniformly
  bounded across the complete controller problem.
- The successive H compatibility equation does not exclude any prescribed
  central exponent triple; its CRT tuples may fail both outer bridges.
