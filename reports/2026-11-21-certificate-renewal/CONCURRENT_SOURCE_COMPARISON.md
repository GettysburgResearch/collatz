Their whole-word recognition theorems, HT2 and FR3, subsume successful recognition of every word handled by our dictionary compiler. Their example `17:(2,3,4)` versus `85:(8)` demonstrates why a total successful compiler requires changed resources, other endpoints, or independent terminal rules.

Our rows B, C and D with extension length zero are exactly PR167's three four-input valuation-one families. Row B also appears in PR168. They receive concurrent credit here. Our explicit unbounded extension theorem FW2, the positive-length D extensions, and rows E--H are additional to the displayed renewable domains of those notes. The generic concurrent recognizers nevertheless accept these resulting words.

## 2. Our paired descents realize two cases of the singular grammar

PR168 [NEGATIVE_TEMPLATE_GRAMMAR.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e4599b32e4b1a955ac9315d46782d4ad6a5db96c/research/closure-frontier-20261121/NEGATIVE_TEMPLATE_GRAMMAR.md), NT1--NT3, gives a common decreasing ordinary parameter for eight displayed templates. Their parents satisfy `M=-1 mod2048` and are odd. Our parents satisfy `M=14 mod32` and are even. Thus the two finite catalogs have disjoint direct domains, and neither subsumes the other.

They can coexist safely because both decrease the same positive integer parameter. They do not directly alternate: our children are always `967 mod2048` or `417 mod2048`, while PR168 requires `2047 mod2048`; its children stay odd and therefore cannot enter our even parent classes. This is a coverage issue, not a failure of the established descent identities.

Substitution gives the displayed scales. The zero-numerator step is not an exact accelerated valuation. The actual positive lifts and supplied-certificate transfers are justified by our ordinary affine-prefix proofs, not by assigning an odd valuation to zero.

## 3. New analytic interfaces do not bound the moving residue tail

PR167 [CLOSING_ENERGY_INTERFACES.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e83cc8b35ad614c616df7c00318cc9645a174c34/research/least-failure-closure-20261121/CLOSING_ENERGY_INTERFACES.md), CI4, obtains finite directed energy on the retained least-failure sieve at and above its critical exponent, conditional on the pinned sieve theorem. The remaining discarded-index obligation is an incoming dependency-column bound, or a justified substitute using the actual label support. Its [EXCURSION_FREQUENCY.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e83cc8b35ad614c616df7c00318cc9645a174c34/research/least-failure-closure-20261121/EXCURSION_FREQUENCY.md) proves a stronger necessary lower bound on visits outside any fixed finite shifted-core family, using its named S-unit input. PR168 [FINITE_CONDUCTOR_RIGIDITY.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e4599b32e4b1a955ac9315d46782d4ad6a5db96c/research/closure-frontier-20261121/FINITE_CONDUCTOR_RIGIDITY.md) specifies an ordinary approximation threshold. Its [TERNARY_STATIONARY_BOUNDARY.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e4599b32e4b1a955ac9315d46782d4ad6a5db96c/research/closure-frontier-20261121/TERNARY_STATIONARY_BOUNDARY.md) supplies weighted mixing and a bounded-error conversion using its explicitly imported Tao input, while disproving the proposed uniform square-integrability shortcut. Neither constructs the required approximation to an actual ordinary component label.

There is an exact probability normalization connecting the subjects, but with different exponent laws. Define

\[
\pi_q^{(4)}(t)=\frac{H_q(t)}{(27/5)^q}.
\]

For each word, its normalized weight is

\[
\frac{J_w^4}{(27/5)^q}=\prod_{i=1}^q\frac{15}{16^{a_i}},
\qquad \sum_{a\ge1}\frac{15}{16^a}=1.
\]

Also `r_(w,a)=(3 r_w+1)/2^a mod3^(q+1)`. Thus these endpoint probabilities are exactly the ternary projections of the affine Syracuse law with independent exponent probabilities `15/16^a`. Its depth-q projection forgets an arbitrary starting ternary value because the affine slope is divisible by `3^q`. The representation is an identity for the weighted word sum; it is not an independence assertion about a fixed integer's successive valuations.

PR168 instead uses exponent probabilities `2^(-a)`. Its stated regularity theorem therefore cannot be transferred directly to our tilted law. Our open sum can be written exactly as

\[
Z_q=(27/5)^q
 \sum_{\substack{q<t<2\cdot3^q\\t\text{ odd}}}
 \frac{\pi_q^{(4)}(t)}{t^2}.
\]

Control of the small positive representatives under this tilted law would be a concrete analytic approach to the remaining question. None of the compared notes provides that bound.

## Consequence for research priorities

The constructive target is a complete selector of certified terminal cases and strict parameter descents, with rules that can change witness resources. Further homogeneous recognition alone cannot supply it. The analytic alternatives need an absolute bound for actual uncovered defect energy, a suitable ordinary approximation, or the stated moving-residue moment estimate. The fifth-denominator theorem, the new explicit singular descents and the concurrent restricted bounds are useful partial results; their combination does not yet establish universal convergence or nontrivial cycle exclusion.
