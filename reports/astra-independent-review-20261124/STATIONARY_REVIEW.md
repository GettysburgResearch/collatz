**Stationarity and projection.** The exact physical telescope and distinctness give $\sum_{i<M} g_i\le\alpha M+O_q(\log(M+1))$. Select a subsequence realizing any prescribed ternary empirical limit. Fixed-length forward windows are tight by Markov and finite cost sublevel sets. A diagonal further subsequence produces consistent finite marginals. Deleting a boundary endpoint changes an empirical coordinate law by $O(m/M)$, giving stationarity and identifying each ternary residue pushforward with the specified limit through the exact affine identity. Truncation gives $\mathbb EG_0\le\alpha$. PR180 uses reversed past windows, PR189 forward windows. Reversing coordinates preserves block entropy and collision, while the corresponding arithmetic projection is reversed with the word. PR180 starts on ternary units. An initial ordinary multiple of three enters that domain after a single odd step.

**Strict critical inequality.** Gibbs bounds the one-letter entropy by $\Phi(\alpha)$, with equality only at the geometric law of mean $\alpha$. If one block has a strict deficit, stationary subadditivity propagates it linearly. If none does, all finite blocks have maximum entropy and are exactly the product geometric laws. Their word collision is $(2\alpha-1)^{-m}$, with $\log(2\alpha-1)<\Phi(\alpha)$ by strict Jensen. Grouping masses by residue only increases collision. This proves the strict upper collision-dimension inequality for each fixed ternary weak limit, without a universal gap. PR180 §7 states the sufficient contrary target using *lower* dimension at least the threshold. PR189 §1.3 explicitly uses *upper* dimension at least the threshold, an immediate weaker sufficient premise from PR180's own equation (17). PR180's separate moving-window conclusion in the finite-deficit branch uses tail entropy control at each fixed block size, followed by literal finite mixture domination.

**Conditional KL and ordinary support.** At any fixed sampled canonical word, selecting alternate digit-one edges makes the relevant pairs of *reference* random variables independent. The support restriction holds for the original conditional word law. PR189 correctly subtracts the relative entropy loss from cross entropy. Its sliding budget includes both endpoints with the appropriate boundary term; the coefficient used when substituting the carry bound is positive. Physical terminal carry control and clock-fixed reconstruction are both necessary in S3. RUN_PARTITION.md uses a stronger support partition and OCCUPATION.md keeps the two time directions separate.

**Unresolved fixed-source premise.** An orbit with subexponential canonical complexity and sufficiently small drawup cannot escape, and an orbit in a specified finite phase has sparse occupation. Neither hypothesis on an arbitrary escaping source follows from these deductions. The constant-one symbolic gap process already has zero entropy and a ternary atomic law; its 2-adic source is $-1$. Local symbolic legality alone cannot give a critical lower dimension for an actual positive integer. A generic inverse law does not give that dimension either. No argument here excludes an arbitrary positive cycle.
