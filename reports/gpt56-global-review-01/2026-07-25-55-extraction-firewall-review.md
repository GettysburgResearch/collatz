## New results

### T-7701 — eventually periodic supercritical firewall

Every eventually periodic shortcut parity schedule whose periodic tail has multiplier greater than one has a negative rational `2`-adic realization. Therefore no autonomous deterministic finite-state high-drift parity generator can produce a positive ordinary Collatz counterexample.

This closes the whole class represented by the explicit `(1110)^infinity` ghost, not merely that example.

### T-7702 — finite architecture portfolios do not extract

For finitely many fixed nested survivor towers,

```text
intersection over depth of their union
=
union of their individual all-time intersections.
```

Hence a finite portfolio of unresolved machines has an ordinary survivor only if one constituent already does. Genuine switching requires exact cross-machine physical transitions and creates a new architecture.

## Blunt assessment

The project is not wholly circular. Complete strict classes have been eliminated, several source chains have survived independent reconstruction, and false bridges have been caught. Those are genuine mathematical advances.

The positive ordinary-existence funnel, however, has not crossed its decisive boundary. After the extraction theorem is known, additional finite depth, branch capacity, inverse-limit encodings, unrestricted foundries, conditional drift, or quotient refund are treading water unless they prove bounded least roots, eventual zero transported blocks, one all-time ordinary seed, or a full finite return.
