## Main mathematical frontiers at launch

The latest packets retain several different possible proof architectures. PR218 gives a signed parity-mean sufficient condition and a simultaneous real/ternary selector, but does not prove the required ordinary-source conditional sign. Its killed rational bounds do not automatically transfer to integer components. PR217 retains an open median-collision lower bound and proves a semiprime sufficiency reduction, without convergence for the entire family. PR219 develops affine support, anchored rigidity and ordinary-source sampling, with its global realization step open. PRs212–215 contain extensive cycle, inverse, carry, and critical-clock results; none asserts a complete accepted proof.

These descriptions are orientation only. Reuse requires reading the exact path, hypotheses, proof and declared source scope. Local Lean receipts in a previous PR are previous evidence, not recompilation in this pass.

## Research policy for this pass

The three researchers have wide freedom to choose and change attacks. The coordinator reconstructs candidate arguments and publishes coherent checkpoints. New material stays within this packet and its reports; main, archived evidence and existing mathematical statuses are unchanged. A counterexample to a proposed intermediate inequality redirects the research rather than ending the pass.
