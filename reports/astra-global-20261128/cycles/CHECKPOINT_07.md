All appended primes in each occurrence have exponent one. The constant depends on the word. This yields both directed visit patterns between the disjoint targets 5 and 85 under the stated predecessor-density input.

The arithmetic argument uses Ford's fundamental lemma of sieve, Banks–Shparlinski's short character sum estimate at powers of two, the prime Dirichlet polynomial bound of Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Shao–Tao–Teräväinen, and the counted-pair Collatz synchronization theorem. A positive subpopulation of products of rough integers preserves deep binary residue bounds even while earlier orbit events weight the preceding factors. The theorem establishes positive lower frequencies, leaving exact joint probabilities on adjacent links undetermined. It holds in the presence of multiple positive or escaping components.
