# Entropy of an ordinary first-hit deadline and repulsion on rare grade slices

For a fixed positive odd target $y$ with predecessor density $d>0$, [the critical dilation theorem](../../../research/astra-global-20261128/cycles/CRITICAL_DILATION_ENTROPY.md) studies the single computable bit that asks whether a true visit occurs at a date $m$ satisfying $4^m\le 3^m n^2$. Every fixed collection of $r$ odd affine sources with pairwise multiplier ratios outside $3^{\mathbb Z}$ has limiting lower joint entropy at least $rI_d>0$ on ordinary octaves. This includes $n,pn,\ldots,p^{r-1}n$ for every prime $p\ge5$. A lower bound on covariance eigenvalue tails excludes a single common critical fluctuation at five distinct such dilations for a growing subpower first-landing floor. In a log-window stationary realization the true deadline bit has absolutely continuous covariance spectral mass at least $d^2\arcsin(\log(4/3)/(2\log2))/(2\pi)$. The ordinary orbit inputs are the complete landing template, counted translation synchronization, the fixed-target Gaussian/positive-density law and the previous checkpoint's initial joint Brownian theorem. Independence of all late clocks and positive multiplicative entropy of pure eventual fate remain open.

[MedianDeadline.lean](../../../research/astra-global-20261128/cycles/MedianDeadline.lean) proves the integer bit equals a finite search of the actual shortcut run, with search bound strictly below $5B$ for every $n<2^B$. The examples verify that $3$ meets the deadline for $1$ and $27$ misses it despite reaching $1$ on ordinary date 70. All three public theorem environments compile strictly under pinned Lean 4.34.1. Only propositional extensionality and quotient soundness appear. The independent [executable](../../../research/astra-global-20261128/cycles/check_critical_dilations.py) checked 10,240 native deadline/target pairs and 204,930 integer clock comparisons. Each of the 16 four-site patterns appeared at both the $5$ and $7$ progressions in the finite octave $[1024,2048)$. [Exact output](CRITICAL_DILATIONS.json)

[The biased grade theorem](../../../research/astra-global-20261128/cycles/BIASED_GRADE_REPULSION.md) conditions ordinary sources on an exact generally exponentially rare departure count $k_t(An+a)=k$, $k/t\to p\ne1/2$, through any $2^t=o(X)$. Every fixed nonsmooth companion lies strictly closer in grade density to $1/2$ outside relative probability $O(\exp[-ct/(\log t)^4])$. At the physical critical bias $p=\log_3 2$, the selected true endpoint changes size by a factor $\exp(o(t))$ and each companion decreases by an exponential factor. For the two-adic tilted sources the affine pullbacks of any two biased Bernoulli parity laws with nonsmooth slope ratio are mutually singular. The general slope uses Bugeaud–Laurent's fixed-ratio two-adic logarithm theorem; the two nonresonant classes modulo eight have an elementary exponential exceptional estimate. The finite control checked 2,505 exact disintegrated grade cells and 38,208 native predictable-parity comparisons. This result addresses conditional ensembles; it does not select the specific source realizing any proposed positive integer cycle.
