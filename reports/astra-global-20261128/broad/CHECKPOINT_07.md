## Which stationary gap processes can share the same ternary source?

`DEPENDENT_GAP_DIMENSION.md` treats every stationary ergodic positive-integer gap process with finite mean $a$ and entropy rate $h$. Put $\Delta=a\log2-\log3$. Its ternary source has exact dimension $h/\log3$ whenever $\Delta\le0$. The entire gap sequence is then almost surely recoverable from that state. Its source law is mutually singular with that of every different stationary ergodic finite-mean gap process, even when the competitor has positive real contraction. For $\Delta>0$, the joint real/ternary evaluations have information rate $h$ on rectangles of radii $e^{-\Delta n}$ and $3^{-n}$. This pair recovers the gap sequence almost surely. The ternary coordinate by itself has conditional one-step branching entropy at most $\Delta$. The argument uses exact integer fibers, typical prefixes and Markovization of the stationary one-edge occupation.

For a hypothetical escaping positive ray the mean bound applies to its entire stationary empirical limit. Individual ergodic components require their own means in these assertions. ## Adaptive approximations and the critical path component

`ADAPTIVE_WEAK_MARGINALS.md` constructs finite stationary rolling policies whose weak one-edge limit has a positive component $\rho J_P$ for any specified stationary ergodic finite-mean gap law $P$. The absolute constant $\rho=\delta_* /(1+\delta_*)$ comes from the fixed-total modular routing of Weaver and Wren, paper 48, Theorem 8. The prelimit means tend to $\rho a+2(1-\rho)$. For the critical geometric law, its decoded iid branches imply that the **whole critical path process** occurs as an ergodic component of weight at least $\rho$. The other part of the policy still incurs a nonvanishing cost. The same construction can give a signed fixed atom positive weight. ## Exact spectrum of minimum inverse cost

`INVERSE_COST_SPECTRUM.md` gives the Hausdorff and packing dimensions of the original-source sets with normalized minimum costs oscillating between $u$ and $v$, for every $1\le u\le v\le a_*$. These dimensions are $H(u)/\log3$ and $H(v)/\log3$, respectively, with $H$ the positive-geometric entropy at the indicated mean. For every interior $b$ and any prescribed nonnegative $g(n)=o(n)$, each eventual inequality $C_n\ge bn+g(n)$ and $C_n\le bn-g(n)$ still occurs on sets of dimension $H(b)/\log3$ where $C_n/n\to b$. The second assertion also reaches $a_*$. Simultaneous finite-depth versions hold for $X^{H(b)/\log3+o(1)}$ ordinary positive odd unit inputs in every large octave, through depth $\log_3X+O(1)$. The construction imports Leen's geometric-law Shannon dimension theorem. A separated-block construction and uniform composition counts handle competing paths, the limsup along high stages and the all-depth sublinear corrections.
