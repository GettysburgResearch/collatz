## Compatible lags and strict first differences

Indeed a strict reversal from $2^pN<2^bN'$ requires $p>b$ and $N'-2^{p-b}N=dj>0$ with $j$ odd and $3j<2^{p-b}-1$. Thus $p-b\ge3$ and $N'>8N$. Equality would identify the next odd parts at distinct chronological lags. If the order of $\omega$ were a proper divisor of $g$, the cyclic differences for the corresponding nonzero lag would have both signs. This proves $\mathrm{ord}_d(\omega)=g$.

Let $l$ be the inverse of $a$ modulo $q$ in $[1,q)$, $b=\lfloor la/q\rfloor$ and $\beta=\rho_l$. The exponent identity $la-qb=1$ gives $2=\beta^q\omega^{-l}$ and $3=\beta^a\omega^{-b}$. Hence $\beta$ has order $T=S/g$ modulo $\langle\omega\rangle$. At positive offset $t<q$, the complete $g$ candidate lags $lt+jq$ have multipliers $\beta^t\omega^j$. This accounts for the whole fibre, including $T\nmid q$. A compatible multiple $kT<q$ lifts to $kh_*$ since its accumulated fractional increment has yet to reach one octave.

The adjusted gap $G_i=2^{c_i}K_{i+h}-K_i$ is a multiple of $d$ by the exact modular recursion. It is nonzero by distinct odd parts. For $H\le2$, the least adjustment of the lowbit difference is $-3$, so a positive gap has nonnegative next numerator and cannot vanish. For two labels at the same fundamental residue and different ranks take the orientation of their increasing fundamental ranks. Its octave carry is zero. This proves the unwrapped order assertion.

## Wide offsets and quadratic packing

Delete an $h_*$ edge exactly when its phase increment wraps. The resulting paths start at all $g$ copies of ranks $0,\ldots,T-1$. Each positive phase increments by $T$ until the next wrap, so there are exactly $gT=S$ paths with every departure accounted for. The difference of two consecutive $kT$ gaps along a path is the sum of $k$ octave-adjusted unit-step curvatures. This equality remains valid when the endpoint of the wide gap lies beyond the seam. The lack of wrap between the consecutive starts is what removes the outer factor of two. Thus each difference is at least $kd$ and each fixed gap has at most $S$ occurrences. Summing positive first differences with second differences at least $d$ across a path gives $2dX\ge\frac12d(L-1)L$ for its length $L\ge\lfloor P/S\rfloor$.

## A sharper sufficient asymptotic condition

The same proof works on any such $H\le1$ sequence with $X\to\infty$ and
$$
 \frac SP\big(\log(2+X)\big)^5\longrightarrow0.
$$
In particular $S/P=o((\log P)^{-5})$ suffices by coset packing and $X\le P^6$. Choose $n=\lceil\log_2(2X)\rceil+1$. Then $2^n>2X$, while $n|\theta-\lambda|\ll n/X=o(n^{-5})$ follows from the physical height inequality directly. The residue-spacing hypothesis gives $T/q=S/P=o(n^{-5})$, so the published lower bound clears the three-point clusters for all $1\le j\le n+2$ as before. More generally the same argument substitutes any verified lower-bound exponent $\nu$ in $\|j\lambda\|\gg(j+1)^{-\nu}$ for five. This additional sufficient condition follows separately from the audit and is not necessary to the frozen theorem.
