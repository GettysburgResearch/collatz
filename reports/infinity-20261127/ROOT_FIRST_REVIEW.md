## Independent mathematical reconstruction

- **Resonant loss.** Rechecked the three suffix carries, their circular
  coverage, the resonance modulus, the principal-source lift, and the
  next genuine odd valuation. Reconstructed the cyclic parent count and
  the tilted-walk survival/bridge estimate used in the written asymptotic.
  The deaths occur in the true population but remain in its growing
  source tail. They cannot be used to infer extinction of an old atom.
- **Old sources.** Reconstructed the nonnegative single-factor polynomial,
  telescoping rank product, exact edge-product cancellation, repeated-lap
  deletion, propagation of the integer clearance, and the strict first
  arrival before failure. Read the actual valuation adapter. The condition
  does not assert that failure occurs, and terminal testing requires the
  supplied previous survival hypothesis.
- **Ordinary Pell packing.** Reconstructed the discriminant equation,
  fundamental-unit parity restriction, ratio bound, odd return norm, and
  physical-clock height inequality. Read the external A1 square-gap
  argument at the pinned PR192 commit and checked the numerical rank
  calculation. Read PR192's P2 same-field return bound and credited the
  new norm estimate as its sharpening. The published initial verification and Hercher's search
  remain external inputs; their official primary sources and corrigendum
  were consulted. The finite arithmetic was independently rerun.
- **Weighted pivot sizes.** Root supplied the weighted proof and formal
  finite-subset kernel. The arithmetic agent independently checked pivot
  injectivity on an affine translate, the divisibility weight, the sum
  estimate, the fixed-parameter asymptotic limit, and the conditional
  `6/5` consequence. No rank upper bound follows. The exact statement of
  Chudnovsky's Theorem 11.1 was consulted; its proof archive was not built.
- **Joint targets.** Read both new Lean modules and reconstructed the
  same-clock comparison, forward comparability, and explicit 113/509
  counterexample. Read the pinned PR146 finite-clock estimate. The
  density corollary remains qualified by that written input and, where
  a positive fraction is asserted, by the separately named fixed-target
  density theorem. The four-path merger preserves different dyadic
  scales on its negative and positive sides.

inventories, expected control JSON and resulting output bytes. It counts
repeated dependencies once. Every successful source group was compiled
with Lean 4.34.1 commit `5045d0056413266e57c625dcd7c365b10e377c52`,

was incomplete and was not accepted. The parser was corrected to retain
multiline lists, and the entire ordered group was freshly rebuilt. The
published complete replay supersedes those incomplete runs. Earlier
resource/intermediate compilation attempts are likewise not counted as
successful evidence.

source commit, original path, Git blob and SHA-256 recorded in
`research/infinity-20261127/mass/pr200-pointwise/SOURCE.json`. They are
counted as prior dependencies, not new authored modules. Third-party
compiled Mathlib/package objects are reused. No stronger independent
kernel or all-dependency rebuild is implied.

The two mass control expectations were first captured from the reviewed
root runs and then compared in a separate ordinary/optimized replay.
The arithmetic and coupling expectations were supplied with their frozen
packets and checked independently by the root. Output agreement is finite
evidence; the written infinite conclusions require their stated proofs.

The required five clean-checkout validators are run on the publication
tree separately. Their function is integration and frozen-file integrity,
not verification of the mathematical assertions.
