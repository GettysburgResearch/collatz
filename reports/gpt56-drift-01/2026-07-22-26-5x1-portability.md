## Starting hypothesis

Porting active `3x+1` exclusions to a positive-drift `5x+1` control universe
should separate three causes that are often conflated:

1. universal parity-word format;
2. arithmetic existence of a desired chart;
3. drift-sensitive quantitative constants.

A mechanism that cannot certify or even represent the easier control universe
should not be treated as evidence against a `3x+1` counterexample mechanism.

## Hidden connection

The ordinary-section repetition obstruction survives on an exact chart whose
macro multiplier is `5/4>1` and whose fair-parity model drift is positive. Its
logical source is therefore not negative drift. It is a product-formula style
collision between:

- deep `2`-adic agreement supplied by a repeated symbolic block; and
- small archimedean height of the eventually periodic rational approximant.

The exact strength is controlled by

```text
delta(U,V)=log_U(V)-1.
```

Near-critical expansion makes the obstruction stronger, even on the expanding
side. This reframes several active “rigidity” results as tests of the ordinary
certificate format rather than tests of whether the dynamics can grow.

## Failed approaches

### Literal length-6, weight-4 phase port

The `a=3` chart has three direct same-phase blocks because

```text
3^4-2^6=17
```

divides the relevant corrections. At `a=5`,

```text
5^4-2^6=561
```

divides none of the fifteen corrections. The literal chart shape therefore
fails arithmetically before any drift or completion argument begins.

This failure led to the smaller exact length-2 chart and is preserved as useful
negative information.

### Constant expanding directives

Repeated use of either exact `4 -> 5` phase creates arbitrarily large finite
positive towers, but their compatible infinite cylinders converge to `-1` or
`-2` in `Z_2`. Expansion alone does not produce an ordinary positive seed.

### Numerical divergence attempt

The seed-`7` ledger reaches a large value and never returns to the starting
scale in the frozen prefix, but no finite computation supplies the needed
infinite conclusion. No extrapolation was made.
