## Questions that survive those packets

The original finite signed statistic has an exact transport identity
with positive transport excess and genuine same-target cancellation.
The required exponential subsequence upper bound remains open. A
generic weighted-norm contraction is false even after a one-digit
mean-zero restriction, so that route needs the special actual signed
rows rather than a generic norm estimate.

The native reciprocal mass has an exponentially decaying reserve for
newly born least representatives, but an old ordinary source can retain
its own atom. The reserve does not give a deadline for its death. The
critical-clock analysis in this pass therefore follows actual orbit
minima and their true later futures.

The binary–ternary inverse transfer has a uniform absolute cylinder
floor and a unique constant-mass positive entrance family. At the
original grade of an escaping orbit the normalized posterior is
atomic; the absolute floor is diluted by its growing total trace.
Additional congruence coordinates must preserve that normalization
distinction.

The cycle theorems constrain full reduced denominators, small mismatch
families, cyclic width, and reference-component lifetimes. An
exceptional period or a changing family of reference components still
survives. This pass tests full-return and complete-calendar identities.
