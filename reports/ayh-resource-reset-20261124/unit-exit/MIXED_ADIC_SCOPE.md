# Mixed-adic predecessor floors and the selected ordinary cell

The [PR 201 prefix-density manuscript](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/73a145ca99448a78219bd149e273f33b6e3fda4b/research/killed-green-head-closure-20261124/prefix-density/README.md), blob `8dd9d53dea58a99a561dd6c90913974cd417814f`, already proves its Theorem A on the written extra-depth atom floor from rStation paper 37 v4. Every sufficiently high octave admits a linear-size antichain of ordinary unit predecessors of a fixed target. Distinct anchors have disjoint ordinary predecessor sets. Its Theorems B–D1 obtain positive relative density in real intervals of length at least \(X/(\log X)^\nu\), \(\nu<5/133\), with the specified sublinear digit constraints, phase conditions, and optional synchronized blocks. They use their F and the stated Diophantine input D, with S added for blocks. These are existing conditional results.

The [PR 203 fixed-target depth bank](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/3f2c89f12b057b8bb8655bd6fc794c772aa8b65d/research/mixed-adic-basin-density-20261124/fixed_target_depth_bank.md), blob `86e77570121079a5fefffc65ad23442604bb0f44`, uses its stronger source-qualified F from rStation paper 48, Lemma 7 and the prepending in Theorem 8. On fixed-shape macroscopic intervals it allows joint linear mixed-adic depth, both prescribed first-hit phases modulo fixed integers, and, with S, blocks of length \(\exp(c\sqrt{\log X})\). Neither source treats the selected discordant condition in `LOCAL_COLOR_INFORMATION.md`.

In that information theorem the exterior quotient restricts the coarse base \(m\asymp2^{(1-2.9a)d}\), \(a=1-\tfrac12\log_2 3\), to a real cell of width at most \(2^{.31d}\). Its relative width is at most \(O(2^{-.08d})\), smaller than every fixed inverse power of \(\log m\); one also conditions on the chosen source set and two physical arm seeds. Even a phase-population lower floor on larger windows gives neither a distribution of the current trit-color answers nor a conditional upper bound on mutual information with the next \(h\) coarse bits on this restricted source. The short-window input needed to contradict (1) is missing. No new antichain claim or inference of orbit merger is made.
