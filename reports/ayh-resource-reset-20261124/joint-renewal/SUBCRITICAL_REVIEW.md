# Independent review of all first-fusion moments below three halves

Reviewed source: TRIPLE_SUBCRITICAL_MOMENTS.md, SHA-256 `82f207e0f09c8342706396348cb6e1aa1c4fdef17e0a5d415b2b8754cde4e04e`. I independently checked the covariance, derivative, stopping and summation steps. The resulting statement is $\mathbb E(1+\sigma)^p<\infty$ for every fixed $0<p<3/2$ and fixed triple. It gives no endpoint assertion at $p=3/2$.

1. In a deep starting prefix the order cannot change in the next $B$ physical clocks. Before completing either quota the number of mixed jumps is too small to hit grade gap one. Every holding time is almost surely finite by the pair-chain holding bound. Thus the completion event in equation (21) of the [mixed-clock source](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/13/v1) has probability one. This use of the unconditioned varying-initial-gap bound is valid even after fixing the exact joint past. Each future pair retains the correct marginal. The three pair counts need not be independent.
2. With increments $(d_1,d_2)$ of the fixed ordered gaps, $d_1^2,d_2^2,(d_1+d_2)^2$ are the three mixed indicators. The uniform mixed fractions $1/2+O(\eta)$ therefore yield conditional expected block covariance per step $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1/2&-1/4\\-1/4&1/2\end{smallmatrix}\right)+O(\eta)$ entrywise.
3. At $U=u+v>0$ the derivatives of $uvU^\gamma$ give $f_{uu}=2\gamma vU^{\gamma-1}+\gamma(\gamma-1)uvU^{\gamma-2}$, the expression with $u,v$ exchanged for $f_{vv}$, and $f_{uv}=(1+\gamma)U^\gamma+\gamma(\gamma-1)uvU^{\gamma-2}$. Consequently $(f_{uu}+f_{vv}-f_{uv})/4=(\gamma-1)(U^\gamma+\gamma uvU^{\gamma-2})/4$. The negative margin is uniform in the ratio $u/v$. The whole Hessian has size $O_\gamma(U^\gamma)$ and its variation within the block is $O_\gamma(B U_*^{\gamma-1})$. Multiplying by $B$ gives the correct freezing cost $O_\gamma(B^2 U_*^{\gamma-1})$. The centered first-order terms vanish conditionally, without independent increments across time. The deep factor $U_*/B$ can be chosen sufficiently large at fixed $\gamma$.
4. A block beginning with a small labeled gap keeps that very pair within $(K_1+1)B$ at all its still-live clocks. Its starting classification is already measurable. At strict order the ordinary expected one-step increase is bounded by $O_\gamma((1+U)^\gamma)$ by the Hessian bound; killing only decreases the terminal potential. At a tie the new smaller gap is at most one, so the additional amount is bounded by $(1+U)^{1+\gamma}$. The two charges in (8) therefore suffice. On the Azuma cutoff, (2) gives total shell small-gap count $O(B^2/H)$ and tie count $O(H^{-1})$. With $B\asymp H^{1/4}$, each resulting weighted charge is $O_\gamma(H^{(\gamma-1)/2}(\log H)^{(1+\gamma)/2})$. The polynomially rare complement is summable even under the deterministic bound $U=O_{\rm init}(H)$. The dyadic blocks tile each shell exactly. Telescoping to a complete terminal endpoint leaves a nonnegative terminal potential and gives finite total spread occupation.
5. The final low-spread charge with $K=\sqrt{\epsilon H}$ is $O(\epsilon H^{1+\gamma/2}P(H/2))$ for sufficiently large $H$. The entire preceding quarter shell lies below a finite cutoff and its weighted survival sum dominates a constant multiple of $H^{1+\gamma/2}P(H/2)$. Bounded overlap and small fixed $\epsilon$ absorb this term before taking the cutoff to infinity. This avoids any presupposition of the moment being proved. Monotonicity and the vanishing weighted tail on the preceding half-window yield the little-oh estimate. The two-full-block argument transfers it uniformly in the location of an ordinary interval, with constants depending on the fixed family.

The fixed first-merger tail concerns three distinct symbolic classes. Its proof leaves the exactly-two-class basin contribution unrestricted.
