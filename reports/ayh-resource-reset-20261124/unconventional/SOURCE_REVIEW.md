## Aaronson–Yolcu–Heule lineage

The primary paper is Emre Yolcu, Scott Aaronson and Marijn J. H. Heule,
*An Automated Approach to the Collatz Conjecture*, Journal of Automated
Reasoning 67, article 15 (2023),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10817-022-09658-8).

for weakenings. A no-removal theorem for a class of interpretations is an
obstruction to that proof class, not a proof or disproof of Collatz.
