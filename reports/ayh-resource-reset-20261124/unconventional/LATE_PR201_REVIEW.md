**The closest denominator overlap is the full-prefix theorem.** For a first-ladder block of \(m\) odd steps and \(s\) shortcuts, put \(B=3^m-2^s\) and let \(\Delta\) be the height increase. The exact reduced closing denominator is \(B/\gcd(B,\Delta)\). For the complete prefix from the fixed origin \(q_0\) to its \(k\)-th odd step, at coefficient-record indices, (L4) gives \(d_k\ge (L^{-1}+o(1))2^{s_k}\), where \(L\) is the source's corrected height. The same closing tends to \(-(L-q_0)\) in the reals and to \(q_0\) dyadically. The note also bounds the large-prime part of \(d_k\). Our `FORCED_CLOSINGS.md` takes a past suffix and a future prefix about one moving center, with clocks comparable to the logarithm of its height. Its conclusion counts how many small denominators one of those two collections can contain. The full-prefix lower bound is a stronger result about its own individual denominator. It supplies no scarcity count across the two collections. The upstream bridge separately controls binary proximity at the earlier state and ternary proximity at the later state of a first-ladder block. Our directed all-clock bound in `PRECISION.md` measures the two primes in the opposite endpoint order. A positive lower bound for elapsed native time divided by the sum of logarithmic record heights forces at least a \(\log_3 2\) proportion of exceptional bits. The separate harmonic repetition theorem treats bounded recurrence of exact complete words of each length. These results permit complex future itineraries. The sharp Hausdorff gauge and its endpoint describe the ternary image of the entire critical tree. Its lower measure bounds do not transfer to a prescribed ordinary source. In `adelic/FIXED_SOURCE_TOPOLOGY.md` different positive rational cycles with nontrivial reduced denominators approximate one chosen ordinary \(x_*\) at every individual finite place and reproduce increasingly long true parity prefixes. Their real heights tend to infinity while a logarithmic phase is prescribed. These cyclic rational approximants do not imply that \(x_*\) is periodic or identify them with an entering and a leaving closing at a moving record.
