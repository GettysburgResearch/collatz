The assumption is a little-o upper bound on genuine failed truncation mergers throughout the sufficiently high levels of the one band [7·81ʲ,8·81ʲ). A suitable upper bound for failed finite certificates also suffices, because it bounds a superset. The project currently has neither upper bound at the required exponent.

1. **Radix tail.** Each integer child contributes one cell of length 81^(−j) to the rescaled band. Its parent is exactly the integer represented at the preceding level. Different convergence status implies failure of any future meeting. Thus the L¹ difference of adjacent color functions is at most Dⱼ/81ʲ. Under the assumed o(81^(αj)) bound, α<1, the remaining L¹ tail after j is o(81^((α−1)j)), by the supremum of the vanishing normalized errors times a fixed geometric sum. The L¹ limit takes only the two original colors almost everywhere.

2. **Correct index and mask.** Fix k≥0 and an integer l. Put c=2ᵏ/81ˡ and restrict to t with both t and ct in [7,8). At index j+l, the rescaled argument is exactly 2ᵏ81ʲt. On the subcells where the fractional part of 81ʲt is below 2^(−k), its integer part equals 2ᵏ times the original integer part. Doubling invariance gives equality of the two finite colors there. The subcell indicator tends weakly to 2^(−k) by interval averaging followed by L¹ approximation. Applying it to the fixed limiting difference is legitimate because the two approximation errors go to zero in strong L¹, the dilated error acquiring a factor c⁻¹. There is no limit involving a simultaneously varying test function.

3. **Partial action.** The resulting equality is on the overlap of the arc and its rotated image, for every fixed nonnegative rotation time. In logarithmic coordinates the rotation angle is irrational. Two positive-measure opposite colors P,Q inside the arc would have |Q∩SᵏP|=0 for every k≥0. The forward saturation of P is positive, mapped into itself, and has the same measure as its image. It is invariant modulo null sets and hence conull by irrational-rotation ergodicity. This contradicts |Q|>0. As an independent argument, the permissible additive shifts k log 2−l log 81 are dense in the reals with k≥0. Translation continuity in L¹ extends overlap-equality to every real shift on compact subintervals of its overlap. Fubini then gives zero integral of the pairwise absolute color difference on the square of the arc. Neither argument assumes invariance under an orbit that stays inside the arc at every intermediate step.

4. **Counts across levels.** Once the limit is a constant color, the opposite color has o(81^(αj)) representatives in the j-th band. The geometric sum, including the possible partial last band, gives o(X^α) on their union. Compactness supplies a finite choice of dyadic multipliers taking each sufficiently large integer into the *union*. It need not take it into a specified level. This map preserves color and has bounded multiplicity and bounded dilation, so the o(X^α) estimate propagates to all integers. Krasikov–Lagarias Theorem 6.1 for the same shortcut map supplies ≫X^0.84 examples of each color in the event of nonconvergence, by using target 1 and a fixed nonconvergent unit target. The optional stronger exponent depends on its separate source-qualified input.

The conclusion controls actual failed comparisons through their effect on the spatial color profile. It does not replace a nonconvergence count by the density of failures at one step of an arbitrary decreasing selector. The use of exact dyadic invariance, complete radix fibers, summable changes, and a comparison that retains the original integer addresses that earlier obstruction.
