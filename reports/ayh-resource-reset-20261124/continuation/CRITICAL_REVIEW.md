# Independent transfer review of the critical compression argument

I reconstructed the written counting argument and read the exact Lean statement in the working packet. The checked arithmetic and the inherited analytic input have different scopes.

For each large critical fiber, $e=d/5+O(1)$, and $u=(3n+1)/2^e$ parametrizes a single reduced residue modulo six in an interval of length $\asymp 2^{d-e}/d$. Oddness and the inverse mod-three condition are exactly the two restrictions. Since $3H\le d/4$, the discard for an incomplete first binary grid, as a fraction of the fiber, is $O(d2^{3H-(d-e)})$. The first input is odd. For the unconditioned parity grid the probability of fewer than $H$ even inputs by $3H$ is exponentially small in $H$, and imposing this odd bit costs at most two.

At the stopping cell write $u=2^h t-a$. Signed affine transport gives $T^h(u)=3^p t-r$, $p=h-H$, where the positive mirror orbit has $1\le r\le3^p$. Choosing $2^p g\equiv a-1\bmod3$ for $g\in\{1,2,3\}$ makes $v=2^p(t-g)-1$ congruent to $u$ modulo three because $H$ is even. Its $p$ initial odd steps give a second arm differing by $D=r-3^p g-1$, of logarithmic size $O(H)$.

The mod-three conditioning leaves $t$ in exactly one step-three progression. The first arm endpoint $3^p t-r$ therefore has odd stride $3^{p+1}$, covering the complete depth-$b$ binary grid once per $2^b$ consecutive terms. Each cell has $L\asymp 2^{d-e-h}/d$ surviving terms, uniformly, so $b=\lfloor\log_2 L\rfloor\gg d$. Counting from the first term of the cell, one full block and at most one further block contain that progression. The exceptional proportion at the finite PR 146 fusion clock is accordingly $O(\sqrt{(H+1)/d})$. Testing affine-profile fusion, which is a function of the binary residue, is essential for this transfer. Physical positivity is preserved in the original cell. No claim that successive compression outputs are fresh uniform inputs follows.

Removing the lower-edge width $4\cdot2^{3H}+2^H+O(1)$ costs at most $O(d2^{3H-(d-e)})$ of the *original* critical fiber. Off that edge, $2^H v\ge u-4\cdot2^{3H}$ gives $v\ge3\cdot2^{d-e-H}+1$. Therefore $m=(2^f v-1)/3\ge2^{d-5H/4}$. The exact numerator upper inequality $2^{5H/4}(3m+1)\le3n+1$ gives strict source compression and preserves the marked upper edge. Both the power-of-two conjugacy and mod-three attachment hold since $H$ is a multiple of eight. Their conjunction with the new lower bound places $m$ in the intended octave.

The Lean declaration assumes $\operatorname{Odd}(v)$ and an actual merger. It proves the size inequality, the critical membership of $m$, the displayed surplus identity, and common ordinary futures. To identify the supplied exponent on the original $n$ with its exact valuation, the application additionally uses $\operatorname{Odd}(u)$ from the critical input parametrization. That oddness is not a hypothesis of the Lean transport declaration. The finite-grid probability estimate, parity concentration, and discard counts remain written. This review checks their use conditional on PR 146's cited estimate. It does not give a new proof of the underlying oriented-state probability theorem.
