# Independent check of the finite menu of deep entrances

Reviewed `FINITE_FLOOR_FRONTIER.md`, SHA-256 `3e1209d5cf22913bb89e20431f70829589ad8d0022bf8ed3c7d99277d4eeca16`. Both the fixed finite-menu theorem and the local sum-of-excursions refinement pass this written audit, using the physical entry and killed-excursion bounds cited in the manuscript.

For a fixed good source, the set of single floor parameters supporting a live low-grade first crossing has measure at most $q$. Each physical time supports a half-open interval of log-floor length at most one and hence at most one integer log-floor. Integrating the $m$ external parameters on the product measure gives $|E_n|^m$ on this one deterministic trajectory. The source-dependent bad set is paid once, and the native survivor fraction multiplies $q^m$ because the occupation bound is pathwise outside that same bad set. The exponent condition $m(b-2\beta)>1/2$ and both Tonelli and finite minimizing-menu conclusions follow. The operational rule scans decreasing heights: previous larger first passages and their grades are known when the next threshold is reached (ties allow a deterministic ordering). On true disagreement absorption never occurs, so absent an exceptional source the first eligible crossing has the claimed grade and the later arm needs at least $(\log_2 3)K-O(1)$ more steps. No independence of arms, physical clocks, or observed grades is assumed.

A band of log-heights $[B,2B]$ meets only $O_\gamma(1)$ whole or partial geometric descent stages. Before hitting grade one the uniform conditional occupation bound at each interior entry supplies $\mathbb E e^{cQ/K^2}\le C$, including truncations. The strong Markov property at the successive *fair* entry times bounds the exponential moment of the sum of the first deterministic $m$ such excursions by $C^m$; padding with zero after killing preserves the inequality. This does not condition on the random excursion count or any future length. A finite stage suffix with at most $m$ entries is bounded by those completed occupations and at most one partially observed excursion, dominated by a full fair continuation (an additive one in $m$ is harmless). Markov at $C'mK^2$ gives an exponential tail in $m$. The separate entry-count bound from a polynomial-offset grade-two restart and the single initial cut excursion apply as used. The time remaining in the original stage never exceeds the full $k_i$-word from the chosen physical restart, and the area transfer unions its deterministic time, state counts and stage, overcounting the adaptive restart legitimately. With $m=C_A\log d$, each of the boundedly many stages contributes $O_A(K^2\log d+(\log d)^2)$ in total including the previously established frontier costs.

Substitution gives the one-floor summable endpoint $1/(d(\log d)^{1+\varepsilon})$. In the growing menu, $q=O((\log d)^{-\varepsilon})$ and $q^{m_j}=d^{-c\varepsilon+o(1)}$; multiplying native survival proves summability for $c\varepsilon>1/2$. The infinite parameter sequence uses its first $m_j$ coordinates at each row, so no independence from one row to another is needed. These conclusions provide an adapted large-grade witness and leave the discordant large-grade color mass open.
