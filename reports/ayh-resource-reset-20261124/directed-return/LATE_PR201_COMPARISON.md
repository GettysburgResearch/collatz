**Directed residue covers.** The first manuscript sends every odd source $n$ to $3n$ on a bounded directed path, with every additional move a forward tripling at an odd unit in a single class fixed in advance. Its two complete tables have 948 leaves and 2,325 tripling positions for $1\bmod6$, and 1,263 leaves and 2,701 positions for $5\bmod6$. Query positions are injective positive-slope affine maps on their own source progressions, with bounded height distortion. Telescoping ordinary binary labels gives global-bridge (1) for each crossing orientation separately. Its combination with the PR 146 directed return transfers the full odd cut to either selected orientation in either residue, without an exponent loss, by (2). Combined with the separately pinned Krasikov–Lagarias and PR 146 inputs, this gives the $0.84$ lower count on unbounded octaves for each of the four choices. The PR reports actual kernel replays of the two covers and semantic applications. We did not rebuild those artifacts or independently audit its full tables in this comparison.

The new PR 197 packet uses a different directed substitution from $1\bmod16$ into $11\bmod32$ and the exact ordered-label exchange with $17\bmod48$. The latter is one of **eight** residue classes modulo 48 within $5\bmod6$. A lower count or a required implication on the entire $5\bmod6$ class supplies no such conclusion on the specific $17\bmod48$ subclass. Global-bridge itself credits the preceding PR 197 finer binary target. Its all-odd source scope and separately signed exponent are important prior independent results on the coarser classes. The PR 197 $0.84$ conclusion at $17\bmod48$ still counts both signs together, because the earlier all-odd to $1\bmod16$ count used there is two-sided. The new PR 201 proof is neither an input to the finer certificate nor a strict-return estimate for its actual original sources.

**A stronger conditional trigger on the scheduled heads.** Global-bridge transfers each chosen unit class and sign to $x=81y+57$ in a prescribed mantissa arc and row congruence, with $y=7\bmod8$ and any prescribed fixed minimum child valuation. It also prescribes the parity of $v_2(x)$ by choosing the unit residue modulo six. The finite multiplier choices and recovery of the odd unit from $x$ control the reciprocal weights. Under M and the pinned PR 146 harmonic-cut argument, a counterexample implies a divergent **unweighted** sum of normalized original scheduled head counts for each selected sign and either valuation parity. Its equation (3) additionally retains PR 201's particular common-clock GOODPROFILE under P. Its moving-window lower bound (4) has a further PR 193 input.

This gives a direct intersection with PR 197's separate good envelope. If $b_j$ denotes PR 201's prescribed signed count divided by $R_j=81^j$, convergence of $\sum_j d_j^{-2}$ with $d_j=\log_2R_j$ forces $b_j\ge d_j^{-2}$ on unbounded rows of a divergent series. Removing PR 197's original exceptions $O_A(d_j^{-A})$ with $A>2$ leaves an inverse-polynomial population. On a fixed suffix, normalization by $R_j/81$ instead changes the fraction only by a constant. The fixed trit-variation theorem can consequently use this particular signed, scheduled population under its own P and R assumptions and the additional M-based trigger. Neither the finite certificate nor the variation implication conditional on a supplied polynomial-density population acquires a new dependency. The classical $.84$ result alone does not give this trigger. PR 201 already records that even its own $O(R_j j^{-5/4})$ good-profile discard can exceed a power-$0.84$ lower count.

**Exact inverse rows.** The second manuscript studies a different sample. An ordinary unit source has exactly $b$ odd steps, total dyadic valuation $2b$, endpoint $q$, and relative carry $\beta/q\ge\varepsilon$. Its future-tested maximum $M_b$ imposes $U^{h(b)}(q)\ne1$. Under a counterexample some fixed rational $\varepsilon>0$ gives at least $2^{\lfloor b/16\rfloor}$ such sources at a common $q$ on every sufficiently large row, separately in each allowed unit class modulo $3^K$, with $2\cdot3^{K+1}+1\le b/40$. Its recorded-path variant tests $q>b/64$ or a repeated path state and yields the threshold $2^{\lceil b/32\rceil}$. The constructions use a binary inverse family, padding by two gaps and a suffix of the actual nonconverging orbit. An escaping ray permits varying terminals; a fixed nontrivial cycle terminal permits repeated suffixes. The eventual-zero side uses the finite simple-path cutoff and assumes universal convergence. The required opposing cofinal upper estimate remains open.

These inverse-row dichotomies avoid the extra inverse atom floor and M. They count sources in a fiber of a single terminal and an exact valuation budget. They supply neither joint ordered disagreement for $n,81n+r$ nor a polynomial fraction of any original ordinary head row or selected real cell. They do not supply an upper bound on the new color variation. No reviewed scientific claim in the finer packet is superseded by that dichotomy.
