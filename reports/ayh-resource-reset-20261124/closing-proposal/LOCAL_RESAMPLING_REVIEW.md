# Independent review of the local resampling argument

I compared the proposed manuscript with SELECTED_TRIT_CELLS.md at PR197 commit 7ad81ec56947146fac854d2586651ca0c934a536 and its physical query construction in unit-exit/LOCAL_COLOR_INFORMATION.md. The original-source deduction works under the same native merger bound P and physical regeneration statement R. The finite Lean core proves the color consistency of chains of bounded ordinary meetings and the lattice stride identity. It does not formalize the probability estimate, the density premise M or Collatz.

1. **Original-source census.** For a fixed native low residue, each \(m\) has at most \(A_*\) original parents, with \(2^{a_0} c_0 A_*=O(1)\). Putting the new high cutoff at \(L=\Delta_{t_*}+2g\) leaves \(O(c_0N/2^L)\) exterior choices per native residue, since \(2^L\ll c_0 N\). Multiplication by \(2^L\) possible low words, the occupancy cap, the native residues, at most polynomially many seed states and \(3^u\) advice strings bounds the normalized cost of any event of uniform-cube probability \(O(e^{-\lambda g})\) by \(O(d^C3^u e^{-\lambda g}/w)\) relative to \(S\). Taking the union over \(O(d)\) blocks still costs \(O_A(d^{-A})\) at the stated \(g\).

2. **Predictability with the extra exterior.** The finer exterior fixes the older exterior used to check physical admissibility. With the added exterior, seeds, native residue and ternary advice treated as fixed parameters, the modal choice at a clock depends only on free low bits already exposed and recursively selected replies at that same clock. All \(h\) test bits are strictly below the fixed exterior since \(r\ge g\ge h\) eventually. On the full cube the allowed chosen parent runs bijectively through residues modulo \(2^h\), and P applies to the bounded suffix. The possible policy uses \(S\) to define a function, without putting any conditional fair measure on \(S\). Splitting the clock sequence modulo \(h\) makes each successive \(h\)-bit test measurable before the next test of that residue class. Conditional exponential moments give the claimed tail.

3. **Actual dissent.** If a genuine regeneration clock has only modal replies, the simulated search returns the actual discordant head, so its bounded nonmerger test passes. After summing the upper bound on passes in every full length-\(g\) block and paying the final \(<g\) clocks, at least \(\eta W/2-O(g)\) genuine regeneration clocks have a first dissent. Only the whole-slab lower bound \(\eta W\) was used. After the first differing reply the alternative sources need not regenerate at that same clock.

4. **Minority mass.** At one clock and fixed exterior there are \(2^{\Delta_t}\) low prefixes and polynomially many complete actual reply words, including length. Multiplying these and the preceding seed, residue, and advice counts by \(\epsilon A_*2^r/3^u\) gives \(O(\epsilon N d^C)\), since \(L=\Delta_t+r\). Dividing by \(wN\), then summing over \(W\le d\) clocks proves the stated discard. The surviving individual's entire reply-word cell lies on its first dissenting branch. In the original policy the other branch has at least that many sources. Deletions are only a test on individuals and do not redefine the policy. With fixed \(m\bmod 2^{\Delta_t}\) and \(m\bmod3^u\), distinct quotients give distinct literal ordinary sites separated by multiples of \(3^{e+u}\). The cap \(A_*\) can be removed from each branch count. This is the same adaptive query **formula** at different ordinary arguments, so the two values of its invariant color can differ.

5. **Finite obstruction and its quantifiers.** Fix finite integer cutoffs, a horizon and a row. Enumerating arithmetic reply paths overestimates the true queries. Direct bounded intersections give a finite graph; globally invariant color is constant on every connected component, even at unequal meeting times. Two sets of at least \(\lceil d^J\rceil\) differently colored sites force every component to omit that many sites. Empty intervals should be discarded; each query produced from its original datum has an actual parent argument. Under P, R and M the divergent head series yields one suffix with infinitely many surviving rows of weight at least \(d^{-3}\), allowing for fixed constants and the polynomial original discard. The lower bound is uniform in the tested horizons. Conversely, if Collatz holds, all finitely many candidate sites on one row hit one, so some single finite horizon connects each nonempty candidate graph. Thus a half-\(d^{-3}\) upper threshold on every large row and every suffix with a freely chosen finite horizon is already sufficient under the inputs, and true under Collatz. The actual contrary large-row upper bound is open. No short or computably bounded runtime is inferred. If describing an actual numerical evaluator, fix the integer schedules and rationalize comparison thresholds with slack. The manuscript defines a finite statistic mathematically rather than delivering that numerical evaluator.

6. **Short-horizon control.** The standard affine recurrence gives \(0\le B_i<3^i\), including the zero-clock case. In a collision with unequal slopes the nonzero integer coefficient of \(x\) has magnitude at least one, while the remaining side has absolute value less than \(6^H(L+2)\). Equal slopes force equal times and grades. Then \(B_i/3^p=\sum_l2^{s_l}/3^l<2^i\); the difference of two such nonnegative numbers is \(<2^i\), contradicting spacing at least \(2^H\). On the good rebase, \(e\ge cW\) follows by taking the constant in the \(\Theta(W)\) lead large compared with the grade-envelope error. The logarithm of the query center is \(\Theta(d)\), while the log diameter is \(O(W)\). This justifies singleton components for \(H=o(W)\), with no conclusion about eventual color.
