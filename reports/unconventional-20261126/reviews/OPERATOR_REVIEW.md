## Computability and numerical controls

The forward empirical measures have a one-edge total variation bound \(2/S\) even at repeated vertices. The row Cauchy–Schwarz factor is \(3\cdot2^r-2\), and summing a squared column uses its norm bound \(M\). The vector-valued triangle inequality gives both localization errors with their indicated signs. Rectangular matrices must include every output of a vector in the input ball. The enumeration works by **starting each monomial at each input basis vector in** \(B(z,S)\), following the factors in the order in which the operator acts, and adding entries for a given output. Every intermediate vertex then lies in \(B(z,S+r)\), and the extra unit of radius covers domain checks. Arbitrary row-centered explorations starting from the outer output annulus could instead exceed this radius. This is an implementation clarification. The finite representative proof correctly allows permanently identical affine maps as well as isolated collisions, and uses the same residue for every prefix. Applying the procedure to \(P-tI\) permits the computable norm bound \(M+|t|\) or \(2M\) on the stated mesh.
