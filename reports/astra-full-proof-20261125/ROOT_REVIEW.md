## Final attempt at the missing universal bridge

After the structural theorem was established, the global agent was tasked again with forcing its hypothesis from a least ordinary counterexample. The following checks locate the failure in the attempted construction; they do not assert that every possible bridge fails.

* In the cofinite topology on an ordinary infinite orbit the actual Collatz update has finite fibers and is continuous. The parity partition is not generally clopen. Adjoining finitely many parity coordinates makes the current parity observable continuous, but does not make the next unknown parity a continuous rational choice. The delayed-line calculation in the global packet expresses that obstruction on the one forward trajectory.
* At a first bad pure power `3^r`, the lifting-the-exponent formula shows that every earlier pure power shares at most `2+floor(log₂ r)` initial parity bits. Their known good tails do not produce comparison histories at every depth from these seeds alone.
* Enlarging to a whole component supplies arbitrary finite incoming histories, including for the component of one. To see this, choose an odd ternary unit `m` in that component. For a prescribed word with length d, q odd inputs and affine carry C, choose arbitrarily large e with `2^e*m = 1+3*2^(-d)*C (mod 3^(q+1))`. Two generates the units modulo powers of three. Then `y=(2^e*m-1)/3` is an ordinary predecessor of m and `x=(2^d*y-C)/3^q` is a positive ordinary source following the word to y. These sources can exceed any prescribed lower bound. They are different incoming witnesses; they do not make one fixed forward trajectory repeat or make its refinements stabilize.

These checks supplied no universal rational model, scalar Mahler relation or all-source certificate selector. The closing decision is therefore to publish the actual results and leave the universal statement explicitly open.
