# Evidence for the affine branch spectrum

**Scope.** The [written proof](../../../research/astra-wild-nine-hour-20261128/arithmetic/AFFINE_BRANCH_SPECTRUM.md) gives a grade-by-grade exponential cardinality. Its only deep input for the plus-map count is Leen’s written entropy-dimension theorem. Invariant-color and directed-separation consequences additionally import the published statements of rStation papers 212 and 225.

The original Leen TeX for version-specific Zenodo 23268832 was available in two earlier source workspaces. Both have SHA-256 `3855fc67c0c30c0e8e78af3de93fd85279d4376f2cb7c1e5d63d24433ce70afc`. I inspected its definition of the geometric parameter, Theorem C and §6, including the theorem’s finite residue entropy convention. This is a primary-source inspection; I did not independently verify Leen’s entropy-dimension proof. Wren paper 64 v2 §10.5 explicitly gives the binomial-versus-ternary upper bound and the total exponent 0.9654 as an upper bound. Paper 212 v1 identifies equal continuation functions with equal affine branches. Paper 225 v1 gives directed separation and a Fibonacci lower bound on the count/binary witness cutoff. I located no matching lower exponential spectrum in these sources.
