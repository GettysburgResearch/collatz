## Closing attempts and non-circularity

The prime-height closing attempt checks actual prime flux, fixed-modulus
recurrence, and finite ordinary description. The first gives an endpoint
identity, the second supplies only order-N height, and the third is
defeated by a fixed-source non-Collatz comparison. None forces persistent
prime retention on a surviving Collatz orbit.

The restricted merger reduction is complete only after its hypothesis is
assumed. Its functional tripling law and transfers from F back to f already
depend on those mergers. Reusing the selector to establish the hypothesis
would require a new physical certificate or a well-founded ordering of
all hypothesis calls. Neither is supplied. The pair route similarly keeps
the carry cost separate from the proved stripping decrease.
