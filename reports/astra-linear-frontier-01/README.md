## Attempt and result

The intended completion was an exact finite inverse-frontier method with
sufficiently cheap retained branching to force a successful lower-rank merger.
A product estimate improves the negative precision budget from quadratic to
linear. More importantly, a dyadic comparison becomes an ordinary signed integer
after at most its initial denominator length in inverse shortcut steps. Its
remaining odd-step budget is then linear, without assuming anything about the
negative Collatz problem. These bounds support the new whole-section-clock
obstruction and positive repayment family.

unbounded shortcut lengths. Yet the obstructing sources are immediately
contracting for the previously available n+5 component. This is a concrete
rank-selection problem, not evidence that those sources are exceptional. The
minimum and maximum of the two ranks both fail on their first edges. A separate
exponent-lifting construction supplies fully convergent hard sources with exact
compressed paths to1.

The first unsupported step in a universal completion remains selection and
repayment on the complement of the explicit guards. No complete Collatz proof,
SC* proof, global cycle exclusion, unsafe-return estimate or status promotion
