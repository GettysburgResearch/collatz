## The extracted finite rectangle

Suppose the total rival grade is $i+h+d$ with $0\le d\le D$. Strip the final $i$ native steps (possible by the previous paragraph), and choose a cut $k$ attaining exactly $d$ earlier odd departures. It exists because the last $i$ steps contain at most $i$ odd departures, and the chronological grade increases by at most one per clock. Write $j=k+L+i$, $Y=T^k y$, $z=T^L Y$, $r=P_L(Y)$, $v=P_i(z)$. Thus $r+v=i+h$ and $r\ge h$. The last-step carry comparison gives $z\ge3^r a$, hence $Y=2^L a+b$ with $b\ge0$ by the same strict transport. Lifting the actual binary parity cylinder gives $P_L(b)=r$ and $z=3^r a+T^L b$. In fact the last $i$-clock equation now reads $3^v T^L b=3^i-2^i-c$, so $T^L b<3^i$, including clocks ending at an even gate.

Set $S=2^{k+L}$ and $P=3^{h+d}$. Again $P(y+1)\le S(x+1)$, and $3^d y\ge Sa$ would imply $P y\ge S x$, $P\le S$, and $y\ge x$. Therefore $3^d y<Sa$. The early carry estimate $2^kY\le3^d y+2^k3^d$ gives $b<3^d$ by subtracting $Sa=2^k2^L a$. Also $Sa\le P a+ S3^d$, whence $S\le2P$ since $a\ge2\cdot3^d$. It follows that $2^L\le2\,3^d3^r$. This is a genuine small ordinary orbit segment from $b<3^D$ to $T^L b<3^I$ with grade at least $h$. The variable clock $L$ has not been bounded in deriving it.

## Why the finite rectangle gives properness

For fixed positive $b,e$ there is at most one visit to $e$ unless the trajectory becomes periodic there. In the latter case every complete return lap of length $l$ and grade $p$ multiplies $2^t/3^{P_t(b)}$ by $2^l/3^p>1$. A positive lap contains an odd departure and the product of its positive additive corrections is strictly larger than one. Consequently only finitely many visits to $e$ can meet a fixed upper bound on that ratio. This recovers the lemma independently, without assuming convergence for $b$. The submitted proof uses a different implementation. Each previous departure from an odd gate multiplies its marked lower distortion bound by $1+1/(3e)$. Bernoulli and the supplied ratio bound give finitely many visit ranks, and a source and gate with fixed rank determine at most one native clock. Iteratively halving positive even gates transfers an endpoint and doubles the ratio cap without changing its grade. The exceptional zero source has all grades zero. Taking maxima over finite $b,e$ yields one **grade** bound at fixed $I,D$; it is not an effective universally computed clock cutoff and is not claimed as one.

Apply this bound with seed cap $3^D$, endpoint cap $3^I$ and ratio cap $2\cdot3^D$. It excludes the extracted grade $r\ge h$ as soon as $h$ exceeds the finite bound, proving the advertised order of quantifiers. The argument does not supply an endpoint after $I$, exclude mergers of larger grade, or force any purported least bad source into this family.
