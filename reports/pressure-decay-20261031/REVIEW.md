orthogonal for this nonnormal operator, so separability gives no contradiction.

PF-004 retains changing sources; they are not one infinite ordinary orbit.
PF-005 is explicitly a different map and establishes only the stated
insufficiency of generic averaged/packing/distortion arguments.

PF-006 retains repeated cycle visits at each fixed clock; their coefficients
decay rather than being killed at 1. The all-source energy has the fixed

PF-007 bounds first hits AFTER a finite prefix and uses an omitted-source
upper allowance, not an assertion that missing sources fail to hit.
The maximum is over odd states because the target variable is odd.

Its finite-bank moment is a lower approximation. The all-source upper
allowance grows exponentially and cannot be omitted. The escaping-source
lower bound first gives cubic cumulative growth. Sorting distinct orbit
values and applying the inherited packing theorem strengthens it to a power
strictly greater than three. Thus an O(q^2) moment bound WOULD suffice, but
no such bound is proved. The external quadratic Haar bound is for a different
law and domain; it is not imported. The literal source-27 excursion refutes
stepwise moment contraction, not eventual periodicity.
