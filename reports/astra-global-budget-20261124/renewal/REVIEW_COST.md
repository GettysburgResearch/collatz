# Independent review of the weighted-duration and carry argument

**Scope.** The ordinary map is the parity shortcut. The claims below use actual physical prefixes up to first visit to one. Prose and code source pins are SHA-256 digests listed below. Bounds with a pending global debit are conditional.

* **Factor complexity and collision.** In a finite balanced binary word two right-special factors of equal length would have a longest common suffix v preceded by different bits. Their two possible right extensions force both 0v0 and 1v1, whose odd counts differ by two. Thus at most one factor at each length is right-special. Deleting the last letter of an extension gives the bound on the next complexity even when some factors have no right extension. Starting with the empty factor gives at most h+1 words of length h. The h+2 physical windows therefore contain two equal names. Congruence of the sources modulo 2^h and the explicit upper-height bound force equality as ordinary integers. The shift in window lengths matters: a word of length 2D, D=h+1, contains every required window starting at clocks 0 through D.
* **Circular extension.** Let i<j≤D be an ordinary repeated state and m=j−i. Ending the doubled period requires i+2m=2j−i≤2D. Consequently all wrapped factors through length m occur inside the original balanced word. Longer windows add whole copies of the common period. For the shifted state Y=2x+1 the update is 2Y'=3^bit Y+1 at either parity. Over a positive cycle the homogeneous multiplier must be strictly less than one by the positive additive contribution. For two cyclic endpoints, the series obtained by iterating into the past has corresponding length-k terms differing by at most a factor of three. Their k=0 terms agree, so max Y<3 min Y, including the strict inequality. This gives max x≤3 min x by integrality. Equality would put a multiple of three on a positive cycle, impossible since every predecessor of a multiple of three is its double. The remaining factor-three band is exactly the hypothesis of the earlier ordinary cycle exclusion in rewriting.md.
* **Toll and logarithmic debit.** For the suffix of any block, bounded remaining length r in terms of current binary length b_i is usable because every suffix before the first violation is balanced, with the final violating bit allowed separately. The terminal binary length B is at most b_i+r. Combining both estimates gives r+B≤(2K+1)b_i. The toll of that edge is ≤1/b_i, so summing over r=L down to 1 costs at most (2K+1) log((B+L)/B). Rewriting this logarithm as log(b_start/B)+log((B+L)/b_start) telescopes on consecutive blocks. The remaining debit is nonnegative since binary length falls by at most one per step. The logarithm does not telescope a second time. Bounding the accumulated residual on every ordinary input is still open.
* **Carry gain and strict records.** At fixed clock, odd count and ordinary positive endpoint, two integral affine rows with carries differing by 3^p d have sources differing exactly by d, with the larger carry giving the smaller source. A positive endpoint and full word legality are essential when constructing the candidate. Strict records of first-hitting time among convergent starts are unbounded since the powers of two have unbounded hitting times. For such a record of time t, no smaller source merges at common clock s≤t−2: a smaller convergent source either has already entered {1,2}, which the record has not yet done at those clocks, or the shared remaining time forces its first visit to be t. A nonconvergent smaller source cannot merge. This excludes positive same-grade carry gain for the entire prefix without settling later clocks or asynchronous mergers. At t−2 the record is 4, since its unique preterminal two-step tail is 4,2,1. Crossing every lower binary-length threshold down to that of 4 charges the corresponding harmonic sum.
* **Gain class and final eligible clock.** For equal length and odd count, appending the next common parity multiplies a competing carry difference by its odd slope, retaining the old source displacement. A full-ternary integral inverse has the correct parity and positivity at every step. For convergers, equal score at their first visits implies the two first-hit clocks agree modulo two and permits synchronized continuation to one. Hence the limiting gain is the distance to the smallest member of the score class. The closing-clock estimate assumes a current ordinary prefix whose odd inputs are all at least three. Its product gives \(2^t\le x(10/3)^p\); if \(2p-t\le A\), square and simplify to \((6/5)^t\le x^2(10/3)^A\). Since \((6/5)^4>2\), this forces \(t<8(b(x)+A)\). The lower-source score cap uses strong induction. It excludes equal-grade gains after the cutoff and supplies no forced gain before it.
* **Abundant changing witnesses.** At the latest different three-bit block the carries differ by a ternary unit times \(3^{2r}\) (the powers of two are units); all earlier differences vanish mod \(3^{2r+1}\). Thus there are \(2^m\) different residue classes. Replacing a nonmaximal prefix would strictly increase the full carry by a multiple of \(3^{2m}\), so each maximizing full word has all prefixes maximizing. The balanced-tile code has length \(1056+6u/32<u/4\) at \(u=32768\). Choosing the exceptional tile positions and allowing arbitrary exceptional tiles gives the stated exponent strictly below \(N/3\). For these words physical preterminality follows directly from \(T^N(x)>(9/8)^m x>2\) for large \(m\); earlier arrival at one would leave the endpoint in \(\{1,2\}\). This safeguard is necessary for the positive toll assertion. The witness can vary with \(N\).
