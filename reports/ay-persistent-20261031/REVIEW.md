## Proof obligations checked

1. Homogeneous escape is quantified over every positive odd prefix, not sampled prefixes. The analytic proof uses the exact two cosets of powers of three modulo a power of two. The sharper cap-ten bound checks all odd residues and lifts by reduction modulo 2^20.
2. The separated-block lemma retains one original input, one fixed cut, the actual shadow valuation sum and strict disjointness. A shadow at 1 does not imply a physical step out of killed 1. The equality case n=5 is a retained counterexample to weakening the strict guard.
3. The adaptive core is not assumed forward invariant. The counterexample 27->41 at cap four is explicit. Direct orbit replay includes such excursions, and the analytic theorem forces their eventual cap exit.
4. Every finite cap has a computable finite core. This proves bounded-popcount eventual periodicity, not exclusion of arbitrary positive cycles. The 3n-1 cycle 5->7->5 is retained as a control against confusing the two assertions.
5. The cap-ten theorem combines COMPLETE core replay with a proved outside-core bound. The hitting rank is not circular: its universal finite horizon is established before the rank is defined.
6. The sparse forest covers arbitrary bit positions by exact affine lifting from the ORIGINAL source. The standalone checker verifies all parity-affine numerators and all child inventories. Exact bit-reversed interval sorting independently checks that exported cylinders are disjoint.
7. Sparse descent is not convergence by itself. The terminally admissible source set can be left after a numeric decrease; changing to a larger cap can raise the capped rank. Those are explicit failed-guard boundaries, not silently paid transitions.

Corresponding complete output bytes agree. Actual commands, wall-clock durations, source SHA-256s and output SHA-256s are in `receipt.json`.

The cap inventories include 4,361,785 total core-source checks across S=2..10. Cap ten alone contains 3,409,915 sources. The verifier follows each source directly without the producer's orbit memo, and separately reconstructs the entire inverse basin at each cap. Forward and backward SETS agree, not merely counts. It tests all 524,288 odd residues for each of nine homogeneous thresholds.

The sparse verifier reconstructs all 618,416 nodes, 72,618,524 shortcut operations (72,618,522 physical plus two explicitly shadow-root operations), every affine numerator and every residue cylinder. The forest and core streams are regenerated and hashed; millions of raw rows are not committed. The digest is an integrity check, not a substitute for the universal lifting proofs.

Integration checks: 7,421 literal SRS edges; 525 complete capped schedules using leftmost, rightmost and seeded-random choices; 720 separated-block cases; 700 unbounded-position sparse lifts up to 19,997 bits; 1,000 dense lift tests, of which 907 are accepted and 93 correctly return outside this selector. These random test counts are not the proof of coverage or exact density.

They are NOT eight independent full-corpus corruptions. Additional negative examples explicitly preserve the sharp-gap failure, cap exit, non-invariant core and 3n-1 cycle.
