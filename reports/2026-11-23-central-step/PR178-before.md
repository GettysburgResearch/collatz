### 1. Construct the actual triadic candidate and locate every denominator failure

[Arithmetic construction](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e6ed7f1429ced4b58b85ca9006edd9a3a12ca193/research/profound-step-20261123/arithmetic/TRIADIC_CONSTRUCTION_ATTEMPT.md) builds the parity-matched candidate
`eta_K = (2^K (1+e_K)-D_K)/3^b_K`
from one fixed positive ordinary orbit. It matches the complementary prefix through K+1 bits and becomes constant after the actual orbit enters 1,2. Its exact paired defect product is `-(4/3)^(K+1)`.

Its reduced denominator is determined by the first backward integrality failure: once a power of three appears, earlier backward steps cannot cancel it. The attempt to transfer extra cancellation from the original source's determinant returns the original identity. The full Subspace product retains the archimedean cost and already tends to one on source 1; algebraicity of the unknown dual is also unproved.

The final [failure-position attempt](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e6ed7f1429ced4b58b85ca9006edd9a3a12ca193/research/profound-step-20261123/arithmetic/FAILURE_POSITION_RESETS.md) gives all four exact transition cases. Even-even steps reset the full denominator. An actual prefix from 3 disproves monotone decrease of the failure position; the explicit source-27 controls show that complete denominator cancellation alone still need not meet the witness horizon.

**Missing step:** after reducing a candidate to `a_K/3^r_K`, prove that some actual clock satisfies
`8 bl(n)+(9r_K+8) bl(max(1,|a_K|,3^r_K))+2 <= K`,
or construct a different witness meeting that finite certificate. The note does not establish this.

### 2. Exact coalescence error and whole-mass trace-profile escape

[Actual-source dynamics](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e6ed7f1429ced4b58b85ca9006edd9a3a12ca193/research/profound-step-20261123/dynamics/COALESCENCE_AND_CRITICAL_COLLISIONS.md) derives, under the pinned actual-component hypotheses,
`TV(zeta_(N,r),eta_N) = N^(-1) sum_(j<N)(1-v_j/v_(j+r))`.

Its supremum over finite inverse clocks equals
`E min(tau,N)/N -> 0`,
where tau is the actual path's coalescence time. With PR180's trace comparison, the retained high-profile fraction improves to **asymptotically the entire mass above every nonnegative threshold B_N=o(N)**, uniformly over inverse clocks.

The collision comparison is at the square-root scale:
`|sqrt(Col_Q(P))-sqrt(Col_Q(P'))| <= sqrt(2) TV(P,P')`.
At Q comparable to `N^(1/theta)`, a sufficient negligible source error is `E min(tau,N)=o(sqrt(N))`.

A fixed absolutely continuous tilt on the **same hypothetical ordinary path space** preserves the enumerated harmonic/path and boundary-dimension properties while making the uniform coalescence error as slow as 1/log(N), or a prescribed slower tail. This does not establish a slow rate for the distinguished canonical law or construct an escaping orbit. It shows exactly why the listed properties alone do not imply the desired rate.

**Missing step:** a quantitative property of the distinguished law sufficient for this comparison, together with the needed collision estimate, or a direct original-source residue-pair upper. Neither is proved.

### 3. Fixed-arity unit equations and the full arithmetic-height cost

[Cycle attack](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e6ed7f1429ced4b58b85ca9006edd9a3a12ca193/research/profound-step-20261123/cycles/UNIT_EQUATION_HEIGHT_ATTACK.md) encodes each actual odd edge as
`(-3n_i)+(2^a_i n_(i+1))=1`.
The source-qualified Beukers–Schlickewei bound yields
`p <= 2^(16t+24)`,
where t is the multiplicative rank generated by the actual odd states. Its finite-prefix version forces unbounded rank along an infinite nonperiodic ordinary orbit. No upper bound on that rank or unrestricted cycle exclusion follows.

The stronger ESS small-height-neighborhood attempt fixes the full algebraic divisible two/three group. For each ordinary n>=2 prime to six, every proposed factorization in that setup instead satisfies
`h(eta)/(1+h(gamma)) >= log(n)/(1+log(3n+1)+2log(n)) > 1/8`.
This lies outside the external theorem's allowed neighborhood. The proof keeps all finite places and the factor two under affine-vector inversion. The conclusion blocks this application, not integer cycles or all unit-equation methods.

### 4. Ordinary q-carries and an explicit failed real/2-adic transfer

[Carry construction and transfer control](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/e6ed7f1429ced4b58b85ca9006edd9a3a12ca193/research/profound-step-20261123/transfer/REAL_ADIC_TRANSFER_ATTEMPT.md) derives the exact q-adic correction series from ordinary binary carries. The finite coefficient-section structure and required polynomiality remain unproved; Christol's finite-automaton hypothesis is not replaced by computability.

The forced real itinerary starting at 3/2 is bounded, aperiodic, and has odd lower density at least `log(2)/log(5/2)`. Its real Bernstein sum is **-3/2**. The same sums have a 2-adic limit in Z_2, so their limits differ. Prepending one digit gives an odd-denominator real value **-4/3**, again unequal to the 2-adic limit.

This explicitly refutes the intermediate even-denominator assertion in López–Stoll, arXiv:2101.12747v1, Section 2. It does **not** establish that their final Theorem 1 is false, determine rationality of the constructed 2-adic limit, or give a positive ordinary Collatz counterexample.
