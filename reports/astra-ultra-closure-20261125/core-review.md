## Sorted gaps

**VERIFIED for the stated ordered positive lap.** I re-derived the rank shift for primitive ordinary odd phases in the interval $[s,8s)$. Distinct positive odd integers have distinct dyadic mantissas. On the quarter grid, a raw slope of 3 compensates for the largest possible correction difference $3/4$. Equality would identify two successor mantissas, contradicting injectivity. The last image is below twice the first. The sorted permutation therefore lifts to a translation. Its displacement equals the sum $h$ of the valuations, because the extra octave changes telescope.

For rational cycles the rank recurrence is an additional assumption. Given it, with $z_r=4dt_r$, using the doubled seam gives $\sum G_r=4ds$. Subtracting successive equations with the same correction gives $2^aG_v=3G_r$, including at the seam. A run of $k$ unmarked departures forces $3^k\mid G_v$. Each marked departure spoils at most one terminal for each of the last $k$ positions. This gives $3^k\max(p-kB,0)\le4ds$. Here $d=D/\gcd(D,C)$, with $D=2^h-3^p$ and $C$ the full affine carry, because $Dx=C$ exactly. The denominator prime to 6 is invariant under the odd step. The seam is always marked since $c_p=8$ and $c_{p-1}\le4$.

I checked the mixed linear-form version of Wu and Wang's Theorem 1 at the publisher. It applies with coefficient triple $(0,h,-p)$ and exponent $4.1163051+\epsilon$. The positive form is at most $p/(3s)$ and $h\le2p$ if $s\ge1$, giving the upper bound $O_\epsilon(p^{5.1163051+\epsilon})$ on $s$. Combining it with the rank bound gives $B\gg p/\log p$ when $d=1$. Under the *separate*, source-qualified period estimate of PR200 Theorem S, the accumulated log error is at most $p/(3s)$ over one lap, giving $O(p)$ error in each mantissa, a maximal gap $O(s/p)$ and the stated uniform upper bound on each unmarked stretch. The generic consistency theorem is mechanized. Recovering sorted order from the ordinary strip and these analytic consequences are written mathematics. Independent centered bits contribute $o(p)$ uniformly by the stated maximal variance estimate. Zero lap sum extends the discrepancy bound to longer backward segments.
