# Arithmetic checkpoint

The two written results concern one candidate ordinary bounded cycle and, for the spacing inequality, the full reduced denominator of one ordered rational cycle. The [two-place window theorem](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/README.md) gives Haar probability for the folded integer coordinate at every interior binary/ternary depth pair and every polynomial-length chronological segment. The actual odd coordinate keeps its band marker, giving an upper density bound of three. The [inverse-curve argument](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/INVERSE_WRAP.md) proves the written estimate $d\max(p^2,s/p^{1/3})\gg p^{1000/479}$. It still requires a new spacing estimate to exclude the whole branch.

Lean 4.34.1 and mathlib commit d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612 checked [FrozenAmplitude.lean](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/FrozenAmplitude.lean) with warnings as errors. The seven declarations establish the literal integer fold, its real coordinate, the amplitude recurrence, the step variation and interval freezing, the common incoming ternary residue, and the transfer from an exact residue collision to a nearby real integer. The external exponential-sum estimates, clock estimates, strip count and asymptotic arguments are written mathematics. The [kernel output](LEAN.log) lists the transitive foundational axioms.

The PR200 written strip count is pinned at commit 81ea4c1754d73c98d1451447527e6b2ba56977cc, theorem blob 341039e2f7683b6c52e2425f2786e3ced5a70809. Its source script blob ef05205788d57847b40c16a6d7df99fb0455f57a was read from Git and its strip function replayed directly. The 555 starting bins and 4096 odd steps gave a 3106-bit count satisfying the strict power comparison. The [replay record](PR200_STRIP.json) preserves the exact result and source hash.

The [finite checker](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/check.py) verified the common denominator, actual odd steps, all dyadic seams, integer rank envelopes and incoming residue fibers at periods 1, 5 and 41, with complete denominators 1, 13 and 44,835,377,399. On three separate synthetic meshes, exact integer root isolation tested 21 amplitudes through the cube of the mesh size. All proximity counts were resolved at the chosen isolating precision. Ordinary and optimized Python produced identical [control records](CONTROL.json). These finite model counts carry no uniform upper bound at large amplitudes.

## Later arithmetic results

The [uniform moving-center argument](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/MOVING_CENTERS.md) gives a three-form $S$-part estimate with explicit moving-center cost and an arbitrary-background adjacent-swap application. Its seven exact affine and divisibility declarations have a [separate Lean receipt](MOVING_LEAN.log). The [all-phase mass theorem](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/PHASE_PAIR_MASS.md) allows arbitrary partitions of the actual discrepancy clocks. Its [four Lean declarations](PHASE_LEAN.log) check the grouped finite endpoint. The [spatial windows](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/SPATIAL_WINDOWS.md) and [short residue circuits](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/RESIDUE_CIRCUITS.md) strengthen the rank-window information and record the finite-difference counterexample to the associated real-only argument.

The [two-branch kernel theorem](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/MIXED_MAHLER.md) compares the binary and ternary Cartier kernels in both directions; its [five Lean declarations](MIXED_LEAN.log) derive the three residue sections of each branch from ordinary coefficient balance and prove the entire binary kernel is closed under ternary sections. The [arbitrary-gap transfer](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/ALL_GAPS_MAHLER.md) gives rational rigidity for every characteristic, every finite ceiling, and signed incoming sums. Its [five Lean declarations](ALL_GAP_LEAN.log) derive the incoming packet and bound every depth-$k$ ternary expansion between binary depths $k$ and $Ek$. Lean 4.34.1 with warnings as errors checked all three receipts above.

The [holonomic theorem](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/HOLONOMIC_RIGIDITY.md) is a written argument over $\mathbb C$ using the exact section expansion, Levelt–Turrittin formal slopes, Fuchs convergence, analytic continuation of all branches, and finite total variation. Its branch-weighted extension uses regular singularity at infinity. These external differential-module theorems and the resulting classification are not formalized in Lean. Polynomial cycle indicators survive the classification. The [affine obstruction](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/AFFINE_SHADOWS.md) gives a power lower bound for any universal multiplicative rescaling of the two-branch graph, and an explicit finite cycle-specific backward selector.

The [second finite checker](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/arithmetic/check_sections.py) verifies the unrestricted exponent coordinates for 50,000 positive odd starts. It compares 6,156 exact symbolic section expansions against independently traversed physical inverse trees at ceilings 2, 5 and 8, with up to 88 incoming leaves. Its integer congruence controls realize four ordinary shadow-obstruction words through length 96. Ordinary and optimized Python give identical [records](SECTIONS.json).
