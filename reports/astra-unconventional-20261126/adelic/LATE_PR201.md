## Exact comparison on a single ordinary component

Use the [pointed odd grades](../../../research/astra-unconventional-20261126/adelic/POINTED_SPECTRUM.md) \((j,h)\) rooted at an ordinary odd \(a\) in \(D\), and put \(L=\Lambda(a)\). Let \(N_{j,h}\) be the number of positive odd sources in that component having these grades. Every such \(z\) satisfies \(z\le\Lambda(z)=L3^j2^{-h}\), so each count is finite. Each member of the full shortcut component has a unique expression \(2^e z\), with \(e\ge0\) and \(z\) odd in \(D\), and \(\Lambda(2^e z)=2^e\Lambda(z)\). Nonnegative summation gives the exact decomposition
\[
 Z_D(s)=\frac{L^{-s}}{1-2^{-s}}\sum_{j,h\in\mathbb Z}
                3^{-sj}2^{sh}N_{j,h},\qquad s>1 .
\]

At level zero \(N_{0,-\Delta}=|F_\Delta|\le L2^\Delta\). The contribution after delay \(T\) is \(O_{a,s}(2^{-(s-1)T})\). Across all levels \(j\ge J\), each ordinary odd source is at least the component floor \(m_J\to\infty\), and so are its even multiples. Their total contribution is \(O_s(m_J^{\,1-s})\). The positive term from the fixed source \(a\) remains at every cutoff in the limiting component mass. Consequently the exponentially numerous high-delay fibers and their concentration near the real point \(L\) are compatible with exactly the same positive escape atom.

Under the separate critical-mean hypothesis and the source-qualified central floor used in the pointed-spectrum note, \(|F_\Delta|=2^{\Delta-o(\Delta)}\). The level-zero series \(\sum_{\Delta\ge0}|F_\Delta|2^{-s\Delta}\) then has abscissa of convergence exactly one. Its terms do not tend to zero for real \(s<1\), and the geometric upper bound proves convergence for \(s>1\). This comparison leaves the boundary value \(s=1\) undetermined. Neither component counting at that boundary nor the universal ternary Hausdorff gauge forces the vanishing of \(Z_D(s)\) for \(s>1\).
