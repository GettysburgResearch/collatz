# Independent source-level semantic audit

Reviewed the Round 13 sources listed below against the packet and their actual imports. This is a statement and proof-structure audit, plus a separate integer replay. Compiler receipts belong to their respective runs. No compilation was repeated for this audit.

**VERIFIED — the marked reduction is on the physical shortcut map.** ShellCover.step halves evens and sends odd n to (3n+1)/2, orbit iterates this map, Basin means some iterate is exactly 1, and Universal quantifies all strictly positive natural starts. Reaching 1 under this shortcut is equivalent to reaching 1 under the usual map since the compressed odd step skips the even 3n+1. Hits and basin_at_hit use actual iterates (including the 1,2 cycle). ShellCover, OffsetDescent and ForwardSection are byte-identical to their repository counterparts. ForwardSection.Marked is oddness and the explicit upper-offset inequality. Its unconditioned collatz_iff_marked invokes bounded_visit. No witness of convergence, section coverage or unknown classification is supplied as a theorem parameter.

PairedLanguage.Accepts is an inductive finite derivation on literal natural parameters, using only a terminal congruence, one odd return, or the six-step arm exchange. The accepted_half equality uses different offsets k and k+1 for the two sources. MarkedHalfReturn strips the smaller partner to its positive odd part and proves equal basin membership through a common orbit vertex. It need not prove that n directly visits the smaller mark. Actual strong induction is on n with a smaller marked witness. Thus the iff has the stated ordinary meaning, with the uncovered-mark obligation still open. Neither the limiting densities nor exhaustive recognition of arbitrary infinite scripts follow from these Lean declarations. Earliest meeting, as distinct from meeting at the supplied time, is written analysis.

**VERIFIED — subtotal arithmetic; identification scope explicit.** The first closed theorem compares the recurrence accumulator at shell 511 after 507 updates, using the common denominator 2^510 and the additional factor 3 from the source weight 1/(3q). The second proves one integer cross multiplication for the geometric remainder. Both use decide +kernel. I independently compared every shell 4 through 511 with Python math.comb sums and the exact bounding powers, including the special initial row, and replayed the final strict finite inequality. The field invariant, orbit injection/counting proof, infinite series comparison and proof that a given physical S obeys the envelope are written mathematics. The endpoint theorem assumes the exact product identity, positivity and strict product lower bound, and the numerical budget. The positive-length condition matters.

**WITH FIXES — endpoint shorthand in the draft README.** Qualify “any distinct-state segment” as any nonempty segment with pairwise distinct source vertices in such a high invariant set. A primitive full lap is eligible. The detailed counting note already does so.

**VERIFIED — high-floor algebra; GAP to an end-to-end formal theorem.** With z=3^n and s=2^S the eight source weights in the seed have binary exponents 0,1,2,3,5,6,8,10, the prefix sums before the corresponding gap, and the full clock contributes 2^12 s^8. The prefix polynomial includes both e_0=0 and e_(n-1)=S. The shared factor f is positive on actual parameters, so the ordinary boundary fraction becomes P(3)B_s(z)/V. The integral Bezout certificate gives gcd(V,B_s) dividing 7 since V is odd and s is a power of two; the separate natural gcd transfer bounds gcd(V,P B_s) by 7P, using absolute values when moving to naturals. For the final Lean inequality the independent hypotheses are positive z,s,g, 2P<sz, 1≤8s²−z², g≤7P, and gd=V. These are justified in the written specialization by a geometric sum bound for P, positivity of the full clock denominator and exact lowest-terms reduction. It is a conditional symbolic inequality, not a theorem taking an arbitrary positive gap word as input. Grouping the carry terms, word-level realization, exact valuations, primitivity, near-critical clock construction and the arbitrary-floor existence conclusion remain written proofs. CycleNormComplete separately proves full residual coprimality at t=2^k for its specified fixed seed. It does not establish the abstract prime-power classification in Lean.

**VERIFIED — Round 11/12 finite interfaces only.** RepetitionWindow assumes every edge in a supplied finite path obeys one of the two input affine equations (denominators 8 and 32, carries 7 and 5). four_window_reject requires the initial size bound, H matched bits and an offset t<2H exhibiting mismatch. It proves incompatibility for that supplied path. PhasePacking telescopes a supplied clock law including both boundary carries, normalizes a supplied complete edge, compares a supplied canonical path, and identifies endpoints below the specified coprime modulus. It provides no existence of a scalar witness, global confinement, or asymptotic counting theorem. The reviewed draft PhasePacking header still says uncompiled; update it only after the coordinator's independent exact-source run.

Source scan for proof holes, local axiom or unsafe declarations and native_decide found none in the pinned Lean modules.
