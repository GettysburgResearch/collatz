* **§3, cyclotomic splitting and (4).** Odd b forces full fibres. For even b any proper factor occupies a single orbit of b/2 roots over each conjugate image. Hence the b/2 power of a fixed root lies in the cyclotomic image field, which makes 2^a eta a square there. Conversely, splitting the binomial using that square and applying the orbit lower bound and tower law gives exactly two rational factors. Since a is odd, square-class testing reduces to 2 eta. For odd conductor or twice an odd conductor the relevant cyclotomic field contains neither square root of plus or minus 2. At 2-adic conductor exponent two, the remaining square class is represented by plus or minus 2i, both squares. At larger exponent the field contains sqrt(2), but a square root of a primitive hth root has order 2h and generates an extension of degree two. Thus the exception for prime-power g is exactly g=4 and even b. The positive-cone geometric quotient in (4) exceeds its leading term.
