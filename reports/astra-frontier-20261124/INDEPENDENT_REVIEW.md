## All binary endomorphisms and output blocks

**Classification and formal series, VERIFIED.** An infinite output from one surviving letter image repeats that image. A nonerasing binary irreducible matrix of period two interchanges the possible first letters, so it has no one-sided fixed word. In the diagonal case, iterating the first expanded letter leaves the preceding individually fixed letters unchanged and fills the suffix with one letter. A nonperiodic fixed word therefore falls under the primitive theorem or the triangular form \(0\mapsto v,1\mapsto1^d\), with both letters occurring infinitely often. When \(a=|v|_0\) and \(b=|v|_1>0\), counting the ones preceding the \((ai+t)\)-th zero gives

\[
k_{ai+t}=bi+dk_i+h_t.
\]

At \(i=t=0\), \(h_0=0\) and \(d>1\) forces \(k_0=0\). For \(a=1\), the equation at \(i=1\) is impossible. Thus \(a\ge2\). For \(d=1\) an arbitrary prefix of fixed ones is included through \(k_0\). Substitution in the zero-indexed series \(V=\sum_iX^iY^{k_i}\) gives the displayed homogeneous functional equation. Its multiplier has constant term one modulo 2 at every physical iterate. Diagonal specialization recovers the zero-indicator series, so MPS-3 also proves the formal transcendence of \(V\).

**Moving and repeated-eigenvalue cases, VERIFIED.** For \(d>a\), direct matrix powers yield a positive rational dominant vector, a secondary eigenvalue \(a\ge2\), and nonzero Perron coefficient from the strictly positive initial second valuation. This supplies the hypotheses actually used in the rational-weight part of MPS-5. For \(a>d\ge2\) or \(a=d\ge2\), put \(T_n=a^n+bL_n+d^n\) as in the source. Then \(v_2(x_n)\gg T_n\), \(h(y_n)=o(T_n)\), and \(\sum_{j<n}T_j=O(T_n)\). At equality, \(L_n=na^{n-1}\) and \(b>0\) are essential. Under putative algebraicity, the propagated zero-series values have height \(O(T_n)\) with a constant independent of the auxiliary polynomial.

Choose the auxiliary polynomial over \(\mathbb Q(Y)\) and clear denominators. Its \(X\)-leading coefficient after substitution is a nonzero integral polynomial \(f_t(Y)\) since every \(X\)-coefficient of \(V\) is a polynomial in \(Y\). Distinct rational \(y_n\) eventually avoid its finitely many roots. The upper valuation of \(f_t(y_n)\) is \(O_R(1+h(y_n))=o(T_n)\), while every higher coefficient at \(y_n\) is 2-adically integral. Thus the leading term dominates. For fixed auxiliary degree \(m\), division of the height bound by \(T_n\) and passage to \(n\to\infty\) eliminates both \(O_R(1)\) and \(\deg_Y(R)h(y_n)\). Only then does one let \(m\) increase. The product formula contradicts quadratic auxiliary order against linear normalized height. Arbitrarily large \(Y\)-degree creates no missing uniformity assumption.

**Fixed parameter, VERIFIED.** For \(d=1\), the rescaled positive coefficients satisfy \(u_{ai+t}=c_tu_i\), \(c_0=1\). If a polynomial recurrence holds, apply it on the indices \(a^Lh+i\) for a fixed \(i\) with \(a^L>i+r\). Each term shares the nonzero factor \(u_h/u_0\), including an initial fixed prefix. The leading coefficient in \(h\) of the resulting polynomial identity supplies one nonzero constant recurrence valid for every \(i\). Writing the resulting rational generating function in lowest terms as \(A/B\), the functional equation gives \(A(Z^a)\mid A(Z)\) by coprimality after substitution. Thus \(A\) is constant, \(\deg B=1\), and \(u_i=u_0\gamma^i\). Positivity and \(u_a=u_1\) force \(\gamma=1\). The strictly positive rational base \(y\ne1\) then makes every difference \(k_{i+1}-k_i=b/(a-1)\) constant, forcing eventually periodic output. This proves formal transcendence on the desired nonperiodic word, allowing the ordinary one-variable height contradiction at the physical parameter.

**Output classification, VERIFIED.** For positive accelerated gaps the inverse maps preserve the odd 2-adic integers. For shortcut bits they act on all of \(\mathbb Z_2\). Each model has unique forward symbols. When the two nonempty output blocks have a common fixed source, both repeat to form that source's one itinerary. Each block length is a period of this itinerary, so arbitrary concatenation produces the same periodic word. One erased output block produces an infinite repetition of the surviving one whenever the output is infinite. A periodic output tail gives a rational affine fixed point. The inverse prefix is an invertible rational affine map for any finite cut, including cuts inside blocks. These statements complete the output dichotomy. Whether arbitrary exceptional orbits have presentations in this class is open.

## Ordinary prefixes and collision laws

**Finite word with prescribed source residue and target, VERIFIED.** In the notation of the source, integrality and the source residue require

\[
2^r\equiv(2^A+3C+3^{n+1}u)(2^A t)^{-1}\pmod {3^{n+m+1}}.
\]

Both factors are ternary units for every allowed \(u\) and \(t\). The induction \(v_3(4^{3^j}-1)=j+1\), followed by the sign of 2 modulo 3, proves that 2 generates the unit group, including modulus 3. The congruence gives exactly one exponent class. It also makes \(Q=(2^r t-1)/3\) integral. Splitting the carry at time \(i\) shows \(3^{n-i}\mid 2^{A-A_i}Q-C_{i,n}\), since \(2^{A_i}\) is invertible modulo \(3^{n-i}\). These integral states satisfy each backward equation. For \(r>0\), \(Q\) is positive and odd. If \(z\ge1\) is odd and \((2^g z-1)/3\) is integral with \(g\ge1\), that predecessor is also positive and odd. Induction gives exact valuations for every step. This proves the source statement for all its cases, including \(n=0\), \(m=0\), and \(3\mid u\). It also proves that *every* positive representative works.

**Explicit collision-one family and canonical scalar, VERIFIED.** The terminal inverse at 1 has gap \(r=3^{N+m}+1\). Each preceding prescribed gap one gives \(q_i+1=(2/3)(q_{i+1}+1)\). The factorization of \(2^{3^j}+1\) yields \(v_3(q_i+1)=m+i\). The first \(N+1\) states are strictly increasing when compared successively, have collision one at precision \(m\), and the next step reaches 1. The actual discounted gap scalar from the fixed future \(2,2,\ldots\) gives \(h(q_i)=1/2+3^{i+m-1}+1/(2\cdot 3^{N-i+1})\). For \(m\ge1\) these are the printed integer carry and centered fractional phase, with canonical digit one even at the final large gap. An orbit hitting an injective future is itself injective by determinism. The general theorem therefore also places such finite collision-one segments in any escaping component, conditional on the existence of that component.

## Checks and boundary

An independent reverse-enumeration check took all 40 words of lengths zero through three with gaps in \(\{1,2,3\}\), each \(0\le m\le2\), and every ternary-unit odd target up to 31. It tried every exponent in two complete periods starting at 1, performed each inverse by integer division with a remainder check, and replayed the actual gaps forward. All 11,440 successful starts covered exactly two representatives per source residue at the predicted spacing. Separate direct inversion of the explicit family checked 45 cases with \(N+m\le8\) and 120 exact canonical digits when \(m\ge1\).

Finite substitution-prefix controls also checked 83,000 zero-position recurrences in 260 triangular presentations with image length at most six and \(1\le d\le4\), including a nonzero prefix of fixed ones when \(d=1\). They checked 33,200 of the rescaled digit-exponent identities. The unbounded conclusions above rest on the reconstructed written proofs. The formal Lean state, literature comparison, ancillary phase classifiers and possible Collatz counterexamples are outside these verdicts.
