In the remaining triangular incidence matrix, write the mixed image counts as $(a,b)$ and the pure image as $1^d$. When $d>a$, both proofs recover the positive rational Perron direction by exact matrix powers. When $a\ge d\ge2$, both impose the first $(m+1)^2-1$ vanishing conditions over $\mathbb Q(Y)$, with individual $X,W$ degrees at most $m$. Coefficients at a fixed $X$ degree are integral polynomials in $Y$. Their arbitrary $Y$ degrees at a fixed auxiliary degree cost only $o(v_2(x_n))$. Fixing the auxiliary polynomial before sending $n$ to infinity is essential. The source uses $G_1$ with finite rows of ones and our proof uses $G_0$ with one monomial per row. Their telescoping relation preserves algebraicity at the physical points.

For $d=1$, both normalize the zero-clock coefficients to a radix sequence with $H(z)=P(z)H(z^a)$. PR194 assumes algebraicity and evaluates the monic minimal polynomial at the transformed argument. Comparing constant coefficients yields $H^l\in\overline{\mathbb Q}(z)$, where $l$ is the degree. Any nonzero finite divisor order of $H^l$ that were nonzero modulo $l$ would propagate to all $a^n$ preimages at depth $n$. Its finite divisor rules this out. The order at zero is zero, and the order at infinity follows by degree zero of the divisor. Thus $H$ is rational over the algebraically closed constants. The identity $f_{ak}=f_k$ permutes the finite exponential bases of its partial-fraction coefficient sequence, making them roots of unity and their polynomial weights constant. Repeated digits then force their positive weights to equal one, making the gap word eventually periodic. This use of a positive real embedding is solely formal. The 2-adic auxiliary argument returns to rational coefficients. These steps give the claimed contradiction.

The current packet takes a different route in that last case. Separating high and low radix digits converts a hypothetical polynomial recurrence into a constant one. A coprime numerator-denominator argument then forces a constant numerator and a linear denominator. The packet also states the erasing-input cases explicitly, allows empty output blocks with infinite total output, and treats arbitrary finite raw shortcut-parity blocks via $J_0(t)=2t$ and $J_1(t)=(2t-1)/3$. The erasing cases reduce to periodicity, and the parity case uses the same analytic argument with exact inverse parity. These should be described as additional coverage and an alternate proof, without a priority claim for the underlying binary rigidity theorem.
