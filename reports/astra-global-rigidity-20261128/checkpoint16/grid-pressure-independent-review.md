1. The lifted full pool of periodic representatives is strictly order preserving and bijective, so its sorted ranks undergo one integer translation. The seam carry only increases the winding in the digit cap \(0\le d<2^b\). Uniform sampled physical angles converge weakly to Haar by invariance under the limiting irrational rotation. Uniform convergence of their distribution functions then identifies the ordered physical angles with \(i/P+o(1)\), uniformly in the full pool.
2. The total winding through \(n=\lceil\log_3(2K)\rceil\) dates gives \(2^{B_n}\ge3^n/2\ge K\). An identical winding and digit word implies the initial difference, smaller than \(K\) in absolute value, is divisible by this power of two. Thus the word labels determine each pooled start. The spatial bin and winding calendars are jointly constant on at most \((m+2)n\) half-open cells. Conditional subadditivity and the Gibbs entropy upper bound, summed with the actual stationary uniform rank law, give the written information bound.
3. In the limit, \(w(v)=2^{-v}\) satisfies \(2^{\lfloor v+\gamma\rfloor}w(v)=3w(\{v+\gamma\})\). In the logarithm of the exponential digit sum this changes the Haar integral to the ternary three-term pressure, for each sign of the real parameter. Its reflection symmetry gives both symmetric ends of the interval. At full cardinality exponent, the two derivatives at zero coincide and give \(1/(6(\log2)^2)\).
4. Bondareva, Luchin and Salikhov's printed Theorem 1 gives the strict lower bound with exponent \(4.116201\) on \(|h_1\log2+h_2\log3+h|\). Taking \(h=0\), \(h_1=a\) and \(h_2=-q\), with \(a\asymp q\), gives the stated \(q\gg K^{1/5.116201}\) after the grid clock excess. The primary result here is on the linear form itself. The published reduced cycle bound \(A/p\le\eta_{2m}\) and Taylor expansion on \(y\ge m\) yield the stated conditional reciprocal consequence.
