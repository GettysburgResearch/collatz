# L-0110 — Morphic / S-adic schedules still obey precision accounting

Claim ID: `L-0110`  
Title: Automatic, morphic, and S-adic block schedules do not evade `T-0101`  
Status: `PROPOSED`  
Authoring agent: `grok45-01`  
Reviewing agents: none yet  
Created: 2026-07-21  
Last updated: 2026-07-21  
Dependencies: `T-0101`, `L-0103`  
Scope: infinite schedules generated by finite automata, morphisms, or S-adic products  
Related counterexample candidates: none (obstruction)

## Statement

Let \(\mathcal W\) be a finite alphabet of chronological parity words. Let
\(\sigma\in\mathcal W^{\mathbb N}\) be an infinite schedule that is:

- **eventually periodic**, or
- **k-automatic** / generated by a finite automaton reading \(n\) in some base, or
- **pure morphic** (fixed point of a morphism on \(\mathcal W^*\), or its image
  under a coding), or
- **S-adic** (generated by composing a sequence of morphisms from a finite set).

Write \(\sigma_{\le N}\) for the concatenation of the first \(N\) blocks, of
total bit-length \(\Lambda_N\).

Then:

1. Each finite prefix \(\sigma_{\le N}\) obeys `T-0101`(A)–(B): followers form
   one cylinder of modulus \(2^{\Lambda_N}\), with generic tax \(\Lambda_N\).

2. Any \(x\in\mathbb Z_2\) realizing the whole schedule is the unique inverse
   limit of those cylinders (`T-0101`(C)).

3. If \(\sigma\) is eventually periodic and the cyclic concatenation is
   supercritical, then \(x\) is the corresponding negative rational fixed
   point (`T-0101`(D)).

4. Automatieity / morphicity alone does **not** create free 2-adic parameters
   or compact positive IFS certificates. It only constrains which unique
   2-adic point (if any) is selected.

## Motivation

Blocks the hope that “use a Sturmian or paperfolding schedule of mild
supercritical blocks” automatically yields an ordinary divergent orbit.
The schedule still specifies a single 2-adic cylinder tower.

## Proof

(1)–(2) apply `T-0101` to each finite prefix, regardless of how the schedule
was generated. (3) is `T-0101`(D). (4) is the observation that the generative
mechanism does not appear in the tax or cylinder formulae — only the
concatenated bits matter.

## Gap audit

- Does not prove that aperiodic morphic supercritical schedules have
  non-integral 2-adic points (that would need Diophantine / automata tools
  beyond this packet, e.g. Cobham-type constraints used in the symbolic-rewrite
  program). It only says accounting still applies.
- Subcritical morphic schedules may still be cycle candidates (handoff
  `directions/D-CYCLE-*`).

## Adversarial tests

`X-0114`: Sturmian-like mechanical schedules of two mild words; verify cylinder
moduli and absence of long ordinary survivors.

## Suggested next attack

Import automata-theoretic non-integrality tools for aperiodic supercritical
codings, or stay with accounting and move to multi-chart generalizations.
