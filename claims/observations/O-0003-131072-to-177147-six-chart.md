## Statement

\[
\boxed{
T^{17}(131072q+9090+j)=177147q+12302.
}
\tag{1}
\]

```text

11000100111111011
```

Each contains eleven odd steps. Therefore

\[
\frac{3^{11}}{2^{17}}=\frac{177147}{131072}>1.
\]

The induced partial map is

\[
\boxed{
H(131072B+j)=177147B+j,
\qquad0\le j\le5.

\[
A\equiv3212\pmod{46075},

\[
n(A)=\frac{177147A-2181514}{46075}.

## Proof

`X-0001` exhaustively discovers the run and computes the six parity words. Applying `L-0001`, their affine constants are

\[

\[

\[
177147(9090+j)+B_j=131072\cdot12302,
\]
