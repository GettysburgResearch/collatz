## Significance

The dyadic stage controller does not need an oracle to choose the next **stable frontier bit**. A finite control state of size at most 486 can emit that bit periodically.

This substantially narrows the remaining stack burden:

- the slowly stabilizing low frontier is finite-state;
- the target-type cap is at most six bits by `L-0022`;
- the unresolved information is the moving bulk of the current inverse-power prefix.

## Caution

The periodic stream is the limit of changing finite connector words. It does not mean that the entire connector language is regular or eventually periodic. At stage \(m\), the full connector prefix has length \(\Theta(2^m)\), while only \(\Theta(m)\) low bits have stabilized to the rational frontier.

Preloading the complete 2-adic rational would not prove an ordinary finite start. A valid grammar must append each periodic frontier bit through a forward finite rewrite while also transporting the moving bulk and the ordinary marker.
