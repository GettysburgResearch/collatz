## 17. Priority order by distance to a full disproof

This ranking measures dependency distance, not subjective probability.

### Tier A — one finite or sharply stated certificate from a disproof

1. **Positive cycle synthesis:** exact valuation word and replay; start with the \(m=8,\ K=13\) packet.
2. **Regular sanctuary:** explicit DFA plus exact closure/nontriviality certificate.
3. **M1 centered witness:** explicit \(\xi\) or eventual-zero appended-block itinerary.
4. **H ordinary survivor:** one stabilized positive H cylinder sequence.
5. **Third component/ray:** one binary fixed ray outside the two known components.
6. **Coverage deficit:** one rigorous \(C(x)<x\).

### Tier B — decisive shared lemmas or architecture pivots

1. classify the maximal collision-stage class covered by PR #33's almost-\(S\)-unit exclusion;
2. design a variable-width, growing-rank, or quotient-refund collision architecture that provably escapes that class;
3. derive a quantitative H fresh-prime mass theorem, using PR #33 as the fixed-width model;
4. prove a native all-itinerary carry theorem for \(64\to81\);
5. prove a point-spectrum-to-component extraction theorem;
6. derive a generic symbolic-cube sanctuary theorem beyond structured gate normal forms.

### Tier C — useful filters but not full-path closures

- period-ten special-vector irrationality;
- further finite survivor minima;
- deeper finite collision fibers;
- additional fixed-depth safety state counts;
- more measure-level EQ without an ordinary-section bridge;
- generalized-map or control-map experiments without transport.

## 18. Recommended research allocation

1. **Run the finite \(m=8,\ K=13\) cycle packet now.** It is bounded, proof-producing, independently replayable, and tests the direct finite route.
2. **Stop treating the frozen collision class as an open seam search.** Map exactly which `T-9705` hypothesis a successor architecture must violate; do not spend more computation choosing words inside the proposed-excluded class.
3. **Generalize or escape the almost-\(S\)-unit template.** The generalization target is variable-width collision/H recurrences; the constructive target is growing internal rank, substantial outside-prime height, controlled degeneracy, or quotient refund.
4. **Keep the centered lane native.** Work on appended blocks, first positive block, and all-scale recurrence; do not route through the quarantined four-phase identity.
5. **For analytic lanes, attack extraction before computation.** No large spectral or truncated-cone experiment should precede a theorem saying exactly what certified data would imply a new component.
6. **For sanctuary synthesis, replace concrete antecedent accumulation with symbolic transition cubes or arithmetic carry lemmas.**

## 20. Bottom line

The project has not produced a counterexample. Its main achievement is to make the ordinary-integer boundary explicit in several mathematically equivalent forms, eliminate many low-memory ways of crossing it, and—at proposed level—close the most developed fixed-width corrected-stage architecture by an almost-\(S\)-unit finiteness argument.

The global choice is now clear:

- **construct an integer-first finite certificate** such as a cycle or sanctuary; or
- **solve the all-scale realization problem** by coupling high symbolic complexity, fresh arithmetic support, and extremely low completion height.

Everything else is either a filter, a control experiment, a restatement, or an indirect route awaiting a transport theorem.
