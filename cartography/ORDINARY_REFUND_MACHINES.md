## 1. Common normal form

The strongest current constructions all reduce a physical Collatz subsystem to a deterministic partial map whose edge has the affine cylinder form

```text
q = rho_s + 2^E ell
 ->
q' = sigma_s + M_s ell,                 (1)
```

where:

- `s` is finite exact control or a current arithmetic type;
- `q` is one ordinary nonnegative quotient/counter;
- `rho_s mod 2^E` is the complete next-domain condition;
- `ell` is the actual most-significant lift, not a preloaded inverse-limit digit;
- `M_s` is odd;
- the next type and physical block are determined from the current finite state and quotient.

When `M_s` dominates `2^E`, every noncanonical lift `ell>=1` refunds the consumed top boundary. The missing theorem is never local existence or drift. It is:

```text

```
