# Research tasks and decisive obstacles

The [research map](../../docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md) explains the programs and the [catalog](../RESULTS_CATALOG.md) states what survives. These tasks are alternatives, not stages of one accepted proof. A small rigorous contribution can clarify one interface without solving its global target.

## Centered dependencies

**Useful first contribution.** Reconstruct the three dependency clauses used by the [nonconstant factor-complexity repair](../integrated/factor-complexity/README.md): canonical stabilization/ordinary reconstruction, centered-power equivalence and the strict recurrence cone.

**Deliverable:** a readable local derivation of exactly those clauses, with the map and `A_0>=2` hypotheses, followed by the existing pigeonhole argument. Check the constant-code zero completion, the real endpoint codes `1000...`/`0111...`, and the distinction between nonconstancy and non-eventual constancy. Retain exact sources; a changed formulation needs its own review. Do not attempt to verify the entire centered source branch as one object.

**Decisive next step:** apply the repaired screen to a natural symbolic language or prove ordinary least-root escape. Weighted EQ or density zero alone does not exclude every ordinary seed.

## Ordinary root decision

For one specified aperiodic chart, prove boundedness or escape of its least positive legal roots. In the six-branch chart use the [current root–cap recurrence](../../claims/lemmas/L-0041-six-branch-root-cap-recurrence.md) and [two-sided overlap](../../claims/theorems/T-0046-two-sided-cap-root-overlap.md).

**First deliverable:** one source-dependent lower bound or exact exclusion for every feasible overlap length at selected times, retaining the same stabilized initial source and every physical extension. `m_0=1` is not the empty residue zero. One sequence of lengths with the right limiting ratio is not enough. The corrected-256-stage exclusion is a model of an all-directive negative theorem, not permission to transfer its conclusion to this chart.

## Coefficient and cycle tasks

SC* needs a finite coefficient-subcritical prefix for **every fixed positive source**. A useful partial result is a proved source-dependent stopping window beyond a genuinely specified class; longer finite censuses do not supply universality. [Current coefficient theorem](../integrated/coefficient-stopping/README.md).

For FC*/cycle work, prove a complete ordinary displacement obstruction or construct a whole-denominator witness with actual replay. Keep all proper-prefix inequalities, every prime-power factor and one common displacement. Handle zero affine remainder before logarithms. [Orbitwise/echo guide](../integrated/orbitwise-boundaries/README.md) · [Cycle algebra](../RESULTS_CATALOG.md#full-denominator-cycle-algebra-and-height-gates).

A separate small review task is the exact corrected periodic-tail formulation: zero/signed/trivial cases, finite preperiod and productive deterministic fixed-block controllers. It is not a new review of all cycle work, and passing it would not prove cycle exclusion or the proposed SC*/FC* bridge.

## Survivor-specific transfer

The [current timed bootstrap](../integrated/exceptional-mass/README.md#current-timed-bootstrap) spells out the physical descent, recursive clock, scale reduction and dynamically surviving endpoint set. Prove a pullback saving for **that set**, with constants reusable at every required scale.

**First deliverable:** a rigorously justified positive local saving and survivor-specific fiber gain, or a precise counterexample to a proposed state/weight model. Distinguish strict descent below the source from descent to `X^r`. A counting bound for roots or orbit minima is not a bound for every start in their basins. The all-subset positive-saving premise is already refuted by mass conservation.

**Closing target:** an actual fixed-floor survivor estimate strong enough for its full source-qualified predecessor comparison, or a valid global killed-weight criterion. Neither an illustrative exponent choice nor finite Green mass supplies it.

## Unsafe transport

[Safe estimates](../integrated/renewal-transport/README.md) leave a retained resonance/unsafe process. Find a function space or amortized inequality preserved by actual re-entry.

**Deliverable:** state the map, killed states, projections, norm, input envelope and return operator; then prove the input hypothesis after each return. Test against the unsafe first-moment explosion and fresh-shell/transported-distribution counterexamples. A finite-time enclosure should retain a tail bound depending honestly on the horizon.

## Complete merging

[Physical rank certificates](../integrated/rank-merging/README.md) need a total successful selector, not just finite witness spaces or infinite guarded families.

**First deliverable:** a new guarded family with exact ordinary sources, finite clocks, common endpoint and strict decrease of one specified rank, plus explicit coverage and uncovered cases. Test the 121 inverse-only obstruction, mode switches and phase mismatch. **Closing target:** recursively complete guards with a well-founded proof that selection and repayment always terminate. An algorithm returning UNRESOLVED is not that result.

## Padé beyond a fixed family

The [native stack theorem](../RESULTS_CATALOG.md#padé-and-periodic-2-adic-repetition) covers eventual periods 1–9. The same complete delayed equal-phase family cannot cross the required exponent at period ten.

**Deliverable:** a reduced-height combined-moment, orthogonal-polynomial or related approximation construction with explicit nonzero error and denominator bounds. Another raw determinant or phasewise delay is not a new mechanism. Preserve `M>=rn+1` where the existing family is used. A growing nonperiodic directive additionally needs period-uniform estimates.

## Exact interfaces and control systems

For H, quotient-refund or regular-language work, a useful contribution is a local proof of one source-pinned native interface with exact ordinary decoding; do not admit missing legacy claims with it. For the broader algebraic-section extension, address a specific constant-branch, derivative/finite-difference, coefficient-field or analytic-reference requirement before applying full-tree rigidity.

For `5x+1`, repair and independently replay the finite depth-50 integer-width defect while keeping the comparison map distinct. For rewriting, prove termination of the actual rules, not just value-preserving administrative normalization. [Scope and source routes](../RESULTS_CATALOG.md).

## How to submit useful evidence

Choose a task or propose a different direction in [active research](../README.md#active-research). Submit the precise statement or counterexample, assumptions, proof route, what was actually checked and what remains missing; [contribution guidance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) supplies the workflow. No new registry entry is required to explore. Proof-producing code should include an exact artifact and an adversarial check, but finite evidence must retain its finite scope.
