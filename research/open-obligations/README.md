**Collatz is not proved by this repository.** These are mathematical obligations, not promises that a larger finite experiment will close them. The [research map](../../docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md) and [resident packets](../integrated/README.md) provide context.

| Obligation | What is missing | What does not supply it |
|---|---|---|

| Universal charge/local SC windows | Prove the upper charge hypothesis or all required local coefficient windows for actual sources. | Conditional source-height/location estimates or finite simple-path records. |
