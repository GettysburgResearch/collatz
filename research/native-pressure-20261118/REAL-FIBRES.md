# True ternary-cylinder conditionals at every depth

Let (R,Z) have the stationary joint affine law generated by the outer
maps z↦(3z+1)/2^a with probabilities 2^(−a), in the real and ternary
completions. For every fixed positive odd unit t the probability laws

    law(R | Z≡t mod 3^k)

converge weakly as k→∞ to the law of the real series B₀ on the canonical
inverse path from t. Convergence also holds for the transport cost
|x−y|^s for every 0<s<1. Every one of these finite-precision
probabilities has infinite first moment, whereas the limit is supported
in [0,t]. The normalization by the actual ternary cylinder mass at
each depth matters here.

The prior canonical-fibre theorem proves convergence of unnormalized
Cesàro averages. Its real approximation works at single depths; removing
the average requires the every-depth pointwise ternary trace.

[Canonical fibre theorem, §9.2, equations F1–F8](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/19/v2)
· [All-depth ternary trace](EVERY-DEPTH.md).

Use g_k(t)=3^k P(Z≡t mod 3^k), and write H(t)=lim g_k(t).
In the normalization h of the other notes, H=(3/2)h at positive
ordinary integers. It is finite and bounded below on odd units,
and the normalized inverse kernel reverses x→t with weight
J H(x)/H(t), J=3/2^a. Write u=g₁/H on this state space, bounded since
g₁∈{1,2}. The recursion gives P_t^j u=g_(j+1)(t)/H(t)→1.

For any bounded function F of the infinite inverse path,
E_t[F u(X_j)]→E_t F. First condition on any fixed prefix, using the
pointwise convergence at its endpoint and dominated convergence; then
approximate F in L¹ by its conditional expectations on finite prefixes.
Uniform boundedness of u controls this last error independently of j.

To compare the real laws, put k=j+1 and peel j outer letters from the
stationary series. A compatible word w gives offset β_w in the real
line, multiplier J_w=3^j/2^(Σa), positive odd integer inverse endpoint
x_w, and t=β_w+J_w x_w. In the residual tail require Z'≡x_w mod 3.
The unnormalized stationary measure 3^k P(R∈·,Z≡t mod 3^k) thus has
the exact mixture representation in F4 of the source. Dropping the
residual real displacement J_w R' gives the bounded-support measure

    H(t) E_t[u(X_j) δ_(β_j)],

where β_j is the realized prefix offset. For 0<s<1 the transport
cost of this drop is at most

    3 E[(R')^s] Σ_(unit-compatible w) J_w^(1+s)
      ≤ C_s H(t) E_t[J_j^s] → 0.

The real iid tail has finite s-moment by E(3/2^a)^s<1 and subadditivity.
The inequality uses the positive unit lower bound of H. The realized
J_j tends to zero by the strong physical law (also directly by cycle
exit and distinct integer inverse states), and J_j≤t from the integer
identity. Dominated convergence applies. The common mass before and
after dropping the tail is g_k(t)→H(t)>0, so the cost also tends to zero
for the probability normalizations.

Along the canonical path β_j increases to B₀≤t. In the dropped measure
one may replace β_j by B₀ with cost tending to zero by bounded
convergence. The resulting probability converges even setwise to the
law of B₀ by the path-test identity applied to F=1_(B₀∈A). On [0,t],
weak convergence implies convergence in cost |x−y|^s: partition into
finitely many intervals of small diameter with limiting-null interior
endpoints, match their common masses cell by cell, and use the bounded
diameter to transport the vanishing unmatched mass. This proves the
asserted convergence for the true conditional measures.

For sharpness, peel k rather than k−1 letters. Compatibility modulo
3^k is then determined entirely by the prefix, independently of the
remaining stationary pair. There is at least one compatible word since
the cylinder has positive mass. On any such word, R=β_w+J_w R' with
J_w>0 and E R'=∞; the latter follows by summing the positive series
and using E(3/2^a)=1. Hence the conditional first moment is infinite
at each finite depth.
