# X-DI-001 — exact survivor densities and coefficient-stopping record holders

Status: exact computation (density table) plus a bounded scan (`n ≤ 10^10`); **EMPIRICAL** where it concerns individual integers. Nothing here is an all-depth theorem. The scan exists to ground the surplus vocabulary of `L-DI-001`/`T-DI-002` and to give `RD-SC-001` an explicit finite table of `m_N`; it is not evidence for or against `SC*`.

## Objects

Shortcut map `T`; `q_k(n)` odd steps among the first `k`; coefficient `C_k = 3^{q_k}/2^k`; coefficient stopping time `τ_c(n) = min{k ≥ 1 : C_k(n) < 1}` (`IC-SC-001`); survivor sets `S_N = {n ≥ 1 : C_k(n) ≥ 1 for 1 ≤ k ≤ N}` and least sources `m_N = min S_N`. Since `n ∈ S_N ⇔ τ_c(n) > N`, the record holders of `τ_c` are exactly the jump points of `m_N`: `m_N` is the least record holder `n` with `τ_c(n) > N`.

The comparison `3^q < 2^k` is made exact through bit lengths (`3^q < 2^k ⇔ k ≥ bitlen(3^q)`); the table `bitlen3.h` was generated by exact big-integer arithmetic (`bitlen3[q] = (3**q).bit_length()`, `q ≤ 4000`).

## Exact natural density of `S_N`

`S_N` is a union of residue classes modulo `2^N`, one per length-`N` all-supercritical parity word, so `dens(S_N) = W_N/2^N` exactly, where `W_N` counts words whose prefix weights satisfy `3^{q_k} ≥ 2^k` for all `k ≤ N`. [`exact_density.py`](exact_density.py) computes `W_N` by dynamic programming; output in [`exact_density.out`](exact_density.out) for `N ≤ 400`.

Selected values (the last column is `log_2(dens)/N`; the entropy heuristic predicts the limit `H(α) − 1 ≈ −0.050`, `α = log 2/log 3`):

```text
N=10   dens = 6.25e-2   rate -0.400        N=100  dens = 2.39e-4   rate -0.130
N=40   dens = 5.82e-3   rate -0.186        N=200  dens = 3.06e-6   rate -0.095
N=60   dens = 1.92e-3   rate -0.150        N=400  dens = 1.16e-9   rate -0.074
```

This is the exact form of the classical statement that all-supercritical prefixes are rare. It says nothing about `m_N`: a union of residue classes of tiny density may still contain a small integer, which is the repository's firewall in miniature.

Observations (bounded evidence only):

- Every record holder `n > 1` has `σ(n) = τ_c(n)`, as the coefficient-stopping conjecture predicts: the coefficient crossing and the actual descent below `n` coincide on this list.
- The peak surplus on the supercritical prefix stays small, between `4.4` and `15.7` in `log_3` units, while the prefix lengths grow from `59` to `447`. In the language of `L-DI-001`, the longest finite Lane-A prefixes below `10^10` are low-surplus, near-linear-growth stretches, which is the sub-lane an averaged fixed-target theorem can see (`T-DI-002`).
- `τ_c` grows roughly like `20 log n` along the record sequence (`27 → 59`, `2788008987 → 447`).

## Boundaries

- A bounded scan proves nothing about `m_N` for `N ≥ 447` or about `SC*`.
- The density table is exact but measures a different quantity from the least source.
- Peak surplus on a *finite* prefix is not the `sup_k D_k` of a hypothetical all-depth Lane-A source; the table only illustrates the vocabulary.
