# Research: baseline and active work

The [scientific status](../STATE.md), [cumulative results](RESULTS_CATALOG.md) and [research map](../docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md) describe one integrated baseline across the project. The [statement/proof index](integrated/README.md) is the starting point for reading mathematics, not a chronology of research passes.

The catalog includes reviewed source-pinned work as well as resident proofs. A link to a source commit is a legitimate proof location, not a claim that the proof is local, self-contained, or newly reviewed. Current restrictions and proof dependencies take precedence over an unqualified old source sentence.

## Active research

The [open research PRs](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/pulls) and [research issues](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/issues) expose live work. They are proposals and discussions, **not extensions of the accepted baseline by default**. Consult [concrete tasks](open-obligations/README.md) for work that follows from the established results; alternative approaches are welcome.

Local source collections are also available for detailed exploration:

| Collection | What is there | How to interpret its status |
|---|---|---|
| [Critical mass and inverse frontiers](astra-critical-mass/README.md) | Proof attempts, finite-time bounds, physical merging, inverse pruning and counterexamples. | Use the mass/rank topic guides to identify reviewed clauses; author-stage labels and failed global attempts are retained. |
| [Section renewal and moving ranks](astra-three-routes/README.md) | Complementary operator, source-height, coefficient and rank constructions, including distinct alternative continuation packets. | A shared directory or experiment number does not identify one theorem. Use the specific path, map and rank named by the guide. |
| [External Collatz dossier](external/mazur-2026/README.md) | External result summaries, source evidence, local analyses and proposed strengthening routes. | External source-qualified theorems, native reviewed components and open strengthening hypotheses have different statuses. |
| [Other source-pinned programs](RESULTS_CATALOG.md) | Centered sections, H, Padé, cycle synthesis, quotient-refund, regular languages, rewriting and controls. | Follow each entry's exact reviewed source; subsequent branch changes need their own review. |

Some source files still say PROPOSED because they preserve the submitted version. That is not a reason to discard a later reviewed clause. Conversely, review of one clause is not acceptance of the whole file, its open target, or its later revisions. [Scientific status](../STATE.md) and the adjacent qualifications resolve this distinction without requiring the reader to reconstruct review history.

## Contribute or inspect evidence

For a first contribution, select one task and its expected deliverable in [open obligations](open-obligations/README.md). Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for access and submission, or [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for agent work. A failed strengthening with an exact counterexample is useful research.

[Replay instructions](../docs/REPLAY_POLICY.md) explain what each type of evidence establishes. [Audit history](../archive/README.md) contains exact source/review records, integration decisions and execution receipts; those records support the science rather than define its organization.
