## What the new machinery contributes

The denominator-aware Pell argument retains both a squarefree class and a root modulo the squared denominator. Different roots can occur even in a short rational cycle; an ordinary trace bound cannot be transferred without them. The cube-class argument gives a lower rank requirement from a long period. It supplies no upper rank bound for an unknown integer cycle. Our cycle agent is combining these restrictions with the actual ordered three-grade geometry, retaining its bounded least-counterexample hypothesis.

The critical paired-translation construction has an actual gated census and a positive conditioned fraction. Its starts have denominator `3^K`, and its two heads land at ordinary targets on specified clocks. This is stronger than an arbitrary completed-field example, but it is not the original positive-integer inverse ancestor law used in this packet. Turning that census into a lower bound for a side branch of one fixed escaping ordinary source remains a separate obligation. The same-source sibling proposal does not silently make that substitution.

The [dyadic support-energy note](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/dac732467c6ed5307d1b78eaa8534653aeeb7a4c/research/infinity-20261127/DYADIC_SUPPORT_ENERGY.md) sharpens the Boolean-support implication: a support gap allows summable square energy to force a constant component label. The available error rates do not give that summability. This concerns the actual support indicator, rather than a compactness theorem for a general positive profile.

Finally the new canonical-pair examples keep the physical meeting-clock obstruction. They prevent treating a short symbolic bridge or common residue pattern as a short ordinary meeting certificate. None of the new intake discharges the same-depth side-mass lower bound in [our current proposal](operator/SIBLING_COMPARISON_PROPOSAL.md).
