# Detecting the complete cycle denominator from short parallel windows

**PROPOSED.** For each near-critical order pair in the bounded least-tripling-disagreement alternative, the complete physical cycle divisibility has an exact local test. Every failing decoration admits a certificate specifying only $O(\log p)$ of its rank letters. Those letters lie in $O(\sqrt{\log p})$ parallel temporal windows, each of length $O(\sqrt{\log p})$. The windows can be far apart. A certificate rules out all completions of its specified letters for the same order pair. Both directions of the finite criterion hold for all input words with the stated numerical bounds.

The result decides a **supplied word**. Universal failure for all nontrivial, physically admissible least-disagreement words remains **OPEN**. The accompanying construction gives unbounded-period nonordinary controls with both actual rotation orders, odd width strictly above three and positive mismatch density at every shift. ## Rank recurrence and complete denominator

Fix coprime positive $p,h$ with $p<h\le2p$ and $D=2^h-3^p>0$. Give base ranks $0\le r<p$ letters $1\le c_r\le C$ in the integers. Extend each rank array by $f_{r+p}=2f_r$, and let $(Sf)_r=f_{r+1}$. Then $S^p=2I$. The unique rational array satisfying $(S^h-3)Z=c$ is
$$
 Z_r=D^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}3^{p-1-j}c_{r+jh}.
$$
This follows by factoring $S^{ph}-3^p$. All denominators divide $D$, which is prime to six. Writing $Rr=(r+h)\bmod p$ and $b_r=\lfloor(r+h)/p\rfloor$, the equation is $2^{b_r}Z_{Rr}=3Z_r+c_r$. All reduced denominators are equal, since both 2 and 3 are units at every divisor of $D$ and $R$ is transitive.

In the physical application $c_r=2^{2-k_r}$, $k_r\in\{0,1,2\}$, and $x_r=Z_r/c_r$. If $a_r=b_r+k_r-k_{Rr}$ is 1 or 2 at every rank, $2^{a_r}x_{Rr}=3x_r+1$. The physical lap formula has odd numerator and denominator dividing $D$ at every rotation, so $x_r$ is integral exactly when $Z_r$ is. Its valuation is then exactly $a_r$. In particular the test checks **all** of $D$ against the affine carry. Actual sorting, the minimum and its ordinary basin conditions belong to the application. ## Finite criterion

Choose two positive pairs $(q_i,a_i)$ on opposite sides of $h/p$, with $|q_1a_2-q_2a_1|=1$. Set $v_i=|q_i h-a_i p|>0$. The opposite signs and determinant give the exact seam identity
$$
                          q_2v_1+q_1v_2=p.                     \tag{1}
$$
For either pair write $q,a,v$ for its parameters and choose any integer $m\ge1$. Put
$$
 W=(S^v-I)^m Z,\qquad J=(S^v-I)^m c .
$$
Let $B$ be an integer bound for $|W_r|$ and $J_*$ an integer bound for $|J_r|$ on base ranks. Explicit word-independent choices are below. Take $L$ with $M=3^L>7B+J_*$. At each target rank $r$, start with residue zero $L$ steps earlier and run the following update along $R$ to the target:
$$
                 y_{Rt}\equiv (3y_t+J_t)2^{-b_t}\pmod M.       \tag{2}
$$
At the target take the unique centered integer representative $y_r$ in $[-(M-1)/2,(M-1)/2]$.

**Criterion.** All $Z_r$ are integers if and only if $|y_r|\le B$ at **every** base rank for **both** filters. The two orders $m$, the bounds and the moduli may differ.

Indeed the exact rational $W$ satisfies $2^{b_t}W_{Rt}=3W_t+J_t$. Its denominator divides $D$, invertible modulo $M$. After $L$ updates the initial residue contributes a multiple of $3^L$, so (2) recovers $W_r$ modulo $M$, regardless of its rational denominator. If $W$ is integral then its centered representatives are the actual values. Conversely if they are all small, each integer $2^{b_t}y_{Rt}-3y_t-J_t$ is zero modulo $M$ and has magnitude at most $7B+J_*<M$. Thus the representatives satisfy the exact affine recurrence at every edge. Uniqueness follows from its homogeneous return multiplier $3^p/2^h\ne1$. This proves $W=y$ and hence $W$ is integral.

To recover $Z$, set $N=DZ$ and encode its base array as $\sum_{r=0}^{p-1}N_r X^{p-1-r}$ in $(\mathbb Z/D\mathbb Z)[X]/(X^p-2)$. Multiplication by $X$ realizes $S$, including the doubled last entry. The monic relation gives unique degree-below-$p$ representatives, also for composite $D>1$. The case $D=1$ is immediate. Put $x=X^{v_1}, y=X^{v_2}$. By (1), $x^{q_2}y^{q_1}=2$. Therefore $1=(x^{q_2}-1)y^{q_1}+(y^{q_1}-1)$ belongs to the ideal generated by $x-1,y-1$. Their respective positive powers also generate the unit ideal, by the binomial expansion of a sufficiently high power of this identity. If both $W$ arrays are integral then both powered differences kill $N$ in the quotient ring. Hence $N=0$ there, giving $D\mid N_r$ for every rank. This is the reverse implication from the local tests to the **whole** denominator. The single-filter implication can fail algebraically. In $\mathbb F_5$ take $p=7$, $X=3$, so $X^p=2$ and $X^4-1=0$ on every nonzero element as well.

## Explicit bounds for either filter

The base-rank bound $0<Z_r\le X_*=2Cp\,2^h/D$ follows termwise in the lap formula using $3^p<2^h$ and $c_{r+jh}\le C2^{(r+jh)/p}$. Define
$$
 (\lambda,H)=\begin{cases}(3^q/2^a,2^a)& qh>ap,\\
 (2^a/3^q,3^q)& qh<ap.\end{cases}
$$
Then $S^v Z=\lambda Z+e$. If $qh>ap$ the exact error is $H^{-1}\sum_{j<q}3^{q-1-j}S^{jh}c$. If $qh<ap$ it is $-H^{-1}\sum_{j<q}3^{q-1-j}S^{v+jh}c$. Both follow by unrolling $q$ steps on the doubled lift, in the second case starting at $r+v$. Thus $|e_r|\le E$ throughout $0\le r\le p-1+(m-1)v$, where
$$
 E=\frac C H\sum_{j<q}3^{q-1-j}2^{\lfloor(p-1+mv+jh)/p\rfloor}.
$$
Writing $\eta=|\lambda-1|$, valid bounds are
$$
 B=\left\lceil\eta^m X_*+ E\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}2^j\eta^{m-1-j}\right\rceil,
 \qquad J_*= C 2^{\lfloor(p-1+mv)/p\rfloor}2^m .
$$
For the first formula factor $A^m-t^m$ with $A=S^v-I$ and $t=\lambda-1$, so $(A-t)Z=e$. Each $A^j e$ uses $j+1$ entries of the signed difference and has magnitude at most $2^j E$. These bounds depend only on the order data and the alphabet ceiling. One violation of the criterion persists for all completions having the same sampled letters.

## Short-window asymptotic

Take fixed $C$, $\alpha=\log_2 3$ and let $p\to\infty$ with $0<h/p-\alpha\le1/p$. This covers the bounded least-disagreement branch for all sufficiently large $p$. Put $Y=\sqrt{\ln p}$ and $Q=e^Y$. The homogeneous specialization of Wu and Wang's Theorem 1 gives $|q\alpha-a|\gg q^{-4.5}$ near $\alpha$, hence consecutive continued-fraction denominators eventually satisfy $q_{j+1}\le q_j^5$. It also gives $h\ln2-p\ln3\gg p^{-4.5}$. Since $0<h\ln2-p\ln3\le\ln2$, $1-3^p/2^h\gg p^{-4.5}$, so $X_*\le p^6$ eventually. [Wu and Wang, Theorem 1, Journal of Number Theory 142 (2014), 264–273](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2014.03.007)

Choose $q_j\le Q<q_{j+1}$ and use the consecutive convergents at $j-1,j$. Both denominators are at most $Q$. Both errors $|q\alpha-a|$ are at most $1/q_j\le Q^{-1/5}$, and at least a constant times $Q^{-4.5}$. The perturbation $q|h/p-\alpha|\le Q/p$ is smaller than the latter bound, so the pairs still strictly bracket $h/p$. In particular $v/p\le 2Q^{-1/5}$, $\eta\ll Q^{-1/5}$, and (1) holds. Set $m=\lceil36Y\rceil$. Then $mv/p=o(1)$ and $\eta^m X_*=o(1)$. In $E$, $2^{jh/p}/3^j\le2^{q/p}=O(1)$ and $3^q/H=O(1)$ for both signs. The factor arising from $p-1+mv$ is bounded, so $E=O_C(q)$. Thus $B,J_*=\exp(O(Y))$ and one may take $L=O(Y)$ for both filters.

A failing rank uses $J$ at $L$ past temporal ranks. Each $J$ reads at most $m+1$ base letters with known lift factors. This uses $O(\log p)$ specified letters. The corresponding parallel windows are shifted in **time** by multiples of $q$ since $v\equiv\pm qh\pmod p$. Their overall temporal span is at most $L+mq=\exp(O(\sqrt{\log p}))$. The $O(\sqrt{\log p})$ bound is the length of each separate window. No bound on the least $p$ at which a given choice of asymptotic constants works is required for the finite criterion.

## Evidence and open step

The ordinary integer test requires every filter at every rank. Full-denominator integrality is exactly captured under the stated input hypotheses. A counting contradiction would need an additional theorem forcing some failing window in **every** admissible least-disagreement decoration. The pair $(W_{1,r},W_{2,r})$ need not distinguish the $p$ ranks. For example the integer array with $Z_r=1$ on all base ranks and doubled extension has arbitrarily long intervals on which both finite differences vanish when $mv_i\ll p$. This example addresses rank injectivity of the filters in the ambient lattice and makes no physical orbit claim. The known certificate transfers at PR192 also require specific ordinary source congruences or actual certificates. They provide no uniform forbidden window at all cycle candidates.
