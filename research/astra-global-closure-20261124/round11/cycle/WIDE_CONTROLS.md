# Two rotation orders allow odd width above three at every large upper convergent

**PROPOSED rational control.** At each sufficiently large upper convergent $h/p$ of $\log_2 3$ there is a positive rational shortcut cycle of reduced denominator greater than one with all the following properties.

- The odd period is $p$, the physical shortcut period is $h$, and all odd valuations are 1 or 2.
- Its odd minimum $s$ and maximum $M$ satisfy $3s<M<4s$. The minimum has $s\equiv1\bmod9$ and $s\equiv3\bmod4$ in the local residue rings.
- Sorted dyadic mantissas of the odd phases advance by $h\bmod p$. Sorted ternary mantissas of all physical phases advance by $-p\bmod h$.
- At **every** nonzero cyclic shift of sorted dyadic ranks, more than $p/8$ octave labels differ for sufficiently large $p$.

The width threshold in PR209 uses ordinary integrality. This construction shows why both order properties and the round-ten all-shifts mismatch inequalities are insufficient by themselves to force width below the PR209 thresholds. No special upper-convergent exponent beyond the usual $p^{-2}$ is imposed. This construction supplies no ordinary cycle or least-disagreement witness. ## Adding a high excursion to a rational construction

Use the binary free-interval construction in PR192. Let $d=h-p$, $Rr=(r+d)\bmod p$, $I=\{1,\ldots,\lfloor p/3\rfloor\}$ and $u=\lfloor p/5\rfloor$. Start with independent fair bits $k_r$ on $I$ and put zero outside $I$. Override the three bits at $u,Ru,R^2u$ to one. Their limiting normalized ranks are $1/5$, $1/5+\log_2(3)-1\simeq0.785$ and $1/5+2(\log_2(3)-1)-1\simeq0.370$. The incoming source of the first bit is outside $I$, at normalized rank about $0.615$, and the exit target from the third is outside $I$, at about $0.955$. [PR192 rational construction and uniform order proof](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/570476be82f706122cdd23ebd7a00f3d4ff90cd8/research/astra-ultra-closure-20261125/cycle/RATIONAL_ORDERS.md)

The incoming edge wraps, so it permits the rise $0\to1$. The exit edge does not wrap and permits the fall $1\to0$. Both internal edges keep the label. All other rises and falls follow the prior free-interval rule. Hence $a_r=b_r+k_r-k_{Rr}\in\{1,2\}$ on every physical odd edge. Set $c_r=2^{2-k_r}$. The positive rational fixed array $Z$ from the [finite criterion](README.md) gives exact odd states $x_r=Z_r/c_r$. All physical fixed-point numerators are odd and all denominators prime to six. Coprimality of the counts $p,h$ excludes a shorter repetend. The first forward edge from zero has exponent one, and its two preceding edges have exponent two, since none of these sites received an override. This gives the two stated residue conditions.

For clarity about the order hypothesis, put $t_r=Z_r/4$, $w_r=2^{-r/p}t_r$, $\lambda=3\,2^{-h/p}$ and $f_r=2^{-(r+h)/p-k_r}$. Then $w_{Rr}=\lambda w_r+f_r$. The upper-convergent error gives $V=\bar f/(1-\lambda)\gg p^2$. PR192 proves that with probability tending to one all centered cyclic partial sums of $f$ in rotation order are $o(p)$, hence $w_r=V+o(p)$ uniformly by Abel summation. To see the uniformity, the expected rank field is a piecewise smooth function of $r/p$ times $2^{-h/p}$. Bound it above and below by trigonometric polynomials with integrals arbitrarily close. Each fixed nonconstant Fourier mode has sums bounded uniformly over all subsegments and starting ranks because $d/p$ tends to an irrational and the finite geometric sum has a bounded reciprocal denominator. The deterministic centered means therefore give $o(p)$ discrepancy. In rotation order the centered random terms of one lap are independent with bounded variance. The maximal prefix inequality gives probability $O(p^{-1/2})$ that a prefix exceeds $p^{3/4}$ in magnitude. Subtracting the realized mean and allowing cyclic segments changes this bound by an absolute factor. The discrepancy conclusion follows. Overriding three bits changes each cyclic sum on a segment of length at most $p$ by $O(1)$, even after centering at its new mean. Thus the same discrepancy bound holds with the *new* value of $V$. Nominal rank spacings of $V2^{r/p}$ are $\gg p$, giving strict dyadic order on all ranks and at $2t_0$. The minimum is $s=t_0$. All odd sites lie below $4s$. At $Ru$ the odd site's ratio to $s$ tends to $2^{1+1/5+\log_2(3)-1}=3\cdot2^{1/5}>3$.

As in PR192, assign label $L=r+pk_r$ to an odd site. An intervening even site is twice its odd successor, so add $p$ to that successor's label. In physical order the label changes by $h-p$ on an odd departure and by $-p$ on an even departure. The residues advance by $-p\bmod h$ and traverse all $h$ residues. Every $L<3p<2h$ for large $p$. Writing $L=\sigma+\ell h$, $0\le\sigma<h$, one has $\ell=0$ or $1$. The corresponding physical height divided by $3^\ell$ is $2^{\sigma/p}(V+o(p))$, since $(2^{h/p}/3-1)V=\bar f/\lambda=O(1)$. The virtual upper endpoint $3s$ has the same accuracy. These heights strictly increase with $\sigma$ and begin at $s$. This identifies the claimed actual ternary mantissas.

## All shifts and nonordinary realization

Fix $t\ne0\bmod p$. Count mismatched pairs starting only at the free ranks $I\setminus\{u\}$. Each such site's bit is fair. Its target is a different independent free bit or a fixed bit, so the expected count is $(|I|-1)/2$. Changing one free bit changes this count by at most two. Upon successively revealing fair bits, the two possible conditional means at each revelation differ by at most two. The centered increment therefore has moment generating function at most $\cosh(\lambda)\le e^{\lambda^2/2}$. Multiplying these conditional bounds and applying Markov gives probability $e^{-\Omega(p)}$ for a downward deviation to $p/8$, uniformly in $t$. A union bound over all $p-1$ shifts proves the simultaneous assertion. This estimate and the preceding order event hold together with probability tending to one.

There are at least $2^{|I|-2}$ distinct resulting patterns with all properties for sufficiently large $p$. Their rational minima are distinct. Strictly increasing mantissas, $k_0=0$ and nonnegative octave indices make $r=0$ the unique odd minimum. Its rational value determines its full ordinary-valuation orbit, every dyadic mantissa relative to that minimum, and the entire sorted octave word with rank zero fixed. A cyclic reindexing therefore contributes no extra counted pattern. From the finite-criterion report $s\le X_*\le p^6$ eventually. Thus at most $p^6$ of those different minima can be ordinary positive integers. This proves the claimed nonordinary existence for each sufficiently large upper convergent. It does not exclude exceptional integral patterns among the surviving candidates.

For the finite control $p=306,h=485$, put ones on the odd ranks from 1 to 102 and also on $60,239,112$. The checker reconstructs all odd and shortcut phases, verifies both actual orders, $3s<M<4s$, a 476-bit reduced denominator, at least eight mismatches at every nonzero shift, and 104 at the temporal shift. This finite example satisfies the round-ten bounds. The stronger $p/8$ assertion above uses the asymptotic construction.
