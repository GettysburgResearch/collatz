# A positive fraction of the dyadic bands change at every nonzero rank shift

**PROPOSED written corollary of the lifted-gap estimates.** On each sufficiently large bounded least-disagreement cycle in the [main theorem](README.md), one has $E_t>p/83$ for every $1\le t\le p/10$. For every nonzero shift in $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$, one has $E_t>p/747$. The conclusion applies to the physical dyadic-band coloring in sorted normalized rank order.

It suffices first to treat $p/(\ln p)^2<t\le p/10$. Write $X=st$ and choose the same $L=\lceil\log_3(1+20X/p)\rceil$. Now $X\gg p^{1+1000/479}/(\ln p)^2$, so $4p^3/X<1$ for large $p$. Thus the integer lifted gaps $G_r$ are all **distinct**, by the multiplicity bound. As $t\le p$, one has
$$
       L\le (5.12/\ln3)\ln p+O(1).
$$
The centered product argument in the main proof applies to every supplied $(b,J)$ window, including those meeting the input cut. Hence the $p$ window starting ranks have pairwise different $(b,J)$ lists.

For each window record both its $b$-list and the indicators of the input cut $r_i+t\ge p$ at its $L$ ranks. There are at most $3L+1$ such joint lists. The $b$-list comes from $L$ partial sums each with at most one jump in the starting rank. At any one time $i$ the input-cut indicator is membership in a cyclic interval, with at most two changes along the ordered starting ranks. The common partition therefore has at most $3L+1$ parts.

Here is a probability assignment to finite codes. Give every realized joint list equal probability. Choose independent Bernoulli marks of parameter $\theta=1/82$ at the $L$ positions. At a marked position choose an ordered pair of distinct base values in $\{1,2,4\}$ uniformly among the six possibilities. At an unmarked input-cut position choose a common base value uniformly among three possibilities. The rest of the unmarked positions carry no further label. These choices have total probability one. A realized window uses marks exactly at the physical unlifted band mismatches, with the labels given by its own colors. Its code reconstructs $J$ even on the cut, where $J=2c_{(r+t)\bmod p}-c_r$. Consequently all $p$ realized codes are distinct.

Let $Q_r$ be the assigned probability of the code of the window starting at rank $r$. Then $\sum_rQ_r\le1$, and AM–GM gives $p^{-1}\sum_r-\ln Q_r\ge\ln p$. There is no probabilistic assumption on actual ranks or their colors. In this average, the mismatch count of a window is exactly $L E_t/p$, and its cut count is exactly $Lt/p$. The latter upper-bounds the average number of unmarked cut labels. If $E_t\le p/83$, then $E_t/p\le\theta$ and, since $6(1-\theta)/\theta>1$, the average code cost is bounded by
$$
 \ln p\le\ln(3L+1)+L\big[H(\theta)+\theta\ln6+(t/p)\ln3\big].
$$
By the exact comparison in the main theorem, $(H(\theta)+\theta\ln6)/\ln3<42/521$. The right side is therefore at most
$$
 [5.12(42/521+1/10)]\ln p+O(\ln\ln p).
$$
The bracket is $60224/65125<1$, a contradiction. Together with the previously proved short-offset range this gives the first assertion.

For a shift $t$ of additive order at least ten, the ten ranks $0,t,\ldots,9t$ are distinct modulo $p$. Among their ten circular gaps there is a positive one of size $v\le p/10$. For some $1\le j\le9$ we have $v\equiv\pm jt\pmod p$. Translation invariance and the triangle inequality for Hamming distance give $E_v=E_{jt}\le jE_t$. The first assertion implies $E_t>p/(83\cdot9)$. The remaining orders require temporal edge legality.

## The temporal shift detects all small orders

Write $E_h=E_{h\bmod p}$. At each temporal edge the normalized exponent $b_r$ and physical exponent $a_r=b_r+k_r-k_{r+h}$ belong to $\{1,2\}$, with subscripts here reduced modulo $p$. If the bands agree then $a_r=b_r$. If the bands change, $a_r=3-b_r$. Thus a length-$N$ physical word is determined by its normalized $b$-list and its binary band-change mask. The $b$-lists have at most $N+1$ possibilities as in the main proof.

Take $N=\lceil\log_3(1+8s)\rceil$. Two equal physical words have equal terminal odd site modulo $3^N$, by subtracting their affine identities and canceling the power of two. Since every odd site is positive and below $8s$, their terminal sites are equal. Primitivity makes every terminal phase distinct. Consequently the $p$ codes are distinct, and $N\le(5.12/\ln3)\ln p+O(1)$. Give the realized $b$-lists equal probabilities and the mask independent Bernoulli weights of parameter $1/20$. If $E_h/p\le1/20$, the average code cost is at most
$$
    \ln(N+1)+NH(1/20).
$$
The AM–GM argument again bounds the same cost below by $\ln p$. But $5.12H(1/20)<\ln3$, equivalently $20^{640}<19^{608}3^{125}$. This proves $E_h>p/20$ for sufficiently large $p$.

Fix $t$ of additive order $2\le d\le9$. Let $V$ be the union of its nonmonochromatic cosets, meaning cosets of the subgroup generated by $t$ on which the band has at least two values. Each such coset has $d$ sites and at least two mismatches in its cyclic $t$ order, hence $|V|\le dE_t/2$. Every coset meets the interval of base ranks with $b=2$: that interval has length $h-p>p/2$ because $h/p>\log_23>3/2$, while the spacing in a coset is $p/d\le p/2$.

Consider the temporal edge $r\to r+h$. If both endpoint cosets are monochromatic, take $u$ in the source coset with $b_u=2$. Then $u+h$ belongs to the endpoint coset, and $a_u\le2$ implies $k_{u+h}\ge k_u$. Constancy gives $k_{r+h}\ge k_r$. Every strictly decreasing temporal edge therefore starts in $V$ or ends in $V$. There are at most $2|V|$ such edges. Band changes on temporal edges are unit rises or unit falls, and their signed sum is zero around a lap. Hence
$$
        E_h\le4|V|\le2dE_t,\qquad E_t>p/(40d)\ge p/360 .
$$
Together with the order-at-least-ten case, this establishes $E_t>p/747$ at every nonzero shift.

This strengthens an exclusion of sparse rank decorations. The positive lower bounds at many shifts remain compatible with the legal word constraints. Whole-denominator nondivisibility in the remaining cases and a contradiction after the separate first crossing remain open.
