## 1. Complete denominators, including quadratic-degree noncyclotomic factors

For a legal odd-step word, write

\[
 2^{e_i}x_{i+1}=3x_i+1,\quad p=\#\{e_i\},\quad A=\sum e_i,
 \quad D=2^A-3^p,\quad C=\sum_{i<p}3^{p-1-i}2^{E_i}.
\]

Its periodic rational source is \(C/D\); a positive integer cycle requires \(D>0\) and reduced denominator \(d=|D|/\gcd(|D|,C)=1\).

**Whole-mask theorem.** Suppose \(\gcd(A,p)=1\), \(p<A\le Bp\) for fixed \(B\), and the normalized discrepancy exponents \(pE_i-Ai\) form a proper generalized arithmetic progression. Then, uniformly over its rank, digit sizes and steps,

\[
                 \log d=\log|2^A-3^p|-o_B(p).
\]

The complete numerator is included, with every prime-power multiplicity. The physical word makes the exponent set an Apéry set closed under the two increments \(p\) and \(A-p\). This bounds the successive digit steps and permits a sum of square-root order costs of size \(O(p)\). Varjú's composite-modulus order estimate for the group generated by 2 and 3 makes the resulting cancellation subexponential. The number of factors may grow and no polynomial common order is assumed.

**Moving-unit theorem.** For a fixed number field \(K\), a unit \(\theta\in\mathcal O_K^\times\), and integers \(r,s\) with \(As+pr\ne0\), the logarithmic common prime-ideal divisor of \(D\) and \(2^r3^s-\theta\), divided by \([K:\mathbb Q]\), is

\[
 o_K\!\left(\sqrt{|As+pr|+(A+p)(1+h(\theta))}\right).
\]

This theorem does not require coprime clocks. Its proof combines a weighted two-dimensional relation lattice, successive minima, and Corvaja–Zannier's fixed-field \(S\)-unit theorem. It allows a core of bounded degree and height \(O(p)\), in one fixed splitting field, pulled back by an exponent of size \(O(p^2)\).

There are actual physical applications beyond the progression class. Every fixed finite digit transversal lifts to a near-critical family whose **entire** denominator has the first asymptotic above. For a concrete noncyclotomic core,

\[
 H(X)=(1+X+\cdots+X^6)(1-X^2+X^3)
     =1+X+X^3+X^4+X^5+X^6+X^9.
\]

The constructed full masks have the form \(S_L(X^a)H(X^m)\), with \(m\asymp p^2\). The cubic pullback has degree \(3m\), all its roots are nontorsion, and the other factors are also controlled. The clocks can satisfy \(A-p\log_2 3\to0^+\). Thus these words lie outside every proper progression mask while retaining their full leading denominator exponent.

**The missing general step is structural.** An arbitrary word need not have any of these factorizations. The naive sum of square roots of all individual cyclotomic orders is not even \(O(p)\) for every legal mask: the note gives a primorial counterfamily, whose factors can instead be grouped under one common annihilator. A useful grouping theorem for every word, and control of unrestricted noncyclotomic factors with growing fields, remain unproved. Periodic exclusions also do not exclude one fixed escaping source.

## 2. Summable opposite-phase tests on entire growing neighborhoods

Label an ordinary positive integer \(B\) if it does not reach one, and otherwise \(+\) or \(-\) according to the parity of its first shortcut hitting time. On row \(j\), take the original head pair

\[
 7\cdot81^j\le x<8\cdot81^j,\qquad y=\lfloor x/81\rfloor,
\]

and retain one directed label pair \((B,+)\) on any fixed nonempty open mantissa patch \(J\subset(7,8)\). Give each child weight \(81^{-j}\).

For **every \(0<\beta<1/12\)**, the total weight over all rows is finite for retained pairs having a \(-\) integer anywhere within distance \(\lfloor\exp(j^\beta)\rfloor\) of either endpoint. The witness may be chosen after seeing the entire ordinary source and its future.

The proof first tracks the initial-state constant in the three-arm fusion moment:

\[
 \mathbb E(1+\sigma)^u\ll_u Q^u\quad(0<u<3/2),\qquad
 Q=1+\text{grade spread}^2+\log(2+\text{normalized offset}).
\]

A stopped positive-sibling construction then compresses \(D\) consecutive starts to only \(O((1+\log D)^3)\) expected representatives after \(O(\log D)\) original bits. Applying the uniform triple bound on that very prefix gives

\[
 O_u\!\left((1+\log D)^{3+2u}(1+j)^{-u}\right).
\]

This is a finite profile event containing the eventual three-label event; it does not condition a fresh probability law on a basin. Complete binary grids transfer it back to the original positive children with their original multiplicities.

A second estimate removes, at finite total weight, edges seeing the opposite phase within any prescribed polynomial radius around either actual iterate at **any common shortcut clock** \(s\le j^\alpha\), for \(\alpha<1/4\). At one deterministic clock, \(\alpha<1/2\) is allowed.

The residual directed edges may still occupy neighborhoods with only two labels. Under the separately stated fixed-target density input M, summability of this remaining directed mass would settle Collatz. We have not bounded that mass. ## 3. Ordinary-target bounds and logarithmic entropy loss

Let \(\nu\) be PR200's exact stationary ternary law for completed ratio-five first-return blocks. Its pinned reverse-cylinder estimate is an explicit input. For every positive ordinary target \(q\),

\[
 \nu(q+3^k\mathbb Z_3)\le Cq(k+1)^3 3^{-k}.
\]

This is an **upper** estimate at each specified ordinary integer. It follows by decoding every legal word at a fixed physical clock to a distinct positive integer. Full ternary integrality forces every intermediate parity and positivity; those finite statements are checked in Lean.

Keeping the real affine offset \(R_k\) gives the uniform localized estimate

\[
 \Pr(Z\equiv a\pmod{3^k},\ R_k\le B)\le CB(k+1)^3 3^{-k}
 \quad(B\ge1).
\]

The limiting real offset has every fractional moment below one. Disintegration by its dyadic size yields

\[
 0\le k\log3-H(\nu_k)\le3\log(k+1)+O(1),
\]

and \(-\log\nu_k(Z)=k\log3+O(\log k)\) almost surely. This sharpens the earlier subpower entropy error, without importing a lower atom estimate for a different inverse dictionary.

For actual ordinary sources of height at most \(X\) reaching a fixed marked pair \((q,5q)\) in at most \(J\) blocks, the count is

\[
 \ll \sqrt{qX}+X\min\{1,J/\sqrt{\log(2X/q)}\}.
\]

In particular \(o(\sqrt{\log X})\) blocks cannot give positive density at a fixed target. A useful lower estimate must retain completed return marks and ordinary height. A partial reversed word can be ternary-legal and lift to an ordinary ancestor without preserving the ratio-five pair: \(T^4(37)=7\), but \(T^4(185)=314\). The needed completed-mark lower estimate remains open, and even that estimate needs a matching upper bound on the failed-five population.
