# Exact tripling endpoints at a central clock

**R7-R6.** For every positive odd $x$ and every $e\ge1$, $T^e(3x)$ is prime to three. Consequently the exact pair $(q,3q)$ has **zero** inverse starts of the form $(x,3x)$ at a positive companion clock, for every positive $q$. For each supplied unit $q$, the separate pair $(q,q)$ has at least $c_q G/\sqrt p$ such starts at every sufficiently large central row $|e-2p|\le\sqrt p/2$, conditional only on the one-arm analytic floor F. Here $G=2^e/3^p$. Both arms use the same $e$ physical steps. They have $p$ and $p-1$ odd inputs respectively, and the first-arm real offset is at most $1/2$. In particular each start lies in $[(q-1/2)G,qG]$.

The empty-fiber statement is unconditional. Since $3x$ is odd, $2T(3x)=9x+1\equiv1\pmod3$. Subsequent odd steps have the same nonzero output modulo three, and halving preserves nondivisibility by three. This obstruction applies even if the two clocks or odd counts are allowed to differ, once the companion takes a positive step.

For the positive estimate take $x=32t+23$. At clock five the states of $x,3x$ are both $z=27t+20$, on the entire ordinary cylinder. At its base their paths are $23,35,53,80,40,20$ and $69,104,52,26,13,20$. The source uses three odd inputs, the companion two. The affine rows have the same slope 27 in $t$. In particular the first-arm carry is 19, with initial offset $19/32$.

Use F from R7-R1 with offset constant $F_*$. Fix $u\ge6$ so $q_0=(2^uq-7)/9$ is an ordinary unit larger than $F_*+1$ and
$$
                    (7+9(F_*+1))/2^u\le1/2 .
$$
Such $u$ exist by the powers of two modulo 27. The actual A path from $q_0$ to $q$ has two odd inputs and clock $u$. For $b=p-5$ and $s=e-5-u$ one has $|s-2b|\le\sqrt b$ eventually. Apply F at the supplied $q_0$, depth $b$, total $s$, extra precision $K=4$ and unit class $z\equiv47\pmod{81}$. It gives at least $c 2^s/(3^{b+4}\sqrt b)\gg_q G/\sqrt p$ distinct positive odd $z$ with an ordinary path to $q_0$. Such $z$ tend uniformly to infinity since $z\ge(2^s/3^b)(q_0-F_*)$. Now $t=(z-20)/27$ is an integer congruent to one modulo three and is odd because $z$ is odd. Thus $x=32t+23$ is positive and congruent to one modulo six for all large rows.

Every resulting $x$ and $3x$ follows the identical suffix from $z$ through $q_0$ to $q$. The odd counts are $3+b+2=p$ and $2+b+2=p-1$. The starts are distinct. Through $q_0$ the first-arm offset is at most $F_*+(3^b/2^s)(19/32)\le F_*+1$ eventually. The A suffix gives the stated final bound. If the directed unit-tripling implication fails at $q$, then $q$ reaches one and $3q$ does not. Every member of the displayed large $(q,q)$ family has both arms reaching one. An argument seeking new failed sources must propagate the *color* of $3q$ through an ordinary successor or common future. In particular $3q$ and $9q+1$ meet after one step for odd $q$. The present fixed-target lower bound provides no source whose companion has that failed color. The directed implication remains open.
