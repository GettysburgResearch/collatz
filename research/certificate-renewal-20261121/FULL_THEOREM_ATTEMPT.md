# Where the completion attempt reaches, and where it stops

**Full theorem: OPEN.** The goal of this pass was universal convergence, not a larger numerical verification range. Three proof routes were pursued in parallel, with an additional attempt to construct paired descents from negative rational reference cycles. The successful deductions are in the adjacent manuscripts; the unsupported global steps are stated here.

## 1. A universal supplied-certificate transformation

Use the source-qualified equivalence from PR146:

\[
128k+1\in C\Longrightarrow64k+1\in C
\qquad(k>0,\ 4\nmid k).
\]

If it fails, there is a least ordinary upper antecedent. PR163 bounds its fourth odd valuation and excludes rank four as the globally least failure. The new PR146 sieve further reduces the test to an explicitly sparse set. None of those restrictions bounds the remaining supplied certificate length or its ordinary endpoint parameter.

The hardest concrete rank-five test retained in these sources is

\[
e=119+162j,\quad P=2^e,\quad
X=(1024P-2573)/243,
\]

whose lower arm reaches \(2^e-1\). It passes the new native-horizon sieve for every permitted exponent. The ordinary target can initially grow for an arbitrarily long time.

The two new paired contractions give

\[
k'=(16384k-6049)/59049
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
k'=(32768k-9911)/59049
\]

on their exact congruence domains. Both source and target components are preserved and \(k'<k\). The source certificate becomes ten odd steps longer. This is legitimate because numerical antecedent height, rather than source rank, decreases. It is a successful instance of the desired recursive mechanism.

**First missing step:** prove that every remaining eligible instance admits such a certified descent, a terminal ordinary merger, or a finite sequence whose common integer rank decreases. The two congruence classes do not give an exhaustive dispatcher. Combining a finite menu with source-certificate rank does not prove termination when that rank can increase.

## 2. Multiplication by five and retained energy

The five implication on certified odd ternary units is another full-equivalent source criterion. PR166 compiles words in \((2^+[4,\infty))^*\). The expanded dictionary here proves additional ordinary commutation identities, including prefixes beginning with one and three, and retains a complete inverse-fiber contraction at the subcritical weight \(x^{-21/25}\).

The analytic estimate is a renewal bound: energy on the covered domain is charged to a smaller endpoint with a coefficient below one. It makes finite energy on the whole domain equivalent to finite energy on the uncovered complement. It does not bound the complement absolutely.

The latest PR146 result makes this limitation concrete. Every physical five comparison can be transported, preserving both prescribed endpoints, to one fixed progression beginning \((1,3,3,3,3,3,3)\). This progression is outside the expanded dictionary. Conversely even the earlier covered ladder class receives every five comparison after a bounded multiplicative enlargement. Thus density of covered classes and a contraction coefficient alone cannot be read as a percentage of the full theorem proved.

**First missing step:** establish the required ordinary implication or finite actual defect energy on a full sufficient family. Neither a finite search for extra prefix rules nor the high-exponent label identities supplied in the earlier rigidity attempts proves this.

## 3. Actual arithmetic concentration

For the exact weighted congruence-word sequence \(P_q\), the new deductions replace the earlier pointwise sufficient bound by a more permissive quantile condition. Put

\[
Q_q=P_q+\tfrac12(3/5)^q.
\]

A single nonperiodic ordinary orbit would force

\[
\#\{q\ge1:Q_q\le Y\}\le K Y^{19/40}
\]

for one finite constant \(K\) depending on that same original orbit. Consequently an unbounded normalized quantile count would rule out every nonperiodic orbit. In particular \(\liminf P_q<\infty\) would suffice. The proofs retain the actual dependence between the word weight and its congruence representative.

There is also an unconditional all-time estimate: the full fifth-denominator moment satisfies \(\sum_{q,w}J_w^4/r_w^5<512/175\), including repeated visits on a hypothetical nontrivial cycle. It implies that the contribution from \(r_w\le q\) to the original second-denominator moment has cumulative sum \(o(N^3)\). The positive averaged closing target is therefore equivalent to the same target restricted to \(r_w>q\). This does not bound that remaining contribution.

**First missing step:** prove one of the new sufficient estimates for the actual \(P_q\). Independent-residue models, the total word weight, and the finite numerical values do not supply it. Even that arithmetic achievement would still require a separate exclusion of nontrivial positive cycles for the full theorem.

## 4. Other attempted bridges

The bare uniform-high-exponent label problem was examined for an integer-valid exponent-lowering commutator. The known adjacent equality lies on a restricted progression; inverse moves can leave that progression. No legal all-input exponent-lowering construction was obtained.

A separate search used paired rational reference paths ending in the negative cycles at -1 and -5 to construct candidate positive affine contractions near an index with many trailing binary ones. It found additional individual congruence cells, but no all-residue family or uniform coverage proof. These exploratory candidates are not added as scientific claims: the two fully proved positive contractions already provide a cleaner contribution.

## 5. Exact continuation target

The most direct remaining constructive target is still this: given an eligible ordinary \(k\) and an exact certificate for \(128k+1\), return a certificate for \(64k+1\), with every recursive demand justified by a single well-founded quantity. The quantity may be numerical height, as in the new contractions; it need not be certificate length. A complete proof must account for the parameter classes that all the present local rules leave unresolved.

This pass supplies new verified local mechanisms and a weaker analytic target. It does not supply that total construction or a proof of universal convergence.
