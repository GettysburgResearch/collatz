# Opposite ordinary colors inside one selected trit cell

**PROPOSED written theorem.** Use the positive shortcut map and any fixed binary invariant $f(Tn)=f(n)$. A parent $n$ on suffix $r$ has $7R\le81n+r<8R$, $R=81^j$, $d=\log_2 R$, and this row has $N=R/81$ parents. A true disagreement means $f(n)\ne f(81n+r)$. The source-qualified finite merger bound P and the prior ordinary regeneration theorem R are the analytic inputs. No fixed-target population floor is used.

Here and below a trit query means one literal ordinary value of $f(\lfloor Y/3^v\rfloor)$ in the current four-trit-chain binary search of LOCAL_COLOR_INFORMATION.md. The physical search and its queries run anew at each clock. Alternatives below can be required to come from **any chosen set $S$** of good genuine ordinary disagreements, of original row fraction $w>0$. For instance one may prescribe the ordered original colors. The good envelope has original exception $O_A(d^{-A})$, with the constants allowed to depend on $A$, the suffix and the fixed regeneration arc.

## A population at the actual queried column

Let $L=\lceil\sqrt{d\log d}\rceil$. Choose an interval of $D\ge(\log d)^6$ consecutive clocks inside $[3d,3.1d]$, with $D=o(d)$. For suitable fixed constants $c,C,\kappa>0$ and $B=\lfloor\kappa D\rfloor$, the following holds. Outside relative $O_A(d^{-A})+O(d^C e^{-cD}/w)$ of $S$, at least $cD$ regeneration clocks of each retained original have a trit query with the following properties.

* Other sources in $S$ reach **the same adaptive query**, after the same preceding replies at that clock, with either reply. They share the original's native residue, two physical seeds, entire exterior coarse quotient, all pre-clock coarse bits, and coarse residue modulo $3^B$, as defined below.
* The possible integers at that query form a finite ordinary arithmetic progression within the exterior cell, of stride $3^{e+B}$ for a public nonnegative exponent $e$. Its diameter is at most $2^{C(L+D)}$. Its cardinality is comparable to $2^{H-\Delta_t}/3^B$, which is exponentially large in $D$. Each of the two replies occurs on at least $w d^{-C_A}$ times this cardinality **distinct ordinary sites**, realized by sources in $S$ with the stated data. The progression defines all arithmetic candidate sites. Some of its sites can lack source lifts in the original row.
* The neighborhoods of these different clock queries along one such original can be taken disjoint for large $d$. Each physical height is $2^{d-at+O_A(L)}$, $a=1-\tfrac12\log_2 3$, and their different logarithms are separated by a fixed inverse power of $d$.

Thus a polynomially weighted selected population requires both ordinary colors at polynomial relative density in the **same** deep ternary class and subpower real neighborhood of an actual query, at a positive fraction of the original's clocks. The first assertion alone need not use the absolute counts in the second.

## Rebase near the tested clocks

For the interval starting at $\tau$, take $t_0=\tau-\lceil K L\rceil$, $k_0=\lfloor t_0/2\rfloor$, $c_0=3^{k_0}2^{-t_0}$, $m=\lfloor c_0 n\rfloor$, $\Delta_t=t-t_0$, and $H=\max_t\Delta_t+h+D$. Here $h$ will be a sufficiently large fixed integer. The constant $K$ depends on the simultaneous scalar grade envelope. For either physical arm at any tested clock, its exponent $g=p(t)+4{\bf1}_{\rm child}-k_0$ satisfies $g=\Delta_t/2+O_A(L)$. The same estimate holds for all trit exponents retained between the two physical grades, with a bounded endpoint adjustment. Making $K$ large gives $g\ge4$ for the needed child exponents and $3^g2^{-\Delta_t}\le1$ throughout. The original scalar intercept is nonnegative and bounded by $d^{C_A}$. Hence at $\tau$ each physical arm equals $\lfloor3^g m/2^{\Delta_\tau}\rfloor+b$ with $0\le b\le d^{C_A}$: the dropped fraction and original child suffix cost $O(1)$. The two exponent/correction seeds together have at most $d^C$ choices.

Put $a_0=\lfloor d/2\rfloor$. The public opening data consist of $n\bmod 2^{a_0}$, $\lfloor m/2^H\rfloor$, both seeds, and $m\bmod3^B$. At clock $t$ also expose $\ell=m\bmod2^{\Delta_t}$. The exact one-bit physical updates in LOCAL_COLOR_INFORMATION.md give each queried ordinary site as
$$
                         z(q)=3^e q+c_\ell,\qquad q=(m-\ell)/2^{\Delta_t}.
$$
The site of a returned child has $e\ge4$ and its parent depends odd-affinely on $q$. The public checks of that note still retain the certified chain. In particular $m\asymp2^{d-at_0}$ and $H=O_A(L+D)=o(d)$, so varying over the entire new exterior cell changes the normalized mantissa by $o(1)$; the certified chain has a fixed interior margin. A dummy selection never passes the test. At every regeneration clock the actual binary search returns a true discordant head, and every source in $S$ has at least $\eta D$ such clocks. The full offset estimate and lower bound on physical heights also apply to a retained bottom endpoint by division by 81. These uses concern the current physical queries. The query selected by the proof need not itself be the final edge returned by the search.

For fixed public data the unrestricted values of $q$ occupy $2^{H-\Delta_t}$ consecutive integers before the ternary restriction. Since $2^{\Delta_t}$ is coprime to $3^B$, precisely one class modulo $3^B$ meets that restriction. There are $2^{H-\Delta_t}/3^B+O(1)$ possibilities. The scale bound $e=O_A(L+D)$ gives the diameter assertion.

## A predictable test on the full remaining cube

Fix a native residue and one exterior quotient. Play any specified predictable answering policy with a **fixed** seed pair and ternary advice value. At each clock its output is a function of those fixed data and the exposed low bits; its internal query choices and mock replies use no new bit. Extend it by dummies at undefined prefixes and when its selected parameters do not satisfy its public admissibility checks. Under uniform remaining binary bits, the chosen finite nonmerger test through $h$ has conditional probability at most $p_0=C_0/\sqrt h$ at each clock. This is P on the odd-affine parent for a fixed suffix. Choose $h$ so that $p_0<\eta/8$. Artificial grid completions use the residue version of the test.

The probability of at least $\eta D/2$ passes is $O(e^{-\lambda_0 D})$ for a fixed $\lambda_0>0$. Indeed split the clocks into their $h$ residue classes modulo $h$. Within each class the next inspected $h$-bit blocks are disjoint. Conditional exponential moments of a pass indicator are bounded by $1-p_0+p_0 e^\lambda$ for every $\lambda>0$, and iteration gives the binomial Chernoff bound in each class. The classes partition all $D$ clocks, so one must have pass fraction at least $\eta/2$. This also works on any fixed prefix subcube with its low bits specified through the first clock. The ternary advice is held fixed as a parameter **without conditioning the fair grid on it** in this test.

For a fixed native residue each possible $m$ has at most $A_*=\lceil C c_0^{-1}2^{-a_0}\rceil$ original preimages. This follows from the length of its defining ordinary interval and $c_0^{-1}2^{-a_0}\gg1$. There are at most $Cc_0N/2^H$ exterior cells, because $c_0N\gg2^H$. Summing the full-grid bound over residues, exterior cells, at most $d^C$ seeds and $3^B$ ternary advice values shows that the relative number of originals in $S$ with $\eta D/2$ predictable passes is $O(d^C 3^B e^{-\lambda_0 D}/w)$. Choose $\kappa\log3<\lambda_0/4$ and also $\kappa\log3<(\log2)/4$. No conditional fairness at a true color or at a ternary-selected point was used.

For the specific policy, at each current clock and exposed prefix simulate the modal branch of the true adaptive query tree on the uniform **sources** of $S$ sharing these public data. At each branch, further restrict to the sources giving the mock replies already chosen at this clock, and answer by their majority at the new query. Break ties deterministically; empty nodes can give dummies. These frequencies are definite functions of the public prefix and fixed parameters, however difficult to calculate. At every regeneration clock of an actual source for which the actual and simulated replies agree throughout, the modal policy passes. Except on the exponential exceptional set, at least $\eta D/2$ regeneration clocks therefore contain a first dissent. At that identical query the two source branches have opposite answers, and the modal branch has at least the source mass of the dissenting branch.

## The original-source census

At each clock refine the reached public cell by the **complete actual reply word of that clock**, including its length. There are at most $d^{C}$ words, as the binary search uses $O(\log d)$ queries. Fix $\epsilon=w d^{-C_A'}$. Delete sources in such a refined cell when its ordinary source count is below
$$
                   \epsilon A_*\,2^{H-\Delta_t}/3^B .
$$
The total cost at this clock is bounded by this threshold times
$$
  2^{a_0} (C c_0N/2^H) d^{C} 3^B 2^{\Delta_t} d^{C},
$$
including the seeds, possible low prefixes and all reply words. This is at most $C\epsilon d^{C'}N$. A union over $D\le d$ clocks and the choice of $C_A'$ gives relative discard $O_A(d^{-A})$ in $S$. The $3^B$ factor cancels exactly at this stage. At a first dissent the entire realized word is contained in the dissenting branch, and the opposite branch has at least as many sources. Each of these two masses divided by $A_*$ is a lower bound for its number of distinct $q$, because the native residue and low prefix are fixed. Different $q$ give different $z(q)$. This proves the population claim.

At interior or endpoint probes the effective exponent $k=e+k_0$ gives $z=3^k2^{-t}n+O_A(d^{C_A})$ on the actual source. Two probe centers at clocks $t\ne t'$ therefore have log ratio $(k-k')\log3-(t-t')\log2+O_A(d^{C_A}2^{-.34d})$. Both coefficient magnitudes are $O_A(d)$, and the two-logarithm separation used in HEAD_INCIDENCE.md gives a lower bound $c_A d^{-\nu}$ for the main term. Since each log-neighborhood has radius $2^{-\Omega(d)}$, this proves disjointness.

## One chronological tree of genuine replies

The following second assertion keeps the **same** coordinate and physical seeds for all branches and clocks. Suppose $w\ge d^{-P}$ for fixed $P$. Take any $W=L$ consecutive late clocks. Rebase once at the start of this window as above with $D=W$ in the definition of $H$. Fix a sufficiently large constant $K_1$ and put
$$
 b=\lceil K_1\sqrt{W\log\log d}\rceil,\qquad m_*=\lfloor W/b\rfloor,\qquad B=\lfloor\kappa b\rfloor .
$$
Use the $m_*$ consecutive full **time** blocks of length $b$. Retain a small fixed $\kappa>0$ specified below. The R input is simultaneous on *every* contiguous interval of at least $(\log d)^6$ clocks for each good discordant original. It therefore supplies at least $\eta b$ regeneration clocks for **each such original in each block**, before choosing any branch or ancestor. For large $d$, $b>(\log d)^6$. Merely knowing $\eta W$ aggregate marks would not justify the assertion below.

Except for relative $O_A(d^{-A})$ of $S$ when counting its opening cells with their original source weights, the opening cell supports a full binary tree of depth $m_*$. At depth $j<m_*$ every node chooses a clock in the $(j+1)$-st block and one ordinary trit-query **formula on the remaining coarse quotient**. At that node sources with both answers share all physical and public data before the query, including the genuine earlier replies at that clock. The two children retain their respective actual reply, and every subsequent descendant still gives that reply on its own ordinary integer at that query. All leaves consist of original sources in $S$. They have the same initial native residue, exterior quotient, ternary residue, physical seeds and first clock's exposed coarse prefix. Earlier source/child orientation, if prescribed in $S$, is preserved on every branch. Different leaves contain different original parents and different coarse integers. At each split both outcomes have at least $\exp(cW)$ distinct physical trit sites, of the same stride $3^{e+B}$ in the same interval of length $2^{O_A(W)}$; the site count here uses the reserve of $W$ unseen bits. At a fixed node the shared query formula is determined before either child is chosen. Its clock, common prefix and formula may depend on the population of that node. Different nodes need not choose identical clocks, formulas or integers. The tree is an existence statement per opening cell, and need not pass through every prescribed individual in it.

Here is the relative density used down the tree. With one fixed opening datum and a fixed prefix through $k$ bits of $m$, define for any retained subset $E$
$$
               \delta(E)=\frac{|E|}{A_*2^{H-k}} .
$$
It compares source mass with the unrestricted binary subcube, including sites outside the selected ternary residue. The numerator includes **only actual ordinary parents**. At the first clock the number of cells is at most $2^{a_0}(C c_0N/2^H)d^C3^B2^{\Delta_\tau}$. Hence all but $O_A(d^{-A})$ relative mass of $S$ is in opening cells with
$$
                   \delta_0\ge w d^{-C_A}3^{-B}.
$$
This is the same source counting as above, with no reply word disclosed. The cap $A_*$ is unchanged at every node.

At any node start its block with $E$ inside one actual prefix of $k$ bits. The Chernoff bound on that binary subcube applies to a fresh simulation of its modal policy, defined using $E$ as the source population. An individual grid point has at most $A_*$ ordinary preimages, so the fraction of $E$ having at least $\eta b/2$ predictable passes is at most $C e^{-\lambda_0 b}/\delta(E)$. All past restricted answers are embodied in $E$ itself. They are neither revealed as free fair-bit data nor assumed to be independent of the remaining bits. Provided $\delta(E)\ge e^{-\lambda_0 b/2}$, at least half of $E$ has at least $\eta b/2$ first-dissent clocks among its own regeneration clocks. Some fixed clock $t$ in the block therefore has such a dissent on at least $\eta|E|/4$ of the sources.

Write $Q\le C\log d$ for the maximum search query count. At this clock, for each extending pre-clock prefix of $k'=\Delta_t$ bits there are only $Q$ possible first-dissent positions on its single modal response path. Both the index and the common query at such a position are fixed functions of the reached prefix and the preceding modal replies. Put $\alpha=\eta/(8Q)$. Discard a dissent position if its whole dissenting branch in $E$ has less than $\alpha\delta(E)A_*2^{H-k'}$ sources. Across all $2^{k'-k}$ prefixes and all $Q$ positions the discarded count is at most $\eta|E|/8$. Thus there is a surviving position actually reached at a regeneration clock of some source in $E$. Its modal branch has at least its dissenting mass. Take the two answer branches of this common query, always retaining the common prefix and all preceding replies at that clock. Each has relative density at least $\alpha\delta(E)$ in the resulting binary subcube. A source on the other branch need not regenerate at this *particular* clock; R still guarantees its own per-block marks.

For each child, extend its prefix up through the start of the next block by choosing a prefix maximizing its relative density. The new relative densities average to the old one over equally sized binary subcubes. Thus this extension loses no relative density and keeps every already fixed answer. This also shows why we can discard all the other extensions without claiming unbiased future bits at any chosen continuation.

After $j$ splits $\delta_j\ge\delta_0(\eta/(8Q))^j$. Take $\kappa\log3<\lambda_0/8$ and then $K_1$ large enough that
$$
 m_*\log(8Q/\eta)\le\lambda_0 b/8.
$$
Indeed $\log(8Q/\eta)=O(\log\log d)$ and $m_*\le W/b$. The remaining $O_{A,P}(\log d)$ loss in $\log(1/\delta_0)$ is $o(b)$. Hence $\delta_j\ge e^{-\lambda_0 b/2}$ through every generation, uniformly on the full binary tree. Throughout, $H-k'\ge W+h$, and each branch has at least $\delta_j2^{H-k'}$ distinct quotients after division by the source occupancy cap. Since $b=o(W)$, this exceeds $\exp(cW)$ for a fixed $c>0$ at large $d$. Two leaves separated by the first differing answer cannot share $m$: with the same opening data the iterated physical formulas, the query program and all its true replies at an $m$ are deterministic. The single-coordinate congruence and radius proof above applies at every node.

The resulting depth is $\gg (d\log d)^{1/4}/\sqrt{\log\log d}$. The earlier centered additive ternary trees in SYMMETRIC_FUTURE_FORKS.md hold both recorded physical futures fixed and split the past parity word. The tree here keeps a selected original population and splits actual invariant-color answers on ordinary queried trit columns in chronological order. Exact physical futures need not coincide across its branches.

## Scope and provenance

The conclusion does not bound the total weight of the remaining selected cells. Both compared color values can still belong to the same two globally surviving merger classes, and separate branches can choose different query sites. A contradiction would require a restriction on this many source-conditioned chronological trit color splits, or a summable bound for the original sources admitting them. The tree does not assert an independent third color, a density lower bound on every prescribed real window, or eventual ordinary convergence.
