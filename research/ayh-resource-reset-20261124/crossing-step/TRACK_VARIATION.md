# Positive total variation and the information needed to follow a true interface

**PROPOSED written consequence.** Keep the genuine ordinary head marks and late slab of [the track theorem](LATE_WITNESS_TRACKS.md). Fix a nonempty interior mantissa arc if desired. Outside \(O_A(Rd^{-A})\) original children, on every genuinely discordant source and every slab interval of \(D\ge(\log d)^6\) clocks, choose one regenerated head at **each** marked time \(t_i\), in increasing order. Write \(u_i=k_i-\lfloor t_i/2\rfloor\). There is a constant \(\delta>0\), independent of the source, the interval, and all the choices, such that
\[
                 \sum_i |u_{i+1}-u_i|>\delta D .
\]
Constants may depend on the fixed source-envelope and mantissa parameters. The estimate controls the selected interface even if that choice sees all its actual future colors.

Indeed, for one predictable track of width \(W\), equation (7) of the track proof with \(L=\lceil\eta D\rceil\), \(p=\lfloor c_\eta D/W^2\rfloor\), and sufficiently small \(c_\eta>0\) gives a bound \(C_\eta\exp(-c'_\eta D/W^2)\) on the original probability of \(L\) or more true hits on the common good sample when \(D/W^2\) is sufficiently large. It also carries the factor \(|V|/2^s\) for a prescribed native mark. This counts the finite common-coordinate block tests; the common original envelope exception is paid once. For instance (7) gives \(C_0\sqrt p(C_1W\sqrt{Dp}/L)^p\), and choosing \(c_\eta\) makes the parenthesis at most \(1/4\). Consequently even a bank of \(\exp(c'_\eta D/(2W^2))\) such tracks misses a positive fraction of the obligatory marks except on an exponentially small set when \(\eta\) is smaller than their guaranteed frequency. The bank may depend on the native low bits and its index may be chosen retrospectively. In particular a fixed predictable decoder would need order \(D/W^2\) additional bits of information to cover that frequency, up to a positive constant. This statement is applicable with an arbitrary-power uniform exceptional bound over intervals if \(D/W^2\gg_A\log d\).

For the variation claim take \(W=1\) and \(\eta\) below the fixed mark frequency. Extend the chosen centered exponents to every unmarked clock by repeating the most recent chosen value (use the first value on the initial gap). This does not increase total variation. In the count envelope there are \(O_A(d)\) possible initial values. Integer paths of length \(D\) with sum of absolute increments at most \(S=\lfloor\delta D\rfloor\) number at most
\[
 O_A(d)\,2^S {D+S\choose S}.
\]
Indeed the magnitudes form a weak composition of some integer at most \(S\) into \(D-1\) slots and at most \(S\) nonzero increments need signs. Hence the path bank has logarithmic size \(O_A(\log d)+O(\delta\log(2/\delta)D)\). Make \(\delta\) small enough for this to be less than \(c'_\eta D/2\), and sum the exponential probability bound over \(O(d^2)\) possible intervals. Every chosen path of smaller variation would itself furnish a prohibited track \(k(t)=\lfloor t/2\rfloor+u(t)\). Regeneration supplies at least \(\eta D\) true hits on it. This proves the result, uniformly over the post hoc choices of witnessing heads. A diffusive path can have linear total variation; the inequality does not exclude the remaining sources.
