# Deep smaller partners in exact marked fibers at common scales

**PROPOSED written consequence.** Use the exact critical section $B$ from PR 197. For every fixed $0<\beta<1$ there are arbitrarily high binary octaves $d$ on which, in **each nonempty exact** $(d,e)$ critical fiber, apart from a fraction $O_\beta(2^{-c_\beta d})$, every ordinary input $n$ has a physical common future with some $m\in B$ satisfying $m<n^\beta$. The two witnesses have $O_\beta(d)$ clocks. The comparison may change the eight-valued surplus. Only the displayed common scales are asserted. This differs from CRITICAL_COMPRESSION.md, whose chosen partner preserves the surplus in **every** sufficiently high fiber.

Take an integer $A\ge\max(16,8/\beta)$. Let $Q$ run over convergent denominators of $\log_2 3$ and put $d=AQ$. For a given nonempty fiber, write $3n+1=2^eu$ and $U=2^{d-e}$, where $e=d/5+O(1)$. The exact endpoints $u$ run through one residue modulo six in an interval of length $\asymp U/d$ around $3U$, without multiplicity. This is the elementary endpoint identification in CRITICAL_COMPRESSION.md. Hence $|F_{d,e}|\asymp U/d$ uniformly in $e$.

Apply the one-arm finite-stage bound of POST_DESCENT_TRANSFER.md at scale $U$, altitude $\alpha=1/A$, tolerance $\eta=1/(8A)$ and physical clock $h=\lfloor(1-\alpha)\log_2 U/a\rfloor$ with $a=\log_2(2/\sqrt3)$. Its exceptional count on the enclosing fixed relative interval is $O_A(U^{1-c_*})$. Dividing by the **actual endpoint fiber cardinality** gives a relative exceptional proportion $O_A(dU^{-c_*})=O_A(2^{-c_\beta d})$, for instance with $c_\beta=c_*/2$. This uses a fresh estimate on the densely parametrized odd endpoint interval, not a restriction of an ambient exceptional set of $n$ to the sparse critical sources. Outside this exception the ordinary $q=T^h(u)$ lies between constant multiples of $U^{7/(8A)}$ and $U^{9/(8A)}$. In particular $Q^2\le q\le2^{2Q}$ for all large $Q$. It is a ternary unit: the initial odd $u$ takes an odd shortcut step to a unit, and thereafter either parity preserves nondivisibility by three.

Apply the *four-clock* common-scale construction of BASIN_COUNTING.md (its first section) to this very $q$, which lies in its required range $[Q^2,2^{36Q}]$. Its proof produces a marked eight-excess input $m$ reaching $q$ with $m\le C((16/3)^Q q)^{5/4}$, since the chosen inverse row index is at most $Q$. The odd endpoint of $m$ reaches $q$ in at most $4Q$ clocks, and the initial halvings after its odd step need only $O(Q)$ clocks by the bound on $\log m$. The displayed upper bound for $q$ gives
$$
 \log_2 m\le \tfrac54(2+\log_2(16/3))Q+O(1)<6Q<\beta d
$$
eventually, hence $m<n^\beta$. The original source reaches the same $q$ in $e+h=O(d)$ clocks. This proof measures source exceptions only. A single exceptional failed least root may support arbitrarily many larger successful comparisons, and no all-scale fixed-floor or convergence statement follows.
