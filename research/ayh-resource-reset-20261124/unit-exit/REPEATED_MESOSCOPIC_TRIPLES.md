# Repeated mesoscopic triple tests on one ordinary source

The long-deadline triple estimate in `NESTED_GRADE_REFINEMENTS.md` (SHA-256 `97f040bfffead023e74ca4502a9806cd318634bacf86cb0b9d6e3f45ff62996d`) gives a first-moment bound for all heads and lags \(V=d^\alpha\), summable for \(\alpha<1/3\). A higher-moment argument on **one original coarse coordinate** gives finite original mass for a positive density of successful *time pairs* throughout \(0<\alpha<2/3\). It uses the identical PR 195 triple and PR 197 original projection inputs.

Fix \(0<\alpha<2/3,\ \eta>0\), let \(V=\lfloor d^\alpha\rfloor\), and let \(J=[3d,3.01d]\) be an integer interval. Let \(B(x)\) count pairs \((t,v)\), \(t\in J, V\le v\le2V\), such that some two true heads regenerated at \(t,t+v\) meet the following requirements. Advance the earlier head and parent by \(v\) physical steps, compare with the fresh later head and parent, and require overlap of their initial grade intervals or \(|w-v/2|\le C\sqrt{V\log d}\) for fixed \(C\). Require at least three symbolic classes remaining after \(\lfloor d/10\rfloor\) additional shared bits. The count is on the good original envelope, with any number of qualifying head values at a given pair of clocks counting once. Then
\[
 \sum_j\frac1{81^j}\#\{x\text{ genuinely discordant}: B(x)\ge\eta dV\}<\infty .
\]
The original exceptional mass for the common envelope is summable. One can retain a prescribed interior mantissa arc in this statement.

**One pair of clocks.** Use the original coarse coordinate \(m_0\) and the native residue of PR 197 `crossing-step/LATE_WITNESS_TRACKS.md`, (2)–(5). Let \(h=\lfloor\delta d\rfloor\) for a fixed \(0<\delta<.01\), chosen below. After exposing the low \(\Delta_t=t-\lfloor2.9d\rfloor\) bits of \(m_0\), the original envelope permits at most \(C_A\sqrt{d\log d}\) anchor pairs \((k_*,b_*)\). For each the candidate earlier head is \(z=3^{g_*}q+c\), on the remaining high quotient \(q\), and its parent is \(3^{g_*-4}q+\lfloor c/81\rfloor\). Both slopes are odd. The residues of the parent modulo \(2^v\) are therefore uniform on this next block, conditional on every outer prefix. The source-qualified finite native nonmerger costs \(O(V^{-1/2})\) there. Exclude incoming grade or scalar-intercept failures of unconditioned probability \(O_M(d^{-M})\) per anchor, uniformly from those same \(v\) bits.

On each good incoming prefix there are \(O_{A,M,C}(\sqrt{V\log d})\) later exponents and \(O_A(1)\) relative roundings. These are the overlapping or diffusive possibilities of the preceding note; their multipliers \(3^w2^{-v}\) are less than one. Its proof of \(Q\ll_M V\log d\) and the four-triple union applies to the next \(h\) bits exactly as written. In detail, given the outer prefix and the following \(v\) bits, the earlier parent high quotient on its own native grid is an odd affine function of the remaining original high variable. Changing to this common odd-affine parameter preserves uniform binary blocks, relative grades, normalized offsets, and symbolic equality. The four rays in the two coordinate systems therefore give identical finite fusion tests. Survival of three distinct symbolic classes through the stated longer deadline implies survival to \(h\), where \(\delta\) enters only the constant.

For each fixed \((t,v)\) the union of all the resulting finite tests, including the bad incoming prefix itself as a permitted outcome, has conditional probability
\[
 q_d\ll_{A,u,\delta,C}
  \sqrt d\,\log d\left(\frac{V\log d}{d}\right)^u+O_M(d^{-M+2}) ,
       \qquad 1<u<3/2 .

This multiplies the anchor menu, old native cost, fresh exponent menu and triple cost in that order. The event is measurable through coarse bit \(\Delta_t+v+h\). The constants depend on fixed \(u,\delta\). Every counted true clock pair on an ordinary good source belongs to it. Using an unconditioned bad incoming prefix as one sufficient outcome avoids any restriction on relative rounding on that rare set.

**Several pairs of clocks.** Choose a fixed \(p\). If \(B(x)\ge\eta dV\), greedily selecting pairs with their earlier clocks more than \(3\delta d\) apart gives at least \(c_{p,\eta}(dV)^p\) increasing \(p\)-tuples. Indeed a selected earlier clock removes at most \((6\delta d+3)(V+1)\) time pairs. Choose \(\delta<\min(.01,\eta/(40p))\) sufficiently small, then divide the ordered selection count by \(p!\). Since \(2V=o(d)\), every selected test is measurable before the next test begins. Iterated conditional expectations on the same uniform original coarse grid bound each tuple by \(q_d^p\). The required terminal depth is less than \(.31d\). Applying the original projection (4) once to this whole finite event and to the original native-survivor residues modulo \(2^{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\) gives ordinary normalized weight \(O_A(d^{-1/2}q_d^p)\) for each tuple. The mandatory original native test is on a different source coordinate from the earlier regenerated head's \(v\)-bit test. There is no independence claim about actual colors.

The number of clock tuples is \(O_p((dV)^p)\); divide by the lower count on each source. Choose \(u<3/2\) with \(u(1-\alpha)>1/2\), possible precisely throughout the claimed open range. Choose \(p\) so \(p(u(1-\alpha)-1/2)>1/2\) and then choose \(M\) large. Fixed logarithmic factors can be absorbed in the strict exponent margin. The normalized original weight is \(O(d^{-1-\epsilon})\) for some \(\epsilon>0\), as required.

This result eliminates a positive density of actual mesoscopic comparisons with a third surviving class and nearby initial grades. Widely separated initial grades and long-lived two-class comparisons do not satisfy the hypotheses. The full original disagreement series remains open.
