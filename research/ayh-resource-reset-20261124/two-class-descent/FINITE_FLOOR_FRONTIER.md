# A finite menu of deep true entrances with near-square-root grade

At row R=81^j put d=log₂R. Write t(Y)=min(τ_Y(x),τ_Y(m)) for the earlier true first entrance to Y, S(t) for the absolute corrected odd-count difference, τ for symbolic fusion time and h=floor(log₂(R/81)) for the native deadline. Use [AREA_FRONTIER_EXTENSION.md](AREA_FRONTIER_EXTENSION.md) and [ACTUAL_FRONTIER_OCCUPATION.md](ACTUAL_FRONTIER_OCCUPATION.md) on their original ordinary source rows.

**Fixed finite menu.** Let 0<β<1/2, 2β<b<1, and choose any positive integer m such that m(b−2β)>1/2. For Lebesgue-almost every one fixed tuple (θ₁,…,θ_m)∈[1,2]^m, the normalized series over original rows of discordant sources with
S(t(2^(θ_i d^b)))≤d^β for every i
is finite. The same holds for the larger finite-certificate event requiring low grades at all those entrances and no fusion through the native clock and all the earlier entrances, on their timely realization. An alternative deterministic per-row menu is obtained by minimizing the latter joint count over all m-tuples of dyadic floor exponents in [ceil(d^b),floor(2d^b)]. These are m genuine physical thresholds shared by the entire row. For example two thresholds allow β<3/8, three allow β<5/12, and every β<1/2 admits a finite m. Each entrance lies at clock d/log₂(2/√3)+o(d) outside the existing original-source exception.

This yields one operational stopping rule. Inspect the menu from larger floors to smaller ones, and at each earlier first passage test the present physical grade. The first grade exceeding d^β is detected then, without observing either later arrival or color. Except on a summable normalized discordant mass the stop exists. Its later arm's entrance to the selected floor lags by at least (log₂3)d^β−O(1), by the physical factor-two bound and the simultaneous grade-to-lag comparison. The assertion concerns a single adapted choice from a fixed finite menu. It does not give this near-half exponent at any one floor prescribed in advance.

## Product of floor measures, with the ordinary trajectory held fixed

Let B=o(d), B≫log d, restrict log₂Y to [B,2B], and let θ=log₂Y/B. For a single source n on the good physical sample let E_n be the set of θ for which the pair is live and has low grade at t(Y). A physical clock can first cross only a log₂-length one set of floors, hence
|E_n|≤q=min(1,O(K²(log d)³/B))
for K≥2 by the preceding area/occupation theorem. The same bound up to an absolute factor holds for uniform integer dyadic exponents in the band; a clock can cross at most one such exponent. For each fixed original source, the measure of (E_n)^m is |E_n|^m. This product calculation concerns independently integrated floor parameters on a single deterministic trajectory. It imposes no temporal or cross-arm independence. The probability of surviving the native deadline on the original row is O(d^(−1/2)). Pay the exceptional source set only once. The integral of the normalized simultaneous certificate count is therefore
O_A(d^(−A)+d^(−1/2)q^m).
At K=d^β, B=d^b, the second term is O(d^(−1/2−m(b−2β))(log d)^(3m)), which is summable. Tonelli gives the common-tuple result; finite averaging gives the per-row dyadic menus. Any genuinely discordant timely source survives every certificate. The result therefore also applies to the specified subsets of the original polylogarithmic landing weights W_j.

## Local improvement of the physical occupation bound

At B≫log d, first-passage clocks t(Y), B≤log₂Y≤2B, lie in only a bounded number of physical descent stages. Indeed choose schedule indices with geometric exponents log₂Y_i=γ^i log₂Y₀ bracketing B and 2B. The interval of earlier passage clocks lies between the corresponding stage boundaries; the number of enclosed stages and the two end fragments is O_γ(1). Tied passage times can add endpoints only. In these stages:

* For every interior excursion, killed when its grade first reaches one, the physical number Q of visits to [2,K] has E exp(cQ/K²)≤C, uniformly in its entry grade ≥2 and integer offset. This is the geometric tail obtained in the previous note from the uniform CK² Green bound and the strong Markov property. It also holds for a segment cut by a fixed remaining clock budget.
* From a grade-two entry of polynomial normalized offset, the probability of more than m further grade-two entries before fusion is ≤d^C e^(−cm). This is the killed-entry contraction of the companion proof. Use the first actual grade-two entry inside a stage as restart; the incoming normalized offset is polynomial on the scalar good sample.
* From such a restart, expose the first m interior excursions or all excursions until death, padding the list with zero occupation after death. Between excursions there is a frontier period of zero interior occupation. Iterating the conditional moment bound at the successive entries gives E exp(c ΣQ_i/K²)≤C^m. There is no assumption that the stopping number of excursions is independent of their durations. Markov's inequality at a sufficiently large multiple of mK² gives O(e^(−c'm)). Consequently the complete finite word from that first entry records total interior band occupation O(mK²) except on O(d^C e^(−c'm)) of its full binary grid. A partially observed last excursion is bounded by its full fair continuation. Any initial interior segment before the first grade-two entry is handled separately from its first visit to [2,K], using its same single-excursion bound. No incoming-grade restriction is required by the area transfer.
* Apply the polynomial-cost actual area transfer to each of these truncated k_i-word tests and take m=C_A log d. All exceptions together have original mass O_A(Rd^(−A)). The frontier decisions and capped grade-one waits contribute O((log d)²) physical frontier clocks per stage, as in the companion proof. A grade-zero run on the actual good sample lasts O(log d), by its nonzero integer offset and the valuation drop at a shared zero-grade step.

Thus the physical live low-grade clock count inside the log-height band on the good sample is
O_A(K² log d+(log d)²).
In the above q one may replace K²(log d)³ by this smaller numerator. The few first-passage clocks at stage endpoints can be added directly. The constants and original exception bounds are uniform in the location of the band and the prescribed tolerance. The proof only needs B and 2B between the terminal polylogarithmic floor and the initial row.

**Single-floor logarithmic endpoint.** With B=d/(log d)^ε and K=d^(1/4)/(log d)^(1+ε), ε>0, the new bound d^(−1/2)q is O(1/[d(log d)^(1+ε)]), hence summable. This improves the previously frozen logarithmic denominator by a factor log d while retaining a single selected deep floor.

**Growing menu near the square-root scale.** Given ε>0, take B=d/(log d)^ε and K=√d/(log d)^(1/2+ε). Then q=O((log d)^(−ε)). For m_j=ceil(c log d/log log d) with any c>1/(2ε), the normalized simultaneous low-grade mass is bounded in mean by O_A(d^(−A)+d^(−1/2−cε+o(1))), which is summable. This holds for finite row-minimizing dyadic menus as above. It also holds for almost every single infinite sequence of floor parameters using its first m_j entries at row j, by product measure and Tonelli. Every inspected floor still has log₂Y=o(d).

None of these estimates controls discordant sources that have a large grade at a selected entrance. The gain is to remove simultaneous small-grade alternatives at actual late clocks without making any conditioning assertion for the eventual pair colors.
