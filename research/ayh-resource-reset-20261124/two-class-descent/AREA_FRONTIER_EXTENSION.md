# Removing the exponential grade cost at arbitrary true pre-entrance clocks

This combines the area identity of [FORWARD_SOURCE_REEXPOSURE.md](FORWARD_SOURCE_REEXPOSURE.md) with the original-source sliding estimates and the killed occupation argument of [ACTUAL_FRONTIER_OCCUPATION.md](ACTUAL_FRONTIER_OCCUPATION.md). It improves the polynomial deep-floor range from β<1/6 to **every β<1/4**. Take d=log₂R and fix a suffix r. The common parent n ranges over [7N,8N), N=R/81, with arms n and 81n+r.

**Uniform rare test at a physical visit.** On the scalar good sample, every t up to the earlier true M=d^D entrance has both affine intercepts 0≤e_i(t)≤E=d^C, with C independent of D once the old sliding length b₀=O_A(log d) is fixed. Indeed the physical intercept starts at 0 and follows e'=(3^b e+b)/2; the exact linear products over full b₀-blocks contract, and the shorter remainder costs at most (3/2)^{b₀}. Each physical endpoint up to that time is O(Nd^C) by the sliding estimate. Enlarge D so E=o(M). The original exception remains O_A(Rd^(−A)).

Fix a deterministic time t, count vector (p₁,p₂), and deterministic stage index i as in the companion proof. Sort by the total grades g with slopes λ=3^{g_hi}, ν=3^{g_lo}, ρ=λ/ν. Put H=2^t, F=H/λ, V=Nν/H, and let c_i be the fixed contribution from the original affine offsets at this count vector. The physical endpoint at grade g is 3^g(n+δ)/H+e, where δ=0 or r/81. If this clock occurs in the parameterized scalar stage before the earlier M-entrance, both endpoints are at least Y_{i+1}, or at least M if the last stage is clipped; in the latter case Y_{i+1}≤M. The larger-grade endpoint being O(Nd^C) forces F≥c d^(−C). The smaller-grade endpoint lies in I=[7V+c_lo,8V+c_lo+E]. Since c_lo is at most ν/H and E=o(M), one has V≥cY_{i+1} and E≪V. In particular 2^{k_i}≤V for all sufficiently large d, since 2^{k_i}≤sqrt(Y_i) and Y_{i+1}=Y_i^γ with γ>1/2.

Write the actual pair as z,ρz+D₀. The permitted offsets obey D₀=c_hi−ρc_lo+e_hi−ρe_lo and thus occupy at most 1+(1+ρ)E≤3ρ(E+1) integer positions. For each fixed D₀ let a test of the next k_i common binary parameter bits have full-grid mass at most δ₀, uniformly over all its incoming states under consideration. Cover I by complete periods of 2^{k_i} integers. There are O(V δ₀) possible bad z, since |I|≤2V and 2^{k_i}≤V. For any specified (z,D₀), the upper endpoint is fixed. Bounding its intercept in [0,E] confines **all original parents across the entire row** to an interval of length EF. Its integer multiplicity is ≤1+EF≤C d^C(E+1)F. Multiplying the three bounds and using the exact identity ρFV=N yields at most
C d^C(E+1)^2 N δ₀
original parents per time, count vector and stage. The polynomial factor is independent of the incoming grade and offset. Summing over the 81 suffixes, O(d) physical times, O(d²) count vectors and O(log d) stage indices costs another fixed polynomial. The test need not consume new fair bits of a conditionally uniform actual endpoint. The area argument counts all possible ordinary integer endpoints before projecting back to original sources. Unlike the native-intercept proof in FORWARD_SOURCE_REEXPOSURE, the present source bound applies when t exceeds log₂N: the actual scalar sliding estimate supplies the needed polynomial intercept envelope.

**Consequence.** In test 3 of ACTUAL_FRONTIER_OCCUPATION, the killed uniform bound for the physical occupation of [2,K] in one positive-grade excursion was 2^(−m) for m quanta of order K², uniformly over all incoming grades and integer offsets. The bound survives truncation to the stage's k_i-bit window. Using the area transfer just proved, m=C_A log d now suffices for any 2≤K≤O(d). Tests 1 and 2 still use bounded-grade restarts and yield O(log d) segments per stage, with O((log d)²) frontier clocks. Across O(log d) stages the true pair therefore spends at most
O_A((1+K)^2 (log d)^3)
live clocks at grade ≤K before the earlier actual polylogarithmic passage, outside O_A(Rd^(−A)) original children. The constant and exception bound are uniform in a prescribed K; summation over the O(d) integer tolerances is also available by enlarging C_A.

For any fixed 0<β<1/4 and 1/2+2β<b<1, put K=C₀d^β and inspect the genuine floors Y=2^{θ d^b}, 1≤θ≤2. Repeat the coarea proof using the finite source predicate that a pair has survived the native horizon and is still live at its earlier Y-passage with grade ≤K. The integrated normalized bad mass and the normalized bad mass at the finite least-minimizing dyadic floor in this band are each at most
O_A(d^(−A)+d^(−1/2+2β−b)(log d)^3).
The bound is summable across rows, and Tonelli also yields summability for almost every single θ. A physical lag O(d^β) implies grade ≤K after adjusting C₀. At these floors the earlier true clock is d/log₂(2/√3)+o(d). Restricting the original polylogarithmic landing weights W_j to these source marks preserves the bound on their color-discordant mass. The large-grade, large-lag branch and unrestricted summability remain open.

**Logarithmic endpoint.** Given ε>0, take K=d^(1/4)/(log d)^(2+ε) and average the floors Y=2^(θ B_d) over 1≤θ≤2, with B_d=d/(log d)^ε. The integrated normalized certificate mass and the normalized mass at the least-minimizing dyadic floor are
O_A(d^(−A)+d^(−1/2) K²(log d)³/B_d)=O_A(d^(−A)+d^(−1)(log d)^(−1−ε)).
This is summable, with the direct grade bound at the earlier true passage and the clock-lag bound after a fixed constant enlargement of K. Since B_d=o(d), that passage still occurs at d/log₂(2/√3)+o(d) outside O_A(Rd^(−A)) original children. The same summability holds for almost every single θ.
