## The AYH connection retained

Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule's [primary paper](https://emreyolcu.com/research/rewriting-collatz.pdf) constructs a mixed binary/ternary rewrite system whose termination is equivalent to positive Collatz convergence and obtains automated proofs for selected weakened systems. This packet uses the same basic discipline: search for finite words with exact semantics, then check the resulting certificates independently. Its directed comparison words operate on ordinary positive integers and preserve the order of basin labels. They are not claimed to constitute a terminating interpretation of the full AYH rewrite system.

The earlier PR197 [global-methods packet](../GLOBAL_METHODS.md) shows why the previously attempted polynomial, scalar, monomial and carry interpretations do not automatically complete that program. The positive contraction here is a more flexible certificate: one ordinary arm joins a hypothesized bad component, and its tripled arm reaches a point small enough for the least-defect argument. The finite symbolic verifier checks each branch and its height. The unresolved problem is to cover every actual least-defect configuration by such a certificate or a different valid progress rule.

## Heavy machinery and its exact boundary

Evertse's [S-unit and recurrence paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1984__53_2_225_0.pdf) is relevant to the sparse-gap route. It does not directly turn the repo's prescribed finite itinerary into a contradiction for an arbitrary ordinary future. The source-qualified application in PR206 must still be attached to the complete supplied path; the common post-gate suffix can already account for the required short gaps. The primary paper was inspected as literature context, not independently re-proved or imported into this Lean build.

The fixed-total renewal floor invoked in PR207 produces simultaneous near-natural-scale populations at a balanced row. Its upper localization removes suffixes much shorter than \(\sqrt b\) and much longer than \(\sqrt{b\log b}\). It leaves the intermediate range where the lower construction lives. Likewise, its nontrivial-cycle lower floor uses a diffusive shell while its all-powers upper is superdiffusive. The two bounds do not contradict each other. [Pinned comparison](../../../reports/ayh-resource-reset-20261124/closing-proposal/LATE_DIFFUSIVE_COMPARISON.md).

A later PR206 [proposal](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/b2b56b94175174a680a296478f16c619a8828972/research/clock-itineraries/Proposal.md) separately reduces convergence to an ordinary forward-clock crossing and finite cycle tests. Its distortion bound on distinct recorded odd states yields a smaller value or an actual repeat when the clock crosses. The proposal explicitly leaves both conditions open. This later statement was read, but its companion Lean sources were not rebuilt in this pass; it supplies no crossing at the particular surviving reverse defect.

Neither a stationary average law nor an independent law for a randomized inverse chain is an upper law for the deterministic continuation of a fixed ordinary root. The [gate note](GATE_TRANSPORT.md) retains that distinction with the exact clock inequality

\[
k-e\le\log_2 a-p\log_2 3
\]

when an actual source at least \(2^k\) reaches \(a\) in \(e\) shortcut steps with \(p\) odd inputs. This remains true under all the conditioning in question. It diagnoses a failed transfer; it does not refute a theorem with a correctly retained source measure.

## The next proof obligation, stated for Lean

[FinalProofProposal.lean](FinalProofProposal.lean) makes the present arithmetic target concrete. For every \(z\equiv17\pmod{48}\), every actual finite certificate `Hits (3*z) j`, and upper odd count at least three, prove

\[
C(z)\ \lor\ \exists n>0:\quad 2^{40}n<z\ \land\ (C(n)\leftrightarrow C(z)).
\]

No fixed uniform clock is imposed; the packet proves why such a restriction would be inappropriate. Ordinary reverse edges, legitimate directed queries below the parent, and source-dependent clocks remain available. The checked two-odd theorem establishes the corresponding base layer. The quantitative return has now been formalized in `QuantitativeReturn.lean`; it is no longer an open formalization input to the assembly.

A better all-rank invariant would be a mathematical advance. Raising a search depth without an all-depth coverage theorem would only enlarge a finite bank. The retained certificate files are intended to support that next structural step, with exact ordinary legality, endpoints and height margins available for reuse.
