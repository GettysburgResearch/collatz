# Verification and source audit

This file separates mathematical reconstruction from repository status. All
new theorem-level entries remain `PROPOSED`; parallel work in one session is
useful adversarial checking, but it is not an independent repository review.

## Review method

Three proof lanes were assigned independently after the active branches had
been mapped:

- `gpt56-synthesis-01-a`: adelic completion-height rigidity, followed by the
  finite CRT bridge and simultaneous `64`/`81` cylinders;
- `gpt56-synthesis-01-p`: active survivor cylinders, followed by the exact
  zero-tail valuation dynamics;
- `gpt56-synthesis-01-h`: H carry geometry and the first mixed-sign crossing.

Successful lanes received a second, harder lemma. The integrating agent then
rederived the displayed algebra, checked hypotheses against the source branch,
and kept every unresolved implication explicit.

A second wave reused the same lanes only after the first assignments passed.
It attacked finite inverse-bulk compilation, minimum-root renewal and successor
gaps, genuine H offset families, Padé gcd cancellation, padding-address
transitions, and the exact automata hypothesis needed to turn common cylinder
digits into common directives. Returned formulas were checked independently by
the integrating agent; exact numerical records remain labeled internal rather
than promoted to reviewed computation.

The third wave switched from proposed finite-state shortcuts to exact state
geometry. It derived the causal inverse-cylinder carry before testing reset,
computed the H compiler's real and 2-adic skew products before proposing
return, reconstructed invariant-component periodic rigidity before invoking
Cobham, and identified the survivor inverse limit as an exact metric full
shift. Small exact enumerations were used only as adversarial checks of proved
formulas, never as theorem dependencies.

Waves four through six then attacked the surviving interfaces directly:
translation-fiber order, phase-resolved and set-valued integer packing,
fixed-width ordinary separation, full residual-address corrections, dyadic
Padé state refinement, and finite-section correction grammars. Every closing
claim `L-9862`--`L-9867` received a proof reconstruction by a nonauthoring
lane; finite enumerations and bit-packed recurrences were retained only as
independent adversarial checks of the displayed algebra.

### Independent `L-9839` even-`K` jet audit

The formulas `L-9839/(14a)--(14f)` received a separate algebraic audit. The
convolution exponent was re-expanded as
`F_(M+s)(w+j)-F_M(w)-F_s(j)=sw+Mj-wj`, giving exactly the stated remaining
factor. The phase shift `zeta -> zeta+ar(s-j)` preserves eligibility and the
reduced order, while the zero remainder makes `B_0(1)=1`; hence the same
nonzero value `C_m` occurs for every `j`. Finally, the only surviving terms in
the `(m+1)`-st Leibniz derivative have H-jet orders `m` and `m+1`, with
coefficients `binom(m+1,m)=m+1` and `1`. No defect was found. This audit does
not promote the still-open residual-to-excess-gcd transfer inequality.

## Explicit non-results

- No nontrivial infinite orbit or `K-####` candidate is claimed.
- Finite CRT compatibility is not ordinary realization.
- Exponential forward growth is not a contradiction to stabilization of the
  initial cylinder representative.
- The finite CRT countermodels in `R-9801` vary with depth and do not produce
  one global survivor.
- The affine tuple in `R-9802` is not asserted to be an actual H word.
- Numerical agreement of the reciprocal slopes in PR #16 and PR #20 does not
  identify their symbolic digit streams.
- Transcendental 2-adic value does not imply a nonautomatic digit word;
  Christol's criterion leaves 2-automaticity open.
- The exact minimum and successor at a finite depth are not one ordinary
  infinite survivor pair.
- `L-9847` gives a necessary isolation test and packing bound for translates
  of one signed difference; multiplicity alone does not bound the pointed
  largest gap or certify adjacency against survivor points outside that fiber.
- `L-9853` proves the stronger negative fact that legitimate zero-run twists
  can have exponentially exceptional pointed gaps. It does not show that an
  exceptional point is selected by the actual minimum/successor order, and
  extra zero coordinates may split the displayed gap.
- `L-9865` selects the actual first two nontrivial representatives after one
  width lift from fixedly many bucket-local heads. It does not propagate those
  heads across all widths, bound the selected gap below, or prove infinitely
  many minimum renewals.
- The two-suffix H compiler solves one crossing and does not close the return
  dynamics after renormalization.
- Automatic Padé factors are substantial but quantitatively insufficient;
  this is not a proof that all polynomial or arithmetic gcd cancellation is
  insufficient.
- A regular safety automaton is not a padded-cylinder directive transducer,
  and a reset of its transition skeleton need not preserve its output colors.
- No globally exact zero-reset directive router exists; a pair-specific carry
  valuation theorem is a different and still-open statement.
- The renormalized H phase rotation deletes a physical terminal zero. Its
  Sturmian coding, linear real-fiber drift, and expanding 2-adic tail shifts
  do not constitute a forward H orbit.
- The unique finite physical carry tuple in the nested `10/30` H construction
  converges to a nonordinary 2-adic seed; this rules out that architecture as
  an ordinary return orbit but says nothing about other suffix families.
- Zero-interface runs in that H construction are logarithmic in their starting
  endpoint, and nonzero blocks occur at least logarithmically often; neither
  statement gives positive lower density because nonzero resets are unbounded.
- The exact reset sieve excludes all sufficiently long future zero cylinders
  after a fixed H interface, but its threshold is linear in stage and its
  dyadic congruence always has one abstract solution; it is not a density law.
- The finite-suffix zero-descent criterion in `L-9840` is a no-go and design
  test, not a construction: violating one of its two inequalities is only
  necessary, and the corresponding non-descent quotient must be reached.
- The `{30,60,70}` architecture in `L-9841` closes the raw multiplier phase
  and alternates genuine crossings, but its irrational rotation does not
  erase the affine offset or select an ordinary 2-adic input.
- Its normalized real affine fiber in `L-9843` drifts linearly and has no
  bounded invariant graph or cycle; this is an obstruction in the real fiber,
  not a theorem about canonical dyadic carry stabilization.
- `L-9845` selects a unique inverse-compatible 2-adic endpoint and gives a
  zero-dimensional Sturmian fiber set, but null dimension does not exclude an
  ordinary intersection. Its recursive address is not a literal block coding
  of the endpoint's binary digits, so Sturmian aperiodicity alone is not a
  nonrationality theorem. `L-9846` records the conditional signature, and
  `L-9850` combines decoder-based distinctness with real drift to exclude that
  signature by integer packing. This excludes only the exact raw-return graph,
  not every H architecture or every Collatz orbit.
- `L-9851` is a screening obstruction: satisfying its packing inequality does
  not construct an integer orbit, and the endpoint multiplicity must be proved
  independently for each return system.
- `L-9854` and `L-9855` sharpen packing by residue support and decoded phase,
  respectively. They remain necessary screens; a residue restriction without
  bounded endpoint multiplicity supplies no contradiction.
- `L-9852` realizes exactly one residual-aware physical padding lift. Its two-
  lift obstruction refutes a bulk-only next-bit rule, not a finite grammar
  allowed to read the residual defect, and it does not route the remaining
  `K-H` connector block.
- `L-9858` identifies the complete finite correction as an isometric
  permutation and proves an exact `Q`-bit lower bound only when the stated
  factorization has that correction as its sole residual-dependent channel.
  It does not supply a uniform finite-state realization, bounded rewrite, or
  stage-to-stage update law.
- `L-9863` makes every 2-adic isometry triangular and `L-9867` characterizes
  finite-state realizability by finite rooted-tree sections. Neither proves
  that the physical scale update is an isometry on the required domain or
  that its connector section family is uniformly finite; the square-mask
  example proves triangularity alone is insufficient.
- Finiteness of the central ternary-dilate spine is not finiteness of the full
  ternary kernel, so Cobham cannot yet exclude binary-automatic component
  colorings.
- Uniform finiteness of every fixed-width translated cone is still not a
  uniform bound on their increasing union; only unbounded coheight remains,
  but it remains a genuine automatic-coloring gap.
- Horizontal cycles reduce every ternary offset stratum to one binary-kernel
  representative, but the cumulative representative kernels may still keep
  producing new sequences as coheight grows.
- Escape decorations are individually eventually periodic, and `L-9844`
  recovers their exact cyclic order by a three-state transducer. `L-9849`
  proves that every unabsorbed cycle has exactly `L_k` distinct roots and that
  different unabsorbed widths cannot alias; only eventual absorption into the
  finite core can make the full ternary kernel finite.
- `L-9857` decides absorption only after a width is supplied. Its finite
  ordinary horizon is `2^(Theta(3^k))`, and successive fixed-width decisions
  do not certify a last unabsorbed width.
- `L-9848`, `L-9856`, `L-9859`, `L-9861`, and `L-9862` prove dyadic target
  nonvanishing only for the actual remainder layers
  `s=4,8,12,16,20,24,28,32`. Their exact finite transitions do not prove the
  conjectural all-layer augmentation identity. Thirty-two-channel minimality
  is only among residue-sum quotient states. `L-9864` gives an exact four-step
  operator and a sharp order-eight scalar congruence, but no endpoint-twist
  invariant capable of propagating the large orders. The
  `L-9839` residual envelope still bounds the full gcd sector only after its
  explicit transverse-jet transfer inequality is proved for any surviving
  residual zero.
- `L-9860` gives necessary Hall cuts for partial residue information, and
  `L-9866` proves them sufficient for real-valued fractional allocation only.
  They do not turn an allowed set or stochastic kernel into a deterministic
  orbit-time residue, integer matching, moving-threshold construction, or
  cross-modulus selector.
- Hausdorff dimension `1/6` and Haar measure zero do not prevent the survivor
  Cantor set from meeting the countable ordinary section.
