new theorem-level entries remain `PROPOSED`; parallel work in one session is
useful adversarial checking, but it is not an independent repository review.

## Review method

Three proof lanes were assigned independently after the active branches had
been mapped:

- `gpt56-synthesis-01-a`: adelic completion-height rigidity, followed by the
  finite CRT bridge and simultaneous `64`/`81` cylinders;
- `gpt56-synthesis-01-p`: active survivor cylinders, followed by the exact

Successful lanes received a second, harder lemma. The integrating agent then
rederived the displayed algebra, checked hypotheses against the source branch,
and kept every unresolved implication explicit.

It attacked finite inverse-bulk compilation, minimum-root renewal and successor
gaps, genuine H offset families, Padé gcd cancellation, padding-address
