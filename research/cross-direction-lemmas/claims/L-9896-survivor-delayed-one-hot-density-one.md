## Setup

Fix

\[
m\ge1,
\qquad
v\in\{0,1\}^m,
\qquad
r=\alpha_m(v).
\tag{1}
\]

By `L-9883`, the one-hot word `w_n=0^(n-1)1` exposes the suffix `v` exactly
on one residue class

\[
81^{-n}\equiv(17+64r)^{-1}
\pmod {64^{m+1}}.
\tag{2}
\]

Put

\[
M=64^{m+1},
\qquad
O_0=\operatorname {ord}_{M}(81)=2^{6m+2}.
\tag{3}
\]

Choose one solution `n_0` of (2).  Every exposing depth is uniquely

\[
n=n_0+O_0t,
\qquad t\in\mathbb Z,
\tag{4}
\]

subject only to positivity of `n`.  Relative densities below are densities
in the ordinary nonnegative parameter `t`; Haar measure means normalized
Haar measure on its `2`-adic completion.

For every integer `h>=1`, define the algebraic lift coordinate

\[
D_h(t)
=\left[17\,81^{-(n-h+1)}\right]_{64^{m+h}}
\tag{5}
\]

When `t` is ordinary and `h<n`, it corresponds to the length-`n+m` delayed
one-hot word

\[
\omega_{n,h}=0^{n-h}1\,0^{m+h-1}.
\tag{6}
\]

## Statement 1 -- exact fixed-delay event

The delayed word has exact representative

\[
\boxed{
\alpha_{n+m}(\omega_{n,h})
=64^{n-h}D_h(t).
}
\tag{7}
\]

It is a lower block-zero competitor to the exposed cylinder candidate

\[
A_*=\alpha_{n+m}(w_nv)=64^{n-1}
\tag{8}
\]

if and only if

\[
\boxed{
D_h(t)<64^{h-1}.
}
\tag{9}
\]

Under (9), the depth-`n+m` representative lies below `64^n`, so the final
`m` zeros leave its depth-`n` representative unchanged.  Thus (9) certifies
an exact zero renewal block, not merely a congruence collision.

Reduce (5) modulo `M` and put

\[
c_h
=\left[
17\,81^{h-1}(17+64r)^{-1}
\right]_M.
\tag{10}
\]

Then

\[
N_h={\operatorname {ord}_{64^{m+h}}(81)\over O_0}
=64^{h-1},
\tag{11}
\]

and there is a unique coordinate

\[
D_h(t)=c_h+M Q_h(t),
\qquad
0\le Q_h(t)<N_h.
\tag{12}
\]

The map

\[
\boxed{
Q_h:\mathbb Z/N_h\mathbb Z
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/N_h\mathbb Z
\text{ is a permutation.}
}
\tag{13}
\]

For every `h>=m+2`, condition (9) is exactly

\[
\boxed{
E_h:\quad Q_h(t)<K_h,
\qquad
K_h=64^{h-m-2}.
}
\tag{14}
\]

Consequently every fixed-delay event has the same exact relative density

\[
\boxed{
\mu(E_h)={K_h\over N_h}
=p:=64^{-(m+1)}.
}
\tag{15}
\]

### Proof

The finite cylinder formula of `L-9826` gives (7).  Comparing (7) with (8)
gives (9).  Under that inequality, the full representative is less than
`64^(n-1)`, while reduction of the length-`n+m` cylinder modulo `64^n` is
its length-`n` prefix representative.  The two canonical representatives
are therefore equal, and `L-9885/(7)` identifies the final block as an exact
zero-renewal block.

Equation (2) reduces (5) to (10).  Odd-base LTE gives

\[
v_2(81^{O_0}-1)=6m+6,
\tag{16}
\]

so

\[
g=81^{-O_0}
\tag{17}
\]

has exact order `N_h=64^(h-1)` modulo `64^(m+h)` and generates the kernel of
reduction inside the cyclic subgroup generated by `81`.  Multiplying one
lift of `c_h` by this kernel makes its orbit the whole fiber over `c_h`.  As
`t` runs modulo `N_h`, multiplication by `g^t` therefore visits every lift

\[
c_h+Mq,
\qquad 0\le q<N_h,
\tag{18}
\]

exactly once.  This proves (11)--(13).

The residue `c_h` is odd and hence positive.  Since

\[
64^{h-1}=M\,64^{h-m-2}=MK_h,
\tag{19}
\]

the inequality `c_h+MQ_h<MK_h` is equivalent to `Q_h<K_h`.  The permutation
law then gives (15). **QED**

## Statement 2 -- exact signed geometry

On `E_h`, put

\[
B_h=64^{n-h}D_h(t).
\tag{20}
\]

Then

\[
A_*-B_h
=64^{n-h}\{64^{h-1}-D_h(t)\},
\tag{21}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
v_2(A_*-B_h)=6(n-h),
\qquad
{A_*-B_h\over64^{n-h}}
\equiv-81^{h-1}\pmod {64}.
}
\tag{22}
\]

### Proof

The factor in braces in (21) is odd, proving the valuation.  Every exposing
depth satisfies `n=1 mod4`.  Since `17=81 mod64`, equation (5) gives

\[
D_h(t)
\equiv17\,81^{h-1-n}
\equiv81^{h-1}
\pmod {64}.
\tag{23}
\]

Also `64^(h-1)=0 mod64` for `h>=2`, proving the unit congruence. **QED**

## Statement 3 -- an exact correlation cutoff

For `m+2<=h<=k`, the high-delay coordinate retains the full lower-period
phase:

\[
\boxed{
Q_k(t_1)\equiv Q_k(t_2)\pmod {N_h}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
t_1\equiv t_2\pmod {N_h}.
}
\tag{24}
\]

If

\[
k-h\ge m+1,
\tag{25}
\]

then `E_k` is independent of every condition `S` measurable modulo `N_h`:

\[
\boxed{
\mu(E_k\cap S)=p\,\mu(S).
}
\tag{26}
\]

In particular, take the spaced delays

\[
h_j=m+2+(j-1)(m+1),
\qquad j\ge1.
\tag{27}
\]

The events `E_(h_j)` are mutually independent Bernoulli events of parameter
`p`.  Every finite Boolean pattern has exact relative density

\[
\boxed{
p^{\#\{j:E_{h_j}\}}
(1-p)^{\#\{j:E_{h_j}^{\rm c}\}}.
}
\tag{28}
\]

All possibly nontrivial pair correlations are confined to
`1<=k-h<=m`.  They remain finite and exact: if `Psi_(k,h)` is the permutation
which transports `Q_k(t) mod N_h` back to `Q_h(t)`, then

\[
\boxed{
\mu(E_h\cap E_k)
={1\over N_k}
\#\left\{
0\le q<K_k:
\Psi_{k,h}(q\bmod N_h)<K_h
\right\}.
}
\tag{29}
\]

No independence is asserted inside that short-range band.

### Proof

Modulo `64^(m+h)`, the element `g` in (17) has exact order `N_h`.
Consequently the reduction of `Q_k(t)` modulo `N_h` is a permutation of
`t mod N_h`, which proves (24).

Under (25),

\[
{K_k\over N_h}=64^{k-h-m-1}
\tag{30}
\]

is an integer.  The interval `0<=Q_k<K_k` therefore contains each residue
modulo `N_h` exactly `K_k/N_h` times.  Combining this with (24) proves (26).
Induction over the nested moduli `N_(h_j)` gives mutual independence and
(28).  Without (25), parameterize the joint event by the permutation
`q=Q_k(t)` to obtain (29). **QED**

## Statement 4 -- Haar and ordinary density-one obstruction

The full union of delayed one-hot obstruction events has normalized Haar
measure one in the exposing phase:

\[
\boxed{
\mu\left(\bigcup_{h\ge m+2}E_h\right)=1.
}
\tag{31}
\]

Indeed, almost every formal `2`-adic phase parameter belongs to infinitely
many of the spaced events (27).

The same obstruction has ordinary relative natural density one.  More
strongly, for every fixed `s>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
\delta_{\rm exposure}\left(
n:\ A_*\text{ has at least }s
\text{ distinct lower delayed one-hot block-zero competitors}
\right)=1.
}
\tag{32}
\]

Equivalently, the global finite-depth rank of the one-hot cylinder candidate
tends to infinity in natural density along every suffix-exposure progression.

Every finite event family has a power-of-two period.  Since this period is
coprime to `9`, the same relative-density-one result holds separately in
each residue class of `n mod9`.  In particular, it holds in each of the
eight exposure phases outside

\[
n\equiv1\pmod {36},
\tag{33}
\]

the predecessor phase isolated by `L-9886`.  In absolute depth density, the
set of exposing depths with at least one lower delayed one-hot competitor has

\[
\boxed{
\delta={1\over O_0},
\qquad
\delta_{n\not\equiv1\ (36)}={8\over9O_0}.
}
\tag{34}
\]

### Proof

For the first `J` spaced events, (28) gives

\[
\mu\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^JE_{h_j}^{\rm c}\right)
=(1-p)^J\longrightarrow0.
\tag{35}
\]

The same argument applied after any fixed initial index shows that almost
every phase belongs to infinitely many events, proving (31).

Every finite Boolean pattern is periodic in the ordinary parameter `t`, so
its ordinary natural density equals its Haar measure.  For fixed `J`, omit
the finite initial depths with `n<=h_J`.  The relative lower density of
depths having at least `s` valid competitors is at least

\[
\Pr\{\operatorname {Binomial}(J,p)\ge s\}.
\tag{36}
\]

This tends to one with `J`, proving (32).  The competitors are distinct by
the finite-cylinder injectivity of `L-9826`.

Finally, apply the Chinese remainder theorem to the power-of-two event
period and modulus `9`.  Exposure itself is one class modulo `O_0`; hence it
has absolute density `1/O_0`, and eight of its nine mod-9 subphases lie
outside (33).  This proves (34). **QED**

## What this advances

- `L-9892` chose one pure-power lift at each delay and correctly found that
  selected subfamily to be thin.  The full interval of lower lifts in (14)
  instead has constant density at every delay, and spaced delays decorrelate
  exactly.
- The unrestricted one-hot global-promotion obstruction is no longer merely
  infinite or Haar-positive: it occurs with ordinary relative density one,
  with rank diverging in density.
- The theorem remains valid separately outside every legal-predecessor phase,
  so later-orbit merger exclusions do not remove the obstruction.

## Gap audit

- The set of ordinary exposing depths which avoids every delayed one-hot
  obstruction now has relative natural density zero and formal Haar measure
  zero.  The theorem neither proves nor disproves that infinitely many such
  ordinary depths exist.
- Haar-almost-sure membership in infinitely many formal events does not mean
  that one finite ordinary depth has infinitely many valid competitors.  The
  correct ordinary conclusion is the rank-divergence statement (32).
- Small delays `h<m+2` and multi-hot competitors can only add obstructions;
  they are not needed for the density-one conclusion and are not classified.
- Global rank divergence does not identify the first positive block or the
  unrestricted successor above the candidate.
- No ordinary infinite survivor and no Collatz counterexample is constructed.

## Adversarial checks

- The threshold in (9) is an ordinary inequality between canonical
  representatives.  It is stronger than a residue congruence and is exactly
  what forces the terminal renewal block to be zero.
- The fixed-delay density is `64^(-(m+1))`, not the pure-power density
  `64^(1-h)` from `L-9892`.
- Pairwise independence is asserted only after the exact separation
  `k-h>=m+1`; nearby delays may be correlated.
- The finite-prefix exclusion `n>h_J` is imposed before converting a Boolean
  event pattern into actual delayed words.
- CRT with modulus `9` is applied only to finite power-of-two event periods
  before taking the density limit.

## Remaining uncertainty

Does the zero-density avoiding set contain infinitely many ordinary depths,
and can any of them satisfy the stronger multi-hot and first-positive-block
conditions required for global promotion?  Density methods alone cannot
decide that exceptional set.
