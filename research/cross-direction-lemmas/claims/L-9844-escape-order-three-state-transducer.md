## Definitions

For `k>=1`, put

\[
L_k=2\cdot3^{k-1},
\qquad
r_{k,j}=[2^{-j}]_{3^k}
\quad(0\le j<L_k).
\tag{1}
\]

Write

\[
w_{k,j}=r_{k,j}\bmod2,
\qquad
q_{k,j}=r_{k,j}\bmod3\in\{1,2\}.
\tag{2}
\]

Since `q_(k,j)=[2^(-j)]_3`, it is the fixed alternating clock

\[
q_{k,j}=
\begin{cases}
1,&j\text{ even},\\
2,&j\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]

Use the three width lifts from `L-9842`:

\[
r_{k+1,j+mL_k}
=r_{k,j}+\tau_{k,j,m}3^k,
\qquad
m\in\{0,1,2\}.
\tag{4}
\]

The top digit of the first lift is

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{k,j}=\tau_{k,j,0},
\qquad
[2^{-j}]_{3^{k+1}}
=r_{k,j}+\rho_{k,j}3^k.
}
\tag{5}
\]

For `h>=1`, recall the escape correction and ordered section digit from
`L-9842`:

\[
\begin{aligned}
M_{h,k}&=3^{h+k+1},\\
v_{h,k,j}
&=\left[2^{-1}(1+3^{h+1}r_{k,j})\right]_{M_{h,k}},\\
\zeta_{h,k,j}
&=\frac{2v_{h,k,j}-(1+3^{h+1}r_{k,j})}{M_{h,k}},\\
a_{h,k,j,m}
&=[2(\tau_{k,j,m}-\zeta_{h,k,j})]_3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

If `d_(h,k,j)` is the corresponding escape-decoration sequence and
`P_a f(n)=f(3n+a)`, then

\[
d_{h,k+1,j+mL_k}=P_{a_{h,k,j,m}}d_{h,k,j}.
\tag{7}
\]

## Statement

### 1. First-order primitive-root lift

For every `k>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
2^{L_k}\equiv1+3^k\pmod {3^{k+1}},
\qquad
2^{-L_k}\equiv1-3^k\pmod {3^{k+1}}.
}
\tag{8}
\]

Consequently all three lift digits in (4) are explicit:

\[
\boxed{
\tau_{k,j,m}
=\left[\rho_{k,j}-m q_{k,j}\right]_3
\qquad(m=0,1,2).
}
\tag{9}
\]

In particular, their permutation of `0,1,2` is an affine orbit with common
difference `-q_(k,j)` over `F_3`.

### 2. The canonical-half correction is one parity bit

The correction in (6) has the exact closed form

\[
\boxed{
\zeta_{h,k,j}=1-w_{k,j}.
}
\tag{10}
\]

Put

\[
\alpha_{k,j}
=\left[2(\rho_{k,j}+w_{k,j}-1)\right]_3.
\tag{11}
\]

Then the ordering permutation from `L-9842` is

\[
\boxed{
\pi_{h,k,j}(m)
=a_{h,k,j,m}
=\left[\alpha_{k,j}-2m q_{k,j}\right]_3.
}
\tag{12}
\]

It is independent of `h`. More explicitly,

\[
\boxed{
a_{h,k,j,m}=
\begin{cases}
[\alpha_{k,j}+m]_3,&j\text{ even},\\
[\alpha_{k,j}-m]_3,&j\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
}
\tag{13}
\]

Thus even positions use a translation of `F_3`, odd positions use a reflected
translation, and the intercept is determined by the single top digit
`rho_(k,j)` and the staying bit `w_(k,j)`.

### 3. Exact three-state top-digit transducer

Extend `r_(k,j)` and `w_(k,j)` periodically with period `L_k`. For every
`j>=0`, define the extended top digit `rho_(k,j) in {0,1,2}` by

\[
X_{k,j}:=[2^{-j}]_{3^{k+1}}
=r_{k,j\bmod L_k}+\rho_{k,j}3^k.
\tag{14}
\]

This agrees with (5) on `0<=j<L_k`, and

\[
\rho_{k,j+mL_k}=\tau_{k,j,m}
\quad
(0\le j<L_k, 0\le m\le2).
\tag{15}
\]

Let

\[
e_{k,j}=X_{k,j}\bmod2
=\left[w_{k,j}+\rho_{k,j}\right]_2.
\tag{16}
\]

Inverse halving gives the exact integer update

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{k,j+1}
=\frac{\rho_{k,j}+3e_{k,j}-w_{k,j}}2
=\left[w_{k,j}-\rho_{k,j}\right]_3.
}
\tag{17}
\]

The first equality is an equality in `{0,1,2}`, not merely a congruence. The
uniform Mealy table, with entry `(e,rho')`, is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\rho& w=0&w=1\\ \hline
0&(0,0)&(1,1)\\
1&(1,2)&(0,0)\\
2&(0,1)&(1,2)
\end{array}
}
\tag{18}
\]

Starting from `rho_(k,0)=0`, one left-to-right scan of the staying word
therefore generates every `rho_(k,j)` needed in (12). At the three successive
copies of the cycle origin, the states are

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{k,0}=0,
\qquad
\rho_{k,L_k}=2,
\qquad
\rho_{k,2L_k}=1,
\qquad
\rho_{k,3L_k}=0.
}
\tag{19}
\]

The state does not reset after one old horizontal period; it traverses all
three lift digits before returning.

### 4. Exact ordered width compiler

Equations (7) and (12) give the ordered decoration lift

\[
\boxed{
d_{h,k+1,j+mL_k}
=P_{[\alpha_{k,j}-2m q_{k,j}]_3}d_{h,k,j}.
}
\tag{20}
\]

The staying word itself lifts by

\[
\boxed{
w_{k+1,j+mL_k}
=\left[w_{k,j}+
[\rho_{k,j}-m q_{k,j}]_3\right]_2.
}
\tag{21}
\]

Hence the `S_3`-valued schedule isolated in `L-9842` is not an independent
unbounded arithmetic coordinate. It is generated by:

1. the three-state transducer (18), driven by the old staying word;
2. the fixed two-state clock `q_(k,j)` from (3); and
3. the finite ternary action `P_0,P_1,P_2` on the decoration alphabet.

This supplies the exact cyclic ordering lost by the incidence-matrix
recurrence of `L-9842`.

It does **not** by itself prove full ternary-kernel finiteness. The staying
words have least cyclic period `L_k` by `L-9838`, so their lengths grow by a
factor of three at every width. Formula (20) is a three-block column lift at
positions `j,j+L_k,j+2L_k`, not an adjacent substitution on the decoration
label alone. `L-9849` subsequently proves the sharp root-level dichotomy:
each cycle is either absorbed by one finite binary core or, outside that core,
its staying word makes its width intrinsic. Thus the remaining question is
eventual absorption, not cross-width equality.

## Proof

### Primitive-root congruence and lift digits

For `k=1`, equation (8) is `2^2=4 congruent 1+3 modulo 9`. Suppose its first
congruence holds at width `k`, and write

\[
2^{L_k}=1+3^k+u3^{k+1}
\]

for an integer `u`. Since `L_(k+1)=3L_k`, cubing gives

\[
2^{L_{k+1}}
\equiv(1+3^k)^3
\equiv1+3^{k+1}
\pmod {3^{k+2}}.
\tag{22}
\]

Indeed, every remaining binomial term is divisible by `3^(k+2)`. This proves
the first congruence in (8) by induction. Since `2k>=k+1`,

\[
(1+3^k)(1-3^k)
=1-3^{2k}
\equiv1\pmod {3^{k+1}},
\tag{23}
\]

which proves the inverse congruence.

Let

\[
X=[2^{-j}]_{3^{k+1}}
=r_{k,j}+\rho_{k,j}3^k.
\]

For `m in {0,1,2}`, equation (8) gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
[2^{-(j+mL_k)}]_{3^{k+1}}
&\equiv X(1-3^k)^m\\
&\equiv r_{k,j}
+(\rho_{k,j}-m r_{k,j})3^k
\pmod {3^{k+1}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{24}
\]

Only the coefficient modulo three matters in the top digit. This is exactly
(9).

### Canonical-half correction and affine permutation

Put

\[
y=1+3^{h+1}r_{k,j},
\qquad
M=M_{h,k}.
\]

The canonical ranges give `0<y<M`. If `w_(k,j)=1`, then `y` is even and
`v=y/2`, so `zeta=0`. If `w_(k,j)=0`, then `y` is odd and the canonical
solution of `2v congruent y modulo M` is `v=(y+M)/2`, so `zeta=1`. This proves
(10). Substituting (9)--(11) into `a=[2(tau-zeta)]_3` proves (12).
Equation (3) then gives the two cases in (13).

### Three-state update

Write `r=r_(k,j mod L_k)`, `w=r mod2`, `rho=rho_(k,j)`, and
`X=r+rho3^k`. Canonical inverse halving modulo the odd moduli `3^k` and
`3^(k+1)` gives

\[
r_{k,j+1}
=\frac{r+w3^k}{2},
\qquad
X_{k,j+1}
=\frac{X+e3^{k+1}}2,
\tag{25}
\]

where `e=X mod2`. Because `3^k` is odd, `e=[w+rho]_2`. Subtracting the first
identity in (25) from the second gives

\[
X_{k,j+1}-r_{k,j+1}
=3^k\frac{\rho+3e-w}{2}.
\tag{26}
\]

The quotient on the right is the unique top digit in `{0,1,2}`. Reducing it
modulo three gives `[w-rho]_3`; the six cases are exactly table (18). This
proves (17). Equation (19) follows either by iterating the table or directly
from (9) at `j=0`, where `rho_(k,0)=0` and `q_(k,0)=1`.

### Ordered lift

Equation (20) is (7) with (12) substituted. From (4), and because `3^k` is
odd,

\[
w_{k+1,j+mL_k}
=[w_{k,j}+\tau_{k,j,m}]_2.
\tag{27}
\]

Substitution of (9) proves (21). The transducer and clock assertions now
follow directly. The final paragraph of part 4 records what these identities
do not imply, so no root-state finiteness conclusion is used. QED

## Motivation

`L-9842` reduced every width lift to a finite decoration alphabet but left an
apparently uncontrolled `S_3` ordering cocycle. The first-order primitive-root
congruence (8) shows that the three lifts are not arbitrary: they are one
affine `F_3` orbit. The only correction is the parity choice in canonical
halving, and that choice is read by a three-state machine.

This removes an arithmetic state variable from the automatic-component
frontier. `L-9849` makes the remaining structural obstruction exact: every
unabsorbed width contributes a genuinely new full horizontal cycle despite
the fixed finite transducer.

## Gap audit

- The permutation schedule is finite-state relative to the staying word, but
  `L-9849` shows that unabsorbed staying words of different widths cannot
  represent the same root sequence.
- The transducer reads `w_(k,j)`; it does not prove that `w` or `rho` is a
  function of the finite decoration label `d_(h,k,j)`.
- Column positions are separated by `L_k`; (20) is not by itself an adjacent
  constant-length substitution on one cyclic word.
- A finite generator for a family of finite words does not force absorption
  of their infinite roots into the finite core.
- Full ternary-kernel finiteness remains open, now equivalently as eventual
  absorption of all sufficiently large widths.

## Adversarial tests

- The inverse congruence has a minus sign:
  `2^(-L_k) congruent 1-3^k`. Reversing it changes (9) and the column order.
- The affine slope uses `q=r mod3`, not the staying bit `w=r mod2`.
- The top-digit state does not return to zero after one old period; its origin
  states are `0,2,1,0` across the three lifts.
- Formula (17) is an integer halving rule. The parity bit `e` is essential
  before reducing the result modulo three.
- Every permutation of three letters is abstractly affine; the content of
  (12) is its exact slope, intercept, and three-state schedule.
- Finite-state ordering does not imply cumulative root-state finiteness.
