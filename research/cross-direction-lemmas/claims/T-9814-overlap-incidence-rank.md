## 1. Statement

Fix `h>=1` adjacent corrected stages beginning at scale `m>=12`.  Write their
boundary tails as

\[
W_0,W_1,\ldots,W_h,
\qquad W_r=W_{m+r},
\tag{1}
\]

and let `mathcal S` be a finite set containing every prime divisor of these
boundaries.  Put `s=|mathcal S|`.  There are two complementary overlap
compressions.

### A. Retain all `h` stage equations

Once the `h` source words are fixed, move their fixed coefficients into the
`h` linear equations and place the coefficient-stripped 257-coordinate group
variables in one tuple in `(mathbb Q^*)^(257h)`.  All such tuples lie in a
subgroup generated by at most

\[
\boxed{1+(h+1)s}
\tag{2}
\]

directions.  The `1` is the common scale direction and the `h+1` directions
per prime are indexed by boundary vertices, not by the `2h` left/right
endpoint slots of the separate equations.  Thus for `h>=2` the generator
count is smaller than the naive direct-product count `h(2s+1)` by

\[
\boxed{(h-1)(s+1).}
\tag{3}
\]

After the exact endpoint powers of `2` and `3` are frozen as in `T-9810`, let
`mathcal F` be the remaining primes, so every `p in mathcal F` satisfies
`p>=5`, and put `f=|mathcal F|`.  The displayed fresh-prime ambient group then
has exact rank

\[
\boxed{1+(h+1)f.}
\tag{4}
\]

This exactness is a statement about the explicit ambient generators.  The
subgroup generated by actual trajectory points may have smaller rank; no
independence of endpoint values is assumed.

### B. Compose the `h` recurrences

The internal boundaries `W_1,...,W_(h-1)` telescope completely.  One obtains
one positive S-unit equation with

\[
\boxed{n_h=256h+1}
\tag{5}
\]

terms and only the two outer boundaries `W_0,W_h`.  For a fixed block word its
solution tuples lie in a subgroup of rank at most

\[
\boxed{2s+1.}
\tag{6}
\]

After signature normalization, the corresponding full fresh-prime ambient
group has exact rank

\[
\boxed{2f+1,}
\tag{7}
\]

independently of `h`.

The recurrence is additive, so (7) does not collapse to `f+1`: the left and
right outer-boundary directions are independent in the ambient tuple.  A
further reduction would require a new multiplicative relation between the
two endpoint valuation vectors.

## 2. Quantitative block bounds

Define

\[
\boxed{
C_h=(6n_h)^{3n_h}
=\{6(256h+1)\}^{3(256h+1)}.}
\tag{8}
\]

Consider `N>=h` consecutive corrected stages, with all `N+1` boundaries
retained, and let `s_N` count their distinct prime divisors, including `2`
and `3` when present.  Then

\[
\boxed{
N-h+1
\le
4^{256h}
\left\lfloor
\exp\!\left(C_h(2s_N+2)\right)
\right\rfloor.}
\tag{9}
\]

For the signature-refined version, put

\[
\boxed{K_h=3\cdot4^{256h}\cdot4=12\cdot4^{256h}.}
\tag{10}
\]

Let `mathcal F_N^+` contain the primes outside `{2,3}` dividing the
post-initial boundaries `W_(m_0+1),...,W_(m_0+N)`, and put
`f_N^+=|mathcal F_N^+|`.  Without an incoming signature at `W_(m_0)`, discard
only the first length-`h` window.  For `N>=h+1`,

\[
\boxed{
N-h
\le
K_h
\left\lfloor
\exp\!\left(C_h(2f_N^++2)\right)
\right\rfloor.}
\tag{11}
\]

If `W_(m_0)` is an incoming stabilized boundary, or merely
`1<=v_3(W_(m_0))<=3`, let `f_N` count the primes outside `{2,3}` across all
`N+1` boundaries.  Then

\[
\boxed{
N-h+1
\le
K_h
\left\lfloor
\exp\!\left(C_h(2f_N+2)\right)
\right\rfloor.}
\tag{12}
\]

For example, (12) implies

\[
f_N\ge
\max\left\{
0,
\left\lceil
{C_h^{-1}\log((N-h+1)/K_h)-2\over2}
\right\rceil
\right\}.
\tag{13}
\]

At `h=1`, equations (9), (11), and (12) are exactly the bounds of `T-9805`
and `T-9810`.  For every `h>=2`, the overlap rank is genuinely smaller, but
the available one-equation Evertse--Schlickewei--Schmidt estimate is not
numerically improved: both its dimension constant `C_h` and the block-word
count increase with `h`.  More precisely, in the universal version, if
`C_1=1542^771` and
`K_1=12*4^256`, then for every `f>=0`,

\[
h+K_h\exp(C_h(2f+2))
>
1+K_1\exp(C_1(2f+2)).
\tag{14}
\]

The initialized comparison is likewise strict after replacing the two
additive terms `h` and `1` in (14) by `h-1` and `0`.

Thus `h=1` remains the strongest direct finite-prefix bound.  The content of
the higher-block result is the exact rank compression and the identification
of the missing input: a quantitative theorem for the simultaneous system
that exploits path-incidence rank without replacing 257 variables by
`256h+1` variables.

## 3. Exact retained-system factorization

For `0<=r<h`, abbreviate the PR #3 stage factors by

\[
T_r=2^{\mathcal E_{m+r}},
\qquad
N_r=3^{\mathcal A_{m+r}},
\tag{15}
\]

and write

\[
q_{r,k}
=2^{U_{m+r,k}+\alpha_{i_{r,k}}}
3^{V_{m+r,k}+\beta_{i_{r,k}}}
\qquad(0\le k<256).
\tag{16}
\]

Then `PR #3/L-0031` is

\[
\boxed{
T_rW_{r+1}=N_rW_r+\sum_{k=0}^{255}q_{r,k}.}
\tag{17}
\]

Normalize (17) by its positive left side.  In the notation of `T-9805`, for
one fixed source word there is one scale tuple

\[
g=(g_*,g_0,\ldots,g_{255})
\in(\mathbb Q^*)^{257},
\tag{18}
\]

fixed coefficients, and one parameter

\[
D_{m+r}=2^{m+r-8}=2^rD_m.
\tag{19}
\]

For the full block, concatenate the scale tuples as

\[
\boxed{
G_h=(g,g^2,g^{2^2},\ldots,g^{2^{h-1}})
\in(\mathbb Q^*)^{257h}.}
\tag{20}
\]

For a prime `p`, retain the standard stage directions

\[
L_p=(p,1,\ldots,1),
\qquad
R_p=(p^{-1},p^{-1},\ldots,p^{-1})
\in(\mathbb Q^*)^{257}.
\tag{21}
\]

Define boundary-incidence directions in the `h`-block product by

\[
E_{p,0}=(L_p,1,\ldots,1),
\tag{22}
\]

\[
E_{p,j}
=(1,\ldots,1,
\underset{j-1}{R_p},
\underset{j}{L_p},
1,\ldots,1)
\qquad(1\le j<h),
\tag{23}
\]

and

\[
E_{p,h}=(1,\ldots,1,R_p).
\tag{24}
\]

The labels under the entries in (23) are block positions.  Divide each
normalized stage tuple by its fixed word coefficients, as in
`T-9805/(33)--(34)`, and concatenate the resulting group variables.  This
coefficient-stripped tuple has the exact form

\[
\boxed{
Z_m
=G_h^{D_m}
\prod_{p\in\mathcal S}
\prod_{j=0}^{h}
E_{p,j}^{\nu_p(W_j)}.}
\tag{25}
\]

Equation (25) is the promised sharing: an internal boundary `W_j` contributes
one direction which simultaneously acts as the denominator of stage `j-1`
and the numerator of stage `j`.  It is not represented by two independent
variables.

If one studies a family of occurrences indexed by `mathcal M` and `J_p` is
the set of relative boundary positions at which `p` actually occurs in at
least one member, the support-sensitive generator bound is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}\langle Z_m:m\in\mathcal M\rangle
\le1+\sum_p|J_p|
\le1+(h+1)s.}
\tag{26}
\]

No pairwise coprimality of the `W_j` is used.  A prime recurring at many
boundaries is carried by the corresponding incidence directions with its
actual valuations.

For fresh primes, the independence of the full displayed ambient directions
can be proved rather than assumed.  Fix `p>=5` and suppose a multiplicative
combination of `E_(p,0),...,E_(p,h)` is one.  A nonstar coordinate in block
`r` contains only the exponent from `E_(p,r+1)`, with sign minus, so the
exponents of `E_(p,1),...,E_(p,h)` vanish.  The star coordinate of block zero
then forces the exponent of `E_(p,0)` to vanish.  Distinct primes separate by
their valuations.  Finally, `G_h` is supported only at `2` and `3` and has
infinite order.  This proves (4).

## 4. Exact composition and the two-boundary group

Define

\[
\mathcal T^{[h]}=\prod_{r=0}^{h-1}T_r,
\qquad
\mathcal N^{[h]}=\prod_{r=0}^{h-1}N_r,
\tag{27}
\]

and, for `0<=r<h`,

\[
C_{r,h}
=\left(\prod_{s=0}^{r-1}T_s\right)
\left(\prod_{s=r+1}^{h-1}N_s\right),
\tag{28}
\]

with empty products equal to one.  Induction on `h` in (17) gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T^{[h]}W_h
=\mathcal N^{[h]}W_0
+\sum_{r=0}^{h-1}\sum_{k=0}^{255}C_{r,h}q_{r,k}.}
\tag{29}
\]

For `h=2`, the two toll multipliers are `N_1` and `T_0`, which checks the
orientation in (28).  Every summand in (29) is positive.

Divide by the positive left side.  The result has one endpoint term and
`256h` toll terms, proving (5) and nondegeneracy.  Every binary and ternary
exponent is affine in the single integer `D_m`: by `T-9805`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_{m+r}&=a2^rD_m+a_0,\\
\mathcal E_{m+r}&=e2^rD_m+e_0,\\
U_{m+r,k}&=u_k2^rD_m+u_{k,0},\\
V_{m+r,k}&=v_k2^rD_m+v_{k,0}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{30}
\]

Here `(a,a_0)=(687232,1792)` and
`(e,e_0)=(1082752,2816)`; the integer pairs `(u_k,u_(k,0))` and
`(v_k,v_(k,0))` are the fixed coefficients displayed in `T-9805/(23)--(26)`.

Hence, for a fixed block word, all constant exponents enter fixed rational
coefficients and all unbounded powers of `2` and `3` enter one tuple
`G_h^comp` raised to `D_m`.  The two endpoint directions in
`(mathbb Q^*)^(n_h)` are

\[
\mathcal L_p=(p,1,\ldots,1),
\qquad
\mathcal R_p=(p^{-1},p^{-1},\ldots,p^{-1}).
\tag{31}
\]

Thus every composed solution lies in

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_h^{comp}
=\left\langle
G_h^{comp},\ \mathcal L_p,\mathcal R_p:
p\in\mathcal S
\right\rangle,}
\tag{32}
\]

which proves (6) by generator count.

After extracting the exact powers of `2` and `3` from `W_0` and `W_h`, all
remaining endpoint primes are at least five.  For one such prime, a nonstar
coordinate in (31) forces the exponent of `mathcal R_p` to vanish, and the
star coordinate then forces the exponent of `mathcal L_p` to vanish.  The
scale tuple has only `2`--`3` support and infinite order; its star coordinate
has scale part

\[
2^{-e(2^h-1)}3^{a(2^h-1)}\ne1.
\tag{33}
\]

Unique factorization therefore proves the exact ambient rank (7).  This proof
does not assert that endpoint valuations vary independently along a path.

## 5. Fixed coefficients, signatures, and counting

For the unrefined equation, fixing the `h` source words fixes every coefficient
left after the endpoint values are put into (32).  There are at most
`4^(256h)` block words.

For the fresh-prime equation, the same `h` words determine the binary
valuation of `W_0`, the ternary valuation of `W_h`, and every structural
valuation at an internal boundary.  It remains only to freeze

\[
\rho=v_3(W_0)\in\{1,2,3\}
\tag{34}
\]

and the four-valued current type at `W_h`, which fixes `v_2(W_h)`.  The
chronological overlaps determine all intermediate signatures: the right type
of one stage is the first type of the next, and the preceding stage's last
source type fixes the next boundary's ternary valuation.  Consequently the
number of fixed coefficient contexts is `K_h` in (10), not
`(12*4^256)^h`.

Occurrences of one fixed context at different starting scales give distinct
solutions of (29).  Indeed, the ratio of the `k=0` and `k=1` toll coordinates
from its first stage is unchanged by the common factor `C_(0,h)` and the final
denominator.  It is exactly the scale-separating ratio of `T-9805`:

\[
{q_{0,0}\over q_{0,1}}
=2^{\alpha_{i_{0,0}}-\alpha_{i_{0,1}}
-11(t_{m,1}+1)}
3^{\beta_{i_{0,0}}-\beta_{i_{0,1}}
+7(t_{m,1}+1)}.
\tag{35}
\]

Multiplicative independence of `2` and `3` and strict growth of
`t_(m,1)=2^m+2^(m-8)` separate the scales.

Apply the quantitative Evertse--Schlickewei--Schmidt bound imported in
`T-9805` to the positive `n_h`-term equation.  With rank at most `2s+1`, one
fixed word occurs at most

\[
\left\lfloor\exp(C_h(2s+2))\right\rfloor
\tag{36}
\]

times.  There are `N-h+1` overlapping length-`h` windows, so summing (36)
over the block words proves (9).  The same argument with exact signatures and
fresh-prime rank `2f+1` proves (12).  If the initial boundary lacks (34), the
windows starting at stages `m_0+1,...,m_0+N-h` remain; there are `N-h` of
them, and their endpoints are all covered by `mathcal F_N^+`.  This proves
(11).  Integer inversion proves (13). **QED**

## What this advances

- The endpoint variables of adjacent stages have the incidence geometry of a
  path: one prime direction per boundary vertex, rather than two directions
  per stage edge.
- Composing the positive recurrences removes all internal endpoint words and
  leaves rank `2f+1` independently of block length.
- The signature context also shares across stages.  Only the initial ternary
  and final binary signatures remain external to the `h` source words.
- The theorem identifies why this structural saving does not improve the
  present explicit fresh-prime budget: the only imported quantitative theorem
  is a one-equation result whose constant grows sharply with the term count.

## Gap and scope audit

- Equation (29) is a consequence of the simultaneous system, not an
  equivalence: it forgets the internal positive integer boundaries.  This is
  harmless for an upper count because every genuine block maps to a composed
  solution, but (29) cannot construct a block.
- The exact rank statements concern explicit ambient groups.  Actual endpoint
  tuples may generate smaller subgroups; no independence, pairwise
  coprimality, or genericity of the `W_j` is assumed.
- The additive recurrence imposes no proved multiplicative linear relation on
  `(nu_p(W_0),nu_p(W_h))`.  The two outer directions in (31) therefore cannot
  be merged using the audited source claims alone.
- Projection of the incidence group (25) onto any one 257-coordinate stage
  block retains its two endpoint directions and has the same safe fresh rank
  `2f+1` as `T-9810`.  Consequently, applying the imported theorem to just one
  equation cannot see the global saving in (4).
- Applying ESS separately to the `h` equations ignores (4).  The direct
  one-equation composition (29) uses `256h+1` terms, and its larger dimension
  makes the resulting numerical bound worse than `h=1`.
- The theorem assumes an ordinary corrected-stage block.  It supplies no cap
  stitch, infinite grammar, fresh-prime location, or Collatz counterexample.
