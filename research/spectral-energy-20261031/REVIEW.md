# Adversarial self-audit

**Same-author review. Not independent mathematical certification.**

1. The ordinary map is unabsorbed; the killed finite set is arbitrary and
   explicit. The witnesses avoid any such set before their final visit to 1.
2. The corridor construction is an equality over positive integers, not a
   claim that finite 2-adic compatibility yields an infinite ordinary orbit.
3. The lifting proof was tightened before publication: for ANY integer v,
   `(-1+3v)^2-(-1+3v)+1=3(1-3v+3v^2)` has valuation exactly one.
   No inadmissible restriction on v is needed in its induction.
4. The terminal coefficient vanishes only for `s<1`. At `s=1`, two corridors
   with matched outgoing coefficients are subtracted. Omitting this
   cancellation would invalidate the interior-disk proof.
5. Complex approximate eigenvectors are allowed. Positive-only approximate
   vectors would not prove the whole complex spectrum.
6. Disjoint support and normalized l1 norm give pairwise distance two. A
   Fredholm parametrix would force precompactness, establishing the stated
   essential-spectrum convention. This is not an assertion about all other
   inequivalent definitions of essential spectrum.
7. Negative s uses a power bound with a fixed factor `2^-s`; it does NOT use
   the one-step bound valid for nonnegative s. For s>1, parents of a retained
   fixed unit target prove unboundedness even if 1 is killed.
8. Slowly varying factors give a lower bound when the operator is bounded;
   boundedness and a complete spectrum are not asserted for all such factors.
9. In the Haar calculation the odometer identity is valid for complex L2
   functions; positivity is used separately for the quantitative energy
   increment. The finite law has mean one at every depth.
10. The fixed-point enclosure sums the geometric tail through a cyclic
    resolvent. It does not replace an infinite exponent sum by a cutoff.
    Exact low-depth rational laws test the rounding implementation.
11. All 14-level energy bounds are finite-depth facts. Linear energy growth,
    an ordinary trace theorem, and forward-survivor exclusion remain open.
12. Existing harmonic-spectrum and positive-overlap identities are credited.
    No independent search establishes literature-wide novelty of the new
    extensions. No external Lean dependency chain was replayed.

The finite test suite also retains negative lambda residuals and the
critical paired cancellation; it is not restricted to easy positive-real
outer-radius examples. The theorem remains a proposed written proof, not a
consequence of these bounded tests alone.
