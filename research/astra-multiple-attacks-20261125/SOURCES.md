## Arithmetic and symbolic inputs

Schlickewei's finite-place Subspace Theorem is the main input to the uniform carry and bounded-part discrepancy proofs. The particular formulation with coordinate forms except at one chosen place is reproduced in **Kebis, Luca, Ouaknine, Scoones and Worrell, Theorem 2.1**. The exact positive ordinary affine equation supplies the small dyadic form; its other finite-place factors retain the entire outside-prime numerator part. The deduction is written mathematics, not an invocation of an unproved Lean axiom.

- [Schlickewei, *On linear forms with algebraic coefficients and Diophantine equations* (1977)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-314X(77)90073-7).
- [Kebis et al., *Transcendence for Pisot morphic words over an algebraic base* (2026), primary paper](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~joel/publications/transcendence_pisot_morphic_words26.pdf). Theorem 2.1 provides the number-theoretic interface. Theorems 4.1–4.2, Lemma 4.4 with equations (4.3)–(4.4), and the proof of Theorem 7.1 provide the balanced-pair lift. Arbitrary-alphabet Pisot substitutions retain the paper's pure discrete spectrum hypothesis. No general Pisot conjecture is assumed. The paper's complex-base transcendence conclusion is not substituted for a Collatz statement.
- [Kebis et al., ICALP 2024, article 144](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2024.144), especially the rotation and Tribonacci constructions. The present use preserves physical block length and odd count under arbitrary fixed nonerasing encodings.
- [Evertse, Schlickewei and Schmidt, *Linear equations in variables which lie in a multiplicative group*, Annals of Mathematics 155 (2002), 807–836](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409604), rank definition and Theorem 1.1. The division hull of the group generated by two and three has rank two in that definition, and its Cartesian square has rank four. Finiteness of its two-term unit-equation solutions supplies a fixed complex exceptional set in [SINGLE_RANK_RESULTANT.md](synthesis/SINGLE_RANK_RESULTANT.md). The resulting onset is not computed.

The outside-prime carry mass is a lower bound. It does not imply that the full lap determinant has a surviving prime after two complementary carries are added. The proper-arc gcd theorem and literal rational controls preserve that distinction.

## Fourier and density inputs

- [Leen, *Exact rates in Collatz synchronization*, Theorem 2.11](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23268751), used through its uniform counted-pair survival bound and exact finite-residue realization. The displayed transfer statement and attribution in [Weaver, Paper 13, equation (1)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/13) were read directly. The input concerns actual adjacent starts over complete binary periods. The new proof derives the uniform frequency estimate from its ordinary variation consequence.
- [Wu and Wang, *On the irrationality measure of log 3*, Theorem 1 (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2014.03.007). The primary indexed theorem gives exponent $4.1163051+\epsilon$ for the three-term linear form; setting its constant coefficient to zero permits the exponent 4.2 used here. The publisher's full-page endpoint was inaccessible on a later open. The argument does not rely on a conjectural Diophantine exponent.
- [Mazur, *Positive Lower Density of Collatz Predecessors*, manuscript and verification statement](https://www.proofatlas.ai/formalizations/positive-lower-density-collatz-predecessors/). Only the positive lower density for each **fixed** positive target prime to three is needed for the three-target count and the quantitative Fejér lower bound. The constants may depend on that target. The three external Lean roots were not rebuilt in this pass.
