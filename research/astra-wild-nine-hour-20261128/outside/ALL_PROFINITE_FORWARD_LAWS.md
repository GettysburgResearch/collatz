# The full forward-time classification when only binary equality is relaxed

**PROPOSED, written theorem.** For the continuous shortcut map \(T\) on \(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\), let
\[
 \mathcal U=\{z:z_3\in\mathbb Z_3^\times\},\qquad
 g(\mu)=\mu\{z_2\ {\rm odd}\}\log3-\log2.
\]
The following three conditions on a Borel probability \(\mu\) are necessary for any subsequential empirical limit of a genuinely escaping positive ordinary orbit: \(T_*\mu=\mu\), \(\mu(\mathcal U)=1\), and \(g(\mu)\ge0\). **They are sufficient in the relaxation that drops only the initial binary equality.** More precisely, for every such \(\mu\), every fixed positive ordinary \(x\) and every requested \(N\ge0\), choose a single infinite binary script with the following properties:

* The real trajectory begins at exactly \(x\), stays positive and tends to infinity. At each odd prime its state is always the image of that very same dyadic rational. The binary coordinate is one fixed \(2\)-adic initial point, following its true branch at every tick. Its first \(N\) parities agree with those of \(x\) and its next parity differs.
* The profinite time averages converge weakly to \(\mu\) at all cutoffs, and the real rate is \(\lim_{t\to\infty}\log(x_t)/t=g(\mu)\). This includes arbitrary nonergodic and nonatomic \(\mu\), and critical \(g(\mu)=0\) with real escape.
* The binary point can be made irrational. Simple true positive ordinary segments above a growing floor realize the same finite-modulus limiting distributions when their initial integers vary with the horizon.

The [purely atomic case](PURE_ATOMIC_PROFINITE_LAWS.md) further identifies all such atomic laws as mixtures of signed nonzero integer cycles. Here the approximation of a general invariant law uses its own actual profinite orbit segments. Its states need not be rational.

## Mixed-modulus steering with a prescribed ternary unit

For \(m\ge1\) and \(M\ge1\) coprime to six, every residue modulo \(3^mM\) whose ternary digit is nonzero is reachable from **every** residue by some word in \(F_0(z)=z/2,\ F_1(z)=(3z+1)/2\). The input and all divisions here are in the odd modulus. A uniform length upper bound is \(M-1+m+2\cdot3^{m-1}\).

To reach \(b\bmod 3^m\), first plan the suffix \(1^m0^a\). The \(m\) odd letters send every \(z\) to \(-1\bmod3^m\) because \(F_1(z)+1=(3/2)(z+1)\). Choose \(0\le a<2\cdot3^{m-1}\) with \(-2^{-a}\equiv b\bmod3^m\). For completeness, 2 has order two modulo three and \(v_3(2^{2\cdot3^j}-1)=j+1\) by the binomial expansion at \(4=1+3\). Thus its powers run through all ternary units. The suffix is invertible modulo \(M\). Before using it, steer to the unique \(M\)-preimage of the desired final residue, in at most \(M-1\) letters by the [finite commutator argument](PURE_ATOMIC_PROFINITE_LAWS.md#steering-the-physical-residue-coordinates). The suffix erases any ternary side effect of this preliminary steering. This is deterministic existence at each fixed finite precision, with exact iterates of one dyadic rational at all odd places.

## Finite prescribed orbit segments approximate every invariant law

For \(m\ge5\), set \(D_m=\prod_{p\le m}p^m\), \(M_m=\prod_{5\le p\le m}p^m\) and \(U_m=3^mM_m\). Fix a reference odd-place residue \(1\bmod U_m\). Suppose first \(\rho\) is invariant, gives full mass to \(\mathcal U\), and has **strictly** positive \(g(\rho)\). Given \(\epsilon>0\) we construct a finite word \(W\) returning the reference odd-place residue to itself. In every copy started at that odd-place residue, the full profinite empirical distribution modulo \(D_m\) is within \(\epsilon\) in total variation of \(\rho\bmod D_m\); the word's mean letter increment is positive.

For a large integer \(L\), partition by the finite records of the first \(L\) orbit states modulo \(D_m\), selecting one actual profinite representative in \(\mathcal U\) from each positive-mass record. Assign its record mass as weight. The mean of the record histograms is exactly \(\rho\bmod D_m\) by invariance. Replace the record weights by nonnegative rational weights summing to one, with total variation error as small as desired. The size of this finite set and its common denominator need no uniform bound.

Use the associated integer multiplicities. For each occurrence of a record, first steer the current odd residue to the representative's initial residue modulo \(U_m\), then replay its next \(L+m\) genuine binary letters. The first \(L\) resulting joint states agree with the selected record. At every odd prime this follows from the shared starting congruence. At two there are at least \(m\) matching future letters, which prescribe \(m\) binary digits. Finish by steering back to \(1\bmod U_m\). Every connecting word has length at most the mixed-modulus bound above, independent of \(L\) and of the number of records. Thus the fraction of unprescribed dates can be made arbitrarily small by first choosing \(L\) large relative to the bound and \(m\), then approximating the finite weights. This includes the last resetting word. The odd bit is observable modulo \(D_m\), so make the total variation error smaller than both \(\epsilon\) and \(g(\rho)/(2\log3)\).

For the target \(\mu\), use \(\rho_m=(1-1/m)\mu+(1/m)\delta_{-1}\), an invariant ternary-unit law of strictly positive drift. Take one returning word \(W_m\) with error at most \(1/m\) at \(D_m\). These words have positive total increments and their measured laws at every fixed quotient approach the target.

## One fixed positive real start and all cutoffs

Begin by replaying the requested \(N\) parities of the given integer \(x\), then apply the wrong letter. In each stage insert \(m\) zero letters and \(m\) one letters, use mixed-modulus steering to its reference residue, then repeat \(W_m\) many times. The entrances have uniformly bounded finite length \(E_m\) at each stage. The concatenation proof in the [pure-atomic construction](PURE_ATOMIC_PROFINITE_LAWS.md#concatenation-every-time-convergence-and-critical-escape) uses only the following two properties of the stage words: their total increments are positive, and all full copies from the reference residue have the stated approximation independently of the subsequent script. Both were just proved. Choose enough copies to dominate the next entrance and the next incomplete word in both elapsed time and accumulated log growth. That proof gives convergence of the empirical law at every cutoff, real escape even at zero limiting drift, exact real exponential rate, and the option of imposing the repetition lengths that force an irrational binary initial coordinate. The proof of [simple positive finite witnesses](PURE_ATOMIC_PROFINITE_LAWS.md#finite-positive-witnesses) applies word for word via CRT.

Necessity for the true ordinary diagonal follows by fixed-time telescoping of bounded continuous tests, the ternary-unit property after the first odd input, and the log-height identity on a positive escape. These arguments do not assume a rate of ordinary equidistribution or a stationary predecessor law. In particular a proof based solely on the class of unmarked profinite stationary laws, fixed precision at the binary start, arbitrary odd-place precision, and the sign of the real drift must still distinguish the exact binary integer. The construction never satisfies that last equality.

The concatenation of representative orbit segments is the classical generic-point idea in specification theory. Sigmund's framework constructs generic points for invariant measures under its specification hypotheses. The contribution here is the explicit mixed-modulus connection with a prescribed positive rational at every odd place, the one deliberately different binary coordinate, the critical positive escape reserve, and the finite simple ordinary witnesses. No periodic specification property of the whole profinite Collatz map is needed or asserted. Actual periodic points of that whole map must be signed ordinary cycles.
