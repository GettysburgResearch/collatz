## The exact missing inference

For every prefix-closed ordinary architecture, let

```text
S_n = positive seeds legal through depth n,
m_n = min S_n.
```

The missing implication is not a mysterious form of compactness. It is exactly:

```text
there exists B such that S_n contains a seed <=B for every n.
```

By `T-7401`, this is equivalent to one fixed seed surviving all depths. Without `B`, compatible finite roots may escape to infinity while converging to a perfectly valid nonordinary `2`-adic point.

`R-7401` shows that even the following do not manufacture the bound:

```text
exact raw Collatz parity prefixes,
infinitely many positive roots at every depth,
a compact perfect path set,
positive entropy,
positive 2-adic dimension,
uniform supercritical odd density,
and conditional exponential growth.
```

Therefore no combination of these common local properties can complete ordinary extraction.

## What is merely a reformulation

The least-root theorem is exact and indispensable, but it is elementary. Saying

```text
an ordinary survivor exists iff the least finite-depth roots stay bounded
```

is not an architecture-specific breakthrough. It is a correctness boundary.

It becomes new progress only when a source-specific theorem proves one of:

```text
sup_n m_n < infinity,
```

or

```text
m_n -> infinity.
```

Likewise, changing from residues to carries, stacks, logarithmic coordinates, rooms, transported digits, or quotient markers is not progress toward existence unless the new coordinate yields one of those conclusions.

## What is genuinely weaker than Collatz

The logical direction must be stated accurately.

### Negative strict-subsystem target

Proving

```text
m_n -> infinity
```

for one fixed architecture eliminates that complete architecture. This is genuinely weaker than proving Collatz true because other Collatz counterexample mechanisms remain possible.

### Positive strict-subsystem target

Proving boundedness and extracting a survivor in one fixed architecture is not logically weaker than “Collatz is false.” It is a **stronger structured sufficient condition**. Its value is that it immediately yields an explicit `K-####` object after replay.

### Cycle target

Producing one word with

```text
D=2^A-3^k>0,
D|C,
n=C/D>0,
```

and replaying every valuation is also a stronger structured sufficient condition. Proving no solution in a strict compiler class is a weaker negative theorem eliminating that class.

## The cycle-side missing inference

The cycle funnel has a different blocker and must not be conflated with ordinary compactness.

The recurrent invalid step is:

```text
C is divisible by many factors of D
+ C/D is close to an integer
=> D divides C.
```

The valid global inference is a height contradiction for

\[
R=C-ND.
\]

One must prove either `R=0` directly or that a nonzero `R` is divisible by more finite-place mass than its Archimedean size permits. PR #50's mixed-place gate is therefore a real reduction. Proper-factor hits that remain far below the gate are compiler tests, not near-counterexamples in the proof-theoretic sense.

## Research stopping rule

A future contribution should be classified as changing the full-objective state only if it does at least one of the following:

1. bounds or forces escape of a canonical least-root sequence for a complete strict architecture;
2. gives one explicit forever-defined ordinary seed with exact replay;
3. proves full-denominator equality or a full-height contradiction for one cycle word;
4. eliminates every member of a genuinely infinite, exhaustively specified construction class;
5. refutes a load-bearing theorem and repairs the dependency graph.

The following, without one of those outputs, should be labeled infrastructure or family-specific narrowing:

```text
another long prefix,
another inverse-limit encoding,
another conditional growth bound,
another entropy or dimension estimate,
another SCC or modular lasso,
another proper denominator factor,
or another structured directive exclusion.
```
