# Dependency boundary for the true-gate sampling results

The strict future parity gate is an actual, decidable property of the supplied
positive integer. The new sampling theorems concern its true future. Their
ordinary counting limits nevertheless depend on the physical density and
bridge interfaces already stated in this packet. They are source-qualified
deductions, not new unconditional proofs of those imported interfaces.

## Elementary native part

[The exact parity-record bound](../cycles/EXACT_PARITY_RECORD_HORIZON.md) gives
a finite deadline for the lifetime minimum of the native grade. Its finite
coverage is in `GlobalParityMinimum.lean`. The new
[strict-gate cocycle](StrictGateCocycle.lean) derives attained suffix floors,
unit increases at strict future minima and the actual telescoping identity.
The [return budget](GateReturnBudget.lean) proves the complete native product
bound and its consequence `6^q ≤ n·2^a·5^q` under the stated finite path and
clock premises. A completed gate-return prefix supplies those premises in
the written argument. The independent-excursion total-variation lower bound
in [the supercritical note](SUPERCRITICAL_TRUE_GATE_RETURNS.md) is elementary
and does not assume a component density or a mixing theorem.

## Imported ternary estimates and new extension

[Weaver–Wren48, §8, equations (60)–(69)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/48)
supplies primitive decay with a small fractional depth deficit and the
counting-norm joint offset/total estimate. Root read that precise section.
The sharp-gate proof extends the pairing argument to a bounded test of all
adjacent pair totals, then retains the minimum on even dates. Its proof
includes the prefix collision bound, the extra two-dimensional mark sum,
the even-depth telescope and the physical normalization. This extension
is written here; it is not merely asserted to follow from a scalar mixing
statement.

[Weaver9, Proposition13 and AppendixB](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/9)
provide the finer-modulus collision inequality and the unrestricted moments
used in the weaker two-sided source theorem. The pointwise floor in that
weaker theorem follows from
[Weaver211, TheoremA.1](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/211), which
expressly assumes its stated joint estimate (J). The sharp source law uses
the marginal finite-trit approximation and the actual output-mark limit.
All primary formulas above were read directly. The entire external analytic
packages have not been independently formalized or rebuilt in this pass.

## What the laws do and do not identify

[Sharp gate sampling](SHARP_TRUE_GATE_SAMPLING.md) retains the actual output
deficit and gives the exact ordinary coefficient in every large octave.
[The return-word law](TRUE_GATE_PALM_WORDS.md) follows the actual future from
the sampled gate, including a growing number of complete words below the
square-root-logarithmic scale. [The supercritical experiment](SUPERCRITICAL_TRUE_GATE_RETURNS.md)
retains the terminal mark jointly with the deficit before conditioning on
the rare event. Its survival limit is the density outside the basin of one.
None of these identities supplies an independent upper bound of zero for
that survival probability. An individual retained escaping ray is not
resampled according to the source law.
