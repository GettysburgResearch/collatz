# Raw and corrected triple products on an actual escaping component

Suppose $m$ is a specified positive odd unit on an escaping ray $a_i=U^im$, $i\ge0$, and write $d_m>0$ for its true natural predecessor density. Put $F(v)=\prod_{j\ge0}3U^jv/(3U^jv+1)$ and $P(v)=v/F(v)$ on this escaping component. At every edge $P(Uv)=3\,2^{-e(v)}P(v)$, $e(v)=v_2(3v+1)$. A source $n$ is an ordinary odd uniformly sampled in $[X,2X]$. Independent samples always have this original counting law.

Let $N=\log X$ and choose $L=\lceil K\sqrt N\rceil$ with a sufficiently large fixed $K=K_m$. There exist arbitrarily late intervals $[b,b+L]$ of odd dates on the specified ray, and a common observation clock $t=t(X,b)$, with the following properties for all sufficiently large $X$. Retain just sources that first hit $m$ and whose position at clock $t$ lies on the specified window **after that hit**. Their unnormalized probability is at least $d_m/2$. Among six independent retained sources the unnormalized probability that the two corrected triple products agree is $\gg_m L^{-2}$, whereas the unnormalized probability that the two raw triple products agree is $O_m(L^{-3})$. Every corrected equality gives a pair of raw triple products at log distance at most $2L/H$, $H=\min_{b\le i\le b+L}a_i$. The observation concerns the odd map and compares products of the six observed integer waypoints, not products of their initial source labels.

Indeed the centered first odd grade $J$ at $m$, conditioned on $B_m$, has a fixed nondegenerate Gaussian limit at scale $\sqrt N$. Select $t=b+\lfloor N/D+L/2\rfloor$, $D=\log(4/3)$. On $B_m$ the observed post-hit ray index is $t-J$ when nonnegative; all central grades giving $b\le t-J\le b+L$ are retained. Large $K$ supplies the mass bound. Each individual index has original mass $O_m(1/\sqrt N)=O_m(1/L)$. In a given grade the first-hit crop $[X,2X]$ permits only $O(1)$ total division clocks, because $p=m2^s/3^j$ lies between $X$ and $2X/c_0$, with $c_0$ the uniform simple-path survival floor. Each such row has $O(X/\sqrt N)$ physical integers by the uniform exact row bound. There are arbitrarily late length-$L$ windows with total gap at most $2L$. Otherwise all sufficiently late blocks of that fixed length have sum greater than $2L$, contradicting the product identity $\sum_{i<n} e(a_i)\log2=n\log3+\log(m/a_n)+B_{\infty}(m;[0,n))\le n\log3+O_m(1)$. Here the accumulated correction is bounded on the distinct ray. Since $a_i\to\infty$, one can also demand $H>2^{8(L+1)^2}$ and any higher prescribed threshold. On such a window each corrected product is $P(a_b)^3 3^u2^{-s}$, with $0\le u\le3L$ and $0\le s\le6L$. Hence the distribution of three independent retained corrected factors, of mass at least $(d_m/2)^3$, has only $O(L^2)$ possible values. Its squared atom sum is $\gg_m L^{-2}$ by Cauchy–Schwarz.

On the other hand the raw odd integers in this window form a multiplicative $B_3$ set: equal products of three, allowing repetitions, have the same index multiset. The mass of any triple product is at most $6(O_m(1/L))^3$; summing its square gives the asserted upper. The unrestricted $B_3$ theorem is credited to the [odd segment product rigidity proof, PR213 checkpoint 9 at b710ae3c](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/b710ae3c0bf5c764b8ee575c7c2898156d5f7451/research/astra-global-rigidity-20261128/cycles/UNRESTRICTED_TRIPLE_PRODUCTS.md). Finally $\log F(a_i)-\log F(a_b)$ has magnitude at most $L/(3H)$ throughout the window. The six common reference terms cancel when the two corrected triples agree, giving the displayed $2L/H$ error. Positive near-collisions at this scale are compatible with exact raw separation. No independent upper bound on these near-collisions is supplied by the raw product theorem.
