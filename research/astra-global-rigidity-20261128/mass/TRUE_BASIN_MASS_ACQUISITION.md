# When an ordinary source acquires a fixed fraction of all predecessors

**Source-qualified written deduction.** The natural basin densities, fixed-target clock and bridge, component tightness, and canonical inverse information theorem are the explicitly cited analytic inputs. The complete theorem is not formalized in Lean.

Let $U$ be the positive odd Collatz map, $B_a$ the odd sources visiting $a$, and $d(a)$ its relative odd natural density. This equals the all-positive predecessor density at odd targets. Put $D=\log(4/3)$ and $v=2(\log2)^2/D^3$. Write $y_i=U^i n$ for an ordinary odd source uniformly distributed in $[Y,2Y]$, and $N=\log Y$. The observable at each date is the infinite, true predecessor density $d(y_i)$. It is nondecreasing along every path by inclusion of predecessor sets. For a component $C$ let $p_C$ denote its ordinary density, with components defined by intersection of forward paths, allowing different clocks.

## The law centered at an actual hit

Fix a nonperiodic odd ternary unit $a$ and condition on $B_a$. Let $J$ be the first odd grade at $a$. Let $(X_j)_{j\ge0}$ follow the natural reverse entrance law $Q_a$, with $X_0=a$. For integers $k$, define the increasing two-sided profile
\[
 M_a(k)=\begin{cases}d(X_{-k}),& k\le0,\\d(U^ka),&k\ge0.\end{cases}
\]
There is joint convergence
\[
 \left((J-N/D)/\sqrt N,\;(d(y_{J+k}))_{k\in\mathbb Z}\right)
                  \ \Longrightarrow\ (Z,M_a),\qquad Z\sim N(0,v),        \tag{1}
\]
with $Z$ **independent of the entire profile**. Give the profile its product topology and use arbitrary bounded values at negative actual dates. Its limits as $k\to-\infty$ and $k\to+\infty$ are zero and $p_C$, respectively, for the component $C$ of $a$.

The fixed-target odd-grade CLT is the intermediate grade calculation in the [natural source clock proof](FIXED_BASIN_NATIVE_WINDOW.md), with its signed first-visit kernels, marked bridge and actual proportional source crop. Its grade density there is proportional to $\exp[-(2-\log_2 3)^2 D b^2/4]$, which gives the stated $v$. At fixed reverse depth $l$, an individual unit word ending at $x$ has natural conditional probability tending to $d(x)/d(a)$. Indeed the entire event, apart from starts too short to supply the word, is precisely $B_x$, and on this event $J=J_x+l$. At this nonperiodic target distinct depth-$l$ basins are disjoint and the limiting masses sum to one. Thus the labels converge on the entire countable partition in total variation, including the too-short cemetery state. Conditional on any fixed positive-mass cylinder the same Gaussian follows from the fixed target $x$ by a fixed clock shift. Finite truncation of the partition proves independence from any prescribed finite prefix. The profiles are coordinatewise bounded; finite-coordinate convergence gives (1) on the compact countable product. The cylinder probability on a realized infinite $Q_a$ path is exactly $d(X_l)/d(a)$. The natural inverse information theorem gives $-\log(d(X_l)/d(a))=Dl+O_\omega(\sqrt{l\log\log l})$ almost surely, hence the negative tail tends to zero. In a cyclic component the basin of any cycle vertex is the full component. In an escaping component the sets $B_{U^i a}$ increase, and their densities tend to $p_C$. For the latter assertion, nearly all sources have their attained minimum in a fixed finite set of seeds, by the ordinary minimum tightness theorem. Every such seed in $C$ merges with the ray of $a$ by a fixed finite date on that ray. Take this finite maximum and then remove the omitted minimum mass. This proves the positive-tail statement, avoiding countable additivity of natural density without tightness. For a fixed $0<\delta<p_C$, put $T_\delta(n)=\inf\{i\ge0:d(y_i)\ge\delta\}$. On $B_a$ it is finite. Its displacement $T_\delta-J$ converges on the integers to $\min\{k:M_a(k)\ge\delta\}$, jointly for every finite list of such thresholds, independently in the limit of the displayed Gaussian clock. The displacement is therefore $O_{\mathbb P}(1)$, a statement at the native *unscaled* odd clock. To prove it, choose $K$ with $d(U^Ka)>\delta$ and $Q_a(d(X_K)\ge\delta)$ arbitrarily small. At fixed depth $K$ use total variation of the countable label partition; threshold equality causes no continuity loss. On its complement the offset is a function of the finite profile. Send $K$ to infinity. If a fixed list is considered, use its smallest and largest thresholds for these two truncations.

## A component mixture at the deterministic critical clock

At any finite list of fixed real $z$, put $j_z=\lfloor N/D+z\sqrt N\rfloor$. For an unconditioned uniform odd source the joint limit is
\[
            \big(C(n),(d(y_{j_z}))_z\big)
       \ \Longrightarrow\ \big(C,(p_C\,1_{\{Z<z\}})_z\big),            \tag{2}
\]
where $\Pr(C=C_0)=p_{C_0}$ and $Z\sim N(0,v)$ is independent of $C$. In particular, for $q>0$ the limiting mean of $d(y_{j_z})^q$ is $\Pr(Z<z)\sum_C p_C^{q+1}$. This is a native finite observation time with the full, infinite predecessor density as its observable. It is a limiting law for that density, without a finite certificate for computing it at the observed state.

On $B_a$ with nonperiodic $a$, outside $|j_z-J|\le K$ one compares the density by monotonicity with $d(y_{J-K})$ or $d(U^Ka)$. For a fixed finite list of $z$, the omitted central bands have probability tending to zero for each fixed $K$ by continuity of the Gaussian limit. Positive and negative profile tails from (1), first choosing $K$ large, put the density arbitrarily close to zero or $p_C$. This proves (2) on each such basin. In an escaping component exhaust its density by the increasing fixed nonperiodic basins along a single ray as above.

For a cyclic component fix an odd cycle target $a$ and a depth $l$ larger than its primitive odd period. On first visits to $a$, all incoming depth-$l$ vertices are nonperiodic: an initial vertex on the cycle would reach $a$ first within a single lap. These fixed nonperiodic precursor basins are disjoint and exhaust the component in density. Here is a direct tightness justification for this last assertion. In the physically cropped central first-visit rows of the [natural clock proof](FIXED_BASIN_NATIVE_WINDOW.md), the first $l$ outward letters form a tight countable partition, uniformly on compact standardized totals. This follows from the [pinned first-visit bridge, section “Initial history and real offset”](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/5ac2b4a7ab9fee19d2e1b1c2e4aadecd08413128/research/astra-diagonal-frontier-20261127/inverse/pinned_root_bridge.md), including its primitive-lap exclusion. Given a small tolerance, omit all but finitely many such words under the common limiting probability. A physical interval crop only decreases their omitted absolute row count. In the central range there are $O_B(\sqrt N)$ possible cells of at most $O(Y/\sqrt N)$ sources each, since $p=a2^s/3^j\asymp Y$ and $s$ has boundedly many possibilities for each $j$. The noncentral part is arbitrarily small by the signed-kernel renewal-strip estimate. Fix its grade cutoff $B$ first, then its finite prefix truncation. Short initial sources with $J<l$ lie in the same negligible grade tail. This proves the density exhaustion. The finite-word argument at periodic targets thus requires no artificial completely invariant replacement for $B_a$. Now sum (2) on this component from its disjoint nonperiodic precursors. Across components the finite minimum-seed approximation bounds the omitted ordinary mass, and $\sum_C p_C=1$. This proves (2) including the sample component.

## Structure of the fixed-mass superlevel set

For $0<\delta\le1$ define $V_\delta=\{y\text{ positive odd}:d(y)\ge\delta\}$. It is an actual forward-invariant set. Weaver 202/v1 §6 already proves containment in finitely many forward trajectories by a uniform ancestor-label tail. The refinement here supplies at most \(\lfloor1/\delta\rfloor\) starting gates that themselves lie in the superlevel set. [Prior finite containment](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/202). It is the union of at most $\lfloor1/\delta\rfloor$ ordinary forward orbits whose starting vertices already belong to $V_\delta$.

For a nonperiodic vertex $a\in V_\delta$, at any fixed inverse grade its predecessors in $V_\delta$ have disjoint basins of density at least $\delta$, so there are at most $1/\delta$ of them. There is no infinite reverse path in $V_\delta$ beginning at $a$: its nested cylinders under $Q_a$ would have mass at least $\delta/d(a)$ at every depth, giving a positive atom, contrary to the inverse information theorem. Finite branching then gives a finite inverse subtree in $V_\delta$ at every nonperiodic vertex. Consequently every such vertex descends in forward time from a minimal vertex of $V_\delta$, one having no earlier unit predecessor in $V_\delta$. Minimal vertices in the nonperiodic part have pairwise disjoint basins, since otherwise one lies before the other on a common path. For each qualifying isolated cycle with no incoming nonperiodic vertex of $V_\delta$, add one representative cycle vertex. Its full component basin is disjoint from all the other chosen basins. Each chosen basin has density at least $\delta$, so finite addition gives the stated bound, while their forward orbits cover all of $V_\delta$. A cycle supplied by an incoming minimal ray needs no extra representative. This structural bound covers original physical sites and arbitrary heights, but a single infinite escaping ray can occupy one of its allowed forward orbits.
