## The simultaneous divisors and true returns

At a fixed physical base $x$, equality of the first $L$ parities determines its residue modulo $2^L$ by backwards cancellation of 2 and then cancellation of the unit 3 on odd branches. Conversely, varying $x$ by $2^Lk$ leaves the first $L$ parities unchanged. Through any $j\le L$ matched steps the difference evolves multiplicatively, giving the exact coefficient $2^{L-j}3^{q_j}$ on the *same* free quotient $k$. Taking $k=1$ simultaneously attains every stated coefficient. With any two quotient columns, the two centered row-products therefore agree. Multiplying the pair differences also verifies the written Vandermonde identity for increasing columns. A base-height constraint at each cut handles the zero quotient and prevents truncated subtraction from falsely certifying a cap below the base. These results concern points above the nominated base on one cylinder.

The echo at displacement $d$ implies equality modulo $2^h$. The stated upper height bound puts both positive values inside that modulus, so their equality gives an actual period. At $h=d$ the ratio $(4/3)^d$ beats the fixed base. In particular arbitrarily long square prefixes from one physical point imply periodicity. Dubickas explicitly states that *every* Sturmian word begins with arbitrarily long squares, so applying this argument at the start of any alleged Sturmian tail conflicts with its aperiodicity. The imported combinatorial assertion is external to the Lean module. [Dubickas, primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/ita/2009005)

## The all-shifts real-affine construction

I checked the quantifiers for the independent random bits in the prescribed mask $11b_0\,11b_1\cdots$. For a lag not divisible by three, each of the at least $\lfloor h/3\rfloor$ free positions in the first window must be 1. For a lag divisible by three the necessary equalities give edges between distinct free positions and their translates by a fixed positive distance. The edges form disjoint finite paths even when the windows overlap. Their number gives the exponent of the equality probability. The geometric union bound for $H=\lceil72+(t+d)/24\rceil$ is at most $2^{-23}r/(1-r)^2$, $r=2^{-1/72}<255/256$. This is less than $1/128$. Thus a set of words of measure exceeding $127/128$ has no echo reaching its pair-specific cutoff. The estimate is uniform in shifts and lags and independent of the nominal seed.

The slope of every full block is at least $9/8$, and remainders starting at the fixed origin contain only ones. Across windows starting in any phase the leftover one or two bits have slope at least $1/2$, because there are no two adjacent zeros. For $d\ge48$ the total slope is at least $(9/8)^{16}/2>2$. Nonnegative affine corrections then give $z_{t+d}-z_t\ge z_{t+d}/2$. For $1\le d<48$, the slope $3^q/2^d$ differs from one by at least $2^{-47}$. Its additive correction is at most $(3/2)^d-1<2^{48}$. Since $z_t\ge N\ge2^{125}$, subtracting this correction leaves absolute difference at least $2^{-48}z_t$. Both lag regimes imply the supplied bound $N2^{(t+d)/24-51}$. At the smallest allowed $N$ this is at least $2^{74+(t+d)/24}$, greater than $2^H$. Every permitted echo length therefore satisfies the asserted magnitude inequality at its exact nominal real heights.

The heights in this construction come from applying the indicated affine branch, including when its input is nonintegral or has the opposite ordinary parity. The proof bounds a real absolute difference. It supplies no divisibility of rational iterates by the physical parity modulus and no ordinary integer following that word forever. Each finite prefix can be matched by positive integers of arbitrary size via the parity-cylinder bijection. Since the free-bit law gives zero mass to a singleton and the ordinary integers are countable, almost every word here lacks an all-time ordinary realizer. These facts are compatible with the fully simultaneous real inequality.

Finally the mask imposes a genuine separate ordinary obligation. Directly from $x=4y-1$ the first two odd shortcuts give $9y-1$. For the pointwise criterion, unwind $2^hT^h(n)=3^q n+C_h$, where $q$ counts the first $h$ odd inputs. Appending a halving leaves the offset unchanged. Appending an odd step gives $C_{h+1}=3C_h+2^h$. From $C_0=0$ both cases preserve $0\le C_h\le3^h-2^h$, with equality at every step for the formal all-odd branch. This establishes the sign and scale independently of the distribution of the bits. The compiled `affine_sandwich` uses the slightly weaker sufficient bound $C_h\le3^h$ and proves it by induction from the other end of the word. The sharper offset in this paragraph is a written independent check.

At $h=1024$ and $n=3^rw$, compare $2^{1024}T^{1024}(n)$ against $2^{1024}w$. If $q+r\ge647$ then the main term alone is greater, since $3^{647}>2^{1024}$ and $w>0$. If $q+r\le646$, the main term is at most $3^{646}w$. The available margin scales linearly with $w$. Even at the minimum allowed $w=2^{1024}$ it exceeds $3^{1024}$, by the exact inequality $3^{646}2^{1024}+3^{1024}<2^{2048}$. Thus the endpoint is strictly smaller on this entire side of the threshold. This checks the two boundary integers, arbitrary nonnegative rank, equality at the minimum permitted base, and both strict comparisons.
