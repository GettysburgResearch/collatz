# The native binary prefix inside a single specified clock fibre

**Source-qualified written result.** Consider one fixed nonperiodic positive odd ternary unit \(y\). At odd depth \(k\) and native total \(t=2k+O_B(\sqrt k)\), draw a uniformly random positive integer whose first visit to \(y\) has exactly these two clocks. Include initial halvings in the native total. Alternatively fix one escaping odd ray \(y_N=U^Na\) of actual native clock \(A_N\), and use the first-arrival fibre of \(y_N\) at clocks \((N+k,A_N+t)\), with \(k,N\to\infty\) independently. The same statement holds for the entire escaping corrected-height cell \(P(a)3^{-k}2^t\). In each case the source is sampled from one exact fibre on ordinary integers.

Let \(w\) be its first \(b\) native parity bits, \(d(w)\) their number of odd departures, and \(j\) the original source modulo \(3^m\). For some fixed \(c>0\), uniformly for \(b+m\le ck\), the joint law is within \(o(1)\) in total variation of
\[
       3^{-m}\,\frac{\binom{t-b}{k-d(w)}}{\binom{t}{k}}
       \quad\text{at }(w,j).                                          \tag{1}
\]
The error can depend on the fixed target or ray and \(B\); no effective threshold for its moving pointwise local limit is asserted. The binary factor is the first \(b\) bits of an exchangeable length-\(t\) word with \(k\) ones. It sums to one by counting extensions. For a uniform *odd* source in the same fibre, condition this reference law on the initial bit being one (or introduce that first bit when \(b=0\)). Thus at a linear number of revealed dates the limiting comparison retains the genuine clock conditioning.

For any fixed \(M\), each individual prescribed trit vector with \(3^m\le k^M\) has probability \(3^{-m}(1+o(1))\), uniformly in that vector, and its conditional binary law still satisfies (1) for \(b\le c_Mk\). For \(2^b3^m\le k^M\), each prescribed complete pair \((w,j)\) has probability \((1+o(1))2^{-b}3^{-m}\) on the all-positive fibre. These relative per-cell assertions use the named higher-order mixing input at a correspondingly smaller allowable constant. They are stronger than absolute total variation alone at cells of shrinking mass.

## The true ternary-marked residual row

The arithmetic input is the full extra-depth exact-total estimate (66) of Paper 48, transported to actual positive sources as in the growing-suffix law. At an odd clock \(h\le(\log_2 Z)/32\) on any subinterval of \([Z,2Z)\), it controls in joint sum norm the dependence of \(x\bmod3^D\) on the *actual* pair \((U^h x,S_h(x))\) with weights \(f_h(x)/x\). The bound is \(O_A(h^{-A}+e^{-c'h}+2^{4h}/Z)\) for \(D\le c_A h\), where \(f_h\) is the product of \(w_x=3x/(3x+1)\) along these odd departures. [Full marked physical transfer, equations (2)–(3)](../linear/source_suffix_law.md) · [Weaver–Wren, Paper 48 v1, Theorem 6 (66)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/48/v1).

Write \(M_{r,s}\) for a normalized all-positive row mass, equal to its integer count divided by physical height \(p'=y2^s/3^r\) at a fixed target. For an escaping full cell the divisor is its corrected height. Let \(M_{r,s;v}\) also specify the starting residue \(v\bmod3^D\). All applications below have \(\log p'\asymp k\), \(r\ge c_0k\), and in the moving case the extra genuine inherited clock \(N\) is included in \(r\). Uniformly on these ranges,
\[
       \sum_v|M_{r,s;v}-3^{-D} M_{r,s}|\ll_A k^{-A}
                      \qquad(D\le c_A'k).                             \tag{2}
\]
Indeed the physical height bounds on a simple first visit put every eligible source in \([c_*p',p']\) for an absolute \(c_*>0\). This follows by summing reciprocals along a simple native path, including its initial halvings, with only the odd departures contributing to the product. The same holds for the corrected height of an escaping site. [Weaver, Paper 176 v1, §5.1 after equation (26)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/176/v1). Cover by finitely many octave subintervals, and at odd sources use \(h=\lfloor c'k\rfloor\) actual odd departures within the short-transfer range. The future clock test is a function of the retained endpoint and native total. Multiplying by the remaining forward correction, bounded by one, changes the input weight from \(f_h(x)/x\) to exactly \(1/p'\) on the selected row. At the specified nonperiodic target any physical arrival is automatically a first arrival. For the full escaping cell use its remaining infinite product and corrected-height label at the true endpoint instead. Stripping \(2^e\) from an even start makes a bijective permutation on its ternary residues and multiplies the weight by \(2^{-e}\). At \(2^e\le\sqrt{p'}\) its odd source scale is still exponential in \(k\), allowing the same fixed short clock after reducing \(c'\). The remaining inputs are divisible by a common power of two of size \(\gg\sqrt{p'}\) and have total mass \(O(p'^{-1/2})\). This proves (2) from the full joint estimate. For each coarser residue modulo \(3^d\), its actual row mass is at most \(3^{-d}+1/p'\) by counting the ordinary inputs up to \(p'\).

## A prescribed native word

For a word \(w\) of length \(b\), the genuine affine transport \(z=T^b x=3^d2^{-b}x+F_w\) is a bijection from its one binary source class onto an ordinary progression \(r_w\bmod3^d\) in its positive range, with \(0\le F_w<3^d\). An additional condition \(x=j\bmod3^m\) selects one finer residue \(r_w(j)\bmod3^{d+m}\), bijectively in \(j\). The residual row has clocks \((r-d,s-b)\) and height \(p'=3^d2^{-b}p\), where \(p\) is the original selected row height. In the full escaping case this is precisely the new corrected-height index. For \(b\le ck\), with \(c\) small, \(p/2^b\) is exponential in \(k\) since \(\log p=D_0 k+O_B(\sqrt{k})+O(1)\), \(D_0=\log(4/3)\). In the moving case the extra factor \(F_\infty(y_N)\) stays between an absolute positive constant and one. Every legitimate residual input \(z\ge c_*p'\) therefore exceeds \(F_w\), so its associated original \(x\) is positive. There are at least \(k-b\) residual odd departures in the fixed-root case, and more in the moving case. The correspondence is exact, with none of the source clocks refreshed.

Let \(E_s\) be the sum of absolute errors in (2) over fine residues above coarse residue \(s\bmod3^d\) for the residual row. Its height and clock depend on \(d\), not on the individual word. We have \(\sum_s E_s\ll_A k^{-A}\) and \(3^d E_s\ll1\) by the coarse ordinary count. The contribution of each word to the error is multiplied by \(p'/p=3^d2^{-b}\). For \(N_d(s)=\#\{w:d(w)=d,r_w=s\}\), the exact reversed-gap calculation gives \(3^d2^{-b}N_d(s)\le\rho_d(s)\), with \(\rho_d\) the fair finite Syracuse density. Its Haar \(L^p\) norm is uniformly bounded for any fixed \(1<p<2\). Hölder therefore bounds the summed error for this \(d\) by \(O_A(k^{-A(1-1/p)})\). Sum over \(d\le b\) and choose the input order sufficiently large. This proves for every output \(A>0\), with \(b+m\le c_A''k\),
\[
 \sum_{w,j}\left|M^{w,j}_{r,s}
       -2^{-b}3^{-m} M_{r-d(w),s-b}\right|\ll_A k^{-A}.              \tag{3}
\]
The superscripts specify the original bits and trits, and every \(M\) on the right is the unmarked residual all-positive row. The counting inequality and the finite-moment use are precisely those in the [growing-suffix proof, equation (5)](../linear/source_suffix_law.md). Here the residual label is one exact clock and target and the shifted unmarked marginal is retained.

Write \(g_j(i)=2^{-i}\binom{i-1}{j-1}\) on \(i\ge j\ge1\), extended by zero elsewhere, and use the raw *all-positive* comparison kernel
\[
    g_j^+(i)=\sum_{e\ge0}2^{-e} g_j(i-e)=2^{-i}\binom{i}{j}.
\]
This last equality sums the hockey-stick binomial identity and retains the actual initial-halving convolution; in particular \(g_j^+(i)=(i/j)g_j(i)\) on its support.

For a fixed target its all-positive central mass is \(a_y g_k^+(t)+o_y(k^{-1/2})\), with \(a_y=h(y)/y>0\); for a moving target or full escaping cell it is \(H_C g_k^+(t)+o_C(k^{-1/2})\). The residual masses have that same coefficient times \(g_{k-d}^+(t-b)\) and error \(o(k^{-1/2})\) uniformly across \(d\le ck\), even if their residual total is far from its center. Indeed \(j=k-d\asymp k\), the residual log-heights are comparable to \(k\), and any fixed translate of the signed-source kernel obeys the full bound \(\sup_i |g_{j-u}(i-v)-g_j(i)|=O_{u,v}(j^{-1})\). The one-space difference uses Fourier inversion and \(|\widehat g_j(\theta)|\le e^{-cj\theta^2}\) for \(|\theta|\le\pi\); convolution against any fixed number of geometric letters of finite first moment gives the grade-shift bound. Truncate the fixed-root absolutely summable forcing: an omitted impulse at height less than the square root of the residual height has kernel \(O(k^{-1/2})\) by the height-ratio bound, and the higher impulse heights have arbitrarily small inverse-logarithmic tail. These are global point bounds before integrating the fixed finite translates. All-positive shifts give \(g_j^+\) as above, with \(\sup_i|g_j^+(i)-2g_j(i)|=O(j^{-1})\) since \(\sum_e e2^{-e}<\infty\). The full-cell and moving statements use the corresponding uniform-in-all-cells local laws, with \(N,k\) independently cofinal. [Sharp row forcing](SHARP_UNIFORM_FIRST_ARRIVAL_ROWS.md) · [Stopped moving local limit](MOVING_FIRST_ARRIVAL_LOCAL_LAW.md) · [Full cell local limit, PR 195](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/3fa7ef4a0011b35447a70f587b400878d5ea8ebe/research/astra-six-hour-20261127/collision/LOCAL_HEIGHT_LAW.md).

Since \(g_k^+(t)\gg_B k^{-1/2}\), divide (3) by the actual original row mass. Each binary error from the local approximation carries its explicit factor \(2^{-b}3^{-m}\). The ratio \(2^{-b}g_{k-d}^+(t-b)/g_k^+(t)\) is exactly the binomial ratio in (1). Thus for a fixed subset of trits \(J\), even its absolute sum over all binary words is bounded by \(o(1)|J|3^{-m}+O_A(k^{-A+1/2})\) after subtracting (1). This proves the individual-trit assertion. For a particular pair with \(2^b3^m\le k^M\), the local error has its own factor \(2^{-b}3^{-m}\). For \(b=O_M(\log k)\) the binomial ratio is \((1+o_B(1))2^{-b}\) uniformly in the word by sequential sampling without replacement, since \(t=2k+O_B(\sqrt k)\). Choosing \(A>M+1\) proves the individual full-cell result.

For example, at the genuine first meeting of two paths at \(y=5\) through its distinct parents 13 and 53, their two parent rows satisfy this same prefix law separately at the shifted clocks \((k-1,t-3)\) and \((k-1,t-5)\). Their prefixes therefore have the stated product law when the two sources are sampled independently from the two specified rows. Each member of a prescribed pair of polynomial-size CRT conditions still has the indicated relative frequency, even if both sources are required to start in the same chosen class. The number of incoming equal-clock pairs has its full positive Gaussian order there as proved in [the first-merge charge calculation](FIRST_MERGE_CHARGE.md); its limiting contribution at an arbitrarily late observation clock vanishes on the subsequent orbit of 5. This distinguishes the arithmetic at a genuine common arrival from the fate of its fixed ordinary future.
