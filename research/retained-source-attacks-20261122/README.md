## 4. Nonlocal complete-source surgery

The arithmetic lane worked directly with the full joint least-target,
minimum-grade, largest-source witness. Deleting an arbitrarily long
interior source window from endpoint `u` to endpoint `v`, while keeping
the preceding word of odd depth `i` and ordinary clock `E`, requires

\[
 q'=q+2^E(v-u)/3^i.
\]

The exact integrality condition is `3^i | (v-u)`. An increasing legal
replacement would produce a larger source at a strictly smaller complete
grade. Consequently the joint witness has a residue-record restriction;
only the first logarithmically many source positions can begin a
nontrivial deletion of this particular kind.

The attack then allowed changes to every earlier gap. For the relation
`x=2^h y+c`, a legal changed inverse step has

\[
 h'=a+h-b,\qquad c'=(2^a c+2^{h'}-1)/3.
\]

While the power remains nonnegative, a positive integral carry remains
positive. The resulting smaller source still needs the original target
interval to pay that carry. The full grade hypothesis does not supply
that slack. A same-endpoint gap-four replacement also has an actual
all-length obstruction at endpoints `7 mod 9`.
