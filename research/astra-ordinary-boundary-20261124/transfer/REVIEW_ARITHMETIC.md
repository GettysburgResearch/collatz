# Independent check of the complete-denominator arguments

I reconstructed §§1–5 of the [arithmetic paper](../arithmetic/README.md) at SHA-256 `42fbb80a75a1f81699d33f03673e3d239d3aad9aaef3ebaabc3db6196cee3b4f`. I also checked the final expanded paper at SHA-256 `159f72b043c756eddf046cbe7dd0bdc85bb6996791d032686bed8a8d8fb1d4c5`, with associated Lean file `1bee802b513987bc69bce531b7b9da74c212d9474c451c5c152887e60418c9b4`. This review concerns written proofs. I did not rebuild the external Diophantine inputs.

* In §1 the exponents of the shifted mask cover all residues modulo $p$ by $\gcd(p,A)=1$. In the full composite unit group, $z^p=2,z^A=3$ follow from Bézout and $2^A=3^p$. Thus $z^{pE_i-Ai}=2^{E_i}3^{-i}$ termwise. The geometric sum $S_p(z)$ is a unit because $(z-1)S_p(z)=z^p-1=1$. No prime-modulus division is used.
* In §2 all monic bounded-degree factors come from a fixed finite list. For a nontorsion algebraic unit on this list a conjugate inside the disk exists by norm one and Kronecker's criterion. Applying Jensen at radius strictly between this modulus and one to the original $0$–$1$ mask bounds its multiplicity independently of the clock. I rechecked [Corvaja–Zannier, Proposition 2, (2.8), pp. 7–8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0311030). It gives proper subgroups *through the identity* and isolated exceptions. For $u=\theta^p/2,v=\theta^A/3$, valuations above 2 first force the $u$ exponent to vanish in a putative relation, and those above 3 force the $v$ exponent to vanish. Multiplicities of torsion factors can instead be arbitrary: their possible rational primes divide finitely many $2^m-1$, and the one-variable Ridout estimate displayed in the cited proof bounds their **full** joint contribution to $D$. These steps justify the uniform small-$o$ at bounded degree and bounded $A/p$, without an effective threshold.
* In §3 the two explicit mask constructions have all positive integral gaps by the displayed representative differences. In the binary subset-sum construction the first place of disagreement between digit choices fixes the exact $2$-adic valuation, and a factor at level $h$ has simple zeros with orders of $2$-part exactly $2^{h+1}$. Thus their product genuinely divides $X^{3p}-1$. In the step-two family the complete numerical gcd divides $2^2-1$, which is coprime to $D$.
* In §4 evaluation at $u/v\bmod H$ preserves divisibility of the resultant, even if $H$ and $u-v$ share primes. Parseval gives $g\sum_i(c_i-\bar c)^2$ and for a binary mask it gives $K(g-K)$. The binary estimate (5) needs the assumption that the two named values exhaust all carries; I requested an explicit statement of this hypothesis. Equal-clock block words with distinct parity patterns have distinct carries since each carry fixes its source residue modulo $2^b$. For two-valued nonconstant arrangements, the geometric loss is $O(g\log g)$ in the logarithm and the extracted $\log(1+|\Delta|)=O(b+\log b)$, both $o(gb)$ under the stated assumptions.
* In §5 the convergent product along an escaping positive ordinary odd trajectory gives $\lambda_i q_i\to H(x)>0$ and $\lambda_i\to0$. Direct substitution gives both the real limit of the rational phase and the exact signed correction. Its $2$-adic valuation comes from the odd cycle denominator, while $\gcd(D_i,C_i)=\gcd(D_i,q_i-x)$ uses $2^{E_i}$ invertible mod $D_i$. At $i\ge1$ the pair $(2^{E_i}q_i,3^i)$ is primitive. The product with the displayed two local coordinate-form pairs reduces exactly to $H(q_i)-q_i\ge1/3$. No estimate establishes the required opposite bound on the full denominator of the same growing prefixes.

The final version implements the two-carry correction and adds two estimates. For (2a), Dirichlet pigeonholing gives a nonzero $f$ and $k=|pe+Af|\le p/N$ with $|e|+|f|=O_B(N)$. After the sign reversal $2^e3^f$ still has nonzero valuation above three. This rules out a common root with the monic unit-root polynomial even at $k=0$. Reduction at $z$ proves the gcd divides the entire integer resultant, with no squarefree assumption. Multiplying $|b\theta^k-a|$ over the $r$ roots gives the stated Mahler bound. Jensen and Cauchy–Schwarz on the unit circle give $\log\mathcal M(M)\le(\log p)/2$. Optimizing the free integer $N$ yields $O_B(r+\sqrt{pr\log p})$, including $r>p\log p$ via $N=1$.

For (5a), $H_{\mathcal T}$ divides $H$ as an integer, hence retains all valuations of that divisor without assuming the homogeneous cyclotomic factors are coprime. Because $v$ is a unit, evaluation at $u/v$ kills the *product* of the retained cyclotomics modulo $H_{\mathcal T}$. Its resultant with $J$ is nonzero. The inactive Fourier coefficients vanish, so Parseval on the active frequencies still gives $K(g-K)$. The product lower bound follows from $|1-\zeta|\le2|1-(v/u)\zeta|$ and $|\Phi_m(1)|\ge1$. The rank of the coordinate functionals on the active Fourier subspace bounds the number of distinct binary rotations by $2^{n+1}$, hence $n\to\infty$ for primitive binary words as $g\to\infty$. The extracted carry-difference loss $O(b+\log b)$ is then $o(nb)$, as is the spectral loss $O(n\log g)$ under the stated growth condition.

**Verdict.** Verified at the final paper's written scope. The universal cycle or escape exclusion remains open.
