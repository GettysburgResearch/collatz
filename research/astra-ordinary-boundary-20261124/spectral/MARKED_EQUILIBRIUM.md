# Which invariant functions survive on the binary–ternary chart?

**PROPOSED written theorem.** The ordinary source is positive and the map is the shortcut $T$. Let $L$ be a finite component label with ordinary density vector $\pi$, for example the indicator of ever reaching 1. Assume $L(Tn)=L(n)$ pointwise. Haar in the binary coordinate means the fair law of the *future shortcut parity word*. Every length-$b$ word occurs in exactly one residue modulo $2^b$: the even and odd branches each map their binary half bijectively to $\mathbb Z_2$. Write $W(n)$ for the whole word. The ternary coordinate is the ordinary integer viewed in $\mathbb Z_3$.

## Stationary dynamics and spectral conclusion

On $\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}\times\mathbb Z_3$ the map is

$$S(w,z)=(\sigma w,G_{w_0}z),\quad G_0 z=z/2,\quad G_1 z=(3z+1)/2.$$

These maps are defined everywhere since 2 is a ternary unit. For a suffix of $k$ steps with $v$ odd letters, the difference of two incoming states at the end is exactly $3^v2^{-k}(z-z')$. In particular, $v\ge d$ erases the entire incoming state modulo $3^d$. This calculation holds for the chronologically composed maps. [Formal finite numerator identity](TraceKernel.lean)

On a two-sided iid fair sequence, start the ternary maps at $0$ at clock $-s$ and propagate to zero. As $s\to\infty$, each ternary residue stabilizes almost surely: the common suffix eventually has at least $d$ ones for every $d$. This defines $Z_0$ as a function of the strictly past bits, with law $\nu$, independent of the future. Its shifted versions give the stationary system $(S,\mathrm{fair}\otimes\nu)$. It is the **unique stationary joining with fair binary marginal**, even if an alternative ternary seed initially depends on the entire future. Couple that seed to the stationary one on the same future using an independent past. At time $k$ the future bits are exactly the same; the ternary residues modulo $3^d$ can differ only if there are fewer than $d$ ones. Stationarity and $k\to\infty$ prove equality on every cylinder. The same argument gives convergence at every fixed precision from arbitrary initial ternary seeds having fair binary marginal.

The chart system is a factor of a two-sided Bernoulli shift. Its mixing can be seen without invoking a classification theorem. If a bounded time-zero cylinder observable $A$ sees the ternary state and the first $b$ future bits, and a later observable $B$ sees depth $d$ of the ternary state and finitely many future bits, reinitialize the ternary state independently at clock $b$ with law $\nu$. The recomputed $B$ has its stationary law and is independent of $A$. Its disagreement probability with the true record at $k\ge b$ is bounded by

$$\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(k-b,1/2)<d\}.$$

Thus $|\mathrm{Cov}(A,B\circ S^k)|$ is at most $2\|A\|_\infty\|B\|_\infty$ times that probability. Density of cylinder observables in $L^2$ gives mixing. This is a direct proof for the binary–ternary chart, not an identification of a random rational-affinity Poisson boundary with the physical forward word of an ordinary integer. [Independent affine random-walk boundary in a different model, Brofferio, Theorem 5.1](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2006__56_2_499_0.pdf)

For a uniform ordinary source in $[X,2X)$ the time-zero joint law of $(L(n),W(n),n\bmod3^\infty)$ tends weakly to $\pi\otimes\mathrm{fair}\otimes\mathrm{Haar}_3$. Indeed each cylinder of the last two variables is an individual class modulo $2^b3^d$, and the fixed-modulus progression theorem applies to each label. Run the ordinary map for $k$ clocks, first take $X\to\infty$, then $k\to\infty$. Exact invariance gives the resulting dynamics $(\ell,w,z)\mapsto(\ell,S(w,z))$ with law $\pi\otimes\mathrm{fair}\otimes\nu$. Its invariant $L^2$ functions are **exactly the functions of $\ell$**, because restriction to each positive-probability label leaves a mixing system. The same argument excludes all other unit-modulus Koopman eigenvalues. This proves conditional mixing for each of the two actual convergence labels if both exist. It permits either value of their mass. The full-chart independence is a statement about an empirical limit; no pointwise extension of the integer label to a function of the unmarked stationary chart was proved. ## Simultaneous moving clocks on rare ordinary source classes

There is also a single-limit version. Draw $n$ uniformly from *one* residue modulo $q=2^hm$ in $[X,2X)$, or from any nonempty union of such residues. Let $k\ge h$ depend on $X$. Read the full label $L(n)$, the next $b$ parity bits **after** clock $k$, and $T^k n\bmod3^d$. Suppose

$$\log q+k+b+d=o(\log X),\qquad \Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(k-h,1/2)<d\}=o(1).\tag{1}$$

The joint law approaches $\pi\otimes\mathrm{fair}_b\otimes\nu_d$ in total variation, uniformly in the selected class or union. Here $\nu_d$ is the reduction of $\nu$. In particular $k-h\to\infty$ and $d\le(1/2-\epsilon)(k-h)$ suffice for the binomial condition. The observations can have a growing number of outcomes, and the starting class can have vanishing mass.

**Proof.** All the read data other than the label is fixed by $n$ modulo $P=\operatorname{lcm}(q,2^{k+b},3^d)=X^{o(1)}$. Each elementary $P$-cell has label probabilities $\pi+o(1)$ uniformly by the relative estimate (2) in the companion proof. Summing the uniform *conditional* total-variation estimates against the actual cell weights shows independence of $L$ and every function of the cells up to $o(1)$. This uses the finite fixed alphabet of $L$ and incurs no factor for the number of cells. On one original $q$-class, its refining cells are uniform up to total variation $O(P/X)$ by division with remainder. Under uniform refinements, the first $h$ parity bits are determined, the next $k+b-h$ bits are iid fair by CRT and binary lifting. The ternary seed can be coupled with an independent stationary state at time $h$. The future bits agree under the coupling; the actual initial seed can no longer influence the terminal residue once the common suffix has $d$ ones. The comparison state at time $k$ has law $\nu_d$ independent of the next $b$ bits. The sum of (1)'s binomial error, $O(P/X)$ and the label error tends to zero; mixtures retain the same bound. On these clocks the proof also applies to a fixed-target trace with finitely many defects because $T^i n\ge X/2^k\to\infty$ for every $i\le k$.

One refinement is worth recording. When $k$ grows far enough to use a substantial fraction of the available digits, the *unmarked* chart comparison may remain excellent while the relative ordinary label estimate for its refining cells is no longer small. Conversely a late stopped-pair survival condition is not automatically measurable on a short original $P$-cell. Thus the result controls arbitrarily rare *specified short source observations* without replacing the surviving physical pair by an independent pair of typical sources.

The real place identifies the precise order-of-limits difficulty. In the short-clock regime (1), the actual positive endpoint is at least $X/2^k\to\infty$ deterministically. Hence these same ordinary sources do not approach a tight stationary *unscaled real* endpoint together with the ternary one. For an independent fair affine walk whose real seed is bounded, the real drift is negative and the joint stationary real/ternary law is the object of paper 19. Interchanging a diverging physical integer seed with that bounded seed would discard precisely this height coordinate. [Paper 19, §§1 and 9](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/19)
