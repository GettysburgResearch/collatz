# What the first balanced carry can cost the original source

Let \(s\) be the global least bad positive integer and \(z\equiv17\bmod48\) a bad positive source with good triple. Use the original finite first-hit word of \(3z\), and let \(\tau\) be the first upper clock with \(T^\tau(3z)<s\). At a matched clock write \(x=T^jz,\ y=T^j(3z)\). The first balance is the first clock with \(P_j(z)=1+P_j(3z)\). The earlier first-crossing theorem proves \(x=y+c\) with a **strictly positive integer \(c\)** at this particular clock. Its odd-count guard holds by definition of first balance, and both starts are odd. The remaining upper clocks and odds belong to the actual original word. [PR192, first-crossing kernel strict_before_overshoot](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/ed7a6fe30b8602407f77855ceb74ce0489ce9c10/research/astra-diagonal-closure-20261125/transport/FirstCrossing.lean)

Put \(\theta=H_2(\log_3 2)\). For every sufficiently small \(\eta>0\), any finite simple trajectory visits an interval of \(N\) positive integers at most \(O_\eta(N^{\theta+\eta})\) times. Apply the [recursive translated-strip result](PAIRED_INTERVALS.md) to the diagonal pairs \((n,n)\) from the trajectory. Discard the \(O_\eta(\log(2N))\) terminal positions whose images at the prescribed horizon could fall outside the simple segment. On the remaining chronological positions that horizon has distinct outputs. The constants are independent of the interval location and simple trajectory. In particular the reciprocal sum of sites at or above \(s\) is \(O_\eta(s^{\theta+\eta-1})\) by summing dyadic octaves.

**An unrestricted-rank implication for a simple lower.** Suppose the lower stays simple through \(\tau\), for example if it is nonperiodic. Its multiplicative correction \(D_\ell\) satisfies \(\log D_\ell=O_\eta(s^{\theta+\eta-1})\); also \(D_\ell<6/5\) above the stated verified cutoff by the [explicit estimate](DISTORTION.md). The exact ratio of lower to upper is \(3^{P_\ell-P_u-1}D_\ell/D_u\), where \(D_u\ge1\). Before first balance this is below \(2/5\), while at \(\tau\) it exceeds one. Hence balance occurs no later than \(\tau\). At its positive gap,
\[
        c/x=1-D_u/D_\ell\le 1-D_\ell^{-1}
           \le\log D_\ell=O_\eta(s^{\theta+\eta-1}),\qquad y\ge5x/6.
\]
Thus either \(0<c<s\) and the carry genuinely converges, or **both actual arms at this one crossing have height \(\gg_\eta s^{2-\theta-\eta}\)**. For the least canonical defect \(z\le2^{40}s\), so the latter is a real overshoot relative to the same original source as \(s\) grows.

**A simultaneous cap allowing any repeated lower.** Suppose both arms through \(\tau\) stay at most \(s^\beta\), where \(1\le\beta<2/(1+\theta)\). For all sufficiently large \(s\), the first balance occurs by \(\tau\) and \(0<c<s\). Indeed the upper's first-hit word is simple through its first subfloor point. The interval count gives \(\tau=O_\eta(s^{\beta(\theta+\eta)})\). The repeated lower still has \(\log D_\ell\le\tau/(3s)=o(1)\) if \(\eta\) is chosen sufficiently small. This establishes the first passage by comparing the ratio at \(\tau\). At balance
\[
        c\le x\log D_\ell=O_\eta(s^{\beta(1+\theta+\eta)-1})=o(s).
\]
If the lower is already on the bounded restricted cycle, every one of its shortcut states is at most \(8s+4\), using its odd ceiling and one possible even successor. With this bound on the lower peak, an upper-only cap \(s^\beta\) suffices for any \(\beta<1/\theta\): the final estimate becomes \(c=O_\eta(s^{\beta(\theta+\eta)})\). These are asymptotic statements with constants depending on the exponents.

In fact an on-cycle canonical \(z\) has the **exact first balance at clock five, with \(c=1\)**. Its first two odd edges have exponent two, since \(z\equiv1\bmod16\) and the cycle permits only one and two. Then \(L=T^4z=(9z+7)/16\) is odd. On the upper \(T^4(3z)=3L-1\) is even. The first four lower parities are odd, even, odd, even; the first four upper parities are odd, odd, even, even. At clock five the lower is \((3L+1)/2\), the upper is \((3L-1)/2\), and the grades differ by one for the first time. This five-clock opening also occurs in the previously analyzed ordinary rank-three controls. Existing work rules out *every* one-successor repair with a tripled source anywhere on a restricted cycle. [PR192, first-crossing controls and bounded-cycle one-successor exclusion](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/ed7a6fe30b8602407f77855ceb74ce0489ce9c10/research/astra-diagonal-closure-20261125/transport/README.md)

The subfloor alternative supplies an actual independent hit for \(c\). Its word need not have enough common parity with the same physical lower to be lifted. The [aligned-floor and signed-ratio theorems](CARRY_ALIGNMENT.md) determine what that extra argument would have to pay. The pointwise descent of a least canonical reverse defect is open.

The [original canonical floor theorem](ORIGINAL_CANONICAL_FLOOR.md) gives odd $v_2(z-1)\ge5$ at every bounded original reverse disagreement, including off-cycle ones. The same five-clock calculation therefore gives carry one there as well.
