# Closing status of the six-hour pass

**The positive-integer Collatz conjecture is not proved by this packet.**
The pass makes new restrictions on actual hypothetical counterexamples,
proves finite Lean kernels, and records where the attempted contradictions
stop. A successful local check or a conditional convergence theorem does
not discharge the universal implication.

## The central target that was attacked

Write G(n) for the assertion that the positive ordinary Collatz trajectory
from n reaches one. The existing canonical reduction turns a hypothetical
counterexample into an actual positive source z congruent to 17 modulo 48
with G(3z) and not G(z). The task is to contradict this source while keeping
its actual upper certificate, bad lower component and original height floor.
The earlier [quantitative return interface](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/156652e03fe7a78f9cd4442039779cc622fdf3de/research/ayh-resource-reset-20261124/closing-proposal/QUANTITATIVE_RETURN.md)
already supplies a conditional Lean consumer. Its unrestricted higher-rank
premise is still open. This packet does not relabel that premise as proved.

The six-hour attack used three concurrent GPT-6.2 Astra agents explicitly
configured at ultra, with the root independently checking and combining
their work. The latest external heads were revisited during the pass.

## 1. A simpler original minimum, followed through its actual upper

In the bounded alternative, the new orientation proof shows that the
original least canonical disagreement is the least bad canonical source
outright. Thus a smaller bad canonical ancestor would contradict it even
without separately proving that ancestor's triple good. The result applies
to every source in the explicit interval, including off-cycle sources.
[Proof and finite Lean scope](descent/ORIGINAL_CANONICAL_FLOOR.md).

Write the resulting original source as z=1+2*4^ell*u. Its forced lower
prefix ends at L=2a+1, with a=3^ell*u good. The supplied upper 3z meets T(a)
after exactly the displayed prefix. Every later argument follows this same
good a's first-hit word.

For s>=25, let r be the number of odd departures in that word and H its
first-hit clock. If the bad lower's odd states through H+1 stay at most 8s,
put U_1=0 and U_(q+1)=1000*(1+q+2^(U_q)). Then

    s <= 5r+13  or  H <= U_(r+1),       and       s <= 1+2^(H+1).

Consequently each fixed r gives an explicit floor bound. For an off-cycle
original the alternative can be a larger lower excursion; the distinguished
cycle itself supplies the required ceiling. The general fixed-peak version
retains the same actual certificate. First loss of paid alignment forces
a real upper overshoot, and recovery of the sign can occur after an upper
excursion on an explicitly checked ordinary control.
[All-gap proof](descent/ORIGINAL_UPPER_GAPS.md).

**Remaining implication:** no bound uniform in r has been proved, and no
argument converts all possible upper and lower excursions into the required
smaller bad canonical source.

## 2. Many exact four-phase identities within one integer cycle

This is the most direct new algebraic restriction on the bounded cycle.
It uses the stated physical rank theorem, the exact population certificate,
and the explicitly quoted estimate mu(log_2 3)<=3. Let p be the actual least
odd period and s the ordinary minimum. Actual past and future words define
a bipartite graph with one edge for each actual phase. A future word fixes
a power-of-two residue and a past word fixes a power-of-three residue.
No unrelated formal past and future are joined.

Choose unequal windows so the two moduli have sizes p^(25/24+o(1)) and
p^(49/24+o(1)). Their product dominates the permitted four-term ordinary
height, while the two populations have sizes at most p^(1/2+o(1)) and
p^(49/50+o(1)). The elementary four-cycle-free bound forces at least
p-ceil(p^(199/200)) independent four-phase relations. Each one is an exact
integer rectangle x, x+A, x+B, x+A+B. It persists through the entire
corresponding actual past and future windows, and AB>=c*s^2/p.

Recording rank modulo floor(p^delta), for any fixed 0<delta<1/25, sharpens
this to one retained set of at most p^kappa earlier ranks, kappa<1. Every
other rank is the largest corner of a rectangle with three earlier retained
ranks, and AB>=c_delta*s^2*p^(-1+delta).
[Full proof and exact finite interfaces](arithmetic/HISTORY_TRADES.md).

The attempted contradiction uses the twisted rank shift T with T^p=2I.
The irreducibility of X^p-2 implies that a nonzero rational vector is cyclic.
A proper relation space cannot be invariant under all shifts. The new
relations annihilate a finite interval of shifts, however. An elementary
lattice count gives L+1 <= codimension <= L+O(log p); those inequalities
remain compatible. No full shift invariance or incompatible lower bound
has been obtained.

**Remaining implication:** exclude these actual, chronologically compatible
integer histories, or show their additive relations produce a physical
smaller-source witness. The negative coefficient in a rectangle identity
prevents treating the identity itself as an ordinary merger.

## 3. Which broad methods failed to close

The full three-layer unweighted segment family has an individual absolute
moment cost strictly above the cubic threshold for every fixed real q>1.
The proof combines exact population and pair-collision counts with Parseval,
elementary norm inequalities and a near-zero arc. Lean checks the finite
rational envelope. This excludes that individual-norm Holder estimate;
it leaves joint products and genuinely smaller physical families available.
[Every-moment statement](synthesis/FULL_MASK_MOMENTS.md).

The original-floor inverse tests were extended to arbitrary inverse ranks
and arbitrary detours. A positive virtual control survives their countable
family of eventual affine comparisons. A finite-source version gives a
depth bound in terms of v_3(z-2), including source-dependent crossover
heights. There are actual good-upper families with growing necessary depth.
The fully ordinary control 353 is stronger in a different way: the global
predecessor minima for its forced target 199 are exactly 199, 417 and 353
in the three tested classes. All-time finite certificates are in Lean.
These good sources are controls on the method, not Collatz counterexamples.
[Source conditions and proofs](transport/ORIGINAL_FLOOR_SIEVE.md).

For escape, the pointwise transport work retains a single actual ordinary
component. It gives forced high unit-strip masses, a compact null set of
possible simple-path corrections, and an exact Radon current balance.
The forced centres may belong to that exceptional set. A nonzero singular
control satisfies the balance and positive-floor conditions alone. The
required pointwise contradiction on the actual fibres is still absent.
[Unit strips](transport/UNIT_STRIPS.md),
[common correction set](transport/GLOBAL_CORRECTION_GAPS.md),

## Formal and analytic boundaries

The Lean files prove their named finite identities, exact ordinary path
consequences, integer divisibility statements, interval implications and
finite certificate arithmetic. The written graph, entropy, Fourier,
Subspace-Theorem and limiting-measure arguments are not all formalized.
Their external inputs remain explicit in the source notes. The final
replay rebuilds every new Lean module, audits all public theorem axioms,
and reruns every declared deterministic finite control from an immutable
complete checkout. The repository validators check integrity separately.

The outcome is a research advance with a precisely located unproved
closing step. It is not a complete Lean proof of Collatz.
