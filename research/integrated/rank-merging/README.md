## What a certificate must actually provide

A merging certificate has two positive ordinary sources, finite physical clocks, one common endpoint, and a witness of strictly smaller rank. The witness need not be the smaller ordinary number. A finite witness list is spatial completeness; it does not guarantee that some pair meets at any bounded or unbounded clock. A peak-rank lower bound likewise does not establish the existence of a merger.

The P rank of PR90/91 and moving rank R_* of PR92 are **different functions**. The latter is a proper, computable minimum of an unbounded family, reducible to at most four input-dependent evaluations. This does not contradict a theorem excluding a fixed finite dictionary. See [definitions](../CONVENTIONS.md).

## Next missing lemma

Construct a proved total procedure assigning every required source a finite physical lower-rank diagram, or prove a recursively complete family of guards with a well-founded termination argument. A program that returns UNRESOLVED outside its guard is not that selector. Resource lower bounds and successful infinite families constrain this [open task](../../open-obligations/README.md); they do not remove it.
