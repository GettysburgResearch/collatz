# One certifiable table on a rare state portion of every bounded-mean inverse law

**Status.** Source-qualified consequence of PR200 O15. This strengthens the data-dependent choice in [TERNARY_DECODER.md](TERNARY_DECODER.md). Its methodological predecessor is [PR200, UNIFORM_DECODER.md](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/ad70a3b831867cb8a74d402a2a1abdc419f5a09b/research/unconventional-20261126/trace/UNIFORM_DECODER.md), which already Markovizes a non-Markov edge law and uses minimax for one predictor on an entire finite-alphabet, low-topological-entropy subshift. Its [effective companion](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/ad70a3b831867cb8a74d402a2a1abdc419f5a09b/research/unconventional-20261126/trace/EFFECTIVE_DECODER.md) also searches and certifies finite tables. The present application admits every positive exponent and imposes only a mean bound on the whole sample. Prediction is required on the geometrically specified part \(J_b(d)\), which carries provably positive mass for the physical escaping current. These distinctions in domain and loss are essential.

Fix \(K\ge1\), \(1<b<a_*\), \(r\ge1\), and \(\epsilon>0\). There is a depth \(d\) and **one** deterministic ternary table assigning an \(r\)-word to each residue modulo \(3^d\), for which the probability of a mistake *and* membership in \(J_b(d)\) is less than \(\epsilon\), for **every** two-sided stationary legal inverse process of mean gap at most \(K\). The denominator here is the entire probability measure. Relative to the tested part the assertion is informative when that part has mass bounded below.

To see this, the space of these stationary path laws is compact and convex: each ternary coordinate is compact, the single-letter mean bound gives uniform tightness on the countable discrete alphabet, and simultaneous tightness on the countable product follows by allocating summable errors to the coordinates. Stationarity, legality, and the mean bound persist under weak limits. Their joint \(r\)-window projections are again compact and convex. The zero-error conclusion on \(J_b\) for every such law, followed by the decreasing upper-semicontinuous Bayes errors and compactness as in the main theorem, gives a finite depth with all individual optimal errors below \(\epsilon/8\).

Choose \(E\) so large that \(rK/E<\epsilon/8\). Use output words on \(\{1,\ldots,E\}^r\), including one arbitrary default. The total loss of replacing a best unbounded-alphabet output in a cell by the best truncated output grows by at most the probability of an oversized true word, thus by at most \(rK/E\) after summing cells. On the finite probability simplices of randomized outputs the tested error is affine in each argument and continuous for weak path laws. The finite-dimensional minimax theorem, or separation of the finite table-loss vectors, gives a single randomized table with worst loss below \(\epsilon/4\). Select a maximal coordinate in each row. At any observation with wrong deterministic prediction, the randomized probability of the correct outcome is at most \(1/2\), so this final change at most doubles loss. In particular the table has a strict margin below \(\epsilon\).

For any sequence of arbitrarily long finite ordinary or ternary paths with total mean gap at most \(K\), the same table eventually has mistake count on \(J_b(d)\) at most \(\epsilon n\), uniformly in the paths. A contradictory sequence of empirical path windows has a stationary subsequential limit of mean at most \(K\), contradicting the strict margin. Boundary times where the requested \(r\) gaps are unavailable cost at most \(r\). For paths with total mean \(\le K<b\), the strict prefix-record count, applied with the appropriate chronological origin, gives at least \((b-K)n/(b-1)-O(d+r)\) marked target observations. For the normalized physical collision law take \(K=\alpha\) on its stationary limits as proved in the main packet. No common bound on the maximum label is required.

## An effective finite search

For rational \(K,b,\epsilon\) under the preceding hypotheses, the table and its stationary bound can be found by finite rational linear programs in a terminating enumeration. For a trial depth \(d\) and trial finite integer table, put \(Q=3^d\), \(P=2\cdot3^{d-1}\). Let \(E\) exceed every letter used in a predicted word. Replace every label \(e>E\) by the unique member of \((E,E+P]\) congruent to it modulo \(P\). This leaves the forward ternary state update \(z\mapsto(3z+1)2^{-e}\bmod Q\) unchanged, preserves the truth or falsity of comparison with any predicted word, and cannot increase the gap cost. Retain all smaller labels exactly.

Use a finite directed graph with vertices storing the residue and the previous \(r-1\) reduced labels. Edges choose a reduced label, update the residue by the exact forward rule, shift the stored history, and mark a loss if the new residue belongs to \(J_b(d)\) and the reversed last \(r\) letters differ from the table row. Maximize the mean loss over nonnegative normalized balanced edge flows with mean letter cost at most \(K\). This is a finite rational linear program. Compression sends every original stationary law to a feasible flow with the same loss. Conversely a feasible flow has a stationary Markov realization on this graph (arbitrary transitions can be specified on its zero-mass vertices). Every resulting two-sided reduced label sequence codes a genuine two-sided ternary path by contraction. Its residues agree with those on the graph, since \(d\) chronological letters erase the initial ternary residue modulo \(3^d\). The history registers also agree after \(r-1\) letters. Hence the linear program computes the exact worst stationary loss for this table, including possible mixtures of different recurrent components. Maximizing individual cycle losses only under separate cycle budgets would miss some such mixtures.

Enumerate finite tables and depths, stopping when the program's exact optimum is strictly below \(\epsilon\). The existence proof with margin guarantees termination. A numerical optimizer alone is not an exact rational certificate. No bound on the first successful depth or the complexity of the search is claimed. The computed loss concerns all legal stationary ternary paths in the stated mean budget; it does not decide whether one such path comes from a fixed positive integer for its entire future.
