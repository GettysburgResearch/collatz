# A microscopic rank neighbor has positive conditional entropy

Consider ordinary positive cycles of growing least odd period $P$, binary native valuations $e_i\in\{1,2\}$, and physical maximum $X\le(16s+7)/3$ at minimum $s$. A chronological lag supplies a smaller nonzero rank offset $t$ in the canonical octave. Use the orientation with this offset and put $v=\limsup\log t/\log P$. For uniform cyclic origin take any weak limit of the pair of two-sided valuation processes, denoted $(U,V)$. Entropies below are rates in bits per odd step. With the quoted bound $\mu(\gamma)\le3$ for $\gamma=\log_2 3$,
$$
 h(V\mid U)=h(U,V)-h(U)\ \ge\ \frac{\gamma}{2+v}-\frac{189}{250}.
$$
The bound also holds with $U,V$ exchanged. It is strictly above $1/30$ when $v=0$, and strictly above $1/250$ when $v\le1/12$. Thus for a microscopic rank lag no stationary measurable decoder from the entire first valuation sequence, even with an extra stationary zero-entropy process, can predict the second sequence's present letter with error at most $1/300$. These statements concern each limiting stationary joining. The finite cycles themselves are periodic and have zero entropy rate. **Joint lower bound.** The full-width strict-convexity theorem gives $P$ distinct positive terminal gaps $G_r^{(t)}\le CtX/P$ for an absolute $C$. Take $b=\lceil\log_3(1+CtX/P)\rceil$. A pair of actual length-$b$ valuation words, the two starting grades, and their mechanical and wrap calendars determine the entire signed-drift word. Indeed $e=m+z-z'$ recovers each grade successively. The two calendars and wraps have $O(b)$ common phase cells, so this side information costs $O(\log(b+1))$ bits. The signed recurrence determines the terminal gap modulo $3^b$ and hence uniquely. It follows for the actual uniform cyclic pair that
$$
 H(U_b,V_b)\ge\log_2 P-O(\log(b+1)).
$$
The positive cycle product under the quoted irrationality bound gives $X\le P^{3+o(1)}$, so $b\le(2+v+o(1))\log_3 P$ and $b\to\infty$ by distinctness. For each fixed $d$, stationarity and the four-letter joint alphabet give $H(U_b,V_b)\le\lfloor b/d\rfloor H(U_d,V_d)+2d$. Along the selected weakly convergent subsequence the latter fixed-block entropy tends to that of the limit. Thus $H(U_d,V_d)/d\ge\gamma/(2+v)$ there for every $d$. Taking the infimum in $d$ gives the joint lower bound.

**One-copy upper bound.** The canonical grades are $0,1,2$, with $z=2$ allowed only at rank $r\le P-h$ for $h=A-P$, where $A$ is the total valuation. Apart from the single rank $r=P-h$, the permissible earlier grades on a reverse edge of mechanical increment $m$ are $0\le x\le3-m$. For later grade $y$ impose $m+x-y\in\{1,2\}$. In the reverse first returns to $[0,1/700)$ the exact standard one-copy path counts $N_{w,y}$ satisfy
$$
       N_{w,y}^{250}<2^{189|w|}\qquad
       (|w|=665,971,1636,\ 0\le y\le2).
$$
The three schedules and their singleton endpoints are stable throughout a strict neighborhood of $\gamma-1$ by the clipped affine cut-order certificate. This covers $h/P$ on every sufficiently large cycle. For a fixed length $n<P$, record the mechanical/anchor phase cell and the location of the optional exceptional vertex at total cost $O(\log(n+1))$ bits. Delete partial returns at the two ends and the at most two complete returns incident to that exception. They cost an absolute constant in the logarithmic count because every return length is bounded. At every other return apply the bound conditional on its actual terminal grade, and multiply backwards. This counts every single valuation word with at most $O(\log(n+1))+(189/250)n+O(1)$ bits. First pass to the weak limit at fixed $n$, then let $n$ increase. The two single-coordinate entropy rates are each at most $189/250$. This uniform upper bound and the joint lower bound apply to the same joining. For clarity on decoding, let $Z$ be any jointly stationary process of entropy zero, and let the predicted bit be a measurable function of $(U,Z)$, with its translates furnishing a stationary predicted sequence. This factor together with $U$ has entropy rate at most $h(U)$. The binary disagreement sequence has entropy rate at most $H_2(q)$ if its present-letter error probability is $q$. Together these processes recover $(U,V)$, so $H_2(q)\ge h(U,V)-h(U)$. The inequalities $3^{12}>2^{19}$ and $300^{300}<2^{10}299^{299}$ yield the stated strict margins and $H_2(1/300)<1/30$. This rules out zero-error factor maps and supplies a uniform positive error for every finite-radius decoder in the microscopic limiting joining.
