**Collatz remains open.** The main advance in this fourth checkpoint is a
certified fifth absolute moment for a complete binary path family. It passes
the cubic source-counting threshold that every fixed even moment misses in
the corresponding unweighted method. Its consequences are restricted cycle
theorems with explicitly named external inputs, together with new

## 1. The fifth moment crosses the cubic threshold

Let `F` be the ordinary one-variable numerator polynomial for a binary
two-grade segment of length `a`, with either entrance and exit bit and any
mechanical intercept. As `a` tends to infinity and the slope tends to
`log₂ 3`, the [positive certificate](cycles/FIFTH_POSITIVE_CERTIFICATE.md)
proves

$$
\limsup \frac1a\log\int_{\mathbb T}|F(v)|^5\,dv
\le \frac43\log3-\frac1{7000}.
$$

The proof follows the actual three-preimage phase process generated by a
single Haar variable. It verifies 76 positive dyadic cochains and all 77
mechanical transitions. An integer grid calculation, a degree-80 analytic
tail bound, and a two-variable Bernstein estimate cover the full continuous
domain. The root freshly compiled the C++ checker and reproduced all
10,626 saved integer entries. A separate reviewer rebuilt it under
undefined-behavior sanitization and checked the rational analytic bounds.

The [source reduction](cycles/FIFTH_MOMENT_SOURCE_REDUCTION.md) uses five
actual segments, their actual prime-power multipliers, and a scalar
sampling inequality. Its fixed-place separation input is Weaver 145,
Lemma 4, which uses the integral Subspace Theorem.

For positive primitive rational cycles, write `P` for the least odd period,
`d` for the reduced common denominator, `X` for the largest rational odd
phase, and `W` for the range of `E_i−iA/P`. If all valuations are one or two,
`W<2`, and `log d=o(log P)`, the result gives

$$
\liminf\frac{\log(dX)}{\log P}
\ge \frac4{4/3-1/10000}>3.
$$

The [third-layer extension](cycles/THIRD_LAYER_FIFTH_EXTENSION.md) reaches
`W≤2+1/100000`, with lower exponent `4/(4/3−1/20000)>3`.
The added grade occurs on isolated two-edge pieces; their true complex
matrix norm is bounded by three. The existing positive cochain controls
the clean edges, and the deterministic frequency of the exceptional
phase pays for those pieces. An exact positive rational cycle with width
strictly above two confirms that this enlarges the class.

On the separately quoted preprint bound `μ(log₂3)≤3`, both classes have
only finitely many primitive cycles at each fixed denominator and obey an
eventual positive denominator-power lower bound. The period onset is
ineffective, and the finite exceptions are not identified. These are not
nonexistence theorems for nontrivial integer cycles.

## 3. Sharp all-height statistics for the same ordinary start

For positive odd `x`, define

$$
Q(x)=\sup_{\substack{p,q\ge1,\ (p,q)=1\\3\nmid q}}
\frac{v_3(qx+p)}{\log(2\max(p,q))},
\qquad g(x)=\log(3+\log(x+1)).
$$

The [static theorem](pointwise/ALL_HEIGHT_ORDINARY_CENSUS.md) proves

$$
\sum_{\substack{x>0\ \mathrm{odd}\\Q(x)>c g(x)}}\frac1x<\infty
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c>\frac1{\log2\log3}.
$$

For `0<α<1/2`, follow one uniformly sampled ordinary odd integer in
`[2^k,2^(k+1))` and put

$$
M_\alpha(n)=\max_{0\le j\le\alpha\log_2n}
\frac{Q(U^jn)}{g(U^jn)},\qquad \kappa=(\log2)^{-2}.
$$

The [ordinary window theorem](pointwise/ALL_HEIGHT_LOGARITHMIC_WINDOWS.md)
gives convergence in probability to `κ`. Its exceptional set has finite
reciprocal sum exactly when the threshold exceeds `2κ`.
The [refinement](pointwise/SHARP_OCTAVE_TAIL_REFINEMENT.md) gives the sharp
octave tail order `k^(1−c(log 2)^2)` for `c≥κ`, including order `1/k`
at `c=2κ`. The lower bounds occur on actual runs of native parity bits;
the upper bounds retain the once-sampled ordinary source throughout.

These results transfer to the stated macroscopic original gate laws.
They do not show that one exceptional escaping orbit has the typical
behavior. The [birth-cohort profile](pointwise/CANONICAL_BIRTH_RAY_PROFILE.md)
identifies how old ordinary sources would transport persistent mass onto
the actual ray of an escaping component, under the named entrance and
first-hit inputs. The [small-visitor theorem](pointwise/EARLY_WINDOWS_WITHOUT_SMALL_VISITORS.md)
protects typical early windows against every future state from a
power-sized family of smaller ordinary sources, with unrestricted
visitor clocks. Its packing and ternary-density inputs are explicit.
