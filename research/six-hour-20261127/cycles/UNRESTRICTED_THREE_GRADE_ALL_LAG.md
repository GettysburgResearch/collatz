# All positive valuations in a three-grade strip

Consider a primitive positive rational cycle with odd phases $x_i=N_i/d$, distinct positive odd numerators, fixed full reduced denominator $d$, least odd period $P$, total $A$, rational slope $\theta=A/P>\lambda=\log_2 3$, and real clock-discrepancy range $W$. All accelerated valuations $e_i\ge1$ are admitted. Put $X=\max_i x_i$, $Y=dX$, $g=\gcd(A,P)$ and $q=P/g$. Along any sequence with $P\to\infty$ and $\log d=o(\log P)$, either of the following hypotheses suffices.

- $W\le21/10$ eventually.
- $\limsup X/\min_i x_i<2^{21/10}$.

Then $\liminf\log Y/\log P\ge60/29$. Under the published 2018 irrationality-exponent bound $M=5.116201$ for $\lambda$, there is an absolute positive constant such that
$$\liminf_{P\to\infty}\min_{0<h<P,\ q\nmid h} P^{-1}\#\{i:e_i\ne e_{i+h}\}>0. \tag{1}$$
The height bound uses the fixed-place integral Subspace Theorem. The lag result also uses that theorem, through signed local source sampling. Neither uses the 2026 bound $\mu(\lambda)\le3$. For ordinary cycles $g=1$, and (1) covers every proper lag. With moving denominators the possible exceptional proper lags are the $g-1$ nonzero multiples of $q$, with $g\le d=P^{o(1)}$. For each covered lag and every integer $B$, at least half the counted number of cyclic length-$h$ windows have total valuation unequal to $B$. No finiteness or termination conclusion is asserted for this class. ## Unrestricted modular energy and physical entry

Rotate to a maximizing root, so that $E_i=\lfloor i\theta\rfloor-z_i$, $0\le f_i+z_i\le L=21/10$ with $f_i=\{i\theta\}$. Let $m_i=\lfloor f_i+\theta\rfloor$, $\ell(z)=2^{2-z}$. On $W\le L$ the real cap permits exactly $0\le z_i\le 1+1_{f_i\le1/10}$. For large $P$, $m_i\in\{1,2\}$, by $Y\ge P$, $X\le2^L/(2^\theta-3)$ and the assumption on $d$. The complete positivity condition on a step is
$$0\le z_{i+1}\le\min\{1+1_{f_{i+1}\le1/10},\ z_i+m_i-1\}.$$
It permits downward grade jumps by two. For four paths, $M_j=\sum_{i<j}m_i$, and $\Delta_i=\ell(z_i^1)+\ell(z_i^2)-\ell(z_i^3)-\ell(z_i^4)$, an ordinary fourfold equality gives integral carry $C_j=2^{-M_j}\sum_{i<j}3^{j-1-i}2^{M_i}\Delta_i$ at every cut. Given any inherited carry and grades, the block has at most the modular zero atom $E_{64}$ of all single-path endpoint-free numerators in $\mathbb Z/2^{M_{64}}\mathbb Z$. Indeed each shifted residue is bounded by the zero residue via Cauchy–Schwarz on the pair-count distribution, and dropping entrance/exit restrictions enlarges this count. The zero atom is evaluated by $2^{m_j}C_{j+1}=3C_j+\Delta_j$, $C_0=0$, free integral exit, since end divisibility forces divisibility at all intermediate dates.

As in **Three-grade modular band**, the four separated physical sources pack $a\log3\sim(1-\eta)\log Y/(3+\eta)$ each and the exterior sites cost at most $3^{b-4a+O(1)}$, with $b=(4+\eta)h_0/\theta+O(1)$, $a=(1-\eta)h_0/\theta+O(1)$ and $\log_2Y=(3+\eta)h_0+O(1)$. ## Signed gaps at nonresonant rational lags

The clock-order theorem of **Clock gcd as denominator order** gives $g=\operatorname{ord}_d(3^q2^{-A/g})\le d$, since canonical height is at most two. In particular $q=P^{1-o(1)}$. Put $s=A/g-q$, $r_i=is\bmod q$, and $K_i=\ell(z_i)N_i$. The same mechanical rank $r$ occurs $g$ times, and $2^{m_i}K_{i+1}=3K_i+d\ell(z_i)$. Since $(2^\theta-3)Y=O(d)$, $K_i2^{-r_i/q}$ varies by $O(d)$ per chronological step. In particular $K_i=C2^{r_i/q}+O(dP)$ for one common $C\asymp Y$ over the whole cycle. Comparability holds directly from $K_i\asymp Y$ by the positive rotated affine bounds at width $L$; alternatively $Y\ge P^{60/29-o(1)}$ and $dP=o(Y)$ give the same conclusion. For a lag with $t=hs\bmod q\ne0$, set $u=t/q$, $c_i=1_{r_i+t\ge q}$,
$$ G_i=2^{c_i}K_{i+h}-K_i,\qquad w_i=z_{i+h}-z_i-c_i.$$
Comparing to $C(2^{t/q}-1)2^{r_i/q}$, whose rank increment is $\gg Yt/q^2$, gives
$$ |G_i|\le Z=\lceil C_1(Yt/q+dP)\rceil,\qquad
 B_0=\max_v\#\{i:G_i=v\}\ll g[1+dPq^2/(Yt)]. \tag{2}$$
This counts multiplicities of all copies, with a large enough common $C_1$. The native signed dynamics are
$$2^{m_i}G_{i+1}=3G_i+d\delta_i,\qquad \delta_i=(2^{-w_i}-1)\ell(z_i).$$
Here $w_i\in\{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2\}$ changes exactly at the mismatches $e_i\ne e_{i+h}$. Reversing the orientation sends $u$ to $1-u$, $w$ to $-1-w$ at corresponding sites. Under uniform chronological starting point let $p_w$ be the $w$-population. No independence of this population and the mechanical phase is used.

Assume on a subsequence a covered lag has mismatch fraction tending to zero. Pass to limits of $u,p_w$, both orientation exponents $v=\log t/\log P$ and the height exponent $\xi=\log Y/\log P$. The height bound and BLS with $X\le2^L/(2^\theta-3)$ place $\xi\in[60/29,M]$. The signed-source entropy argument of **Classical full-band all-lag**, §§2–3, gives in either orientation
$$ \limsup\frac{\log P-\log B_0}{\log Z}\le\frac13\sum_{w\ne0}p_w\Gamma(D_w),\qquad
 D_w=(L-|u+w|)_+,\quad \Gamma(D)=\min\{29/20,(5/3)(D-1)_+\}. \tag{3}$$
All logarithms in ratio limits may use any common base. For clarity the changes needed for unrestricted valuations and moving $d$ are as follows.

1. On a change-free block, $f+z$ lies in $J=[0,L]\cap[-u-w,L-u-w]$, of length $D_w$. Rebase by the lower endpoint as in the binary proof. This preserves every original positive valuation, with no upper restriction. If $w=0$ there is just one drift string. Otherwise weighted drift is a fixed nonzero multiple of the rebased grade numerator. At widths below two this numerator has at most two choices on a rotation arc of length $(D_w-1)_+$ and at most one elsewhere. In the ordinary fourfold equality reduction modulo three at each successive last site, the signed bit-pair difference lies in $[-2,2]$, so each optional site has at most six quadruples. This supplies the linear branch using $\log_3 6<5/3$. The uniform block frequency bound follows from approximation by trigonometric polynomials for the irrational limiting rotation. Only $D$ below $1+(29/20)(3/5)=1.87$ requires this linear branch. For the remaining widths the unrestricted modular certificate, also valid on every narrower strip, gives $29/20$. One may round the finitely many limiting $D_w$ up by any fixed small error before choosing a common block length and invoking compact rotation control. This accounts for endpoints at one and for motion of the widths in the limiting sequence.

2. On a fixed long block of $n$ letters, the ordinary weighted drift numerator is $O(n2^{M_n})$, so its modular zero atom is bounded by $O(n)$ times the ordinary zero atom using Cauchy–Schwarz on every integer shift. At a whole-source cut, every inherited carry is covered by the modular zero atom. Divide the four long segments into these blocks; the expected fraction of letters in blocks containing a $w$ change is at most $n\Pr(w_i\ne w_{i+1})$. Changing blocks can be charged their unrestricted bounded-alphabet cost even when a valuation jumps. The phase cells, full $w$-word and all exterior normalized signed drifts form a record. Conditional on a record, use the full product of allowed drift strings before applying the circle interval inequality. It bounds the support of $G_i$ by a logarithmic factor times the geometric mean of the four ordinary fourth energies, as in the signed source lemma. The source polynomial uses the bounded normalized $\delta_i$, while its circle frequency acquires a factor $d$ from the recurrence. The fixed-place separated test for $d$ times a nonzero numerator difference remains valid because $\log d=o(\log Z)$; indeed $Z\gg dP$. 3. The entropy below is $\log P-\log B_0$ by (2). Since $q=P^{1-o(1)}$ and $d,g=P^{o(1)}$, its normalized lower limit is at least
$$\frac{\min(1,\xi+v-2)}{\xi+v-1}.$$
Here $\xi+v-1>1$ ensures the error $dP$ cannot dominate the magnitude exponent. Take the first orientation with $u\le1/2$. If its limiting $u>0$, both offsets have $v=1$ and both lower limits are at least $1/M$.
