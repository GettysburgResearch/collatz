# Strict convexity on rational same-residue rank progressions

Consider a primitive positive rational Collatz cycle $x_i=N_i/d$ of least odd period $P$, full reduced denominator $d$ prime to six, and arbitrary positive odd-to-odd valuations. Write $A=\sum e_i$, $\theta=A/P$, $\lambda=\log_2 3$, $g=(A,P)$, $q=P/g$, $a=A/g$, $s=a\bmod q$, and $X=\max_i x_i$. Rotate to a maximum of $E_i-i\theta$. The grades are $z_i=\lfloor i\theta\rfloor-E_i$, the mechanical ranks are $r_i=is\bmod q$, and $m_i=\lfloor(i+1)\theta\rfloor-\lfloor i\theta\rfloor$. Put $H=\max z_i$. Assume $q>1$ in the rank statements. Define
$$\mathcal U_d=\langle2,3\rangle\subseteq(\mathbb Z/d\mathbb Z)^\times,\qquad S=|\mathcal U_d|,$$
with the trivial unit group at $d=1$. The physical labels are $K_i=2^{H-z_i}N_i$, with lowbits $\ell_i=2^{H-z_i}$ and $2^{m_i}K_{i+1}=3K_i+d\ell_i$. Each $K_i$ has a distinct odd part. Use normalization $\ell_i=2^{1-z_i}$ throughout the convexity statement when $H=0$ or one.

**Order at height two.** For $H\le2$, the subgroup order $S$ is divisible by $g$. Write $T=S/g$. Exactly the rank offsets $T,2T,\ldots<q$ admit an octave-adjusted lag whose two scaled labels always have the same residue modulo $d$. For each such offset $t$ there is exactly one such chronological lag $h\bmod P$, and
$$ 2^{\lfloor(r_i+t)/q\rfloor}K_{i+h}-K_i\ge d \qquad\hbox{at every departure }i. \tag{1}$$
In particular, within a fixed residue class modulo $d$, all fundamental scaled labels are strictly ordered by their mechanical rank. The same tagged order holds at arbitrary canonical height when $X/\min x_i\le8$, by the inversion check below.

**Convexity at height one.** Suppose $H\le1$ and $S\le P/(\log P)^8$. For all sufficiently large $P$, let $h_*$ be the unique compatible lag at rank offset $T$. At every departure $i$,
$$H_i=2^{\lfloor(r_i+2T)/q\rfloor}K_{i+2h_*}
       -2\,2^{\lfloor(r_i+T)/q\rfloor}K_{i+h_*}+K_i\ \ge d. \tag{2}$$
In particular all gaps at any compatible offset $t=kT<q$ take every fixed value at most $gT=S$ times among the $P$ starting departures. Inside each fundamental arithmetic progression in rank of step $T$ with constant label residue, successive such $t$-gaps rise by at least $kd$. Moreover $X\gg (P/S)^2$. The sole imported approximation input is a classical finite irrationality bound for $\lambda$; the exponent eight uses the published 2018 bound. The proof uses neither Subspace separation nor the 2026 logarithm-ratio preprint. These are necessary properties in an explicit subgroup regime. A compatible offset need not exist at small $P$ or outside that regime, and the gap statement asserts no such multiplicity bound for arbitrary incompatible offsets. [Bondareva–Luchin–Salikhov, Theorem 1](https://m.mathnet.ru/links/1d9b9977c1093c37b767dc2d807bd4a2/cheb619.pdf)

## The subgroup and exact order

Set $\omega=3^q2^{-a}\bmod d$. Both under $H\le2$ and under physical ratio at most eight, the preceding **Clock gcd as denominator order** proves $\operatorname{ord}_d(\omega)=g$. Indeed a same-phase, same-residue pair cannot reverse its ordering under the common mechanical divisor. Such a reversal between $U=2^pN<V=2^bN'$ requires $p>b$, $N'-2^{p-b}N=dj$ with $j>0$ odd, and $3j<2^{p-b}-1$. This forces an exponent gap at least three and $N'>8N$. A nonzero lag multiple of the mechanical period and the order of $\omega$ would require a reversal by cyclicity. This is the rational version of the lowbit-order argument of Weaver 114. [Weaver 114](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/114/v1)

For general $h$, take $t=hs\bmod q$, $c_i=\lfloor(r_i+t)/q\rfloor$, $\rho_h=3^h2^{-\lfloor h\theta\rfloor}\pmod d$. Modular iteration of the exact recurrence gives $2^{c_i}K_{i+h}\equiv\rho_hK_i\pmod d$. All $K_i$ are units, so equality of residues at one such pair is equivalent to $\rho_h=1$ and to equality at every pair. This condition is well-defined modulo $P$. Choose $l\in[1,q)$ with $la\equiv1\pmod q$, write $b=\lfloor l\theta\rfloor$ and $\beta=\rho_l$. Since $al-qb=1$,
$$2=\beta^q\omega^{-l},\qquad 3=\beta^a\omega^{-b}\pmod d.$$
Thus $\mathcal U_d=\langle\omega,\beta\rangle$ and the order of $\beta$ modulo $\langle\omega\rangle$ is $S/g=T$. At an offset $0<t<q$ the $g$ lags $lt+jq\bmod P$ have multipliers $\beta^t\omega^j$, since $tl\theta=tb+t/q$. This proves the existence and uniqueness precisely when $T\mid t$. If $T<q$, every compatible lag is $k h_*\bmod P$, $1\le kT<q$.

Write $G_i=2^{c_i}K_{i+h}-K_i$ for compatible $h$. It is a nonzero multiple of $d$, since equal labels even after octave scaling would identify distinct odd parts. It satisfies
$$2^{m_i}G_{i+1}=3G_i+d(2^{c_i}\ell_{i+h}-\ell_i).$$
For $H\le2$ the parenthesized quantity is at least $-3$. Thus a positive $G_i$ stays positive, using divisibility by $d$ and exclusion of zero. Under the alternative physical-ratio condition, a positive $G_i$ cannot reverse because the two compared inputs of $3L+d\operatorname{lowbit}(L)$ share a residue. The inversion check already given applies also if one input has been multiplied by two at the wrap, with unchanged odd part. Finally $\sum_iG_i=\sum_{i:c_i=1}K_{i+h}>0$ since $t>0$. Some positive gap therefore propagates around the whole chronological cycle. This proves (1).

## The two seams

Now take $H\le1$ and $S\le P/(\log P)^8$. Every fundamental $K_i$ lies in the same multiplicative coset of $\mathcal U_d$ and in $[1,2dX]$. Since the $K_i$ are distinct, $P\le S(1+2X)$. Thus $X\gg P/S\to\infty$. The positive affine cycle bound $X\le4/(2^\theta-3)$ gives $0<\theta-\lambda\ll S/P$. For large $P$, $m_i=1+1_{r_i+s\ge q}$, $s/q\to\lambda-1$, $T/q=S/P\to0$, and $2T<s$. In the following formulas $G_i$ denotes the compatible $T$-gap, and all lowbits belong to $\{1,2\}$.

The three octave-adjusted labels in (2) have identical residues modulo $d$, as the compatible step can be extended without forgetting its accumulated octave factor. Thus $J_i=H_i/d$ is integral. By taking the difference of the common unwrapped recurrence before reducing indices, one gets exactly
$$2^{m_i}J_{i+1}=3J_i+D_i,$$
where for $u=\ell_i,v=\ell_{i+h_*},w=\ell_{i+2h_*}$ the drift follows the table.

| Starting rank | $D_i$ | Range | $m_i$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0\le r_i<q-2T$ | $u-2v+w$ | $[-2,2]$ | one or two |
| $q-2T\le r_i<q-T$ | $u-2v+2w$ | $[-1,4]$ | two |
| $q-T\le r_i<q$ | $u-4v+2w$ | $[-5,2]$ | two |

For example the adjusted coefficient at the $j$th vertex in both the label and drift is $2^{\lfloor(r_i+jT)/q\rfloor}$. The carry identity for floor sums supplies the common divisor $2^{m_i}$ after advancing each of the three vertices chronologically. The table permits no direct strict sign reversal, since a successor at a seam is an integer after division by four. A negative can leave its strict sign only on the middle row, with $J_i=-1,D_i=3,J_{i+1}=0$. A positive can leave its sign only on the last row with $J_i=1,D_i=-3,J_{i+1}=0$.

Suppose a strict sign has just reached zero at one of these seams. The starting triple at the next date, and all its first $n=\lceil10\log_2(P+2)\rceil$ chronological translates, lie wholly inside a fundamental octave and share a mechanical letter within each triple. Here are the estimates. The 2018 logarithm bound gives $\|j\lambda\|\gg (j+1)^{-5}$ for every positive integer $j$ (with the constant adjusted for finitely many small $j$). It also gives $X\le P^6$ eventually by the slope approximation and $X\le4/(2^\theta-3)$. For $1\le j\le n+2$ the distance of $js$ from $q\mathbb Z$, divided by $q$, is at least $\|j\lambda\|-j|\theta-\lambda|$. This exceeds $4T/q$ for large $P$, because $(j+4)S/P\ll(\log P)^{-7}$. Each translated three-rank cluster is a translate of $js$ by offsets in $[-2T,2T]$ since its source was in one of the two seam intervals. Being clear of the octave seam in the present and next triples also ensures a common mechanical divisor for the current transition.

At a zero $J$ the fundamental triple is a three-term arithmetic progression with positive spacing by (1). Its spacing is $d b_0$ with $b_0\in\mathbb Z_{>0}$, $b_0\le2X$. When the spacing is even the three labels have the same lowbit: odd labels have lowbit one; the even ones all have lowbit two because multiples of four are excluded. Their three images stay in arithmetic progression with normalized spacing multiplied by $3/2^{m_i}$. If all of the first $n$ propagation steps had even spacing, $2^{\sum m_i}$ would divide the original positive normalized integer $b_0$; this is impossible because $2^n>2X$ eventually. At an odd spacing the two endpoints must be odd, since two even endpoints differing by twice an odd number would include a multiple of four. The lowbit pattern is then $(1,2,1)$. Its drift is $-2$, so the successor of this zero has strictly negative curvature (if the mechanical divisor is four, integrality excludes that step). Thus every zero entered directly from a strict sign necessarily returns to strict negativity before another seam can intervene.

Finally $H_i=2^{\lfloor(r_i+T)/q\rfloor}G_{i+h_*}-G_i$ gives $\sum_i H_i=\sum_{i:r_i+T\ge q}G_{i+h_*}>0$. A positive curvature exists. Along the transitive chronological orbit, a negative could only stay negative or leave through a zero stretch that returns negative. It could never reach the positive curvature. This proves nonnegativity. A zero exists only if some positive first exits to zero, but that zero would in turn reach a negative, also impossible. Hence $J_i\ge1$ everywhere.

## Fibres of compatible gaps

Partition fundamental labels into $gT$ progressions starting at one of the $g$ copies of each rank $0,\ldots,T-1$ and repeatedly advancing by $h_*$ as long as the rank remains below $q$. Each has constant modular residue and strictly increasing rank. For the compatible $kT$-gap, its extended first difference along this progression is the sum of $k$ consecutive extended $T$-curvatures. Each is $H_j$ times its positive octave adjustment, hence at least $d$. Two consecutive starting points of the same fundamental progression do not wrap, so their actual $kT$-gaps increase by at least $kd$. Each numerical gap is therefore assumed at most once per progression. A progression has at least $\lfloor q/T\rfloor=\lfloor P/S\rfloor$ labels, successively increasing by at least $d$ with curvature at least $d$. Since all fundamental labels are at most $2dX$, summing this lower quadratic progression gives $X\gg(P/S)^2$. The constants are absolute.

The case $d=1$ recovers the ordinary strict rank convexity of **Strict convex ranks**. That theorem supplies the ordinary AP obstruction and octave normalization. The subgroup rank spacing, multi-copy seams, the return to a negative sign after arbitrary post-seam zero progressions, and the denominators in (1)–(2) are used here to handle genuinely different residue sheets. Small positive rational cycles need not have a compatible nonzero offset. The certificate tests finite subgroup arithmetic, the actual rational signed interface for every sampled rank offset, and the local AP dynamics with nonunit $d$. The 7,363 sampled cycles did not have compatible nonzero full-denominator offsets; 3,768 nonzero compatible offsets were verified in the separate finite-group controls. No sampled cycle is asserted to meet the asymptotic subgroup regime.
