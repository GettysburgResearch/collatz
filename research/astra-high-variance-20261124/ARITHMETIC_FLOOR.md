**The entire decidable residual set has small first gap.** Take the older full-octave fiber construction [pinned at 9b02334f](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/9b02334f27ff59694a91fe7f018e0aaabad7620b/research/astra-round13-20261124/LATEST_BAND.md). If an odd mark \(n\) has \(e=v_2(3n+1)\ge3\), then \(n=4x+1\) with \(x\) odd and positive, and \(U(x)=U(n)\). The even neighbor \(n-1=4x\) belongs to exactly the same interval \(I_d\): \(n>2^d\) and the interval is inclusive and contiguous. Its odd core is \(x\). Thus for *any* prescribed positive common odd clock \(R_d\), the fiber of \(n\) contains an even source and is discarded entirely. Every residual \(s\in S\) therefore satisfies \(v_2(3s+1)\le2\). This applies to all retained octave minima, without requiring them to be global level minima or outputs of the delayed map. In particular \(S\) and PR197's critical section \(B\) have no common member at octaves \(d\ge10\), even though either section determines universal convergence. **The delayed return incurs a definite octave drop on every critical mark it selects.** Starting with any mark of gap \(e\), carry out \(j=\lfloor(e-1)/2\rfloor\) quarter deletions, obtaining a positive marked odd \(x\) of gap \(a=e-2j\in\{1,2\}\). At each step the odd endpoint is unchanged, the mark persists by deletion, and the octave falls by exactly two. The elementary identities are
\[
       n=4^j x+(4^j-1)/3,\qquad U(n)=U(x).
\]
Thus the projection \(r(n)\) of [ARITHMETIC_RETURN.md](ARITHMETIC_RETURN.md) satisfies \(r(n)\le x\) and
\[
 \lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor-\lfloor\log_2 r(n)\rfloor\ge e-a\ge e-2.
\]
On \(B\), \(5e\ge d+1\), so this drop is at least \((d+1)/5-2\) and \(r(n)\le x< 8n^{4/5}\). For the latter inequality use \(x\le n/2^{e-2}\), \(2^d>n/2\). At sufficiently late actual levels on an escaping source, ARITHMETIC_COST makes the same integer exactly the difference between the total numbers of binary divisions on the two synchronized arms. Alternatively, select \(r(x)\) directly: since \(U(n)=U(x)\), equality with the original orbit already holds at \(R_{d-e+a}\). This alternative can use the smaller cubic delay without assuming \(r(x)=r(n)\).

These are pointwise costs at *actually selected critical marks*. The PR197 supply of arbitrarily large critical predecessors in a component gives no claim that a given forward return chain selects such marks, or that the smaller representative is below an earlier fixed floor. The saving of order \(d/5\) is also already reflected in the original first gap \(e\). It cannot be charged a second time to contradict a one-arm budget. The conclusions here are written deductions.
