# An all-length obstruction for the isolated paid pair

**Scope.** For arbitrary $s\ge0$, no choice of the all-odd ramp exponent gives a uniform affine merger at the boundary of $L^r PP L^s$. All possible later light exit lengths are included. The statement compares equality over all ordinary lifts of a specified word for which the ramp is defined. It leaves source-specific inverse reserves, accidental meetings and alternative target orbits available.

Use $q_0+7=2^{3r+1}v_0$ from the initial light run and put $q_{\rm end}+7=2\cdot9^s v_2$, with $v_2$ positive integral. Simple substitution twice gives
$$ 2^{3s+10}v_2=9^{r+2}v_0+3403.$$
The initial ramp $2^{2r+\ell}v_0-1$ reaches $3^{2r+\ell}v_0-1$ in $2r+\ell$ odd steps. Parameterize $k=\ell-4$. At that point its value is $3^k2^{3s+10}v_2+z_k$ with $z_k=-1-3403\cdot3^k$. A uniform continuation reaching $2\cdot9^sv_2-7$ must, by equality of the multiplicative coefficients, use exactly $t=3s+9$ further clocks and exactly $u=2s-k$ odd steps. The true starting value and $z_k$ agree modulo $2^{3s+10}$ in the rationals of odd denominator. Therefore all these clocks use the signed parities of $z_k$, and equality requires $T^t(z_k)=-7$.

For $k=-d\le-10$ the initial signed value has reduced denominator $3^d$. Every odd step removes exactly one ternary denominator digit until it is integral. Even steps preserve that denominator. Before and including the $d$-th odd visit, $w=z+1$ updates to $3w/2$ on an odd step and $w/2+1/2$ on an even step. At clock $j$ of that visit, $w\ge-3403/2^j\ge-3403/2^d$. The integral signed value is then strictly greater than $-5$. The negative integers $-4$ through $-1$ reach $-1$ and the nonnegative integers remain nonnegative. None can reach $-7$. For $k=-9,\ldots,-1$ exact iteration before the first repeated state gives eventual cycles, respectively, on $2,2,0,0,-1,-1,-1,-1,-25$; none visits $-7$.

For $k\ge0$, suppose a hit exists for fixed $k$ and take the least $s$. On magnitudes, the signed target cycle is $7,10,5,7$. If its last three clocks are a whole turn and $s>0$, removing them proves a hit with $s-1$ and two fewer odd steps. For $s=0$ odd-count feasibility forces $k=0$; direct iteration excludes that case. Thus there are at most two terminal steps after first arrival on the cycle. Before entry every odd magnitude is at least nine: the positive odd possibilities below nine are 1,3,5 and 7, and the first two do not enter this cycle. Only one terminal odd step can start at 5. An odd magnitude $h$ updates by the factor $(3/2)(1-1/(3h))$. Except at 5 its correction is at least $26/27$, and at 5 the ratio to that bound is $63/65$. Even steps multiply by $1/2$. Consequently
$$7\ \ge\frac{63}{65}\frac{3403}{512}\left(\frac{169}{162}\right)^s\left(\frac{27}{26}\right)^k.$$
At $s=2,k=0$ the right side exceeds seven, as it does at $s=0,k=3$. The remaining $s=0,1$, $0\le k\le\min(2,2s)$ fail by direct signed iteration. The base cases and thresholds are reproduced by [descent_macro.py](descent_macro.py).

The obstruction concerns targets after the initial all-odd segment. If a proposed meeting occurs strictly earlier in that segment, its value is $3^t 2^{2r+\ell-t}v_0-1$. The earlier source endpoint is affine in $v_0$ with constant $2\cdot9^s(3403/2^{3s+10})-7$; equality of constants with $-1$ would require $3403\cdot9^s=3072\cdot8^s$, impossible by parity. Thus allowing a free uniform target clock gives no additional case.
