# Leading boundary and trailing consumption in normalized mixed-base rewriting

## Scope and conclusions

For the eleven-rule mixed-base Collatz system, normalize after every physical odd rule, then consume the remaining trailing zeros. This selects actual proper-word paths. The leading boundary trace on any nonperiodic ordinary path has the factor law of a particular irrational rotation. Every fixed-length pattern outside that law has asymptotic frequency zero. Almost every odd row eventually has more than any fixed number of binary runs, in the sense of density. Both conclusions also hold along growing distinct primitive positive ordinary cycles. They constrain hypothetical failures of termination. A separate joint realization theorem shows that any open cell of the leading factor can coexist with any finite list of trailing consumptions on arbitrarily large ordinary paths ending at 1. Together these determine precisely the finite local correlation available for this approach. Universal termination remains open.

Use the alphabet of PR197, exactly at 2fb1f11c959e35eba875338833d84969222dc089, research/ayh-resource-reset-20261124/check_rewrite_certificates.py. The physical rules are ad→d and bd→gd. The six exchanges are ae→ea, af→eb, ag→fa, be→fb, bf→ga, bg→gb. The three left rules are ce→cb, cf→caa, cg→cab. The leading c means 1; a,b denote binary 0,1; e,f,g denote ternary 0,1,2; d marks the end. Yolcu, Aaronson and Heule prove full termination equivalent to ordinary convergence, using their original alphabet and arbitrary strings. This paper works only with proper canonical executions and cites that equivalence for the closure target. [Primary paper, §3.2](https://emreyolcu.com/research/rewriting-collatz.pdf)

## Exact transducer

From an odd row q>1 write q=2u+1 and u=2^k+s, 0≤s<2^k. After bd→gd a ternary carry t=2 sweeps left across the k binary suffix digits of u. Across digit b it emits (3b+t) mod 2 and propagates floor((3b+t)/2). Across a suffix of r digits representing v it therefore propagates floor((3v+t)/2^r). This all-r identity is checked in rewriting_front.lean. At the leading marker the received carry is J=floor((3s+2)/2^k), giving precisely left rule e,f,g as J=0,1,2. This is the normalization of 3u+2, including k=0; then a(q)−1 even rules remove trailing zeros, where a(q)=v₂(3q+1). Denote the next odd state by q'. With B(q)=floor(log₂ q), exact bit-length conservation is

    B(q') − B(q) = 1 + 1_{J≥1} − a(q).                         (1)

In particular evaluation of the leading rules alone does not depend on an assumed choice of a polynomial interpretation or word context order.

## Rotation factor on an actual trajectory

Write x(q)=log₂ q mod 1, alpha=log₂ 3. At odd dates, exactly

    x(q') = x(q) + alpha + log₂(1+1/(3q))  (mod 1).             (2)

If the path does not repeat, injectivity in the positive integers gives q_i→∞. On every sequence of consecutive windows whose starting dates and lengths both tend to infinity, the empirical phase converges to Haar measure. Indeed every limit measure is invariant under rotation by alpha by (2), uniform continuity, and telescoping the endpoints; its nonzero integer Fourier coefficients vanish since alpha is irrational. Windows from date zero also have this property by removing any fixed prefix. More generally the proof works on arbitrary ordinary windows when their minima and lengths both tend to infinity. Distinct primitive positive ordinary cycles have minima tending to infinity, by determinism, and their odd periods tend to infinity: for fixed odd count p, a cycle minimum q obeys 2^A≤(3+1/q)^p≤4^p by multiplying all p equations, and then only finitely many gap lists are possible.

For m=2^{x(q)} the exact carry J differs from the interval label given by splitting [1,2) at 4/3 and 5/3 only within O(1/q) of an endpoint: m=1+(s+1/2)/2^k. Thus the entire block law for r consecutive leading boundary rules is that obtained by sampling one uniform phase on the circle and reading those three intervals for r successive rotations. The endpoints of all shifted intervals form at most 3r cuts. The positive-frequency language therefore has size ≤3r and entropy rate zero. The exact one-letter frequencies are log₂(4/3), log₂(5/4), log₂(6/5). Finite windows follow by uniform continuity of the rotation, bounded accumulated error on a fixed r-step tail, and ignoring arbitrarily small neighborhoods of finitely many cuts. No statement here requires a uniform rate as r grows.

A separate consequence holds for the rows themselves. For fixed K the proportion of odd rows with ≤K binary runs tends to zero on the same windows. For prefix size h, at most 2 times the sum of binomial(h−1,j) for 0≤j<K binary strings have that many runs. Each leading prefix occupies a mantissa interval of length ≤2^{1-h}/ln 2 in log coordinates. First pass to the limit in window length, then h→∞. Rows too short for h vanish by the minimum hypothesis. This argument handles signed binary run decompositions automatically.

One can also locate the six exchanges in the interior of the canonical sweep. At depth j≥1 below its leading bit, the input bit is floor(2^j m) mod 2. The incoming label is floor(3 frac(2^j m)), except within O(2^j/q) of a cut for fixed j: apply the exact suffix formula to the k−j lower bits, replacing the incoming 2 by 3/2. In particular all six exchange frequencies tend to 1/6 as FIRST the windows tend to infinity and THEN j→∞. To see the second limit, the density of m on [1,2) is 1/(m ln 2). Each consecutive pair of depth-j dyadic intervals splits into six equal-length subintervals for the six ordered pairs, up to endpoints. Integrating this continuous density on the cells and letting the mesh vanish gives the claim. No limit with depth comparable to the full number of digits is asserted.

The basic phase equation is an elementary consequence of acceleration. A related preprint gives a base-six circle near-conjugacy for unaccelerated steps. Earlier Benford studies have other sampling regimes; we make no historical priority claim for irrational rotation of mantissas. The conclusions used here are its joint proper-rewrite factor, the exact rule identities and the simultaneous finite realization below. ## Simultaneous ordinary realizations in the basin of one

Fix ANY finite list a₀,…,a_{h−1}≥1 and ANY nonempty open arc I of the phase circle. There are arbitrarily large odd sources in the basin of 1 with precisely those initial gaps and initial phase inside I. In particular choose I inside any open cell for an r-letter rotation word. The same sources can realize that exact r-letter boundary word as well: include any additional desired gaps up to r in the finite list, and take a smaller closed arc inside the cell. All of the finitely many prescribed iterates then tend to infinity, so (2) and the carry formula enforce the stated boundary labels.

Here are the choices and order of limits. Choose r≥1 so that −(r+h+1)alpha mod 1 lies in the interior of I. Starting at 1 build r inverse odd steps t_new=(2^b t_old−1)/3, choosing all b arbitrarily large. Given t_old a ternary unit, choose b modulo 6 so that 2^b t_old≡4 or 7 mod 9. This makes t_new an odd positive ternary unit and gives the exact indicated forward gap (the target is odd). For fixed r and sufficiently large exponents, the resulting tail root t has phase arbitrarily near −r alpha: each inverse step contributes −alpha plus the integer b and the error log₂(1−1/(2^b t_old)). The finite sum of errors tends to zero.

Put A=sum a_i, P_j=sum_{i<j} a_i, C=sum_{j<h}3^{h−1−j}2^{P_j}, with C=0 if h=0. The source reaching t after the given list and a final freely chosen extra gap b is

    n = (2^{A+b}t − 2^A − 3C)/3^{h+1}.

Integrality is one congruence 2^b t ≡ 1+3C/2^A mod 3^{h+1}; its right side is a unit. The powers of 2 generate the units modulo 3^{h+1}, so b runs through an unbounded arithmetic progression. This gluing identity is the finite-prefix construction of PR198, c76acf99ba3fb05e7cc07ca7db7e467954c2d950, research/astra-frontier-20261124/BRIDGE_AUDIT.md. Equivalently the intermediate states have denominator dividing a power of 3 when computed backward, and a power of 2 when computed forward from n, hence are integral. The last target is positive odd, backward division by the odd number 3 preserves oddness, and large b ensures positivity. For fixed t, n→∞ and its phase tends on the circle to the phase of t minus (h+1)alpha. By the preceding choices it is in I. Increasing b preserves convergence to 1 and gives arbitrarily many distinct sources.

## Joint cylinders retain the counting exponents of the entire odd basin

Let B be the basin of 1 restricted to positive odd integers, and D the simultaneous phase-and-gap cylinder just defined. There are constants K and R depending only on the fixed open arc and fixed gap list such that, for X≥1,

    N_{B∩D}(X) ≤ N_B(X) ≤ 2(R+1) N_{B∩D}(5KX).

Here N counts positive odd members up to its argument. Consequently the two sets have exactly the same lower and upper logarithmic counting exponents. This strengthens existence and extends the bounded-multiplicity argument of PR198, research/astra-frontier-20261124/QUANTITATIVE_GLUING.md, to an independently specified real leading phase.

Choose a smaller open arc with its closure inside I, with a fixed margin to the boundary of I. Irrational rotation and compactness supply R so that for **every** initial phase theta some 0≤r≤R puts theta−(r+h+1)alpha in the smaller arc. Start with any odd basin element z coprime to 3; the r inverse steps now start at z in place of 1. Choose each exponent in a single fixed interval [E,E+5] using the class modulo 6. Taking E large makes their total phase error less than half the margin uniformly, since every intermediate value is ≥1 and r≤R. For the final bridging gap choose b in [F,F+2·3^h] in the required class. Relative to its leading term, the numerator of n has the multiplicative correction 1−(1+3C/2^A)/(2^b t); choosing F large makes its log error less than half the margin uniformly in t≥1. It also ensures n>1. All choices can be made deterministic by taking the least eligible index. The bounded lengths and exponents give n≤Kz, membership in B∩D, and exactly h+1+r forward odd steps from n to z. For fixed n there are at most R+1 such targets z, even before using the deterministic choices. An odd basin element z divisible by 3 can first be replaced by 4z+1, a ternary unit with exactly the same next odd value because 3(4z+1)+1=4(3z+1). This preliminary map has multiplicity at most two and increases a value by at most a factor of five. The counting inequalities follow. To prescribe an additional nonsingular finite leading-rule factor, work in a smaller phase cell and take F still larger to guarantee that all its finitely many actual iterates lie away from branch ambiguities, extending the prescribed gaps if necessary.

The construction preserves membership in every **eventual-intersection component** of the ordinary odd map. Explicitly, take any component C, meaning one equivalence class for eventual intersection of forward orbits. The same comparison N_C(X)≤2(R+1)N_{C∩D}(5KX) holds: an inverse chain returning to the unit target stays in C, and the preliminary 4z+1 replacement for a ternary nonunit meets z at their common next odd state. Consequently each component has the same lower and upper logarithmic counting exponents on D as on all odd values in that component. In particular, for any one fixed nonempty such phase-and-gap cylinder D, universal convergence is equivalent to convergence of every member of D. If a nonconvergent component existed, the construction applied to any element would supply a nonconvergent member of that same component in D. When z is divisible by three the constructed trajectory need only meet its future; there is no claim that a positive-length odd inverse path reaches z itself.

## The attempted rank and the open inequality

The low-complexity left factor really is forced by the normalized rewrite, even though the binary run counts of the rows escape. A natural potential credits the terminal for trailing consumption a, charges 1 for each odd step, and charges an extra unit for either of the last two leading rules. Equation (1) identifies its exact balance with binary length. The leading charges average 1+log₂(3/2)=alpha on any escaping path. On a cycle they sum to total trailing consumption, by exact length conservation. Oscillations of any bounded phase correction telescope and cannot change this mean. On a nonperiodic orbit proving a prefix with average trailing consumption exceeding the actual mean charge by enough to erase the initial excess over a smaller source would give descent. Nothing in the positive-frequency leading language forces this. The joint realization proves even a long fixed leading factor can accompany all gap-one steps or arbitrarily large gaps at every one of its positions, on paths that do reach one. The remaining closing problem is a same-source, growing-scale correlation involving trailing consumption or another unbounded resource. A finite cylinder pair cannot supply it by exclusion. This explains where the proposed nonlocal rank succeeds and where the universal proof stops.
