AN EXPLICIT FAMILY OF EXPONENTIAL REPRESENTATIONS

Let E be the positive integers x for which there exist r>=0 and integers

    0=A_0<A_1<...<A_r

satisfying

    3^r x + sum_{j=0}^{r-1}3^{r-1-j}2^{A_j}
        =2^{A_r}.                                          (1)

The empty case r=0 represents x=1.

QUESTION

Prove or disprove that for every integer j>=1,

    y_j=(2^(54j+7)-47)/81

belongs to E.

The displayed y_j is always a positive integer.

A related integer has an explicit certificate:

    x_j=2*y_j-1=(2^(54j+8)-175)/81,

    r=4,
    (A_0,A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4)=(0,2,4,6,54j+8).

Thus the task is a uniform certificate construction for (x_j+1)/2 from this concrete four-term family. The new certificate can have arbitrary finite length and exponents depending on j. An assumed fixed output length, or a fixed finite inspection deadline, would need its own proof.

It may help to study exact recurrences in j, integer-valued exponential identities, or transformations of the exponent tuple. Record the first unhandled case of any proposed recursion.

WORK STANDARD

Treat the requested assertion as an open research target, not as an established theorem. A proof, an exact counterexample, or a precisely scoped new partial result is useful. State every additional hypothesis. Keep finite computations separate from claims for all parameters. If a proposed argument fails, identify its first unsupported implication and preserve any exact obstruction you find.
