RIGIDITY OF A PROJECTED FUNCTIONAL EQUATION

Let

    F(z)=sum_{n>=1} a_n z^n,
    a_n in {0,1},
    a_1=0,

and let omega=exp(2*pi*i/3). The coefficient bound makes F analytic on the open unit disk.

Assume that for every complex w with |w|<1,

    (1/3) sum_{j=0}^2 omega^j F(omega^j w^2)
        = w (F(w^3)-F(w^6)).                                (1)

QUESTION

Is F identically zero the only such function? Prove uniqueness, or construct an exact nonzero solution and verify every hypothesis.

USEFUL INTERMEDIATE TARGETS

Either of the following would suffice:

  A. Every function satisfying the hypotheses is rational.
  B. Every function satisfying the hypotheses admits analytic continuation through some nonempty open arc of the unit circle.

For A, the following reduction is available: a rational solution of (1) with coefficients in any finite subset of C has constant coefficients. Under a_1=0 it therefore vanishes.

For B, a nonzero solution has infinitely many nonzero integer coefficients and radius of convergence exactly 1. The Pólya–Carlson theorem then makes it rational or gives the entire unit circle as a natural boundary. Thus continuation across one arc forces A.

Be careful about functional-equation hypotheses. A single equation mixing arguments w^2,w^3,w^6 and a root-of-unity projection is not, by definition, a pair of separate finite-order Mahler equations in bases 2 and 3. Any theorem requiring those two equations requires their actual derivation.

The ordinary Parseval identity for Boolean coefficients,

    (1/(2*pi))*integral_0^{2*pi}|F(r*exp(i*theta))|^2 dtheta
        = F(r^2),

is available, but it alone supplies no boundary continuation.

WORK STANDARD

Treat the requested assertion as an open research target, not as an established theorem. A proof, an exact counterexample, or a precisely scoped new partial result is useful. State every additional hypothesis. Keep finite computations separate from claims for all parameters. If a proposed argument fails, identify its first unsupported implication and preserve any exact obstruction you find.
