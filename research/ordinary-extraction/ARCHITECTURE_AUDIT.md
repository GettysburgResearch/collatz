## 1. The exact repeated inference

Across the constructive lanes, the recurring data have the form

\[
\forall n\ \exists x_n\in\mathbf Z_{>0}
\text{ satisfying the first }n\text{ gates}.
\]

Finite branching or nested congruences may additionally give

\[
\exists \alpha\in\mathbf Z_2
\text{ satisfying every finite gate}.
\]

The counterexample objective requires

\[
\exists x\in\mathbf Z_{>0}
\text{ satisfying every gate}.
\]

The second statement is not the third.  By `T-7601`, the third is equivalent to a uniform bound on the least finite-depth ordinary witnesses.

The theorem that every extracted path grows proves only

\[
x\in S_\infty\Longrightarrow \text{unbounded orbit}.
\]

It does not prove `S_infinity` is nonempty.

## 2. Status scale

The audit uses four labels.

- **CROSSED POSITIVELY:** one explicit ordinary all-time seed is supplied.
- **CROSSED NEGATIVELY:** an exhaustive fixed architecture is proved to have no ordinary all-time seed.
- **AT THE BOUNDARY:** complete finite cylinders and the ordinary stabilization criterion are exposed, but neither side is decided.
- **BEFORE THE BOUNDARY:** finite prefixes, abstractions, or conditional dynamics have not yet been reduced to the exact least-root/stabilization decision.

No inspected lane has crossed positively.

## 14. Blunt global conclusion

It has genuinely:

- eliminated the complete frozen corrected phase-34 class;
- exposed and repaired false stack and completion interpretations;
- proved that fixed-modulus and several low-complexity certificate formats are ghosts;
- reduced several positive lanes to exact ordinary state machines rather than informal symbolic schedules.

But the leading positive divergent-orbit lanes have all converged to the same unproved quantifier swap.  Their conditional growth theorems are downstream of extraction and cannot compensate for its absence.

At the inspected cutoff, no current positive architecture proves any of:

```text

eventual zero pulled-back residue blocks,
one explicit forever-defined seed,
or a seed-preserving all-time invariant.
```

Accordingly, further finite-prefix amplification or conditional growth inside those lanes is treading water relative to the full objective unless it changes the least-root/stabilization decision.
