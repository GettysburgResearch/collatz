# Many early ratio-five returns in the same fixed-target companion fiber

**PROPOSED, source-qualified written deduction.** This uses the two-ended bridge in PR201 at `73a145ca99448a78219bd149e273f33b6e3fda4b`. Its Input F is the extra-ternary-depth fixed-total lower bound derived there from Weaver, paper 48 v2, Lemma 7 and Theorem 8. Its Input R is PR146's conditional binary-Haar first-return tail and renewal law for the actual ratio-five marks. Shaik's quantitative polylogarithmic passage on ordinary sources is a separate input. Neither the additional analytic floor nor the renewal theorem was formally checked here. [PR201, §§1–3](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/collatz/blob/73a145ca99448a78219bd149e273f33b6e3fda4b/research/killed-green-head-closure-20261124/two-end-bridge/README.md) · [Weaver, paper 48](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/48) · [Shaik, Corollary 1.2 and Proposition 6.4](https://shaikidris.github.io/polylog/paper/)

Fix an ordinary odd unit $y$. For every sufficiently large $p$ there is one $e=2p+O_y(1)$, one $Y\ge1$, and one integer $k$ such that, with $G=2^e/3^p$ and $L_p=\sqrt{p\log(p+2)}$, at least $G\exp(-C_y L_p)$ distinct odd units $x$ satisfy all of the following:

* $T^e(x)=y$ and $T^e(5x)=Y$, with grades $p$ and $p+k$ respectively. The real first-arm offset is at most $1/2$, $|k|\le C_yL_p$, and $Y\le\exp(C_yL_p)$.
* On each of these same ordinary trajectories, at least $c\sqrt p$ positive clocks in the initial $\lfloor\eta(p-1)\rfloor-1$ shortcuts are completed ratio-five marks: the first-arm state is odd, the companion is exactly five times that state and their grades agree. Here $c,\eta>0$ are absolute and the individual mark times may depend on $x$.

**Proof.** Write $B=F_0+1$ for the first-arm offset bound in PR201 §3. Choose $s$ with $2^s y\equiv4\pmod 9$ sufficiently large that $r=(2^s y-1)/3>2B$ and $(1+3B)/2^s\le1/2$. Such $s$ occur in an arithmetic progression since 2 generates the units modulo 9. Set $b=p-1$. The cited §3 at $r$, source class $1\bmod3$, exact shortcut clock $2b$ and grade $b$ supplies $\gg(4/3)^b/\sqrt b$ distinct positive odd units with first-arm offset $\beta\le B$, each with $\gg\sqrt b$ actual completed initial ratio-five marks. Append the fixed scalar odd-to-odd letter $r\to y$ of duration $s$. The earlier two-arm marks are untouched. The resulting first-arm clock is $e=2b+s$, its grade is $p$, and its offset is $(3\beta+1)/2^s\le1/2$. Thus $G(y-1/2)\le x\le Gy$ and the scalar pool has $\gg_y G/\sqrt p$ members.

Take Shaik's ambient exceptional exponent greater than one and apply the resulting bound to the actual $5x\le5Gy$. The map $x\mapsto5x$ is injective, so removing the ambient exceptions costs $o_y(G/\sqrt p)$. On the remaining sources $c_*\log(5x)=e+O_y(1)$ for $c_*=2/\log(4/3)$. Shaik supplies a clock within $O_y(L_p)$ of $e$, with grade within $O_y(L_p)$ of $p$ and height at most a fixed power of $p$. Moving on this same positive trajectory to $e$ multiplies the upper height bound by at most $2^{O_y(L_p)}$ and changes the grade by at most $O_y(L_p)$. There are at most $\exp(C'_yL_p)$ exact pairs of terminal height and grade, so a single pair retains the claimed count. This last finite step is the [clock and record-counting argument](ClockTransfer.lean).

The bridge pays for its initial event by controlling additional ternary digits; a central middle segment remains free. The endpoint $Y$ is selected from the surviving cohort **after** its common first-arm endpoint is fixed. The number of early marks does not impose another mark at $e$. In particular the theorem does not establish $Y=5y$, the terminal grade condition $k=0$, or the large-relative-offset upper tail in PR201 §4.
