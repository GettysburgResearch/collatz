# Rational cycle denominators and ordinary rank curvature

**Collatz remains open.** The first packet gives lower bounds for full rational denominators in fixed-complexity families. The second identifies a local obstruction to strengthening the known rank convexity.

| Statement | Scope and evidence |
|---|---|
| [Fixed-run denominator theorem](SPARSE_DENOMINATORS.md) | Every positive rational shortcut cycle with exactly $k$ cyclic odd runs has $\liminf\log_2(q)/h\ge1/k$ along growing displayed lengths at fixed $k$. Repetitions attain this exponent. Primitive two-run words have exponent one. For all fixed $k$, coprime clock counts also give exponent one. The translate, valuation and height arguments are written, using Laurent, Levin and classical two-logarithm separation. |
| [Dispersed adjacent swaps](DISPERSED_EDITS.md) | For $b$ disjoint mixed adjacent swaps at arbitrary placements, the **difference of the actual rational fixed points** has complete denominator of exponential rate at least $1/b$ at fixed $b$. Two swaps have an exact gcd formula, including every proportional-clock resonance. An explicit primitive four-run family attains exponent $3/4$, ruling out general full-exponent growth based only on primitivity. |
| [Two moving motifs and language families](POWERED_LANGUAGES.md) | A primitive word formed by two changing powered motifs of sublinear total description has full rate at different slopes and rate at least one-half at equal slopes. Fixed bounded expressions permit arbitrarily many actual switches. Every infinite context-free family of primitive positive words contains a subsequence of denominator exponent at least one-half, with an example attaining the bound. |
| [Moving rational reference fragments](MOVING_ARCS.md) | Arbitrary covers by at most $r$ proper arcs with sublinear logarithmic reference heights force denominator exponent at least $1/r$, including unequal arc lengths. Coprime clocks force exponent one. [Cyclotomic clock factors](CYCLOTOMIC_CLOCKS.md) give stronger exponents when the common clock divisors have large prime factors only. |
| [Complete spectral denominator near one octave](SPECTRAL_NEAR_OCTAVE.md) | At high minimum and near-octave odd spread, the full reduced denominator has $\log_2q=(1+o(1))(h/g)J$ for every clock gcd $g$, with $J$ the actual active Fourier degree of the physical rank columns. The optimal degree is $1+\sum_{\ell^v\parallel g}\varphi(\ell^v)$, attained along every prescribed gcd sequence. Subexponential $q$ is possible exactly when the gcd divided by its largest prime-power factor tends to infinity. |
| [Odd rotations and the physical Fourier spectrum](SPECTRAL_ROTATIONS.md) | At high minimum and near one octave, every proper odd rotation satisfies $s(\ell)\log_2(q)/h\ge1-o(1)$. Independent prime clocks attain a product tending to four at an explicit lag, with all odd exponents one or two. |
| [Primary spectra and independent clocks](PRIMARY_SPECTRA.md) | Classifies all optimal frequency sets at arbitrary physical width. For integral cycles the growth of independent factors is limited by a shared chronological entropy budget, even if constant rows occur at arbitrarily different levels. At canonical width three two independent factors of size at least the reduced odd period to the power two thirds are excluded asymptotically. |
| [Coprime near-octave denominator](COPRIME_NEAR_OCTAVE.md) | Every coprime near-octave rational cycle has an exact short rank-polynomial representative for its full canceled factor. At minimum at least $h^{2-o(1)}$ and odd ratio at most $2+h^{-1+o(1)}$, the log cancellation is at most $h^{1/2+o(1)}$. |
| [All-gcd near-octave packing](NEAR_OCTAVE_PACKING.md) | On the same high-minimum, near-octave sequences, every reduced denominator has $\log q\ge h^{2/5.1163051-o(1)}$ even with unrestricted clock gcd. The proof bounds the number of modified mechanical dates, packs repeated physical gaps, and applies Wu–Wang to the reduced clocks. |
| [Independent parity clocks](INDEPENDENT_CLOCKS.md) | Explicit positive primitive rational cycles can have subexponential denominators, nearcritical clocks, minima at least $h^{2-o(1)}$, and odd amplitude only $2+h^{-1+o(1)}$. Independent periodic boundary changes account for the exact canceled cyclotomic factors. |
| [Effective arc height](ARC_HEIGHT.md) | Every partition into arbitrarily many proper arcs of a primitive rational cycle satisfies a computable weighted-variation inequality for their affine fixed points or for separate rational reference fragments. A second bound relates period, number of arcs, full denominator and maximum reference height even when the arc lengths are very unequal. |
| [Integer clock-language equivalence](CLOCK_LANGUAGES.md) | Finiteness of primitive positive integer cycles is equivalent to semilinearity of their complete clock image and to regularity or context-freeness of the language of complete cyclic words. An explicit rational margin yields a finite minimum cutoff. Semilinearity and exclusion of all nontrivial cycles remain open. |
| [Every shortcut-clock rotation](ALL_SHIFT_HEIGHTS.md) | A positive-forcing comparison gives a full denominator lower bound at every shortcut shift. For an ordinary cycle of growing period the Hamming distance from **each** proper rotation grows at least logarithmically, including order two. Published period and height floors give at least 42 mismatches, and at least 134 above $2^{100}$. [Lean paired-trace kernel](ShiftHeights.lean) |
| [Exponential denominator growth through six odd-valuation mismatches](ROTATION_DENOMINATORS.md) | Any growing family of primitive positive rational cycles with a proper rotation differing in at most six actual odd exponents satisfies $q>2^{p/4096}$, without assumptions on clock error, letter sizes or rotation order. Only finitely many ordinary cycles can have such a rotation. The onset is ineffective through Evertse. [Five-cut derivation](FIVE_ROTATIONS.md) · [Chronological six-cut proof](SIX_ROTATIONS.md) · [Twenty-nine full denominator certificate leaves in Lean](DenominatorDuals.lean) |
| [Odd valuation-word rotations](ODD_ROTATION_COMPLEXITY.md) | An additive factor count for shortcut suspension words, combined with Weaver’s finite-descent theorem at a medium-height upward crossing, excludes bounded odd-valuation Hamming distance along every sequence of unbounded rotation order. For arbitrary bounded mismatch counts above six, the growing-period, bounded-order case remains open. |
| [Window-sum denominators](WINDOW_DENOMINATORS.md) | A fixed number $s$ of exceptional elapsed division counts at any odd lag forces rational denominator exponent at least $1/s$ when odd count and shortcut period are comparable. Coprime clocks force full exponent one. The integer sparse-multiple mechanism comes from Paper 22/v4. |
| [Ordinary cycles after summing rank visits](FOLDED_FAREY.md) | For every fixed common clock divisor, the number of changes surviving summation across visits grows as a positive power of the odd period, with arbitrary physical spread. At upper convergents it is at least of square-root order. Signed chronological lifts can vanish on actual positive rational words. A Farey displacement makes every lift positive and controls gaps between supported dates. |
| [Native conversion of rank crossings](NATIVE_INVERSIONS.md) | Each directed dyadic crossing at a resonant odd lag corresponds at the same smaller source to a forward ternary crossing at the matched shortcut lag. Their integer offsets are linked by an actual short Collatz trajectory. [Lean transport and local crossing tests](NativeInversion.lean) |
| [Cubic rank sign pulse](CUBIC_SIGN_PULSE.md) | For any finite padding there are four positive integer trajectory segments with native binary exponents, strict first and second rank differences and physical ratio below four, whose third difference takes both signs. Their labels admit bounded-error geometric placement at a fixed interval. The four segments need not be ranks of one cycle. |

Classical integer $m$-cycle finiteness and several effective $m$-cycle exclusions predate this packet. PR193 treated all fixed-weight words statistically and changed one contiguous interval. PR192 obtained full-denominator estimates for arbitrary shifted words and for repeated backgrounds with varying markers. PR201 obtained strict second differences for the actual bounded integer branch using its named inputs. This packet's rational estimates are pointwise on its fixed-run or fixed-edit families. Arbitrary positive cycles can evade a fixed expression and need not have a two-motif presentation. No semilinear cover staying above the irrational boundary has been established.


[Shared-background odd cells](ONE_VARIABLE_RANK.md) retain the full moving marker Fourier support at any fixed number of copies, allowing unbounded marker sums. [Occupation and folded ranks](PACKED_RANKS.md) combines ordinary once-around packing with rank changes, including a separate width-three source count. [Near-circuit rotations](NEAR_CIRCUIT_FIVE.md) and [consecutive mismatch quotients](MISMATCH_CHAINS.md) show that eight or fewer mismatches and at most $p/4096$ excess divisions outside one specified odd departure force the complete denominator above $2^{p/4096}$ at sufficiently large odd period. The finite duals use exact rational arithmetic. The onset uses Evertse's external theorem.
