## 3. The concrete construction target after exact recognition

For the shortcut map and one unchanged positive input `n`, let `b_K` be
the original even count and `D_K` the complete complementary carry of its
actual length-`K` prefix. A triadic candidate `a/3^r` passes exactly the
congruence

\[
2^K\mid3^{b_K}a+3^rD_K.
\]

The improved specialized certificate uses

\[
F(r)=\prod_{j<r}(1+3^j),\qquad
H=9\operatorname{bl}\bigl(nF(r)\max(1,|a|)\bigr)+1.
\]

The new PR176 and PR177 work shows that passing this paid horizon
recognizes the exact all-time dual. PR182 additionally identifies the
reduced triadic exponent with the number of original-even steps before
the first visit to 2 and supplies a linear-step verifier using an exact
backward equality. None of these results produces a universally
successful exponent.

The arbitrary-odd-denominator certificate in the preceding PR178 packet
offers a larger search space. Its successful conclusion first establishes
eventual periodicity, with period at most twice the supplied denominator.
To settle full convergence through that larger space, the construction
must also control the resulting cycle. The distinction is material:
unrestricted rationality alone does not exclude other positive cycles.

The current construction lane therefore attacked the simultaneous original
and complementary carries. Generic rational reconstruction loses the
needed height margin; normalizing an even denominator can also lose
bits. The pinned simultaneous-place calculation has no strict saving.
The new attempt and its precise remaining real-growth estimate are
recorded in the construction lane, without claiming that a finite
recognizer constructs its own accepting input.

## 4. What an actual collision proof must retain

The preceding PR178 construction can choose one positive harmonic flow
whose inverse endpoint averages and the ordinary empirical endpoints
have relative collision ratio `1+O(N^(-1/2))`, uniformly at every
modulus and every finite deterministic inverse-clock list. Thus a
comparison rate can be arranged under the stated component hypotheses.

The complete source boxes still retain the binary clock, translated
full carry, and translated original source. Their effective count enters
through the square of a sum, with all cross-box terms. The exact finite
ordinary controls in the new PR182 show why exact local product
statistics do not by themselves bound this quantity: changing positive
sources can realize arbitrarily long prescribed shadows while keeping
large residue collisions. Their initial source pays the full dyadic
precision cost. This does not establish the same phenomenon forever
from one fixed source.

The new attempt therefore asks whether that cost can be charged globally
to the unchanged original source. It also tests whether the finite
weighted occupation measure can supply a stationary real law through
a change of clock. The collision lane retains the explicit source-height
term and addresses the conservativity of the proposed time change.
Formal balance of a generator is not silently identified with an
invariant probability of its actual process.

PR180's critical renewal divergence and rational-cycle classification
of atoms are useful restrictions on the actual stationary lift. Its
finite-Shannon-deficit and geometric-iid branches retain their different
limit-order requirements. Nonatomicity, positive boundary dimension,
and density of a compact fibre are not substituted for a quantitative
collision estimate on the original future endpoints.

## 6. Complete cycle arithmetic and chronology

The current full-denominator estimates provide lower bounds on the
complementary denominator. The new cycle attack sought an opposing
restriction from the original positive integer closure. It retained
all cancellation in both numerical carries and checked whether a
prime-weight potential or a multiplicative-semigroup theorem could

A product balance can arise from separate ordinary paths. Therefore
one must distinguish a multiplicative identity from chronological
closure of one orbit. The cycle lane records an exact ordinary
circulation test for the proposed potential class and the precise
boundary of the semigroup literature; it does not treat that identity
as a positive Collatz cycle. Nor is a finite example above one floor
asserted to extend above every prescribed floor.

## 7. Outcome and priorities

This pass made direct attempts at the missing construction and law
comparison, including a literature-based multiplicative approach to
cycle closure. The new lane results and their independent reviews are
linked from the packet index. None supplies a universal witness,
the actual collision upper, or a complete positive-cycle exclusion.

The highest-value proof obligations remain concrete:

1. Construct a candidate for the actual source with enough agreement to
   pay its own horizon, and retain the denominator restriction needed
   for convergence.
2. Control the original future-source quotient and carry alignment, or
   another collision observable sufficient for the same stationary
   contradiction, with the exact order of limits retained.
3. Derive a full-denominator restriction from positive chronological
   closure that contradicts the available complementary capacity bound.
4. Alternatively, produce a genuinely complete physical descent or
   rewrite order whose accounting survives every guard exit and mode
   change.

These are alternative direct targets. Their unresolved premises are
not removed by naming stronger machinery whose hypotheses have not
been established for the actual source and law.
