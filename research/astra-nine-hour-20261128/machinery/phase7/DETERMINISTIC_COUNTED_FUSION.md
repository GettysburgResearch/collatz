# Steering a canonical pair to counted fusion on a positive integer cylinder

A canonical state \((k,C)\), \(k\ge0\), \(C\in\mathbb Z\), denotes current values \(z,3^kz+C\). The second path has already made \(k\) more odd departures than the first. At \(k=0\) the paths can be interchanged. For the shortcut map \(T(z)=z/2\) on even inputs and \((3z+1)/2\) on odd inputs, there is a finite choice of current anchor parities that sends every such state to \((0,0)\). Paper 212 already gives this constructive steering, including the positive-level descent and the two-step contraction at level zero. We reconstruct the actual cylinder lifting and extract an explicit witness length of at most \(7\log(2+|C|)+5\) at level zero for the downstream coding bound. Leen and Weaver give stronger **probability bounds** for the same scalar dynamics. [Lean Assurance Lab, Paper 212, “A translation on a prescribed class”](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/212) · [Leen, scalar update kernel](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23268751) · [Weaver 124, Lemma 2](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/124)

At \(k>0\) and nonzero even \(C\), choose the lower anchor even, making both paths even and halving \(C\). When \(C=0\), choose the lower anchor odd. Both paths are odd and the new constant \((1-3^k)/2\) is nonzero, with unchanged level. When \(C\) is odd, choose the lower anchor odd; the other is even. The resulting state is
\[
                     (k-1,(C-3^{k-1})/2).
\]
The finite division of a nonzero integer by powers of two makes an odd integer, so each positive level is left in finitely many steps. The level never increases in this part.

At \(k=0\), interchange the arms if needed so their difference \(d=|C|\) is nonnegative. For even \(d>0\), choose both even and halve \(d\). If \(d=4k+3\), choose the lower value \(x=4z+2\). At two steps the two values are \(3z+2\) and \(3z+2+(3k+2)\), each with one odd departure. If \(d=4k+1\) with \(k\ge1\), choose \(x=4z+1\). At two steps the values are \(3z+1\) and \(3z+1+(3k+1)\), again with one odd departure each. These are the two contractions in Paper 212. For all these nonterminal blocks the new magnitude satisfies
\[
                d'-1 \le \tfrac34(d-1),
\]
with block length at most two. At \(d=1\), choose \(x=8z+4\); three steps give the same value \(3z+2\) with one odd departure on each arm. To see the clock bound, when \(d\ge2\) each block contracts the positive integer \(d-1\) by at least a factor \(3/4\) until it is below one. At most \(1+\lfloor\log(d-1)/\log(4/3)\rfloor\) such blocks suffice, followed by at most three steps. Since \(2/\log(4/3)<7\), the stated bound follows.

Every chosen branch is realized on an actual ordinary integer cylinder. Initially write the free binary source variable as \(z\). After \(j\) chosen canonical bits the realized initial sources are one complete residue \(r\bmod2^j\), and the current canonical anchor at \(r+2^j l\) has form \(A l+B\) with \(A\) an odd integer. This holds at \(j=0\). Choosing its parity prescribes a unique bit of \(l\). After imposing that bit, either output anchor is obtained by an affine numerator with odd leading coefficient from the old anchor or its partner, followed by one halving. Substitution \(l=l_0+2l'\) cancels that halving, so the new leading coefficient is again odd. This covers the swap at level zero. At the end the parity of the common value can be prescribed by one more residue bit **without taking another step**. Choosing a sufficiently large positive initial lift makes all original starting values positive, after which the ordinary shortcut rules preserve positivity.

In particular, for any nonidentity odd-preserving \(G(x)=2^m x+B\), \(m\ge0\), first advance the second start by \(m\) steps. That binary prefix is fixed by \(B\bmod2^m\), and the result is \(3^kx+C\) with its already accrued count gap \(k\). Force the first canonical bit so that the original \(x\) is odd. This may change the level or swap its orientation, after which the preceding steering works from the new integer state. Prescribe an odd common endpoint at the end. Thus some positive odd sources \(x,G(x)\) reach a common odd endpoint at times \(t,t+m\) with identical total odd counts. When \(m=0\) and \(B=2d\ne0\), the forced initial odd step sends the zero-level offset to \(3d\). The remaining steering therefore takes \(O(\log(2+|d|))\) steps with an absolute explicit constant. This gives the exact source cylinders used in the affine classification and quantitative jitter estimate.

The [Lean kernel](FusionSteering.lean) proves the nonnegative zero-level case as a true orbit statement. For every fixed offset and every odd-slope affine readout of the unread integer, `every_offset` gives a positive-stride progression of parameters with equal physical endpoints, equal odd counts and a common odd terminal value. `odd_source_progression` additionally forces both actual starting values positive odd at every prescribed even displacement. The formal theorem is existential and contains no logarithmic clock estimate; the positive-level signed reduction and the explicit clock estimate above are written arguments.
