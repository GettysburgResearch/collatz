# Only actual time shifts have common mass on the two-sided critical source

Let \(p(e)=\theta(1-\theta)^{e-1}\), and take a bilateral iid gap sequence. Its ternary past coordinate \(Z\in\mathbb Z_3^\times\) has law \(\mu_\theta\) from the maps \(A_e(z)=(1+3z)/2^e\). Its independent dyadic future coordinate \(X\in1+2\mathbb Z_2\) has law \(\nu_\theta\) from the contractions \(I_e(x)=(2^ex-1)/3\). Every \(I_e\) of an odd dyadic unit is odd and has next ordinary forward gap exactly \(e\). Thus this future coding is one-to-one everywhere the positive gaps continue and has cylinder weights \(p(w)=\prod_i p(e_i)\). Write \(M_\theta=\mu_\theta\otimes\nu_\theta\) on \(\mathbb Q_3\times\mathbb Q_2\). The natural forward map reads the next gap of \(X\) and applies the *same* \(A_e\) to both coordinates. This shifts one letter from the future to the past and preserves \(M_\theta\). Above and at the entropy threshold the past code is also recoverable almost surely, giving an invertible bilateral Bernoulli model. Let \(g(z)=2^a3^bz+c\), with \(a,b\in\mathbb Z\), \(c\in\mathbb Z[1/6]\), act diagonally on these two local fields. For every \(\theta_*\le\theta<1\),
\[
       M_\theta\not\perp g_*M_\theta
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g=A_w\text{ or }g=A_w^{-1}
                       \text{ for a finite word }w.                     \tag{1}
\]
For either of the two pure transformations the overlap mass is exactly \(p(w)\); the common measures agree identically on their shared carrier. For the empty word this mass is one. Every other transformation in the stated group gives zero overlap.

Indeed the [ternary affine classification](../phase7/CRITICAL_AFFINE_OVERLAPS.md) first requires a representation \(g=A_uA_v^{-1}\). Cancel all equal final gaps on the two words. Suppose both words remain, \(u=u'e\) and \(v=v'f\), with \(e\ne f\). Starting from any odd dyadic \(x\), the intermediate \(y=A_v^{-1}(x)\) is odd with forward gap exactly \(f\): the first applied forward map in \(A_v\) is \(A_f\). The 2-adic valuation of \(A_e(y)\) is therefore \(f-e\). A negative valuation remains negative under every subsequent \(A_j\), while a strictly positive valuation gives a negative valuation after the next \(A_j\). Thus \(g(x)\) is outside the odd dyadic integers. The two product supports are disjoint in this coordinate. This leaves only the pure cases.

For \(g=A_w\), the only odd inputs with odd output have precisely the required forward prefix (the word in reverse order in the composition convention for \(A_w\)). At the first mismatch, the same valuation calculation shows the final output cannot be odd. This forward cylinder has \(\nu_\theta\)-mass \(p(w)\) and maps onto fresh \(\nu_\theta\). On the ternary side \((A_w)_*\mu_\theta=\mu_\theta|_{C_w}/p(w)\) on its full coded past cylinder \(C_w\). The two factors multiply to give exactly \(M_\theta|_{C_w\times(1+2\mathbb Z_2)}\) on the target, and the rest of the pushforward is singular there. For the inverse, invert this matched restriction. The shared target is the full past coordinate times the required dyadic forward cylinder, again with mass \(p(w)\).

There is a conull Borel set on which the group relation in (1) is exactly the time-shift relation. For each of the countably many singular transformations, remove a null set and its necessary null images so no two retained points are paired by it. For a pure transformation, outside the matched time-shift domains the pushforward is singular and can likewise be removed. Intersect with all integer time translates of the resulting conull set under the invertible source shift. Then any allowed diagonal rational affine relation between two retained points is a finite forward or backward segment of their own orbit, and every finite time shift stays inside the set. This applies to typical points of the product law. All ordinary diagonal points may be removed from it without changing any mass.

For every \(0<\theta<\theta_*\), the ternary result gives some \(k\ge1\) such that \((H^k)_*\mu_\theta\) overlaps \(\mu_\theta\), where \(H(x)=4x+1\). This \(H^k\) has the form \(2^m x+B\) and maps ordinary odd integers to odd integers. The positive counted-fusion witness gives \(A_u=A_vH^k\), so \(H^k=A_v^{-1}A_u\). On dyadic inputs carrying the forward word \(u\), of probability \(p(u)>0\), \(A_u\) gives a fresh odd tail and \(A_v^{-1}\) inserts an honest forward word \(v\). This gives positive overlap also on the dyadic side. Products of the two individual common submeasures give a nonzero product common part. Since \(H^k\) is neither a pure word nor its inverse (its slope is a nontrivial power of two), (1) fails. Thus the all-group statement (1) characterizes exactly the parameter half-interval \([\theta_*,1)\).

In fact (1) classifies **every rational affine map** at and above the entropy threshold, including negative slopes. Indeed the odd future law \(\nu_\theta\) is exactly the physical biased binary source \(\rho_\theta\) conditioned on initially odd. The successive gaps after that prescribed bit have precisely the geometric law \(p\). Thus a common submeasure on the odd binary coordinates requires a common submeasure for the full biased sources. The [complete binary affine criterion](BIASED_BINARY_FUSION.md) forces any rational slope to be \(2^a3^b\) and the translation to belong to \(2^{\min(a,0)}\mathbb Z[1/3]\subset\mathbb Z[1/6]\). All qualifying maps have already entered the proof of (1). The critical parameter is strictly greater than the fair value, so the biased-source criterion applies. In particular the almost-sure relation for rational affine maps on this two-sided source is precisely its time-shift relation.

There is a corresponding statement across the real-drift threshold. Use the full mean-contracting past boundary rather than just its ternary marginal: this is the joint real–ternary law when \(\theta<\log2/\log3\), and the ternary law otherwise. The [joint word decoder](TILTED_JOINT_FIBRES.md) gives measurable recovery of the past word for every parameter in the first range; direct ternary recovery applies in the second. For a map \(g=2^a3^b x+c\), \(c\in\mathbb Z[1/6]\), the proof of the [past affine classification](../phase7/CRITICAL_AFFINE_OVERLAPS.md) requires only (i) almost-sure recovery of the past word, (ii) a ternary coordinate to test unit-cylinder compatibility and (iii) the fact that every real or ternary affine identity obtained from finite counted fusion holds identically on all included coordinates. Hence it applies verbatim to the full contracting past at every tilt: normalize first by word prefixes, exclude each nonidentity normalized odd-preserving affine map using its pair of distinct equal-depth fusion words, then exhaust unmatched pairs by their distinct cylinders. The matches retain exactly their original cylinder weights. Combining this classification with the odd future as above gives the pure time-shift conclusion on the product of the full past boundary and independent odd future, for every \(0<\theta<1\) and every transformation in the displayed group. For every biased parameter, the binary criterion further extends the conclusion to every rational affine map. The argument for the fair parameter on rational maps *outside* the displayed group would need a different restriction than the biased likelihood lattice.
