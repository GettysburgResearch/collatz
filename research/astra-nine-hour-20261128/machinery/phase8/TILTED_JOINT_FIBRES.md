# Conditional real dimension and shrinking cylinders at ordinary ternary roots

Take independent positive gaps of geometric law \(p(e)=\theta(1-\theta)^{e-1}\), total \(S_n=\sum_{i\le n}e_i\). For a word of length \(n\), write its affine map as
\[
         A_w(z)=\beta_w+\lambda_w z,\quad \lambda_w=3^n/2^{S_n},\quad
         \beta_w=\sum_{j=1}^n3^{j-1}2^{-S_j}.
\]
The same infinite word has a ternary limit \(Z\) of law \(\mu_\theta\). If \(0<\theta<\log2/\log3\), its real limit \(B\) is finite almost surely, since \(g=(\log2)/\theta-\log3>0\). It has a moment of some order \(0<\alpha\le1\): the expected multiplier to this power is \(3^\alpha\theta/(2^\alpha-(1-\theta))<1\) for small positive \(\alpha\), and subadditivity of that power bounds the series of expected moments. Write \(H=H(p)\), \(b=(H-\log3)_+\). Throughout, conditioning on an exact individual ternary point is used only almost surely in its marginal law. Conditioning at an ordinary integer below uses positive-mass finite cylinders instead.

## Prefix decoding and the dimension of a typical real fibre

In the mean-real-contraction range, the pair \((B,Z)\) almost surely determines the entire iid gap word. Here is an elementary arithmetic proof. For fixed \(n,S_n\), the offsets \(\beta_w\) sharing \(Z\bmod3^n\) belong to a real lattice coset of spacing \(\lambda=3^n/2^{S_n}\). Their numerators \(2^{S_n}\beta_w\) are distinct: the affine map with that numerator and fixed slope is the same only for the same gap word, by positive-gap word freeness (equivalently peel its final gap from the dyadic valuation after one letter is removed). Set \(B'= (B-\beta_w)/\lambda\), the real mark of the iid tail. Knowing \(\lfloor B'\rfloor\), in addition to \(B,Z,n,S_n\), fixes \(\beta_w\) in an interval open on the left and closed on the right of width \(\lambda\), hence fixes the word. Therefore
\[
 H(E_1,\ldots,E_n\mid B,Z)\le H(S_n)+H(\lfloor B'\rfloor)=O_\theta(\log(n+1)).
\]
Indeed a nonnegative integer of mean \(n/\theta\) has entropy \(O_\theta(\log(n+1))\) by comparison with a geometric law, and the finite positive moment of \(B'\) gives finite floor entropy by comparison with masses proportional to \((1+j)^{-2}\). Given the initial word the map between the residual joint limit and the initial joint limit is invertible; independence and the entropy chain rule make the left side exactly \(nH(E_1\mid B,Z)\). Thus this one-letter entropy is zero and recovery repeats indefinitely. Brofferio's general rational-affine Poisson-boundary theorem identifies the product of the mean-contracting local fields. Here these fields are precisely \(\mathbb R\) and \(\mathbb Q_3\) in this range and the step law has finite global logarithmic moment. The lattice proof gives the explicit word-recovery bound in this specialization. [Brofferio, *Annales de l'Institut Fourier* 56 (2006), main theorem, pp. 500–501](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2006__56_2_499_0.pdf)

For \(\mu_\theta\)-almost every \(z\), the conditional real law \(\eta_z=\mathcal L(B\mid Z=z)\) is exact dimensional of dimension
\[
              \boxed{\displaystyle\dim\eta_z=\frac{(H(p)-\log3)_+}{(\log2)/\theta-\log3}.}       \tag{1}
\]
The numerator is the exact posterior entropy of one gap given the ternary coordinate, by the [ternary entropy identity and Leen's Theorem C](../phase7/CRITICAL_AFFINE_OVERLAPS.md). The ratio is at most one by \(H(p)\le (\log2)/\theta\), the entropy bound from comparison with the fair geometric law. Below the coding threshold it equals one only for \(\theta=1/2\), with strict inequality elsewhere. At and above the coding threshold, ternary decoding alone makes the conditional law a point mass in the contracting range.

Here are details justifying the local assertion. Let \(Q_z\) be the posterior word law. Along a typical iid sample, the conditional prefix probability factors into the successive fresh one-letter posteriors, since the known past prefix relates the two ternary coordinates by an invertible affine map. The logarithm of this probability divided by \(-n\) therefore tends to \(b\) by Birkhoff on the iid shift; the one-letter log loss has finite expectation \(b\). Also \(S_n/n\to1/\theta\), and the real mark \(B_n\) of the shifted tail is eventually at most \(e^{\epsilon n}\), for every \(\epsilon>0\), by the finite moment and Borel–Cantelli. Disintegrate these three full-measure assertions. For each fixed \(\epsilon>0\), for almost every \(z\) the increasing sets \(G_N\) where all three bounds hold at all \(n\ge N\) exhaust posterior probability one. Use tolerances \(\epsilon\) for the first and third bounds and \(\epsilon/\log2\) for the average-gap bound, so \(e^{-(g+\epsilon)n}\le\lambda_w\le e^{-(g-\epsilon)n}\).

Fix such a \(z,N\), and push \(Q_z|_{G_N}\) to the real line, obtaining \(\eta_{z,N}\). In a real interval of length \(e^{-(g+\epsilon)n}\), for each permitted total \(S_n\) there are at most \(2+e^{\epsilon n}\) candidate word offsets: they are spaced by \(\lambda_w\), and an admissible offset must lie to the left of the right endpoint and no further left than \(\lambda_w e^{\epsilon n}\) from the left endpoint. There are \(O_\theta(n)\) totals, and each candidate word has posterior mass at most \(e^{-(b-\epsilon)n}\). Interpolation between successive radii gives lower local dimension at least \((b-2\epsilon)/(g+\epsilon)\) for this restricted measure everywhere it has mass, with the trivial zero bound when necessary. The differentiation theorem for finite Borel measures on \(\mathbb R\) transfers this lower bound to \(\eta_z\) at \(\eta_{z,N}\)-almost every point: on that set \(d\eta_{z,N}/d\eta_z>0\) and the ratio of their shrinking symmetric interval masses tends to that derivative. Exhaustion handles almost every point of \(\eta_z\).

Conversely, for \(Q_z\)-almost every word in \(G_N\), the conditional fraction of its prefix cylinder belonging to \(G_N\) tends to one by martingale convergence. For all large \(n\), at least half its cylinder mass \(\ge e^{-(b+\epsilon)n}\) belongs to \(G_N\). These continuations all have real locations in the same interval of length \(\lambda_w e^{\epsilon n}\le e^{-(g-2\epsilon)n}\), containing the original sample point. Use a centered interval of twice that radius for its total real mass. Interpolation gives upper local dimension at most \((b+\epsilon)/(g-2\epsilon)\) for \(0<2\epsilon<g\). Exhaustion and a countable sequence of tolerances tending to zero prove (1). In particular, for every \(s\) below (1), a countable collection of conditional submeasures with upper interval masses \(C|I|^s\) exhausts almost every fibre law.

At the fair parameter Weaver's reviewed Paper 19 already proves that the joint law is singular to real–ternary Haar, has full product dimension two and has an explicit logarithmic entropy loss. It also gives Cesàro limits at specified ordinary integer cylinders. The equal one-step Jacobian and probability at the fair parameter coexist with this singularity. Formula (1) disintegrates in the ternary direction for all mean-contracting geometric tilts. General projection entropy and dimension formulae for conformal coding maps are a relevant precedent. [Weaver 19/v2, §§9–11](https://rstation.ai/r/collatz/posts/33) · [Feng and Hu, dimension theory for iterated function systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2036)

## A fixed ordinary nonperiodic root at each depth

Let \(y\) be a specified positive odd ternary unit that is nonperiodic under the accelerated odd map. Each word of length \(n\) compatible with \(Z\equiv y\bmod3^{n+1}\) has a positive odd unit source \(x=(y-\beta_w)/\lambda_w\). Indeed the extra ternary digit ensures division gives a terminal unit and the previous digits peel every integer reverse step. Each integral reverse step from a positive odd parent has positive odd child. The path first hits \(y\) at its terminal forward time, because an earlier hit would make \(y\) periodic. Let \(h_n(y)\) be the least such source. These minima tend to infinity: each particular ordinary source first hits the fixed root at most once, while there are infinitely many legal reverse choices and at least one at every depth. Thus \(\lambda_w=(y-\beta_w)/x\le y/h_n(y)\), uniformly over every compatible word. All these offsets belong to the compact genuine first-hit set \(K_y\).

Conditional on this prefix and the displayed ternary cylinder, the tail code is iid conditioned solely on one specified terminal unit trit. Its real mark has \(\alpha\)-moment bounded by a fixed \(C_\theta\), independent of the prefix and of \(y,n\), because there are just two terminal laws of positive probability. Consequently, at every depth,
\[
\Pr\bigl(\operatorname{dist}(B,K_y)>u\mid Z\equiv y\bmod3^{n+1}\bigr)
  \le C_\theta u^{-\alpha}\left(\frac{y}{h_n(y)}\right)^\alpha.           \tag{2}
\]
The conditioning events are nonempty and have positive mass, since there are true inverse words of any depth ending at unit sources, and the two terminal unit trits carry positive probability. Tightness follows from (2) and compactness of \(K_y\); each weak subsequential limit of these fixed-depth posterior real laws is supported on \(K_y\). Earlier work proves \(K_y\) has real length zero, even at ordinary roots where its Hausdorff dimension is one. Thus (1) describes typical exact fibres, while (2) controls the real marks inside shrinking cylinders about a specified exceptional integer and survives arbitrary changes in the total frequency of those cylinders. A pointwise lower bound on that frequency is a separate unresolved input.
