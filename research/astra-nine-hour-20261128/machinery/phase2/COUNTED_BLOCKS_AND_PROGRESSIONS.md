# Consecutive source blocks and arithmetic progressions in a prescribed exact Collatz row

**Source-qualified written deductions.** Fix an actual positive odd unit \(a\), an odd-step depth \(n\), and an integer \(s\) with \(|s-2n|\le\sqrt n\). Write \(L=2^s/3^n\), and use \(T(x)=x/2\) for even \(x\) and \(T(x)=(3x+1)/2\) for odd \(x\). A source in the row satisfies \(T^s(x)=a\) with exactly \(n\) odd departures. For odd \(x\) this is \(U^n x=a,\ S_n(x)=s\).

* There are absolute \(c,F>0\) such that for all large \(n\), at least \(cL/\sqrt n\) distinct odd anchors \(x\in[(a-F)L,aL]\) start a block of \(\lfloor\exp(cn^{1/8})\rfloor\) **consecutive positive integers**, each in the same row \((a,n,s)\). A single anchor's entire block is counted-fused in its first \(\lfloor\epsilon n\rfloor\) shortcut ticks, where \(\epsilon>0\) is a sufficiently small absolute constant. Different anchors may have overlapping blocks.
* For each fixed \(k\ge3\), every odd \(M\le\exp(\delta n)\) and every \(u\bmod M\), the same window contains \(\gg_k L/(M\sqrt n)\) pairwise disjoint nonconstant \(k\)-term arithmetic progressions of odd row members in \(u\bmod M\), after reducing the implied constant by a factor depending on \(k\). Their common differences are at most \(M\exp(C_k(1+\log n)^{A_k})\). Every participating valuation can be capped by \((3/2)\log_2(n+1)+O(1)\).
* For a hypothetical escaping \(m\), these exact-clock rows at \(a=U^nm,s=2n\) realize every sufficiently large corrected height \(2^\ell P(m)\). In particular its full ordinary shortcut level set contains complete consecutive blocks of the indicated increasing length. The modulus and bounded-gap progression conclusion occupies the same level when one restricts to odd sources.

The first assertion uses the previously written deeper **pure ternary** atom floor in Weaver and Wren 48 v2, together with Weaver 124's uniform Haar synchronization bound. The second uses (for \(M>1\) with a coprime-to-three factor) the proposed mixed-conductor enhancement. Its short common difference uses the quantitative, all-length OpenAI progression theorem. These statements concern a fixed ordinary endpoint, count and clock, uniformly in all three.

## Copying a binary prefix without changing its displacements

For a shortcut word of \(t\) bits and \(K\) odd departures let \(N\) be its nonnegative numerator, so \(T^t(x)=(3^Kx+N)/2^t\) on its one binary source cylinder. If it takes \(b\) to \(y\), replacing \(y\) by \(y'=y+3^K h\) replaces its source by \(b'=b+2^t h\). The same word is realized since the first \(t\) bits are unchanged. One can also reverse its letters: successive even inverse steps are \(2y\), and odd inverses are \((2y-1)/3\). Full integral divisibility implies each intermediate division by three is integral (powers of two are invertible modulo three), and each integral reverse step from a positive integer stays positive. Thus \(b'>0\) even when \(h<0\). For several counted-fused words with common \(t,K,y\), this operation translates **every** source by the same \(2^t h\), preserving every displacement. [Weaver 124, equations (7)–(8)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/124)

Choose \(t=\lfloor\epsilon n\rfloor\), and \(D=\lfloor\exp(c n^{1/8})\rfloor\). Under Haar sampling of the first source, the probability that some two elements of its length-\(D\) block have not counted-fused at time \(t\) is at most \(C(1+\log D)^4/\sqrt{t+1}\). For fixed \(\epsilon\), take \(c\) small so this is less than \(1/32\). The first source and its time-\(t\) endpoint are both odd with probability \(1/4\), by the exact independent parity bits through position \(t\). Its count \(K\) in the first \(t\) positions has the binomial law. For an absolute \(B\), the event \(|K-t/2|>B\sqrt t\) has probability less than \(1/32\). Intersecting the events leaves at least \(3/16\) of all binary source residues modulo \(2^{t+1}\). Fusion through \(t\), the common count and odd endpoint parity are all determined by this finite residue. Equality of states for a successful block persists at every lift by the affine identity above. [Weaver 124, Theorem 1 and equations (7)–(8)](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/124)

Fix any one of those residues and take a positive ordinary representative \(b\). Its block fuses to a positive odd \(y\) at \(t\), of count \(K\ge1\), so \(3\nmid y\). Seek an actual odd \(y'\) in the row from \(a\) with \(n'=n-K\), \(s'=s-t\), and \(y'\equiv y\bmod3^K\). Then \(|s'-2n'|\le \sqrt n+2B\sqrt t\), a fixed central window. Choose \(\epsilon\) so small that \(K\) is in the absolute deeper-modulus budget \(K\le\delta_0 n'\) from the pure ternary fixed-total floor. The standard construction allows a fixed central factor greater than one; alternatively first prove the assertion on \(|s-2n|\le\sqrt n/2\), and use the larger fixed central comparison for the outer half. The floor supplies
\[
           \gg\frac{2^{s-t}}{3^{\,n-K}3^K\sqrt{n-K}}
           \gg\frac{L}{2^t\sqrt n}                                  \tag{1}
\]
distinct such \(y'\) with bounded real intercept. The corresponding copied anchors have \(b'\equiv b\bmod 2^{t+1}\) since \(y',y\) are both odd, so \(h\) is even. Distinct \(y'\) give distinct anchors; different initial good residues give disjoint anchor sets. Summing (1) over \(\gg 2^t\) initial residues proves the first count. For each \(y'\), all members of the copied full block run on to \(a\) with the same shortcut count and time.

For the real bound, write the normalized forward affine offset of the initial \(t\) steps as \(\beta_{\rm in}=N/2^t\). Each step has real slope at most \(3/2\) and additive offset at most \(1/2\), so \(\beta_{\rm in}\le (3/2)^t-1\). The combined offset is
\[
            \beta_{\rm out}+(3^{n-K}/2^{s-t})\beta_{\rm in}.
\]
The first term is bounded by the selected outer floor. The second is uniformly bounded because \(s-t=2(n-K)+O(\sqrt n)\) and \(\epsilon\) is small. Every copied source therefore lies in \([(a-F)L,aL]\), enlarging \(F\) once. This argument does not impose an \(O(\log n)\) exponent cap on an entire long consecutive block. A block of odd anchors longer than \(2^J\) necessarily contains a source with \(3x+1\equiv0\bmod2^{J+1}\). [Weaver and Wren 48 v2, Theorem 8 and bounded-real word assembly](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/48) · [Earlier arbitrary fixed central-factor comparison](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/10/v3)

A prescribed odd anchor residue \(u\bmod M\) can also be retained. For each good template require
\(y'\equiv y+3^K2^{-t}(u-b)\bmod3^KM\),
with \(2^{-t}\) taken modulo \(M\) inside the parenthesis. Choose \(\epsilon,\delta\) so that \(K+\log M\) fits the mixed budget. The count (1) gains a factor \(1/M\), and the same sum shows \(\gg L/(M\sqrt n)\) qualifying anchors **in that one class**. Their consecutive block includes all the intervening classes. In particular one can prescribe equal differences \(2M\) for a length-\(k\) progression whenever \(2(k-1)M<D\).

## The quantitative OpenAI progression theorem on the full modulus range

For \(M\le\exp(\delta n)\), take the bounded-real, bounded-valuation sources of the [mixed ordinary row bound, (5)](../ARITHMETIC_PROGRESSION_FLOOR.md). They give a set \(A\) of at least \(cL/(M\sqrt n)\) odd integers in one class modulo \(M\), in an interval of length \(FL\). Write each as \(u+Mz\); the resulting \(z\)'s belong to an integer interval of length \(N\le FL/M+2\). Reduce \(\delta\) so that \(L/M\) grows exponentially in \(n\). Thus the density in the translated integer interval is at least \(c'/\sqrt n\). The restriction that the original sources are odd merely puts these \(z\)'s in one parity class when \(M\) is odd.

OpenAI's written Theorem 1.1 gives \(r_k(B)\le C_kB\exp[-c_k(\log B)^{\eta_k}]\), where \(r_k(B)\) is the largest size of a \(k\)-progression-free subset of \([1,B]\). Choose \(B=\lceil\exp(C'_k(1+\log n)^{A_k})\rceil\) so that \(r_k(B)/B\le c'/(8\sqrt n)\). Then \(B/N\to0\). Divide the translated row interval into blocks of length \(B\), padding the last block. In each block successively remove whole nonconstant \(k\)-term progressions until the remainder contains none. All remainders together have size at most \((N/B+1)r_k(B)\le c'N/(4\sqrt n)\) for large \(n\). This leaves at least \(\gg_k L/(M\sqrt n)\) disjoint progressions of the original stipulated sources, each of common difference at most \(MB\). This proof uses a local count. Even on escape the global reciprocal sum of the synchronized grade is finite.

## The source clock and the deeper-precision counting obstruction

For an escaping positive odd \(m\), putting \(a=U^n m\) and \(s=2n\) gives exactly the levels of [the original-grade shell result](ZERO_GRADE_SHELLS.md). On even members use the shortcut extension \(P(2x)=2P(x)\). For all members of a copied block \(P(x)=2^s3^{-n}P(a)\). Thus a whole increasing consecutive run can indeed have identical corrected height, so pointwise strict increase or short-scale finite-difference curvature for \(P\) along the integers would be a substantive extra hypothesis.

The actual physical clock \(A_n(m)\le n\log_2 3+O_m(1)\) remains below the balanced row. Suppose one keeps a prescribed genuine initial portion with \(K\) odd departures, cost at least \(K\), then attempts to use a fair central suffix with \(n-K\) odds. To land at \(A_n(m)+o(n)\) requires
\[
 2n-K+o(n)\le A_n(m)+o(n),\qquad
 K\ge(2-\log_2 3)n-o(n).
\]
A suffix method **uniform in every** required depth-\(K\) extra ternary target digit must represent all \(\asymp3^n\) terminal classes by words of length \(n-K\). At any fair-central total \(2(n-K)+o(n)\) there are at most \(2^{2(n-K)+o(n)}\) such words, even before any other condition. This forces
\[
       K\le(1-\tfrac12\log_2 3)n+o(n).
\]
The required lower coefficient is exactly twice this upper coefficient. An argument at the actual clock must exploit correlation between the particular source prefix and its target. Uniform finer-modulus routing, even with optimal constants, cannot supply all of those data by a fair suffix. A prescribed full block in a central row still lies entirely outside the asymptotic normalized zero-grade mass established in the shell result.
