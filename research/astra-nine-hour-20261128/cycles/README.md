# Cycle arithmetic

The shortcut map is $T(x)=x/2$ on even cleared numerators and $T(x)=(3x+1)/2$ on odd cleared numerators, with one common odd denominator $d$. Physical periods below are least periods. All results are proposed within their displayed scopes.

- [Deterministic residue quotients](MODULAR_QUOTIENTS.md) shows that a noninjective compatible modulus forces one nonconstant column at its projected clock. A local dictionary at the actual minimum bounds the projected period by $O_B(\log n/\log\log n)$ when $d\le n^B$. Its centered return carry also bounds the entire modulus. Thus every ordinary cycle must exhibit genuine branch ambiguity modulo every odd $M$ in $[n^\varepsilon,n)$ at sufficiently large $n$.
- [Contracting calendars](CONTRACTING_CALENDARS.md) gives the strict whole-denominator inequality $dK>(m-1)(B-A)(B-3A)$ for a fixed calendar with one free bit and two contracting block maps. It gives an independent all-clock ordinary exclusion without a computational Collatz verification floor. The qualitative exclusion also follows by combining the earlier PR191 and PR212 results credited there. In the remaining straddling case, a true minimum forces a long preceding run when the short-block gap is small.

The [finite arithmetic check](check_block_carries.py) compares all 20,465 admissible pairs with $1\le s<256$ and replays 2,331 literal primitive positive rational calendars with $s\le6$ and two to five blocks. It checks 1,650 contracting and 681 straddling examples, 3,608 local-packing instances and 2,381 compatible image instances. These are counts in the stated deliberately bounded tests, allowing rotated presentations. The analytic large-$s$ argument and the whole-cycle statements are written proofs.

[ModularQuotient.lean](ModularQuotient.lean) checks the mixed source, centered scalar return, sliding-window comparisons and the local finite dictionary consumer. [ContractingCalendar.lean](ContractingCalendar.lean) contains candidate kernels for the strict span comparison, the ternary residual and the finite and intermediate-base inequalities. Only the modular-quotient file had a completed strict kernel replay at the initial checkpoint. Each proof's native and external boundaries are given in the corresponding note.
