This checkpoint connects the repository's forward sections, uniform
synchronization, ordinary comparison orbits, and cycle arithmetic. Three
GPT-6.2 Astra agents developed separate routes and independently reviewed
the arguments in the other lanes. The coordinating agent reconstructed the
interfaces and found the mixed-cluster comparison by an exact signed-orbit
search. **The universal Collatz assertion remains OPEN.**

Use the shortcut map

\[
T(n)=\begin{cases}n/2&n\text{ even},\\(3n+1)/2&n\text{ odd},\end{cases}
\qquad U(q)=\frac{3q+1}{2^{v_2(3q+1)}}\quad(q>0\text{ odd}).
\]

An odd clock counts applications of \(U\); a shortcut clock counts
applications of \(T\). Every comparison below retains that distinction.

## 1. A comparison orbit for arbitrary mixtures of two paid clusters

At odd boundaries let a light block \(L\) have successive odd gaps
\((2,1)\), and a paid block \(P\) have gaps \((1,4)\). Their exact
equations are

\[
8q'=9q+7\quad(L),\qquad32q'=9q+5\quad(P).
\]

The new mixed construction allows singleton \(P\)'s and pairs \(PP\)
in arbitrary order, with at least three \(L\)'s between successive
clusters. The comparison at a boundary followed by at least three lights is

\[
y=\frac{183q+1}{256}.
\]

It returns to this same expression through both \(LLLP\) and
\(LLLPP\). The two target paths have respectively 14 and 19 shortcut
clocks, matching the source's total clocks and odd counts. Inside a tile
the target's number of odd steps in an individual block can differ from
the source's; its slope changes accordingly. Extra light blocks propagate
the free comparison. Short initial fragments are handled by a literal
forward drop, so the comparison addresses both the block boundary and its
intermediate odd value.

The resulting theorem constructs a smaller positive ordinary nonconverger
at every odd position of any supplied infinite path in this mixed
language. Hence the globally least nonconverger cannot be a boundary or
intermediate of such an infinite tail.
The language allows arbitrarily long light runs and arbitrary nonperiodic
choices between singleton and paired clusters. No infinite positive
ordinary realization of a nonconvergent input word is assumed to exist.

See [the mixed proof](merger/mixed.md) and
[its Lean theorem](merger/MixedPaid.lean). The earlier
[paired-only construction](merger/proof.md) is retained with its original
Lean proof and review. That note also proves a finite asynchronous reset
on \(L^rPP L^s\), \(r,s\ge2\), and an all-depth obstruction to a
more restrictive common-slope controller. The mixed construction changes
the slopes and odd counts allowed by that restricted template; it does
not contradict the obstruction.

Paid triples, shorter separations, and general odd-gap sequences are not
all covered by these theorems. A smaller ordinary nonconverger need not
follow the same input language.

There is a further written consequence on escaping inputs: the mixed
comparison belongs to a different ordinary component, even when merger
clocks are allowed to differ. A paired-only input supplies at least three
pairwise disjoint escaping components via the two comparisons. Their
exact corrected-height ratios contain the factors 61 and 347, whereas
any eventual intersection would require a ratio generated by two and
three. See [the conditional component theorem](section/DISTINCT_COMPONENTS.md).
The output languages have not been shown to permit repeating this
construction indefinitely.

## 2. The critical section is encountered by the actual forward orbit

PR197's critical marks are odd \(x\) with

\[
(d+1)(x-2^d)\le70\,2^d,\qquad d+1\le5e\le d+8,
\quad d=\lfloor\log_2x\rfloor,\quad e=v_2(3x+1).
\]

Let \(B\) denote this set. Every positive odd ternary unit \(n\)
reaches either 1 or \(U(B)\) in fewer than \(2\sqrt n\) odd steps.
The proof applies PR173's forward steering theorem at the phase
\(\log_2 3\), then constructs the unique critical-strip lift of the
target actually visited. Backward cofinality alone is not used to infer
this visit.

At each visit, synchronize targets in its critical interval after
\((d+2)^3\) odd clocks and choose the least target in its fiber. Its
critical lift is a residual representative. The common endpoint is an
**anchor on the original orbit**. Starting the next search strictly after
that anchor makes all successive anchors occur at increasing physical
times. On an injective orbit the selected representatives are all
different. The residual set \(S\) is decidable and satisfies

\[
\sum_{r\in S}r^{-4\theta/5}<\infty,
\qquad \theta=H_2(\log_3 2)=0.9499555\ldots .
\]

The exponent is approximately 0.7599644. The construction uses PR193's
translated finite-clock packing theorem and keeps its written-proof
status. Its significance for the next step is that the reductions are
repeated on the same actual orbit with controlled merger clocks.

## 3. A single budget on whole classes, and absolute rigidity at minimum height

Fix an injective odd orbit \(q_i\). Let \(C_i\) contain all positive
odd integers which eventually merge with \(q_i\) at equal odd clocks.
The classes at different indices are disjoint. Their minima form a
synchronization transversal. Uniform interval packing therefore makes

\[
D_i=\sum_{h\ge i}\log_2\left(1+\frac1{3\min C_h}\right)
\]

finite, with \(D_i\to0\). This bound controls **every** ordinary
representative in \(C_i\), including representatives of unbounded height
and mergers of unbounded duration.

If \(u,v\in C_i\) merge after \(H\) odd steps, and \(E_u,E_v\)
are their binary clocks, telescoping gives

\[
\left|\log_2(u/v)-(E_u-E_v)\right|
\le\sum_{i\le h<i+H}\log_2\left(1+\frac1{3\min C_h}\right).
\]

The disjoint anchor segments from §2 share this one summable budget.
Their compression ratios tend to one, so eventually their two arms have
exactly equal total binary clocks. The total logarithmic compression is
finite.

There is a sharper representation of the true least ternary-unit members
\(n_i\) of these classes. Define

\[
M(z)=z\prod_{s\ge0}\left(1+\frac1{3U^s(z)}\right),
\qquad M_i=M(n_i).
\]

Eventually \(M_i\) is the least corrected height of a unit in \(C_i\),
and

\[
M_{i+1}=3M_i/2^{a_i},\qquad
1\le v_2(3n_i+1)\le a_i\le b(n_{i+1})\le4,
\qquad M_i/n_i\to1.
\]

Here \(b(y)\) is the smallest clock of a ternary-unit predecessor of
\(y\). Finite windows satisfy the corresponding minimum over **all**
ordinary unit inverse paths into the specified actual endpoint. There
is also an exact nonnegative integer gap between the corrected height of
the original source and the class minimum.

The arithmetic lane strengthens the relative error to an absolute width.
Every unit \(z\in C_i\) at corrected height \(M(z)=M_i\) satisfies

\[
1<M_i-z\le B_i,
\qquad B_i=\frac13\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{M_i}{M_{i+k}}.
\]

If \(B_i\le3\), there is a unique such ordinary unit. In particular,
if the corrected clocks eventually lie in \(\{1,2\}\), with at least
two 1s between successive 2s, then **every sufficiently late minimum
transition is the actual ordinary edge** \(U(n_i)=n_{i+1}\). No strict
replacements remain. This permits a nonperiodic arrangement of the
spaced 2s. Every eventually periodic corrected clock in this sector is
excluded from an escaping orbit by ordinary parity-cylinder uniqueness.

See [the class budget and exact normal form](section/proof.md), §§3–4,
and [the absolute-width theorem and residue argument](arithmetic/optimality.md).
The infinite products, packing estimates, and rigidity deduction are
written proofs. Lean checks their indicated finite arithmetic interfaces.

The [finite-window control](arithmetic/window-control.md) preserves the
opposing quantifier: arbitrarily long ordinary segments can satisfy exact
minimum clocks and heights in every synchronized subwindow while the
corresponding homogeneous clock has widths 5 and 7. These are changing
finite witnesses. Their nominal corrected heights are not identified with
the infinite products of a single escaping orbit.

## 4. Moving intervals and two orderings of the same cycle

For arbitrary prescribed positive heights \(H_0,\ldots,H_k\), actual
odd paths with \(q_j\in[H_j,2H_j)\) admit at most \(k+1\) complete
valuation words. The number of distinct ordinary starts is at most

\[
(k+1)\left\lceil H_k/3^k\right\rceil.
\]

The word bound is sharp. It gives a logarithmic bound on the length of
distinct consecutive states confined to one octave. It does not bound
the number of height profiles available to an unrestricted orbit.

For a primitive ordinary cycle with \(p\) odd steps and total shortcut
clock \(A\), order its dyadic copies and, separately, the triadic copies
of its full shortcut states. With \(g=\gcd(A,p)\), each affine
permutation has at least \(g-1\) inversions, with the same parity as
\(g-1\). Each inversion gives an explicit inequality between actual
states on that same cycle. These are arbitrary-amplitude constraints;
they do not require an economical repeated-block cover. When \(g=1\)
the lower bounds can both be zero.

The companion one-octave argument proves equality of the full numerical
and reduced cycle denominators. Its ordinary balanced-cycle exclusion is
credited to the existing literature, including Knight; no novelty is
claimed for that exclusion. See [the arithmetic proofs and scope](arithmetic/proof.md).
