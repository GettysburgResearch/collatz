# Literature audit — July 2026 positive coefficient frontier

**Inspection date:** 2026-07-31  
**Purpose:** position `T-6901`--`T-6904` and `L-6904`--`L-6905` against recent primary literature; no priority claim  

## 1. Rozier--Terracol, revised May 2026

Olivier Rozier and Claude Terracol, *Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences*, arXiv `2502.00948v5`, published in *Discrete Mathematics* 349 (2026), 115167.

Relevant source results:

- definitions of ordinary stopping time `t(n)` and coefficient stopping time `tau(n)`;
- exact affine remainder and rearrangement framework;
- the coefficient stopping-time conjecture `t(n)=tau(n)`;
- an infinite-stopping integer generates infinitely many paradoxical sequences;
- explicit Diophantine lower bounds used in source-qualified first-crossing estimates.

The paper does not prove the coefficient stopping-time conjecture. `L-6904` isolates its first-crossing form as the sign of one integer canonical defect.

## 2. Verger--Gaugry, June 2026

Jean-Louis Verger-Gaugry, *Divergent trajectories in the 3x+1 problem and a S-Units Equation*, *Uniform Distribution Theory* 21(1) (2026), 1--9, DOI `10.66833/UDT-2026-0001`.

The paper assumes a divergent trajectory and studies an S-unit equation attached to local minima called wave minima, deriving prime-support consequences. `T-6902` supplies a complementary coefficient statement for actual tail minima:

```text
coefficient stopping depth -> infinity.
```

No S-unit theorem is imported into the proof.

## 3. Angeltveit, February 2026

Vigleik Angeltveit, *An improved algorithm for checking the Collatz conjecture for all n<2^N*, arXiv `2602.10466`.

The paper supplies exact finite descent, mod-9 preimage, path-merging, and odd-even-even sieves. Its Theorem 4.1 gives the `485/306` descent criterion used in PR #76. It is a finite-verification algorithm, not the missing pointwise orbit theorem.

These sieves are relevant only if converted into escape of canonical least roots or into a strict canonical first-crossing defect.

## 4. Kramer, July 2026

Oliver Kramer, *Adaptive Search in Collatz Exponent-Code Space via 2-adic and 3-adic Constraints*, arXiv `2607.10041`.

The paper gives necessary residue-rate conditions for a code generated by one fixed positive integer. This supports the ordinary-extraction diagnosis but supplies no converse and no universal lower bound for critical codes.

`L-6904` gives an exact finite two-place instance: the canonical dyadic representative and the positive real rational fixed point are two realizations of the same first-crossing object.

## 5. Chang, March 2026

The preprint *A structural reduction of the Collatz conjecture to one-bit orbit mixing*, arXiv `2603.25753`, explicitly leaves a deterministic pointwise mod-32 balance statement open. Its ensemble-to-pointwise gap is parallel to `R-6901`: a counting law on residue classes does not force one ordinary orbit to sample them with the required balance.

## 6. Tong Niu withdrawal correction

Tong Niu's `arXiv:2605.13886` was withdrawn on 20 May 2026 because Rozier--Terracol v4 already contained the enumerated paradoxical sequences and the seven small `(j,q)` pairs. It is not used as an independent theorem.

## 7. Current verification record

David Barina's public project log, generated 26 July 2026, records exhaustive convergence below `2^71`, reached on 15 January 2025. Angeltveit's paper estimates that its method could make `2^75` or `2^77` feasible, but no completed `2^75` certificate was located in this audit.

A larger finite range would sharpen the first Farey gate but would not by itself prove the cofinal envelope inequality of `L-6905`.

## 8. Baker input for `T-6904`

`T-6904` uses only the classical qualitative consequence of Baker's theory: because `2` and `3` are multiplicatively independent algebraic numbers, there are effective constants `c_0,mu>0` such that

\[
|j\log2-q\log3|\ge c_0j^{-\mu}
\]

for every nonzero form in the declared range.

The theorem file deliberately leaves the numerical normalization source-dependent. A reviewer should instantiate either a primary Baker/Baker--Wüstholz statement or a compatible explicit Matveev specialization before promotion.

## 9. Relationship to PR #80 and PR #81

PR #80 `T-6505` excludes bounded-surplus first crossings with uniformly linear factor complexity. `T-6904` strictly enlarges that class:

```text
bounded bank       -> logarithmic bank;
linear complexity  -> arbitrary uniformly subexponential complexity.
```

PR #81 `Q-6801` states two final obligations, escape of `m_N^sup` and canonical descent at every first crossing. `L-6905` proves that both follow from the single relative envelope

\[
m_{j-1}^{\rm sup}>F_j
\]

at all sufficiently late valid crossing lengths.

## Bottom line

The latest rigorous literature strengthens finite verification, paradoxical-sequence structure, wave-minimum arithmetic, and residue diagnostics. None supplies the missing ordinary pointwise bridge.

The new direct advances in this packet are:

```text
divergence
 -> escaping ordinary tail minima
 -> coefficient stopping depths tending to infinity
 -> an all-supercritical 2-adic tangent;

canonical crossing failure
 -> one nonpositive integer descent defect
 -> m_(j-1)^sup <= r^+(w) <= F_j;

logarithmic bank + zero factor entropy
 -> impossible at unbounded first-crossing lengths.
```
