# Literature audit — July 2026 positive-direction frontier

**Inspection date:** 2026-07-31  
**Purpose:** position `T-6601`--`T-6603` against recent primary literature; no priority claim  

## 1. Rozier--Terracol, revised May 2026

Olivier Rozier and Claude Terracol, *Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences*, arXiv `2502.00948v5`, published in *Discrete Mathematics* 349 (2026), 115167.

Relevant source results:

- definitions of ordinary stopping time `t(n)` and coefficient stopping time `tau(n)`;
- exact affine remainder and rearrangement framework;
- Theorem 3.2: an infinite-stopping integer generates infinitely many paradoxical sequences;
- Corollary 3.3: finiteness of paradoxical sequences would imply Collatz;
- explicit Diophantine lower bounds used in the source-qualified corollary of `T-6601`.

`T-6602` differs by following the actual wave minima of one divergent orbit and proving that their coefficient stopping depths tend to infinity.

## 2. Verger--Gaugry, June 2026

Jean-Louis Verger-Gaugry, *Divergent trajectories in the 3x+1 problem and a S-Units Equation*, *Uniform Distribution Theory* 21(1) (2026), 1--9, DOI `10.66833/UDT-2026-0001`.

The paper assumes a divergent trajectory and studies an S-unit equation attached to local minima called wave-minima, deriving prime-support consequences. The current packet supplies a complementary coefficient statement for tail/wave minima:

```text
coefficient stopping depth -> infinity.
```

No S-unit theorem is imported into the proof.

## 3. Angeltveit, February 2026

Vigleik Angeltveit, *An improved algorithm for checking the Collatz conjecture for all n<2^N*, arXiv `2602.10466`.

The paper supplies exact descent, mod-9 preimage, path-merging, and odd-even-even sieves. Its Theorem 4.1 gives the `485/306` descent criterion used in PR #76. The paper explicitly presents a faster finite verification algorithm; it does not prove the missing orbit-level theorem. The public verification record inspected on 2026-07-31 remains `2^71`.

These sieves are natural candidates for pruning the supercritical tangent tree, but a proof must show escape of its least ordinary roots rather than merely a decreasing fraction of residue classes.

## 4. Kramer, July 2026

Oliver Kramer, *Adaptive Search in Collatz Exponent-Code Space via 2-adic and 3-adic Constraints*, arXiv `2607.10041`.

The paper proves a necessary condition for a code generated by one fixed positive integer: its least 2-adic start representative eventually equals that integer, and its endpoint representative eventually equals the physical endpoint, so both normalized residue rates vanish.

This supports the ordinary-extraction diagnosis but gives no converse and no positive lower bound for arbitrary critical codes. The finite adaptive searches are diagnostic, not verification.

## 5. Chang, March 2026

The preprint *A structural reduction of the Collatz conjecture to one-bit orbit mixing*, arXiv `2603.25753`, explicitly leaves a deterministic pointwise mod-32 balance statement open. Its ensemble-to-pointwise gap is parallel to `R-6601`: a counting law on residue classes does not force one orbit to sample those classes with the required balance.

## 7. Current verification record

David Barina's public project log, generated 26 July 2026, still records exhaustive convergence below `2^71`, reached on 15 January 2025. Angeltveit's paper estimates that its method could make `2^75` or `2^77` feasible, but no completed `2^75` certificate was located.

Therefore PR #76's follow-up `2^75` Farey exclusion remains conditional and must not be promoted.

## Bottom line

The latest rigorous literature strengthens finite verification, paradoxical-sequence structure, wave-minimum arithmetic, and symbolic diagnostics. None supplies the missing pointwise/ordinary compactness bridge.

The exact new bridge in this packet is:

```text
divergence
 -> escaping ordinary wave minima
 -> coefficient stopping depths tending to infinity
 -> an all-supercritical 2-adic tangent
 -> Archimedean escape rather than ordinary stabilization.
```
