# Ordinary pair coupling: a legal descent block and its carry boundary

**Status: PROPOSED elementary results; closure OPEN.** This packet explores
the whole ordinary merger graph through two-coordinate physical moves. It
does not claim a proof of Collatz, a recurrent walk reaching 1, or a new
inverse pressure estimate. Base: `55d8968d2ecbc23a91c9c4733929f34be9bf0d28`.

Three mechanisms were compared:

1. A summable inverse supersolution or a stationary graph walk. The former
   is already an exact Collatz criterion in the resident critical-mass
   packet. Recurrence of a walk inside one component does not identify that
   component with the component of 1.
2. Coupling in relative height or in a compact arithmetic completion.
   Such closeness needs an ordinary height bound before it implies a
   collision. A common affine word also preserves the prime-to-6 part of
   a nonzero difference.
3. A physical pair process that strips ternary congruence and then exposes
   its genuinely different-branch carry. This was pursued explicitly.

The constructive result is a proper pair-state cost that strictly decreases
while common ternary digits are stripped. All three residue classes are
handled, including multiples of 3, by legal positive ordinary diagrams.
The complete block is finite for every fixed ordinary pair. Its first
carry-reset closure is then **refuted** by an infinite exact family: the
carry can increase ordinary height by a factor tending to `16/3` while
creating no new ternary agreement. Thus the debt-stripping theorem is a
usable interface, but the missing carry inequality has not been obtained.

Read [PAIR_COUPLING.md](PAIR_COUPLING.md) for the maps, proper cost,
quantifiers, boundary, and the finite-clock constraint on uniform
ordinary-distance couplings. Its final section explains why inverse
branching does not supply the prime-height retention missing from the
companion adelic route.

The separately authored [ADELIC_REVIEW.md](ADELIC_REVIEW.md) is a narrow
independent reconstruction of ADEL-001 through ADEL-007 at the exact file
hash stated there. It does not extend to later bytes, to the carry packet,

No search or census was used to infer these statements. The carry
