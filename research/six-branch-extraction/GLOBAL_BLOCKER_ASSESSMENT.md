## The exact repeatedly missing inference

For a fixed deterministic architecture define

\[
S_n=\{x\in\mathbf Z_{>0}:x\text{ is legal through depth }n\},
\qquad
m_n=\min S_n.
\]

The sets are nested. The valid ordinary compactness principle is

\[
\boxed{
\bigcap_n S_n\ne\varnothing
\iff
(m_n)\text{ is bounded}
\iff
(m_n)\text{ eventually stabilizes}.}
\]

The invalid replacement is

```text
for every n there exists x_n in S_n
  therefore
there exists one x in every S_n.
```

Compactness in the relevant `2`-adic space gives one inverse-limit point. It does not bound its ordinary least representatives.
