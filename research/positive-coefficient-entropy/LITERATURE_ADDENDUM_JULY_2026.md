## Oliver Kramer — exponent-code diagnostics

```text
Adaptive Search in Collatz Exponent-Code Space via 2-adic and 3-adic Constraints
arXiv:2607.10041
submitted 2026-07-10
```

Kramer studies finite exponent codes for the accelerated odd-to-odd Collatz map. A code determines three simultaneous quantities:

1. real multiplicative drift;
2. the least 2-adic start representative;
3. the least 3-adic endpoint representative.

The paper calls their combination the `2–3–infinity` diagnostic.

Its exact theorem is a necessary compatibility statement. If one infinite exponent code is generated by the accelerated orbit of a fixed positive odd integer `n`, then:

```text
the least start representative is eventually exactly n;
the least endpoint representative is eventually exactly the physical endpoint;
both normalized residue rates tend to zero.
```

The proof is elementary but load-bearing: the start modulus tends to infinity, while the actual initial integer remains fixed; and the physical endpoint grows more slowly than the full `3^k` endpoint modulus.

The finite searches at lengths `100`, `200`, and `400` compare random critical codes, mechanical critical codes, and adaptive evolutionary search. All retain visibly positive start and endpoint residue rates. The paper explicitly presents this as a diagnostic obstruction rather than a verification theorem.
