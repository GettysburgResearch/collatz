# Exact self-returns inside every prescribed ternary neighborhood

**PROPOSED ordinary obstruction to the selector.** The two deep binary progressions in [the sparse criterion](PRESCRIBED_RESIDUES.md) can each contain the entire native time window of a prescribed source inside its forced prefix. There is an explicit family in *every* prescribed ternary-unit neighborhood on which the grade selector makes an immediate successful comparison at many clocks and still cannot decrease the starting height. This concerns the transfer procedure. The eventual behavior of the other arm is not determined by the argument.

Fix $q_0=3$ or $9$ and a ternary unit $\beta$. For each large $\ell$ choose an integer $K$ in $[3^{2\ell},2\cdot3^{2\ell}]$ with
$$
 2^K\equiv1+q_0\beta\pmod{q_0 3^\ell},\qquad u=(2^K-1)/q_0.
$$
Such exponents form a nonempty progression of period $O(3^\ell)$ by the order calculation in the companion note. In both cases $K$ is even. Then $u$ is positive and odd, $u\equiv\beta\bmod3^\ell$, and $v_2(q_0u+1)=K$. Since $\log u\asymp K$ and $3^\ell\asymp\sqrt K$, every such $u$ eventually belongs to the corresponding named sparse test set, even for any fixed positive constant in its two lower bounds.

For $q_0=3$, put $V_t=2^{K-t}$. At $1\le t\le K$ the two physical states are $V_t,3^t V_t-1$. The grades in the coefficient convention of the main proof are $a=1,b=1+t$. This follows by using one odd step then halvings on $u$ and all odd steps on $3u$. The first arm hits 1 at $K$, while the second is then $3^K-1$. At any such checkpoint the lower selector starts at $k=t$. Its output $(2^k V_t-1)/3$ is at least $u$. When the congruence being tested fits the supplied $u$ and the extra minimum exponent is at most $t$, it selects exactly $k=t$. The companion comparison is then an identity. Thus a successful physical comparison can simply return the original ordinary integer.

For $q_0=9$, at $2\le t\le K$ the second physical state is $V_t$. The first is $3^j V_t+1$ if $t=2j+2$ and $3^{j+1}V_t+2$ if $t=2j+3$. These are the upper-lift formulas at the shortened clock. The upper physical arm $3u$ has one odd step and then only halvings. The lower physical arm $u$ has two initial odd steps, then alternating odd and even *until clock $t$*. Indeed after the first two steps its state is $2^{K-2}+1$; at each remaining pair, $4w+1$ goes to $6w+2$ then $3w+1$. Thus $a-b=j$ at even $t$, $a-b=j+1$ at odd $t$, with $b=2$. At $t\ge4$ the published selector uses the $9$ denominator and begins at $k=t$ for even $t$ and $k=t+1$ for odd $t$. In both cases the new unit $(2^k V_t-1)/9$ is at least $u$. At even $t$ it returns exactly $u$ when the residue matches. Here $3u$ reaches 1 at clock $K$ and the first arm reaches $3^{K/2-1}+1$.

One can also allow a much earlier inverse exponent and ask for the new representative to drop to $u^\kappa$, with a fixed $0<\kappa<1$. In either construction its value is $(2^k V_t-1)/q_0$, so this drop forces $t-k\ge(1-\kappa)K-O(1)$. At a native clock $t\in[.75\log_2 u,.875\log_2 u]$, the other new state (the one needing comparison) is $3^k V_t-1$ for $q_0=3$. Its ratio to the old larger state $3^t V_t-1$ is at most $C3^{-(t-k)}$. For $q_0=9$ the two exponents are $\lfloor(k-1)/2\rfloor$ and $\lfloor(t-1)/2\rfloor$ when $k\ge2$, with additive terms 1 or 2; the same ratio is at most $C3^{-(t-k)/2}$. These estimates hold on the actual positive integers. Since $x/2\le T(x)\le2x$ for $x\ge1$, these new and old states cannot meet even at different comparison clocks of lengths $o(K)$. The original native trial's comparison length is sublinear in $K$ on these rows. This excludes a power decrease merely by widening its inverse exponent menu downward while keeping its comparison deadline.

There is a related exact obstruction to inserting a coalescing binary word at will. On *every positive lift* of either forced residue $q_0u+1=2^k V$, $k\ge2$, the two states after $k$ steps are distinct by the same formulas. Hence the states do not meet synchronously at any time through $k$. The three no-meeting theorems in [OrderedLanding.lean](OrderedLanding.lean) verify this finite statement for arbitrary positive lifts. If a subsequent tuned trial identifies its comparison pair and a further word fuses its new unit pair, the original two current states fuse as well: advance both equalities to the larger of the two comparison deadlines, after subtracting the inverse-prefix length from the unit-pair deadline if necessary. The grade menus do not impose that further event. At a forced prefix ending with grade gap $G$, even a symbolic fusion needs at least $G$ further clocks, since each fresh grade difference changes by at most one per step. These are exact obstacles to a uniform short-word closure; they do not decide later paths.

Finally the finite-grid comparison used for (1) begins with two slopes whose ratio is a power of three. If the incoming coefficient is five, multiplying either grade by further powers of three leaves a factor of five in one slope. The two records cannot be written as $V,3^gV+J$ with constant $J$ on a free common integer parameter. The [prescribed ratio-five packing](../paired/README.md) therefore cannot supply a new application of the tripling grid estimate merely by placing many ratio-five starts in a short interval. It also controls the interval length rather than the height of those starts.
