# Ordinary smaller mergers, a sixfold band and positive occupation

**Written theorem with an exact finite energy certificate and two external number-theoretic inputs.** Let \(T(2z)=z,\ T(2z+1)=3z+2\) be the positive shortcut map. A smaller merger through clock \(m\) means \(T^i(X)=T^j(b)\) for some \(0<b<X,\ i\le m,\ j\ge0\). Put \(\theta=4999/15000<1/3\).

For every fixed \(\beta>\theta\), there are \(c_\beta>0\) and a height threshold such that, for every sufficiently large positive integer \(X\) and every \(m\ge X^\beta\), there is a smaller merger through \(m\), or at least \(c_\beta m\) of the first \(m\) shortcut departures exceed \(6X\). The same alternative holds with a smaller positive fraction and some fixed \(R_\beta>6\) in place of 6. The constants and thresholds are ineffective.

For \(\theta<\beta<1/3\), by \(\lceil X^\beta\rceil\) there is a smaller merger or a physical first exit from \([X,6X]\). If the first exit occurs before any earlier smaller merger, its clock and direction agree with the first exit of \(3^{h_i}/2^i\) from \([1,6]\), where \(h_i\) counts odd departures before \(i\). This also holds for some radius \(R_\beta=2^{k/l}>6\) fixed independently of \(X,m\). These results allow upward escape and do not show that all positive sources converge.

We adapt Weaver's sparse located-window count and incidence argument. The fixed-place Subspace Theorem separates the quantized four-copy sums. The quoted Chudnovsky estimate \(\mu(\log2/\log3)\le3\) excludes very short ordinary returns. The new ingredient is an upper bound below one bit of fourth energy per shortcut at the sixfold radius, using the 446 native smaller-source certificates from the preceding checkpoint. ## The finite fourth-energy bound

Write \(\alpha=\log_3 2,\ a=1-\alpha,\ q=1/a-2\). Between consecutive zero increments of the upper mechanical grade \(H_i=\lfloor i\alpha+\phi\rfloor\), away from ambiguous cutting points, the true word contains exactly one even in a support of length two or three. At a baseline zero put \(r=(\{i\alpha+\phi\}-\alpha)/a\). The next support has length \(2+\lfloor r+q\rfloor\), and the new \(r\) is \(r+q\bmod1\). At radius six we allow every slot for the even in both lengths. The sparse-window proof below treats ambiguous cutting points.

For a fixed sequence of support lengths, the four-copy numerator congruence is equivalent to equality of the unordered pair of zero slots at every support. Local numerators are \(1,2\) modulo 3, or \(5,7,10\) modulo 9. Their unordered pair sums are distinct in the respective modulus. Starting at the final support of the congruence therefore fixes its unordered pair. Its entire integer contribution cancels, allowing backward induction. This remains valid for any restricted subset of the words, where adjacent choices need not be independent.

A *cell* consists of 60 supports. A *block* consists of 24 cells. Allow words avoiding \(EE\) throughout the block and avoiding the 446 factors inside each individual cell. Factors crossing a cell boundary may be admitted. Every bank occurrence provides an ordinary smaller visitor when its reference state is at most \(6X\) for a positive native source. The Lean bank rechecks every pair at its least positive dyadic lift and its increment, which removes the earlier sufficient floor 61. An \(OEE\) from odd \(z\) has positive one-step source \((z-1)/4<X\) whenever its odd-step endpoint is at most \(6X\). These word restrictions follow from ordinary meetings with the original threshold \(X\).

In a cell first enforce the unordered-pair congruences and all four copies' \(EE\) restrictions, including the incoming flags. For \(i=1,2,3,4\), the bad event is that copy \(i\) contains a bank factor entirely in the cell. With \(h\) bad copies,
\[
                  {\bf1}_{h=0}\le1-h+\binom h2 .
\]
For a fixed incoming flag and final flag class, let \(Z\) count the unrestricted congruence quadruples, \(A_i\) those clean on copy \(i\), and \(P_{ij}\) those clean on \(i,j\). Here unrestricted still enforces \(EE\) avoidance on every copy. Summation gives the entrywise nonnegative upper bound
\[
                       3Z-2\sum_i A_i+\sum_{i<j}P_{ij}.
\]
This uses no independence among copies. Each summand needs full pattern histories on at most two copies.

A flag indicates which copies ended in \(E\). Six masks are possible, in three permutation classes: none, all, or one of the first pair and one of the last pair. Interchanging either pair and interchanging sides preserves future counts. For each final class, average the row over the incoming class. All four average single-copy counts can use \(A_1\), both same-side pairs \(P_{12}\), and all four cross-side pairs \(P_{13}\). The exact three-by-three upper matrix is the orbit average of \(3Z-8A_1+2P_{12}+4P_{13}\). Before averaging, that compressed expression need not bound an individual labeled row. Symmetry ensures its average equals the uncompressed Bonferroni bound for every labeled start in the class. All four flags, at their class multiplicities, pass across cell joins.

For tracked copies, the verifier constructs a deterministic suffix automaton for the forbidden factors and \(EE\). At each support it follows the specified zero slot. Reachable states with identical labeled continuation vectors on the remaining fixed supports are combined. Untracked copies retain only the last-even flag. A backward GMP integer recurrence evaluates \(Z,A_1,P_{12},P_{13}\), using all permissible ordered quadruples of slots. Two independent direct enumerations on small artificial banks check the entire matrix formula, including cases with three or four bad copies.

A cell's sequence changes only at \(r=-iq\bmod1,\ 0\le i\le60\). All 61 open phase intervals are checked. Every block of 1440 supports belongs to one of 1441 open phase intervals, up to boundary values. Comparisons of \(A+Bq\) reduce to integer comparisons of powers of two and three, since \((1-\alpha)(A+Bq)=A-B+(2B-A)\alpha\). For each block the verifier multiplies its 24 nonnegative cell matrices in chronological order, beginning with the empty incoming mask. For the resulting row sum \(E\) and the physical clock \(T\), it checks
\[
                          16E^5<2^{5T},\qquad T\le4000 .
\]
Thus the ordered block energy is at most \(2^{(4999/5000)T}\). This is a finite integer inequality for every phase. [Exact verifier](sixfold_energy.py) · [Cell recurrence](cell_energy.cpp) · [Finite controls](SIXFOLD_CHECK.txt)

## Sparse windows at the sixfold endpoint

Consider an \(N\)-window of the original trajectory with source \(x_0\in[X,6X]\), every state at least \(X\), and \(t\le\delta N\) observations above \(6X\). Exclude windows with a witnessed smaller visitor through their final clock. Here \(N=(4/3+o(1))\log_2 X\) and \(\delta>0\) is fixed and small. The usual product over odd steps gives
\[
       \log_3(x_i/x_0)=h_i-i\alpha+c_i,\qquad 0\le c_i\le\eta:=N/(3X\log3).
\]
Put \(\phi=\log_3(6X/x_0)\). At each in-band observation \(h_i=H_i\) or \(H_i-1\), and the lower grade is possible only if \(\{i\alpha+\phi\}\le\alpha+\eta\). At a baseline zero the fractional part is at least \(\alpha\). Hence only those in \([\alpha,\alpha+\eta]\) might fail to fix the native grade. Their number among \(N\) clocks is \(o(N)\) uniformly in \(\phi\), by interval equidistribution for the irrational rotation. For a shrinking interval, first majorize by a fixed small interval and then shrink the latter. Exact phase breakpoints cost \(o(N)\) as well. The candidate phase records number at most polynomially many in \(N\), because floor values, ambiguity flags and support sequences change at \(O(N)\) translates of fixed endpoints, including endpoints shifted by \(\eta\).

Starting at the first available baseline zero, cut into complete 1440-support blocks. Record every bit in both incomplete ends and in the following exceptional blocks. A high observation or ambiguous cutting point makes its containing block and both neighboring blocks exceptional, including when the point is internal. Shared endpoint observations can make both adjoining blocks exceptional along with their neighbors. The number of fixed bits is at most \(C\delta N+o(N)\) for an absolute constant \(C\), since each block has at most 4000 clocks and incomplete ends cost \(O(4000)\). Choosing the high mask costs \(2^{H_2(\delta)N+o(N)}\). Recorded bits and phase are common to their product family.

All unrecorded blocks have fixed grades at their baseline-zero ends and admit the mechanical slots described above. No such block contains \(EE\). For a pair of successive even departures include its immediately preceding departure, also in band under the neighbor convention. Three successive halves from this preceding point would leave the sixfold band below the floor. Thus the predecessor must be odd, giving an excluded \(OEE\) witness. A bank factor inside a retained cell is impossible since its reference observation is in band. The initial block, if it lacks a preceding observation, can always be recorded at constant cost.

Allow independent choices from the block languages at all unrecorded blocks and fix the recorded bits. Every product choice preserves the grades at component ends and differs from an actual witness by at most one at each internal mechanical grade. Every actual clock has a preceding in-band observation within \(t+1\) steps, since the source is in band. Thus \(h_i-i\alpha\) stays inside its original strip enlarged by \(t+O(1)\) on both sides. The same bound with one more unit holds for every product word. This gives Weaver 181 §4.2's numerator bound \(F\ll N2^T3^{2t+O(1)}\) on each aligned \(T\)-segment, including when a high excursion spans several recorded blocks.

Let \(p=\lfloor N/(4+\sigma)\rfloor\). Take four disjoint component-aligned segments of lengths \((1-\sigma)p+O(4000)\) with the same boundary margins as Weaver 181 §4.3. Each fixed exterior bit can be a singleton component. For \(\delta\ll\sigma\) the fixed-place Subspace Theorem separation applies as in §§4.2–4.3, because the \(O(\delta N)\) numerator enlargement is smaller than the \(\sigma N\) separation margin. The four-copy congruence for a segment factors over its independent blocks: inside a block use the slot-pair condition, and fixed bits cancel.

For a free block of \(k\) words the collision probability is its ordered energy divided by \(k^4\). Fourier inversion and Hölder across the four segments cancel the \(k\)'s against the source population, exactly as in the earlier band proof. Fixed bits do not contribute choices within a family. Clocks outside the four segments cost \(2^{O(\sigma N)+O(4000)}\). Summing masks, bits and phases, the number \(A_X\) of distinct eligible band sources is at most
\[
       2^{(4999/5000)N/4+O(\sigma N)+[H_2(\delta)+C\delta]N+o(N)} .
\]
At this depth the band source residues fit in a bounded number of quantization cells and its integers are distinct modulo \(2^N\). The \(o(N)\) terms are uniform in entry phase. Given \(\epsilon>0\), choose \(\sigma\) sufficiently small and then choose \(\delta\) sufficiently small. This yields \(A_X=O_\epsilon(X^{\theta+\epsilon})\) for actual integers in the specified interval.

## Incidence, multiplicity and wider upper endpoints

If there is no smaller visitor through \(m\), every state through \(m\) is at least \(X\), since a state below \(X\) supplies its own zero-clock visitor. Let \(h\) count departures above \(6X\), and \(D\) the maximum multiplicity among the \(m\) departures. Of the \(m-N+1\) full windows, at most \(h\) have a high source. All windows together contain at most \((N+1)(h+1)\) high observations, including the terminal observation. With \(Q=(N+1)/(\lfloor\delta N\rfloor+1)\), at most \(Q(h+1)\) windows exceed the high-observation budget. The remainder have at most \(A_X\) different sources, each appearing at most \(D\) times. Hence
\[
                    (1+Q)h\ge m-N+1-Q-D A_X .
\]
If departures repeat, a preperiod leads to a cycle of least shortcut period \(L\), all of whose states are at least \(X\). Its odd grade \(v\) obeys \(0<|v\log3-L\log2|\le L/(3X)\). The cited irrationality exponent gives \(L\gg_\kappa X^{1/(1+\kappa)}\) for every \(\kappa>2\), and \(D\le1+m/L\). For distinct departures take \(D=1\). Choose \(\theta+\epsilon<\min(\beta,1/(1+\kappa))\). Then \(D A_X/m=o(1)\) uniformly for \(m\ge X^\beta\). Since \(Q\to1/\delta\), we may take \(c_\beta=\delta/[2(1+\delta)]\) for sufficiently large \(X\).

On any \(N\)-window above the floor \(X\), the fractional part of \(\log_3(x_i/X)\) follows the rotation by \(-\alpha\) with cumulative perturbation at most \(\eta\), regardless of entry height. For fixed \(6<R<18\), phases of \([6X,RX]\) form a circle arc of length \(\log_3(R/6)\). Interval equidistribution, then subdivision into disjoint \(N\)-windows, bounds the fraction of departures in this shell by \(\log_3(R/6)+o(1)\), uniformly for \(m\ge X^\beta\). Choose a fixed \(R=2^{k/l}>6\) with \(\log_3(R/6)<c_\beta/2\). A positive fraction of all departures must exceed \(RX\).

For \(\beta<1/3\), apply the positive-occupation alternative to \(m=\lceil X^\beta\rceil\). If no smaller merger intervenes, an upper exit must occur by then. If the first exit occurs with no strictly earlier merger, either it falls within this bound or the occupation alternative applied at the deadline would already force an exit. At every observation through the first exit the total log correction is at most \(m/(3X)\), including the first exterior observation. At positive clocks the lower form \(h_i\log3-i\log2\) is nonzero. For \(R=6\) the upper form is \((h_i-1)\log3-(i+1)\log2\), also nonzero. For \(R=2^{k/l}\), multiply the upper form by \(l\) to obtain \(lh_i\log3-(li+k)\log2\). Choose \(\kappa>2\) with \(\beta(1+\kappa)<1\). The two-log lower bound dominates the perturbation uniformly, so physical and coefficient comparisons agree through the first exit.
