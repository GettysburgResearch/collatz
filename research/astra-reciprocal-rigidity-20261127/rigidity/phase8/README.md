# Finite-place obstructions for one-edge escape certificates

[ADELIC_HEIGHT_OBSTRUCTION.md](ADELIC_HEIGHT_OBSTRUCTION.md) proves that a monotone unbounded radial discount, even arbitrarily slow, cannot be combined with its stated finite-prime corrections to obtain a positive one-edge escape certificate. For every such representation the set of violating ordinary inputs has upper counting exponent one. The proof compares genuine integer CRT lifts with a stationary finite-place law. The opening escape implication uses the finite simple-path reciprocal bound T-A3-651.

[PERIODIC_PLACE_VIOLATIONS.md](PERIODIC_PLACE_VIOLATIONS.md) strengthens the conclusion to positive upper natural density for locally constant corrections and finitely many valuation centers under the stated harmonic error hypothesis. An expanding rational periodic word supplies a periodic congruence class of ordinary sources on which the local corrections cancel.

[GeometricScales.lean](GeometricScales.lean) verifies the finite multiplicative and additive iteration underlying the radial contradiction and its boundedness consequence. Its two printed inventories contain only the three standard Lean axioms. Both finite-place obstructions and their ordinary sampling arguments are written proofs.

[adelic_controls.py](adelic_controls.py) implements the exact biased ternary stationary distribution, 11,520 actual integer CRT lifts with fair suffixes, and 512 physical expanding-class returns. Replay with Python and optimized Python and compare with [ADELIC_CONTROLS.json](ADELIC_CONTROLS.json). The controls check finite instances. The conclusions apply to the declared representation class; arbitrary nonlocal height certificates and the Collatz conjecture remain open.
