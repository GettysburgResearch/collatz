# Almost every positive trajectory visits one exact log-square mask before its minimum

**Proposed, source-qualified written theorem.** Write \(T\) for the native shortcut map, \(\tau(n)\) for the first date of its global minimum, and \(j(y)=\lfloor\log_2 y\rfloor\). There is one computable set \(F\) of odd integers with
\[
 |F\cap[2^j,2^{j+1})|\le C2^j/(1+j)^2                                             \tag{1}
\]
such that for every fixed \(B\), a set of natural density one visits \(F\cap(B,\infty)\) strictly before \(\tau(n)\). More precisely, finite subcollections of its marked physical words capture proportions arbitrarily close to one. The same mask has vanishing hit frequency by **every** prescribed \(o(\log n)\) native deadline. This sharpens the fixed lower frequencies at the borderline in [Critical log-square masks](CRITICAL_LOG_SQUARE_MASKS.md).

For every prescribed finite height threshold there is consequently a computable positive-integer map agreeing with \(T\) through that threshold and at every even input, changing an odd output only by raising it by one. It has \(O(X/(\log X)^2)\) changed edges through \(X\) and density-one divergent starts. Typical paths agree with the genuine map throughout every prescribed \(o(\log n)\) initial interval, whereas their laws stopped at their own first global minimum, including the source, have total variation tending to one. A final consequence concerns genuine inverse trees of prescribed transient units: for the natural predecessor law and \(G_k=\max_{0\le i\le j\le k}V_i/V_j\) of [Fixed-root maximum recovery](FIXED_ROOT_MAXIMUM_RECOVERY.md), almost surely \(\limsup_k G_k/k=\infty\).

## Marked finite paths

Fix an integer \(b\ge1\). For each sufficiently large \(e\) use the stopped gap words at threshold \(L_e=b2^e\) in [Critical log-square masks](CRITICAL_LOG_SQUARE_MASKS.md). On octave \(j\) include odd integers in the terminal ternary cylinders at any of \(e-1,e,e+1\), where \(e=\lfloor\log_2 j\rfloor\). Call this computable union \(F_b\); take \(F=F_1\) for (1). The cited word estimate (3) and integer rounding give (1) for each fixed \(b\). A retained **physical pattern** consists of an actual finite odd-to-odd positive path reading a qualifying word, ending at an integer on an accepting octave. Its native duration is \(O_b(\log j)\) at terminal octave \(j\), and the terminal value is at least \(L_e\) times the initial value, by the positive correction in the true affine formula. We can require either endpoint to exceed any prescribed bound. At bounded terminal heights these are finitely many actual patterns: the grades and gaps are bounded, and every preceding vertex is at most the terminal height times two to the native duration. We use the same convention when a finite family is described by both start and end height bands.

For a finite pattern family \(A\), let \(\zeta_A(n)\) indicate that no such full path ends strictly before \(\tau(n)\). This is a finite-valued terminal label. Indeed take \(R_A\) above all vertices of all patterns. For \(n>R_A\), none can start at \(n\); if one occurs later its endpoint is below \(n\), so the first global-minimum comparison for its completion is identical after the first native step. Thus \(\zeta_A(n)=\zeta_A(Tn)\) there. For odd \(n>R_A\), the intervening ordinary even value \(3n+1\) also has this invariance, yielding ordinary-map invariance as required in the primary theorem. Finite Boolean combinations have the same property. Such labels have a limiting law from uniform integers through \(X\), the same in fixed residue classes and hence from uniform odd sources \(W_Y\) in \([Y,2Y)\), by subtracting two fixed-progression counts. [Leen, terminal data and arithmetic progressions](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23269488)

## A finite family gains a uniform amount of previously uncaptured mass

Fix \(B,b\); use only patterns with both endpoints above \(B\). Suppose the supremum \(s_*\) of the natural capture densities over finite subfamilies is less than one. Put \(d_0=1-s_*>0\). Every finite \(A\) has avoidance density \(d\ge d_0\). We will construct a finite additional family \(E\) capturing at least \(\eta(b,d_0)>0\) of the whole source population jointly with \(\zeta_A=1\). The new band and its onset may depend on \(A\); the mass bound will not.

Select a small constant \(c\in(0,1/128)\), then a fixed \(0<\delta<\min(10^{-3},c/1000)\). At a threshold \(Z\to\infty\) consider the reference \(Y=Z^{1+\delta}\), \(j=\lfloor\log_2Y\rfloor\), \(t=\lfloor cj\rfloor\), \(h=\lfloor j/4\rfloor\). A fair native word with first bit odd has the following event \(D\), determined by its first \(t\) bits: a completed stopped word for \(L_e=b2^e\), \(e=\lfloor\log_2 j\rfloor\), occurs using only dates from \(\lceil t/4\rceil\) through \(t-1\). Apply the independent completed-excursion trials (4) of the cited critical-mask note to that subwindow, starting at its first odd date. For each fixed small \(c\) and all large \(j\), its probability \(p\) is at least \(c_b c\), with \(c_b>0\) independent of \(c\): the trial bound is \(1-\exp(-c' t/L_e)-O(e^{-c''t})\). All physical states through that time are between \(Y2^{-t}\) and \(2Y2^t\). Their octave indices lie between \(j/2\) and \(2j\), so the terminal octave accepts \(L_e\). Let \(E\) consist of all actual such patterns with both endpoints in this fixed band and tested threshold \(L_e\). For large \(Z\) the whole band is above \(B,R_A\).

### Conditioning on the rare word

Discard incomplete blocks of \(2^t\) consecutive integers at the two ends of the reference interval, a probability cost \(O(2^t/Y)\). Sample the remaining odd integers as \(n=2^tm+r\), with \(m\) uniform on an interval of length \(N\asymp Y/2^t\) and \(r\) independent uniform on odd residues. Independently sample a second such residue \(r'\) and use \(n'=2^tm+r'\). Conditional on \(D=D(r)\), the second integer has the original trimmed reference law, so \(\Pr(\zeta_A(n')=1\mid D)=d+o_A(1)\) as \(Z\to\infty\) for fixed parameters. We compare their actual terminal labels even under this conditioning.

At date \(t\) the two values are \(3^g m+T^t(r)\) and \(3^{g'}m+T^t(r')\), where \(g,g'\) count the low-word odd departures. Each unconditioned grade has law \(1+\operatorname{Bin}(t-1,1/2)\). Hoeffding, divided by \(p\ge c_b c\) for the conditional first arm, shows outside conditional probability \(O_b(c)\) that
\[
        |g-g'|\le C_b\sqrt{t\log(1/c)}.                                            \tag{2}
\]
Ordering the arms by grade, write the larger-grade value as \(3^u x+D'\) in terms of the other, \(u=|g-g'|\). Since \(T^t(r)\le2^t r\le2^{2t}\), we have \(\log(2+|D'|)\le C(t+1)\), independent of both residues and \(m\). For each fixed \(r,r'\), \(x\) is odd-slope affine in the independent \(m\), hence is uniform mod \(2^h\) up to \(O(2^h/N)=o(1)\). This counts every canonical absorption by \(h\), a union of exact \(2^h\)-residues. Absorption gives true synchronous equality, and all previous states of both paths exceed \(Y2^{-t-h}>R_A\), so their terminal labels coincide.

The needed Haar bound at small \(u\) is explicit in the underlying return estimates. For \(u\ge1\), the first return to zero grade has tail \(\Pr(R>h/2)\le C u/\sqrt h\) by Leen's uniform positive-excursion survival bound. Following return, or immediately if \(u=0\), equation (1) of [Ordinary affine interval profile](ORDINARY_AFFINE_INTERVAL_PROFILE.md) gives \(\Pr(A-R>h/2)\le C\sqrt{(1+\log(2+|D'|))/h}\). Here \(A\) in this estimate denotes absorption time, and the normalized initial offset \(|D'|/3^u\le |D'|\). Thus (2) makes the conditional probability of unequal labels at most
\[
        O_b(c)+O_b(\sqrt{c\log(1/c)})+o_{c,A,b}(1).                              \tag{3}
\]
Equivalently the zero-ratio limit follows from the moving-start physical clock at \(u=o(\sqrt h)\) and the post-return bound. The survival and return results are imported from the [original synchronization paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23268751). Choose \(c=c(b,d_0)\) making the first two terms less than \(d_0/4\), then take large \(Z\) for each fixed \(A\). Restoring trimmed blocks, we obtain \(\Pr_{W_Y}(D,\zeta_A=1)\ge c_b c d_0/2\) for all large \(Z\). In particular a small unconditional merger error was never divided by the word probability: merger itself was estimated **after conditioning**.

### From one reference interval to a natural-density increment

Except with \(o_{c,\delta}(1)\) probability, the first odd landing \(v\le Z\) occurs before the marked window. At native date \(k=\lfloor t/8\rfloor\) the fair odd count is at most \(0.55k\) except with exponentially small probability. Therefore \(\log(T^k n/n)\le -(\ln2-0.55\ln3)k+o(1)\); the correction is \(O(k2^k/Y)\). Our choice of \(\delta\) gives \(T^k n\le Z\). If even there, the next odd occurs by \(\lceil t/4\rceil-1\) except on an exponentially unlikely all-even run. At date \(16t\) the odd count is at most \(9t\) except with exponentially small probability. Since \(9\log_2 3-16<-1\) and \(16c<1\), the same correction yields \(T^{16t}n<n2^{-t}\), strictly below all states through \(t\). These bounds transfer jointly from the fair native tape with first bit one by the exact \(2^{16t}\)-dictionary, costing \(O(2^{16t}/Y)=o(1)\). Hence the marked physical path starts after the first landing, ends strictly before the true minimum, and \(v>R_A\).

For this \(Z,E,A\) use the bounded odd-landing test
\[
   f(v)=1\{v>R_A,\ \zeta_A(v)=1,\ \text{an entire pattern in }E
                                   \text{ ends strictly before }\tau(v)\}.
\]
A first odd landing from an odd start above \(Z\) is a new native record low: intervening even states before any odd observation are larger than their following odd endpoint. Thus all prelanding heights exceed \(v\). A successful test gives \(\zeta_A=1\) for the original source and an actual complete preminimum \(E\)-pattern. The preceding reference argument lower bounds its success-masked landing expectation by \(c_b c d_0/3\) eventually.

Write \(\alpha=1.001\). Leen's Appendix A, Corollary **Cross-exponent source swap** at frequency zero, compares the landing tests of \(W_Y\) and \(W_{Z^\alpha}\) with absolute error \(O_{\delta}((\ln Z)^{-c'})\). Its interval of permitted fixed source exponents contains both \(1+\delta\) and \(\alpha\). The main **Transport** proposition compares \(W_{Z^\alpha}\) with every outer \(W_H\), \(H\ge Z^\alpha\), for the same bounded test and an additional negative power of \(\ln Z\). Both estimates allow arbitrary tests varying with the thresholds, with unsuccessful first landings contributing zero. No uniformity as \(\delta\downarrow0\) is required. First fix \(c,\delta,A\), then fix a sufficiently large finite \(Z\), giving the uniform lower bound \(\eta=c_b c d_0/4\); only then send \(H\to\infty\). [Leen, Transport and Appendix A](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23269488)

The finite Boolean label of avoidance of \(A\) and capture by \(E\) has the same odd-octave limit as its natural density. That density is at least \(\eta\). Choosing a finite \(A\) within \(\eta/2\) of the supremum contradicts its definition upon adjoining \(E\). Thus finite capture densities approach one. This proves the assertion for every fixed \(b,B\), including (1), without assuming independence of events at successive height scales.

## Consequences for perturbations and fixed inverse rays

The rounded forward closure of seeds \(F\cap(B,\infty)\) in [Density-one unit perturbation](DENSITY_ONE_UNIT_PERTURBATION.md) remains computable, has octave density \(O(1/j^2)\) by the geometric closure estimate there, and captures every source that hits a seed. Once captured it follows the increasing odd ray. Almost every true path encounters the closure strictly before its genuine minimum. At the first entry the modified path begins increasing, so its minimum date is strictly earlier than the genuine one. The observed starting integer makes the two stopped-path laws mutually distinguishable on a fraction tending to one. For any \(K(n)=o(\log n)\) the upper bound in [Critical log-square masks](CRITICAL_LOG_SQUARE_MASKS.md) excludes a preminimum hit by \(K(n)\) on density one. The genuine minimum date exceeds \(K(n)\) with probability tending to one, since through that date all heights are at least \(n2^{-K(n)}\) and the actual global minimum law is tight. Thus the entire prescribed initial path agrees on density one. The same reasoning gives this deadline assertion for the mask itself.

Fix now a transient unit \(a\) with positive predecessor density \(h(a)\). Choose \(B\) above every state from \(a\) through its own first global minimum. A successful preminimum pattern with both endpoints above \(B\) on a source reaching \(a\) ends before that first arrival: any such endpoint after the arrival and before the source minimum would lie on the excluded finite segment from \(a\), and cannot equal \(a\). Hence its entire path belongs to the source's outward inverse prefix. For each fixed finite pattern family the event of such an occurrence before arrival at \(a\) is a finite-depth inverse cylinder union, since its finitely many candidate starting vertices which reach \(a\) have bounded first-arrival times. By natural predecessor measure identification its inverse probability is the limiting source probability conditional on reaching \(a\). For finite families of preminimum capture density tending to one, their missing conditional fractions are at most their unconditioned missing fractions divided by \(h(a)\). The ray therefore almost surely sees one such physical pattern for every fixed \(B,b\). Take the countable intersection. Along unbounded patterns, if \(V_j\) is the true starting vertex and \(V_i\) its terminal one in forward order, \(i<j\), then \(G_j\ge V_i/V_j\ge b2^e\). The accepting octave rule implies \(2^e\gg\log V_i\ge\log V_j\), with an absolute constant. Since \(\log V_j/j\to(\ln(4/3))/2>0\) almost surely and \(b\) is arbitrary, \(\limsup_jG_j/j=\infty\).
