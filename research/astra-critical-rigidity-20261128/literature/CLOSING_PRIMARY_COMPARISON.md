## Translated intervals and the functional moderate-deviation question

Weaver's November 21 Paper191 (v1 at 17:09 UTC; v2 at 21:05 UTC) proves first-passage estimates uniformly over the location of every interval of N positive integers. Theorem1 gives simultaneous concentration down to its stated logarithmic buffer. Theorem2 gives upper and lower exponential bounds for early and late passage at one fixed relative threshold, for deviations from a sufficiently large multiple of sqrt(L log L) to a sufficiently small multiple of L, where L=log_2 N. Proposition3 supplies an obstruction near the endpoint for far translated intervals. Its Section7 distinguishes those strict, two-clock crossings from Leen's source-normalized capped clocks. [Original v1 statements](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/191/v1) · [Current v2 statements and Section7](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/191/v2)

It does not claim the arbitrary-translation uniformity of Paper191. Conversely the displayed scalar upper/lower rate bounds in Paper191 are not a stated uniform-topology path principle with that exact rate. The results address different sampling and conclusion requirements. Version2 explicitly leaves the Gaussian rate constant undetermined. The present wording remains a proposed resolution of Leen's named conjecture, without an absolute priority claim beyond the sources examined.
