# Actual finite windows with all good marks

**Ordinary realization.** Prescribe a nonempty finite dyadic cylinder of an odd source $x$, a residue of $x$ modulo $3^b$, and finitely many positive rational affine marks on that cylinder. Require that each mark is physically integral there and that its coefficient of the remaining free parameter is a positive product of powers of two and three. Those conditions hold for the source, any finite collection of its prescribed forward or backward odd phases and the four marks $y-1,y+1,2y-1,3y$ at any of them. For negative affine offsets restrict to sufficiently large positive lifts. There are unbounded ordinary $x$ in the prescribed cylinder at which **all the named positive marks reach one with bounded odd counts**, with bounds depending on the prescribed finite data. All depths are genuine shortcut clocks. These controls exhibit convergence at the lower phases as well. They do not realize a bad cycle.

To prove the statement, use a sufficiently large representative of the joint congruences and a free nonnegative integer parameter $w$. The marks have shapes $2^{d_i}3^{g_i}w+b_i$ with integers $d_i,g_i\ge0$. Advancing the $i$th arm by its own $d_i$ shortcuts requires the parity word of $b_i$ alone and leaves odd free coefficient $3^{k_i}$. Two current forms can now be written $X=3^k w+a$ and $Y=3^l w+c$ with $k\le l$, so $Y=3^{l-k}X+(c-3^{l-k}a)$. The resident deterministic binary procedure refines $w$ and advances both forms for the same number of physical shortcuts until their free slopes and endpoints agree. The positive grade disparity drops at most four clocks per unit on average, by its explicit valuation calculation. Once the slopes agree, an even difference can be halved and an odd positive difference $d$ can be reduced in $2+v$ steps to $d'$ satisfying $2^v(4d'+2)=3d+1$. An equal pair stays equal on every later lift. Repeating this operation synchronizes the finite list, while preserving the original cylinder. Force the common form to take one extra odd step, which is possible since its free coefficient is odd. Its new form is $3^q z+v$ with $q\ge1$ and $v\equiv2\pmod3$. The powers of two run over all units modulo $3^q$: $2$ has order $2\cdot3^{q-1}$ by $v_3(4^k-1)=1+v_3(k)$. Choose $E$ from the resulting infinite progression with $2^E\ge v$, and take $z=(2^E-v)/3^q$. All named trajectories now meet at $2^E$ and take only even departures thereafter until one. Thus the number of odds on each arm is bounded independently of $E$. For the five marks at one source with $L$ prescribed binary bits and $b$ prescribed ternary digits, one pair merge increases $L+b+1$ by at most an absolute factor: at clock $j$ the grade difference is $O(j+L+b+1)$ and the logarithm of any canonical endpoint is $O(j+L+b+1)$. The telescoping two-form bound above gives the same order for that merge. Four merges therefore give $O(L+b+1)$ odds. For a growing number of marks the constants need not be uniform.

**A source-qualified logarithmic physical scale.** For a fixed number of marks on a two-sided window of $L$ prescribed native gaps, the preceding alignment uses $O(L+1)$ clocks and grades, the initial joint modulus has logarithm $O(L+1)$, and the synchronized offset $v$ has logarithm $O(L+1)$. The same bounds hold with $L+b$ for five marks at one dyadic cylinder of length $L$ and ternary precision $b$. The elementary power-of-two landing can cost exponentially many *even* clocks. A stronger existence bound follows from the stated linear routing theorem of Weaver and Wren. It sends every supplied positive odd ternary unit $Y$ backwards to every supplied unit class modulo $3^q$ with at most $C(q+1)$ total inverse valuation, uniformly in $Y$.

For any requested integer scale $R\ge1$, choose $Y=(2^m-1)/3$ with $m\equiv2\pmod6$, large enough that $Y\ge 3^{C(q+1)}(v+3^q2^R+1)$. Taking the first eligible $m$ loses only an absolute multiplicative factor in $Y$. This $Y$ is a positive odd ternary unit and reaches a power of two in one odd departure. Apply the routing theorem to the supplied $Y$ and class $v\bmod3^q$. Its positive ordinary source $u$ satisfies $u\equiv v\pmod{3^q}$ and $Y/3^{C(q+1)}\le u\le2^{C(q+1)}Y$, since each inverse step $(2^e y-1)/3$ lies between $y/3$ and $2^e y$. Thus $z=(u-v)/3^q\ge2^R$ is an allowed parameter. The resulting center $x$ has binary logarithm between $R$ and $R+O(L+1)$, after shifting the chosen scale by at most $O(L+1)$ to absorb $v$ and the initial modulus. More directly the displayed choice gives $\log_2 x=R+O(L+1)$ because $q+\log_2(v+1)=O(L+1)$. Every marked source reaches one using $O(L+1)$ odd departures, and its total clock is $O(R+L+1)$. In particular, taking $R=L$ realizes the prescribed fixed list on an ordinary center of size $2^{\Theta(L)}$, with actual termination of all marked trajectories in $O(L+1)$ shortcuts. The constants may depend on the fixed number of marks. The analytic routing theorem is an external input; the explicit replay below uses the elementary landing.
