# Fifteen upper odd departures at all four physical marks

**Computed conditional exclusion.** Suppose an ordinary least-tripling-disagreement bounded branch exists, and let $x$ be any odd phase of its bad cycle. None of $x-1,x+1,2x-1,3x$ can reach one with at most fifteen odd departures before its first hit. The first three marks are good at every phase. The fourth is good at the native phases specified in [the neighbor theorem](../../astra-unconventional-20261126/cycle/GOOD_NEIGHBORS.md). In particular the fourth result holds at the on-cycle canonical witness $1+(4/3)^r(s-1)$ for all four surviving $r$. Application to the *least* canonical witness still requires its previously established capture condition. The finite search and its independent replay have no kernel certificate.

Every $n\le 2^{18}$ reaches one by strong induction: the positive starts from 2 through $2^{18}$ each strictly descend within 135 shortcuts, by exact arithmetic. Hence the bad cycle minimum $s>2^{18}$. Up to 256 shortcuts, the odd correction product on any subinterval is at most $(1+1/(3s))^{256}\le 3s/(3s-256)$. The physical height bound and positivity now put the largest-to-smallest ratio of all observed odd coefficients below $267/50$, since $(16s+7)/(3s-256)\le267/50$. Each native edge costs one or two shortcuts.

The Python enumeration lifts the common odd source one dyadic bit at a time. At a surviving node of depth $t$, let $x$ be the least nonnegative representative of the source residue. For each lift $x+2^t z$ the two actual endpoints are $l+3^p z$ and $u+A3^q z$, where $p,q$ are the observed numbers of odd departures and the upper mark is $A x+B$. Split into the two next bits and reject an upper sixteenth odd, two consecutive lower evens, or lower coefficient extrema violating the displayed comparison. Reject a cylinder if both endpoints and both lift slopes agree, because then the whole cylinder has the same final label at the two marks. At each node solve $u+A3^q z=1$ for $z\ge0$ and replay both actual first hits at its candidate source. The initial source-one endpoint of $2x-1$ at time zero concerns only the known good source one. The potentially zero mark $x-1$ at $x=1$ cannot hit one. All four trees empty strictly before the depth bound.
