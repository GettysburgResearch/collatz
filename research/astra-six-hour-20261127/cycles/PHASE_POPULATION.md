# Logarithmic upper odd counts on almost every physical phase

Let $s$ and $p$ denote the least odd phase and the odd period on the restricted bounded alternative. Set $t=\lfloor\log_2(s-1)\rfloor-1$. For each of the four marks $y=x-1,x+1,2x-1,3x$, the first $t$ physical departures occur before any hit of one: $y\ge s-1>2^t$ and $T^j(y)\ge y/2^j$. This does not require a hitting-time hypothesis.

For any family $\mathcal W_t$ of parity words of length $t$, **at most $O(|\mathcal W_t|)$ phases** have a given mark's prefix in that family. Every parity word determines a unique positive source residue modulo $2^t$. The marks for different phases are distinct and lie between $s-1$ and $16s+7$. This interval contains a bounded number of representatives per residue. This is a count of genuine common starts, and a union bound permits any fixed finite list of such affine marks.

Suppose an irrationality exponent bound $\mu(\log_2 3)\le M$ is supplied. The cycle product gives $0<h/p-\log_2 3\le 1/(3s\log 2)$ for total native shortcut period $h$. The logarithm inequality implies $p\gg_\epsilon s^{1/(M+\epsilon)}$ on every unbounded sequence of candidates. Thus whenever the binary entropy $H_2(\eta)<1/M$ with $0<\eta<1/2$, all but $O(s^{H_2(\eta)})=o(p)$ phases have **at least $\eta t$ odds and at least $\eta t$ evens simultaneously at all four marks** before $t$ shortcuts. Indeed each exceptional tail has at most $2^{H_2(\eta)t}$ words by the binomial inequality. The argument independently excludes any upper prefix family of growth $2^{ct+o(t)}$ with $c<1/M$ at all but $o(p)$ phases. This gives a logarithmic budget lower bound for most phases, complementing the iterated-log bound for every phase. For absolute numerical values we use the stated homogeneous two-logarithm consequence of Chudnovsky's November 2026 preprint, which gives $M=3$ when the two positive rationals are multiplicatively independent. Its ineffective constant does not give a numerical cycle onset. Setting $\eta=1/20$ gives $H_2(1/20)<1/3$. The qualitative positive constant also follows from any classical finite irrationality bound, using a smaller $\eta$. [Chudnovsky, arXiv:2611.30820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30820)

The same conclusion is nonvacuous on the on-cycle canonical witnesses. If the cyclic native word has $n_1=2p-h$ ones and $n_2=h-p$ twos, it has at most $n_1$ maximal nonempty two-runs. There are at least $n_2-n_1=2h-3p$ twos beyond the first of their runs. Each run has length at most five for large $s$: a six-step run would have starting value at least $1+(4/3)^6(s-1)$, exceeding $(16s+7)/3$. Consequently at least $(2h-3p)/4$ run starts have at least two twos. At every such start the previous exponent one gives $x\equiv2\pmod3$, and the next two exponents equal to two give $x\equiv1\pmod{16}$. Hence $x\equiv17\pmod{48}$ and $3x$ is good there by the earlier neighbor theorem. Their density has lower limit at least $(2\log_23-3)/4>0.042$. All but $o(p)$ of those actual canonical sources have the displayed lower bound on their good tripled upper first-hit grade. An exceptional set of $o(p)$ can still contain the particular least canonical source. [Canonical floor and good marks](../../astra-unconventional-20261126/cycle/CANONICAL_FLOOR.md)

For comparison, uniform full-cycle measures on the binary parity shift have all weak limits of entropy at least $1/M$ bits per shortcut along such a sequence. At word length $d=\lceil\log_2(16s+8)\rceil$ every shortcut phase has a distinct word, giving entropy $\log_2 h$. Stationarity and block subadditivity imply the limiting entropy bound at every fixed block size, then at the entropy rate. This is the periodic instance of the [ordinary height-to-entropy argument](../escape/ENTROPY.md). A Haar component in the ensuing height-averaged binary windows does not force fair shortcut parities.
