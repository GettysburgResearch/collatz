# Arithmetic entropy of the actual orbit at auxiliary moduli

**Status.** Proposed written theorems. The finite observation decoder, exact same-clock collision test, canonical-alphabet observation bound, two-word translation identity and signed return determinant are checked in `AuxiliaryResidues.lean`. The entropy, stationarity and limiting arguments are written proofs. The Collatz conjecture remains open.

Fix one positive odd integer and its literal iterates $2^{a_i}q_{i+1}=3q_i+1$, $a_i\ge1$. Logarithms and entropies use nats. At a subsequence of prefix clocks, sample the starting date uniformly and take a stationary two-sided limit of the actual marked sequence of gaps and ordinary residues in $\widehat{\mathbf Z}$. Extend the negative dates arbitrarily before taking the limit. The endpoint equations hold almost surely on every finite window with finite gaps. On an escaping orbit the exact product, or simply distinctness and the harmonic sum, gives $\sum_{i<N}a_i\le N\log_2 3+O_{q_0}(\log N)$. Markov's inequality supplies tight gaps and a finite limiting mean. This construction keeps the gap word and every residue on the same sampled trajectory. Write $X_i$ for the limit residue coordinate and $\nu_M$ for the distribution of $X_0\bmod M$.

## AR1. Decoding an ordered word from two residues

For a word of $n$ positive gaps set $A_i=\sum_{j<i}a_j$ and

$$D(w)=2^{A_n}+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}3^{n-1-i}2^{A_i}.$$

Its exact endpoint relation is $2^{A_n}(q_n+1)=3^nq_0+D(w)$. Also $D(\varnothing)=1$ and $D(aw)=3^{|w|}+2^aD(w)$. Every $D(w)$ is odd. From $n,D(w)$ recover the first gap by taking the exact dyadic valuation of $D(w)-3^{n-1}$, then divide by that power and continue. The terminal one recovers the last gap as well. Consequently $q_0\bmod M,q_n\bmod M,A_n,n$ determine the word whenever $D(w)<M$. This holds for every positive modulus, including composite moduli sharing factors with six; there is no modular division in the observation. It holds as a congruence for every finite word in the stationary lift as well.

## AR2. A positive-entropy component has large residue entropy at every prescribed modulus

Suppose the stationary gap marginal is ergodic, has finite mean $b$, and has entropy rate $h$. The joint residue lift may have additional invariant information. Then

$$\boxed{\liminf_{M\to\infty}\frac{H(\nu_M)}{\log M}\ \ge\ \frac{h}{\max(\log 3,b\log2)}.}$$

The lower limit ranges over **all** integer moduli. Thus it holds, for example, along all powers of 5 or along any prescribed sequence of pairwise different primes. The theorem imposes no bound on the individual physical gaps and no assumption on the real escape rate. For a nonergodic joint law the same left side is at least the ergodic average of the displayed right side, using the individual means and entropy rates of its components. This follows by concavity of finite entropy and Fatou's lemma. In particular a subsequence of moduli with zero lower normalized residue entropy forces zero gap entropy on almost every ergodic component.

**Proof.** Put $L=\max(\log3,b\log2)$. Birkhoff gives $A_i=bi+o(i)$ almost surely, hence $\max_{i\le n}|A_i-bi|=o(n)$. Every term of $D(w)$ is at most $\exp(nL+o(n))$, uniformly over the terms of that sampled word. Fix $\epsilon>0$. With $n=\lfloor\log M/(L+\epsilon)\rfloor$, the event $D(w)<M$ and $A_n\le (b+1)n$ has probability $1-\delta_n$, where $\delta_n\to0$. No uniform bound is needed on words outside this event.

Truncate individual letters to a fixed finite alphabet of size $d$ (a single overflow symbol suffices). On a good block the two observed residues, the total among $O_b(n)$ possibilities and AR1 determine the whole truncated word. Supply one good/bad bit, the total on a good block and the truncated letters themselves on a bad block. The resulting conditional entropy bound, given the two boundary residues, is $O_b(\log(n+2))+\delta_n n\log d$. Concatenate $k$ successive blocks and reuse their $k+1$ boundary residues. The entropy of the $kn$ truncated letters is at most

$$(k+1)H(\nu_M)+k\big[O_b(\log(n+2))+\delta_n n\log d\big].$$

It is at least $kn h_d$, with $h_d$ the truncated gap entropy rate. Taking $k\to\infty$ for each $M$ removes the extra boundary. Then let $M\to\infty$, remove the truncation, and let $\epsilon\downarrow0$. In detail a finite first moment implies finite one-letter entropy (compare with a geometric distribution), and $0\le h-h_d\le H(a_0\mid\mathrm{trunc}_d(a_0))\to0$. This proves AR2. Discarding atypical *untruncated* word entropy without a tail estimate would not suffice.

For the triadic coordinate a stronger, previously available exact-dimension theorem in its stated drift range is recorded in PR182, `research/closure-round5-20261123/TERNARY_INFORMATION.md`. AR2's additional content is the bound at every auxiliary, arbitrarily chosen modulus. Its entropy is Shannon entropy. It gives no opposing order-two collision bound at the triadic place.

## AR3. A finite residue bias costs entropy; at equality the entire residue law is determined

Let $M\ge2$ be coprime to six, and let $p$ be a reference law on positive gaps with $p_1p_2>0$. Assume the actual stationary gap process has finite cross entropy $\mathbb E[-\log p(a_0)]$ and gap entropy rate $h$. Put $\Delta=\mathbb E[-\log p(a_0)]-h$. For the **actual lift**,

$$\boxed{\|\nu_M-\operatorname{Unif}(\mathbf Z/M)\|_{\rm TV}\le \frac{2(M-1)}{p_1p_2}\sqrt{\Delta}.}$$

In particular if the actual gap marginal is iid $p$, $X_0\bmod M$ is uniform and independent of the entire two-sided gap word. This also holds jointly for any finite list of prime-to-six residue coordinates by passing to their least common multiple, with the usual consistency on overlapping factors. At the entropy-extremal CW2a clocks from `CANONICAL_WINDOWS.md`, it applies to the **actual** raw gaps with their limiting three-letter law. It requires no independence hypothesis between the ordinary residue and the future gaps.

**Proof.** For a word $W$ of length $s$ let $J_0$ and $J_s$ be the joint laws of $(X_0\bmod M,W)$ and $(X_s\bmod M,W)$. Appending each word acts by a permutation $F_W$ of the residue fiber, so these joints have equal entropy. The entropy rate satisfies $s h\le H(W\mid X_0\bmod M)$. Indeed split $ks$ letters into adjacent $s$-blocks, adjoin the single starting residue, and use that its value and preceding letters determine the residue at each block boundary. Divide by $k$ and let $k\to\infty$. Consequently both joints have relative entropy at most $s\Delta$ from $\nu_M\otimes p^{\otimes s}$, since their word marginal is the same and their residue marginals are $\nu_M$. Pinsker's inequality in the half-$\ell^1$ convention gives a distance bound $\sqrt{s\Delta/2}$ at either endpoint.

For a single word $w$ of positive reference mass $p(w)$, the actual ending slice is the permuted starting slice. Comparing both slices to the reference in half-$\ell^1$ gives $p(w)\|(F_w)_*\nu_M-\nu_M\|_{\rm TV}\le 2\sqrt{s\Delta/2}$. The words $12$ and $21$ have affine maps $(9x+5)/8$ and $(9x+7)/8$. Their difference is translation by $1/4$, a generator of the additive residue group. Hence translation by that generator changes $\nu_M$ by at most $4\sqrt\Delta/(p_1p_2)$ in TV. Average its first $M$ translates, summing the telescoping distances, to obtain the displayed inequality.

If $\Delta=0$, both endpoint joints are exactly the product for every $s$. For past and future together, use independence of the starting residue from a full finite word beginning in the past, and transport that uniform residue to time zero by the word's prefix permutation. Every cylinder follows. Under bounded positive-gap iid (and in fact for every such two-sided path), the dyadic present state is determined by its entire future through its unique dyadic source cylinders and the triadic present state by its past through the factor $3^n$ in the complete equation. Thus the auxiliary uniform variable is independent of the *pair* of these actual local states. This is a fixed-modulus result, not an independence estimate at complementary growing precisions.

**A strict height-exponent penalty for an observed bias.** Suppose $R_N\le N^{A+o(1)}$ on a subsequence of an escaping ordinary source. Any further joint empirical limit has raw gap law equal to its canonical law, with mean $\log_2 3$ and entropy $h\ge\log3/A$ by CW2a. Take as $p$ the optimizing three-letter law from CW2; then $\mathbb E[-\log p(a_0)]=H_3$. If the same prefix residues at one fixed $M$ coprime to six converge along these clocks to a law at TV distance $d$ from uniform, then

$$ A\ \ge\ \frac{\log3}{H_3-[p_1p_2d/(2(M-1))]^2}. $$

This excludes the critical exponent for that subsequence whenever $d>0$ and gives a numerical gap once the bias is specified. No such positive bias is assumed or proved universally.

## AR4. The same prefix at a prescribed growing modulus

Let $g=\log3/H_3$ with the optimized three-letter entropy $H_3$ from CW2. Suppose one injective ordinary source has clocks $N\to\infty$ with $R_N\le N^{g+o(1)}$. For each fixed integer $k\ge1$, $\epsilon>0$ and *every* choice of integers $M_N\ge N^{g/k+\epsilon}$, the actual empirical residue law $\pi_{N,M_N}$ of the first $N$ odd states satisfies

$$\boxed{H(\pi_{N,M_N})\ \ge\ \frac{\log N}{k+1}-o(\log N).}$$

The error is uniform over these moduli (for the given source, subsequence, $k,\epsilon$). More generally if $R_N\le N^{A+o(1)}$ along a subsequence with fixed $A<\infty$, the same conclusion holds for $M_N\ge N^{A\log16/(k\log3)+\epsilon}$ without any iid conclusion. This actual finite-clock assertion does not require a speed of stationary convergence, and it includes powers of a single prescribed auxiliary prime. It bounds Shannon entropy, so it does not bound the largest atom or the collision probability in the required direction.

**Proof.** From CW1--CW2 at the same source, write $H_N=\max_{i\le N}q_i$, $m=\lfloor\log_3 H_N\rfloor+1$, and $n=\lfloor m/k\rfloor$. We have $m\le(A/\log3+o(1))\log N$ and $m\to\infty$. All but $O((\log N)^2)$ of the $N-m+1$ starts have a clean $m$-window (the actual gaps equal the canonical digits). These clean words are distinct, since equal words give equal terminal residues modulo $3^m>H_N$, violating ordinary injectivity. Sample a start $J$ uniformly from those $N-m+1$ positions, including the contaminated ones. The entropy of its canonical $m$-word is at least $(1-o(1))\log N$ by conditioning on cleanliness. Cropping to $kn$ letters loses at most $k\log3$. Each of the $k$ blocks is typically reconstructible from its two boundary residues and clock; on a contaminated or oversized block transmit the $n$ canonical letters instead. The clock of a clean block has at most $3n$ possibilities. Given failure probability $o(1)$ per block, the $k+1$ boundary entropies therefore total at least $\log N-o(\log N)$.

For the general stated cutoff every clean observation satisfies $D(w)\le16^n<M_N$ eventually, by AR1 and its finite bound. At the critical clocks CW2a yields convergence of every fixed canonical word to the iid law with mean $\alpha=\log_2 3$. In particular for a uniform start of a growing block of length $n$ the maximum centered partial sum over that block is $o(n)$ in probability. Here is the triangular-array detail. Split into disjoint chunks of a fixed length $d$, leaving fewer than $d$ letters. The maximum centered partial sum divided by $n$ is at most the average absolute full-chunk deviation divided by $d$, plus $O(d/n)$. Each sampling offset shifts the uniform starting distribution by $O(m/N)$ in TV relative to the original prefix. The expectation of this bound has upper limit at most the iid expectation of $|\sum_{j<d}(b_j-\alpha)|/d=O(d^{-1/2})$. Let $d$ tend to infinity and apply Markov. The estimate holds for all $k$ fixed block positions. Thus $D(w)\le \exp(n\log3+o_{\mathbb P}(n))$ on clean blocks. The exponent margin in $M_N$ gives the required failure probability $o(1)$.

Each boundary has the distribution of $q_J$ over a shifted interval omitting at most $O(m)$ positions of the full prefix; including the terminal time $N$ adds at most one more. All these marginal laws can be compared on a shared underlying support of at most $N+1$ *ordinary indices*, independently of the number of possible residues. Coupling the indices first bounds the entropy difference after any residue projection by $h_2(O(m/N))+O((m/N)\log(N+1))=o(1)$, where $h_2$ here denotes binary entropy in nats. Subtract at most $k[O(\log n)+o(n)]$ nats of side information. This proves AR4.

## AR5. What a local atom can represent

Let an ergodic stationary joint component contain an atom $z$ in the $p$-adic present-state marginal for any rational prime $p$. Then its raw gap word is supported on the shifts of **one periodic rational odd Collatz word**. Its $p$-coordinate follows that same rational cycle; so do its dyadic and triadic coordinates. The rational cycle may be negative and can have a nontrivial odd denominator. This statement classifies a component of a limiting law; it does not make the original positive orbit periodic.

Indeed recurrence gives repeated visits to $z$ almost surely on its shift saturation, which has full measure. A return of $n>0$ letters and total $B$ gives $(2^B-3^n)z=C$ in $\mathbf Q_p$ for the positive integer carry $C=D(w)-2^B$. The integer multiplier is nonzero. Thus $z$ is the indicated rational point. Every intermediate state of this return is the fixed point of the corresponding rotated affine word, with the same denominator $2^B-3^n$ and an odd numerator. All these rational numbers are dyadically odd, and the gaps of the return are therefore their literal, unique odd rational Collatz valuations. Every return from $z$ follows its same deterministic rational cycle. Recurrence in both time directions fills the entire path. This polynomial identity works at $p=2,3$ as well, since it is read in the field after the exact integer-coefficient equations have passed to the limit. On a periodic gap path the dyadic residue must equal the rational phase by its unique future, and the triadic residue by its unique past (or iterate the homogeneous difference through arbitrarily many periods at the respective contracting place). In particular any ergodic component with nonperiodic gap law has a nonatomic marginal at *every* prime, even when its gap entropy is zero.

## AR6. A heavy moving residue forces long rational-cycle words on the same ordinary prefix

This finite inverse statement applies to **every** positive ordinary prefix, with no injectivity or entropy hypothesis. Sample dates $0,\ldots,N-1$. Fix any modulus $M\ge2$ and residue $r$ visited at $s\ge2$ dates $i_1<\cdots<i_s$. For consecutive visits $i<j$ put $k=j-i$, $B=A_j-A_i$ and $t(i,j)=k\log3+B\log2$. Write

$$V=(N-1)\log12+\log q_0,\qquad T_0=V/(s-1),\qquad T=\log(M/2)-T_0.$$

Choose a cheapest consecutive return $w_0$, of cost at most $T_0$. It has a rational odd-cycle center $z=C_0/D_0$ with $D_0=2^{B_0}-3^{k_0}\ne0$, $C_0>0$. Its primitive period $d$ is at most $T_0/\log3$. If $T>0$, every consecutive return of cost strictly less than $T$ follows a positive power of that **same** rational period starting at $z$. There are at most $V/T$ other returns. The rational may be negative or nonintegral. The conclusion is about the actual ordered gap words on the positive orbit.

**Proof.** The return intervals have disjoint sets of edges. Since $2^{a_i}q_{i+1}=3q_i+1\le4q_i$ and $q_{N-1}\ge1$, telescoping gives $A_{N-1}\log2\le(N-1)\log4+\log q_0$. Thus their total cost is at most $V$. For each return use the usual unaugmented carry $C=D(w)-2^B$, where $D(w)$ is the observation from AR1; call the signed slope difference $D_w=2^B-3^k$ to distinguish it from that observation. Then

$$D_w r\equiv C\pmod M,\qquad |D_w|< e^t,\qquad 0<C<e^t.$$

The last inequality follows from $C=\sum_{j<k}3^{k-1-j}2^{B_j}< 2^B 3^k$, where $B_j$ is the return's local prefix clock. For a second return, elimination of $r$ gives $M\mid D_0 C - D_w C_0$ in the integers. Its absolute value is less than $2e^{T_0+t}$, hence less than $M$ for $t<T$. Thus $C/D_w=C_0/D_0$ exactly, even if either slope difference shares a factor with $M$. Each rational fixed point has unique physical rational odd-gap iteration, as in AR5. Both words are powers of its primitive return word with the same initial phase. Markov counting on the disjoint edge costs bounds the number of exceptions.

**Quantitative shadow.** For every $L\ge1$, at least

$$s-L-LV/T$$

of the $s$ **residue dates** start an actual length-$L$ gap word equal to the first $L$ gaps of the infinite periodic itinerary of $z$, within the sampled prefix. Indeed at most $L$ dates lack $L$ subsequent consecutive returns. Each exceptional consecutive return can spoil at most $L$ candidate starting dates among the hits; $L$ good consecutive returns contain at least $L$ physical edges. This counts distinct starting dates, without inferring a return-frequency weight from the elapsed lengths of their excursions.

Consequently, suppose along some sequence $N\to\infty$ one fixed positive source has a stationary limiting raw gap law $\mu$. Take *any* $M_N\to\infty$; the maximizing residue and prime support of the modulus may change at every clock. If $\limsup_N\max_r\pi_{N,M_N}(r)=\delta>0$, then $\mu$ gives mass **at least $\delta$ to a single periodic right-infinite word at a particular phase**. Choose a subsequence attaining this upper limit. The corresponding $T_0$'s are uniformly bounded, so a further subsequence fixes the cheapest finite return word among finitely many possibilities. Now $T\to\infty$ and $V/N=O_{q_0}(1)$. For every fixed $L$ the displayed count gives limiting cylinder mass at least $\delta$; taking the decreasing intersection of these cylinders proves the assertion. If the primitive period has length $d$, stationarity gives total cycle mass at least $d\delta$. In particular a stationary gap limit with no periodic component forces $\max_r\pi_{N,M_N}(r)\to0$ and $\sum_r\pi_{N,M_N}(r)^2\to0$ on that same sequence, **uniformly for all moving moduli above any prescribed diverging cutoff** (otherwise select a violating subsequence). This conclusion includes zero-entropy aperiodic limiting laws, positive exponential source growth and unbounded carries. It supplies no power decay exponent and permits periodic components of a positive escaping orbit's limit.

Even maximal fixed-prime equidistribution does not settle the extremal growth exponent. At the CW sample depth $m\asymp\log N$, the word entropy can already exhaust $\log N$, the entire entropy of the sampled index. One cannot add the unconditional auxiliary residue entropy to the full $m$-word entropy. At a fixed small modulus the two are independent in the stationary iid lift. At complementary growing precisions the initial residue may be correlated with the sampled growing word and the finite sample has only $N$ indices. A useful missing estimate would control such **conditional** entropy quantitatively on that same original prefix. No such estimate, contradiction for exponential escape, or exclusion of cycles is derived here.
