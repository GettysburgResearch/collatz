# Attempt ledger and next-pass decision

## Full objective retained

The goal was an end-to-end proof through all three routes, not simply more
compatible finite words. A complete proof was not obtained. The pass nevertheless
produced proof-bearing partial results in all three directions and executable
finite certificates. None of the open hypotheses below is silently adopted.

## Route 1: aggregate mass

Attempt: retain a simple summable power weight, but sum over actual survivors
instead of seeking a pointwise inverse supersolution already obstructed in #90.

Result: exact killed Mellin transfer; an explicit all-time 69/200 ternary-bias
ceiling at H=64 that would give M_(k+1)<0.993 M_k; and a source-cutoff-free
finite-depth certificate based on complete inverse cones and analytic zeta tails.
The ceiling passes for 0<=k<=36. A stricter 1/3 ceiling is exactly false at k=19.

The first unsupported inference would be extrapolating that finite ceiling to
all k. No temporal independence or monotonicity of the residue share has been
proved. The inverse cones themselves grow quickly, so brute-force extension is
not a proof program by itself. A structural history-conditioned ternary estimate
or nonsummable dissipation theorem is needed.

The critical-exponent mass identity adds a second interface: a hypothetical
E(X)=O(X^0.901) basin must have limiting weighted residue-2 share approximately
0.32233917. This is forced bias, not evidence that the basin exists or that the
critical counting bound holds.

## Route 2: ordinary return echoes

Attempt: retain the same first-crossing source/end equation, but spend the
endpoint displacement as forced continuation information rather than insist on
descent exactly at the crossing.

Result: v_2(d) determines the first future parity disagreement. One exact
inequality can certify descent there, and eliminating the source gives a
common-d lower threshold for each displacement stratum. The rational compiler
shows a substantive prefilter, removing 6,431 of 9,779 formal pairs through
length 21, but no ordinary positive-displacement pair was present in that finite
population to begin with.

The obstruction is high prefix coefficient bank at the disagreement, or the
opposite parity at that point. The entire d=0 cycle lane also remains. No
uniform source-height estimate, all-word echo-safe exclusion, or SC* proof was
obtained. The next target is joint compatibility of an echo-safe displacement
with support loss, source minimality, and the whole denominator—not a separate
local factor hit.

## Route 3: a broad candidate rank class eliminated

Attempt: supplement logarithmic height with finitely many unbounded valuations
of affine forms, and allow any fixed shortcut block length.

Result: an all-class obstruction. Even branches force a coefficient transport
inequality. Finite support removes all nonzero odd-prime roots and restricts the
dyadic coefficients; a synchronized ordinary all-odd family then contradicts
nonincrease. The argument covers every finite floor and every fixed block size. A separate
late extension proves the same scalar-template obstruction for the adaptive
whole-run macro, using its infinitely many branch preimages. A finite feature
list is insufficient even though its valuation values are unbounded.

Positive infrastructure: carry sorting is normalized exactly by an inversion
measure, with a unique mixed-radix normal form. The odd-run macro retains its
unbounded quotient and valuation data exactly. No decreasing semantic rank for
that adaptive macro was found. The separate macro theorem excludes the stated
finite linear affine-valuation class there too, but does not exclude nonlinear
ranks, infinite feature sets, or every other adaptive macro.

## Cross-route information gained

The rank obstruction does not kill route 1: aggregate weights need not be
pointwise nonincreasing. The return-echo sieve is a concrete example of the
history and ordinary-source information that a residue-only aggregate model
would lose. A next joint attack should retain that information when studying
survivor-conditioned ternary mass.

For a next pass restricted to two routes, the present evidence favors aggregate
Mellin transport and return-echo/denominator compatibility. That is a research
allocation judgment, not a probability of solving Collatz. The rank route now
has a more precise excluded class and a clean handoff for a genuinely different
semantic invariant.
