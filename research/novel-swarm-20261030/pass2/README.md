## Mathematical additions

[Invariant rigidity](RIGIDITY.md) proves that every finite-dimensional binary
linear Collatz invariant is constant, over any field. It separately proves
that an automatic invariant in **any integer base** is constant. Over a finite
field, an invariant's generating series can therefore be algebraic only when
it is the trivial constant-positive-source series. The proof combines a
credited finite-family merger construction, a self-contained inverse-ladder
cone argument, and finite-dimensional observability. It does not assume that
the actual convergence indicator has any of these finite-complexity properties.

[Inverse pressure](INVERSE_PRESSURE.md) establishes an all-depth theorem for
**every fixed positive odd target**, including possible nontrivial cycles:

    G_s(t) = sum_(all positive inverse histories ending at t) J^s < infinity
    for every s>1, where J=3^(odd steps)/2^(total valuation).

First-hit source injectivity controls all depths at once; a proved geometric
factor handles every cycle return. The result strengthens #142's fixed-depth
bound and gives a simple-pole upper bound for its original critical resolvent.
A finite source census with a rigorous infinite-source tail certifies

    2.685114973562 < G_2(1) < 2.685132412180.

[Exact ternary laws](EXACT_DENSITY.md) provides a cyclic convolution algorithm
that sums all exponent tails exactly, both for the original geometric law and
for its square tilt. The tilted point densities sum to G_2(t), connecting the
residue computations to the independent forward-source certificates.
