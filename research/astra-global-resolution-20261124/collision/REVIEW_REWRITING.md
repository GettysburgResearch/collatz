# Independent review of the seven-return reductions

Use $T(2n)=n$ and $T(2n+1)=3n+2$ on positive integers. An ordinary label satisfies $P(n)\leftrightarrow P(Tn)$. A step on the scaled coordinate $7x$ is an actual shortcut step there. Division by seven after a first return gives an additional relation on labels of the unscaled $x$'s only under the transfer hypothesis in the pertinent theorem.

Strong induction now covers all positive integers. For the convergence color the reverse implication from universal convergence is immediate, so this is also a conditional equivalence.
