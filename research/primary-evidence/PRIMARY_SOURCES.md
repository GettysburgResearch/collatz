## Reconstructing the first-landing deduction

In inverse order let $S_i=\sum_{j\le i}a_j$, $J_i=3^i2^{-S_i}$ and $F_i=\sum_{j\le i}J_j/3$. Equation (39) of #12 retains words at **one specified central total** with $\max_i J_i\le R_0$ and $F_k\le B_0$. It lower-bounds the unnormalized joint mass $3^k\Pr(F_k=z\bmod3^k,S_k=d,\mathrm{restrictions})$ by $c\pi_k(d)$ on one fixed ternary cylinder. Its source is Wren's fixed-total adaptation of El Jaouhari's selected balanced blocks. Mazur's September predecessor-density theorem instead gives a positive coefficient depending on the target for unrestricted predecessor sets. It does not by its printed statement give this shell, barrier or target-uniform coefficient. [Wren #10 v3](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/10/v3) · [El Jaouhari](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23377671) · [Mazur](https://www.proofatlas.ai/formalizations/positive-lower-density-collatz-predecessors/)

The difficult normalization in Appendix A was checked as follows. At each selected prefix, group by its exponent total $D$. The weighted residue-counting bound at that single $D$ is an $L^1$-to-sup estimate independent of the number of totals, by spacing the distinct physical inverse lifts. Summing it costs the **sum of the per-total mean errors**. In (54)–(55), conditioning a spatial projection at a shorter depth convolves its clock with an independent negative-binomial law. Summing over a finite set $W$ of totals costs at most $\min(1,|W|\sup \pi)$ times the full joint oscillation. In (56)–(59), rejection of the next block has an independent suffix of length at least $n/1616$. The ordinary central clock denominator is $\gg n^{-1/2}$. The mixing error uses this independent suffix factor except at the last adjacent generation, where $n=O(b_N)$ absorbs the lost $\sqrt n$ against $b_N^{-2}$. The errors after normalization form a summable sequence. For each fixed base the negative-binomial ratio tends uniformly to one on the central window after truncating the fixed prefix clock, with an absolute dominating ratio. The last unrestricted tail uses grouped oscillation summed over **distinct** residual totals. All retained word bounds extend over this final partial block using the selected-prefix multiplier $16^{b_0-b_j}$. Thus the exact retained subdensity really does bound every inverse-prefix multiplier and offset needed to keep the physical path above its barrier. The only joint analytic estimate in this assembly, (53), also follows from the independently reconstructed [natural-depth clock mixing](FIXED_PRECISION.md).

Given $a\le M$ odd and $3\nmid a$, choose $r=(2^e a-1)/3$ on the positive cylinder with $2KM-1/3\le r<C_bM$ by the generator $2$ of the units modulo the fixed $3$-power and increments of its period. Take $K$ so $r>2B_0$ and $r-B_0>R_0M$. Each compatible inverse prefix is then physical and exceeds $M$. For $L=\log_2(X/r)$ and $|k-L/(2-\log_2 3)|\le h\sqrt L$, choose the integer $d_k$ in $L-1<d_k-k\log_2 3\le L$. At $\gg\sqrt L$ such depths, $\pi_k(d_k)\gg1/\sqrt L$. The corresponding sources are $(r-F_k)2^{d_k}/3^k\in(X/4,X]$. Each contributes the same geometric probability at fixed $k,d_k$ and is uniquely identified by its actual path. Across depths its **first landing time** distinguishes it even if $a$ is periodic. This verifies (40) from the retained floor and identifies the stronger content than an ordinary predecessor count.

## Scope of the oversampled renewal calculation

In #48 Lemma 2 the first-exit distribution (9) is Tao's Lemma 7.7 for the holding walk, with a Gaussian-plus-exponential horizontal bound and an exponentially decaying overshoot. On a common finite window of length $P$, all observed locations lie in a single $O(u(P+1))$ rectangle with high probability. The later large triangles have lower tips confined to this vertical band. Their left corners are separated on a common lattice row at height $b+\lfloor S/2\rfloor$. This is the origin of one window cost $O(u(P+1)/S)$ rather than summing that probability over $P$ times.

The stronger stopping estimate (59)–(60) keeps $\max(e^{D(A+1)},20(R+1))$ as its induction threshold. In the profile union (30)–(35) each realisable profile restricts the same **unconditioned** first horizontal exit coordinate, so no conditional version of its density bound is used. The product estimate (31) needs the width of each later interval at least the preceding spacing. This holds since the completed large records increase the height by at least twice their duration. The combinatorial factor grows like $\exp(O(R\log(K+2)))$ at $P=\lceil e^{KR}\rceil$, with $R=O(A+1)$; one fixed large $K$ absorbs it. With the maximum retained, (63) allows a fixed fractional deficit depending on the required Fourier power. In Theorem 6 the $17q/20$ prefix and the $q\log_2 3$ total cutoff leave a suffix at conductor $\gg q$ and an exponentially small discarded prefix mass. The squared collision estimate follows by counting distinct numerators within each fixed clock and a geometric moment. Clock Parseval and weighted Cauchy–Schwarz turn this into (66), including its normalization on counting-measure vectors.

For Lemma 7, equation (77)'s preservation of the old mixing errors under a valuation cap is justified by the grouped positive arithmetic bound on **every subfamily** from #12's (52) and the absolute per-total errors (57). Monotonicity alone would not compare a signed difference of two capped approximations. Each new rejected block before final normalization costs $O(b_i2^{-J})$ after summing over distinct residual totals. Their normalized sum, including the last free tail, is $O(n^{3/2}2^{-J})$. At $J=\lceil 1.5\log_2(n+1)+C_0\rceil$ this can be made sufficiently small uniformly. Our own [fixed-precision replacement](FIXED_PRECISION.md) bypasses these extra-depth estimates for pointwise ordinary convergence. It gives no fixed *fractional* deficit by itself.
