## Headline conclusion

The exact inference repeatedly missing from the positive-orbit programs is

```text
compatible finite integer cylinders
  -> one inverse-limit point
  -/-> one ordinary integer.
```

Compactness supplies a point of `Z_2`. It does not put that point in `Z`.

For a whole deterministic architecture, ordinary realization is equivalent to boundedness—hence eventual constancy—of its least positive depth-`N` survivor.

`R-7801` proves that the common local data currently emphasized by the refund programs cannot repair this implication. Any prescribed nonordinary dyadic completion can be realized as the unique integrality completion of an exact nonautonomous affine system in which:

- every finite prefix has infinitely many positive ordinary starts;
- every legal finite trajectory is integral and positive;
- the odd numerator multiplier can dominate the dyadic divisor by an arbitrarily large factor at every step;
- every legal step can be made to expand by any prescribed amount;
- yet no ordinary integer realizes the infinite path.

Therefore growth, quotient refund, branch abundance, thinness, prime renewal, modular lassos, and finite-prefix compatibility are not extraction theorems. They remain useful only after or alongside an architecture-specific bound on the least initial representatives.
