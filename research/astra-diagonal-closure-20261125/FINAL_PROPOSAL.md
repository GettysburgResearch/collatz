## What cannot be used as the final theorem

1. Arbitrarily large odd-work surplus after extending a known upper hit does not supply a new pointwise certificate within the original budget.
2. The saved upper suffix is attached to a pair separated by a positive integer carry. It is not automatically attached to a ternary tower or to a smaller source.
3. A local ordinary inverse exclusion does not prove that every bounded critical word contains that exclusion. The positive ordinary controls pass all tested windows without forming a cycle.
4. Physically attainable seeds at unrestricted height do not put a source into the required interval. Completeness of each binary residue class is not a source-population estimate.
5. A formal query path is not necessarily realizable by the true orbit-invariant color. The grid union deliberately contains those formal paths.

## Concrete next proof tasks

The first pointwise task is to use genuine badness and the exact saved suffix to control the positive carry. A successful statement must identify the new positive ordinary source, prove its required basin orientation, and exhibit the time and odd-work accounting for its upper certificate. The current theorem only performs the repair when the carry's hit and the upper endpoint's binary alignment are supplied.

For the bounded-cycle alternative, the new exact optimization pair at the minimum must hold simultaneously with its all-window inequalities and full physical integrality. A successful argument must exclude that conjunction for every nontrivial word, not just exhibit some forbidden windows or count a tested range. The first-crossing alternative from PR205 still needs its own pointwise contradiction.

For the statistical route, the proposed convenient complete-grid enlargement is too large: its many-clock event has a positive lower bound, or a lower bound of order at least \(d^{-1}\) after the genuine native filter and unrestricted-height realizability. Any replacement must prove its lower incidence from actual discordant parents **and** its upper estimate on the same smaller event. In particular the original row-height constraint cannot be dropped and silently restored.

These are explicit mathematical gaps. The four modules and the accompanying written proofs are research progress toward them, not a completed proof proposal with an implicit final assumption.
