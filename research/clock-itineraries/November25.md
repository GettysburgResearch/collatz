# November 25 proof attempt

**Collatz remains open.** The new Lean proposal proves the following equivalence for the ordinary positive integers. For each positive odd $a$ coprime to three, write $S_k(a)$ for the total number of shortcut steps through its first $k$ odd gaps. Universal convergence holds exactly when both claims hold.

1. For every such $a$ there is a finite integer $M_a$ with $3^k\le M_a2^{S_k(a)}$ for every $k$.
2. At arbitrarily large depths the executable balanced-path test from the earlier proposal returns false. One true test certifies an actual nontrivial cycle.

The first condition is exactly eventual repetition on every individual orbit. The bound may depend on the starting integer. Both global conditions remain to be proved. Lean also proves that their conjunction is equivalent to successful finite gate counts on arbitrarily large octaves. ## What the three orbit arguments establish

**Ternary towers.** Given any odd $w$ and any starting exponent $s$, some $0\le d\le2^{1379}$ gives 513 consecutive inputs $3^{s+d+r}w$, $0\le r\le512$, with the same ordinary endpoint after exactly 1,381 shortcut steps. That endpoint is below $3^{s+d}w$. At clock 1,024 a separate residue class gives 537 simultaneous descents below the same base. Its population guarantee and PR #209's 116-rank density guarantee differ. The prescribed first bad rank can lie outside the certified block. For each predetermined rank and deadline, Lean constructs arbitrarily large bases with disjoint adjacent orbit segments through that deadline, even allowing different clocks. [Full statement, certificates and comparison](TowerFrontier.md)

**Escaping orbits.** If the odd orbit of one supplied integer never repeats, at least $17/41$ of its odd times, in lower asymptotic density, end among 403 of the 4,374 ternary unit classes modulo $3^8$. The stricter slope $18/11$ gives at least $23/287$ of its times in 143 classes. The strict record, incoming clocks, and residue computations refer to the same trajectory. Its critical clock records also exclude every fixed positive integer as a ternary accumulation point. The written limiting argument describes the joint distribution with the real logarithmic phase. Converging trajectories can realize the exceptional pattern on arbitrarily long finite segments. [Record and residue theorem](EscapeFrontier.md) · [Critical records and ordinary representatives](EscapeRecords.md)

**Periodic orbits.** For a hypothetical nontrivial cycle of least odd period $p$ and minimum $m$, more than $p/10{,}000$ distinct odd phases exceed $2.08m$. More than $p/25{,}000$ both exceed this height and have residue two modulo three. The all-period Lean theorem takes its three numerical bounds on $m,p$ explicitly. Applying them to all positive cycles uses the external estimates documented with the theorem. Irregular wide cycles still satisfy these restrictions. ## Attempting to combine them

The octave theorem uses actual representatives of each specified target in a common interval. Its test allows fewer than a Pascal sum of exceptions and accepts a common gate whose own convergence has yet to be established. It can also bound the number of ordinary components when several gates cover the interval. A tower theorem alone supplies no such cover. Each undoubled tower $3^rw$ has at most one member in $[X,2X)$ because neighboring ranks differ by a factor of three. Across different bases, the certified tower endpoints may vary. [Counting proof and finite examples](WindowCapture.md)

There is a further written consequence of the arbitrary-length affine merger construction cited in the tower note. On each odd tower there are arbitrarily long consecutive blocks sharing one convergence label, beyond every prescribed phase. If the label word on each tower were uniformly recurrent, it could contain no adjacent change. Indeed, a two-letter word exhibiting a change would have to occur with bounded gaps, contradicting those constant blocks. Universal equality of the convergence labels of $w$ and $3w$ implies Collatz by the tripling descent argument, which uses only invariance under actual steps and under multiplication by three. In the converse direction, convergence makes all the words constant. This supplies a recurrence criterion for the whole conjecture. Uniform recurrence of the actual basin labels remains open. The finite parity prefixes along each tower are periodic in the rank, but a fixed meeting time at an exact ordinary endpoint depends on the upper digits as well. The separate basin-profile argument would suffice with strong convergence along a single rescaling subsequence and the source-qualified positive predecessor density theorem. Exact dilation identities force any such limit to be constant. Comparison of empirical averages, including on the shrinking windows proved there, still permits fine oscillation of the labels.
