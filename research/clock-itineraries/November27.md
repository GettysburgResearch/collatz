# November 27 research handoff

The positive-integer Collatz conjecture remains open. This packet uses the shortcut map that halves even inputs and sends odd \(n\) to \((3n+1)/2\). The results below identify what can be proved for a prescribed target, a prescribed starting value and an entire ordinary component.

## Ordinary integer results

* **Sharp simultaneous preimages.** Every positive target coprime to three is the common image of \(x,3x\) in exactly 14 steps and of \(x,3x,9x\) in exactly 28. Both thresholds are optimal, even when the target tests are restricted to the sufficiently late doublings of any one specified unit. There is also an explicit infinite family of odd triples for which all three first stopping times are exactly \(21+1458t\). [Statements, formulas and proofs](FreeTowerFamilies.md)
* **Counts inside each basin.** Every positive starting integer admits a distinct encoding by a synchronized triple. The base grows by at most a fixed factor, and the triple retains every subsequent visit to a unit target. This transports the Krasikov–Lagarias predecessor exponent and Mazur's positive lower density to synchronized triples. The ordinary map in Mazur's theorem uses a full odd step. Lean checks the conversion for odd targets, constructs an odd unit ancestor for every even unit within four shortcuts, and proves positive density in every sufficiently large interval of triple bases. For a proof of full Collatz it now suffices to find arbitrarily large stages with an arbitrarily small missed fraction at one gate per stage. [Finite counting reduction](SynchronousBasinCount.lean) · [Positive-density reduction](SynchronousBasinDensity.lean) · [Density of the actual bases](SynchronousDensityTransfer.lean) · [Krasikov and Lagarias](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0205002) · [Mazur, Theorem 1.1](https://www.proofatlas.ai/papers/positive-lower-density-collatz-predecessors/paper-v1.pdf)
* **Every Mersenne exponent.** For each \(t>0\), the actual orbit of \(2^t-1\) meets that of some \(Y\equiv17\pmod{48}\) whose multiple \(3Y\) reaches 1 with exactly three odd departures by \(t+2081\) steps. Convergence of these lower starts \(Y\) remains the missing implication. At 183 of 192 previously specified delayed inputs, Lean also constructs smaller canonical parents with physical upper hits. [Mersenne representation and fixed-source results](ReturnDescent.md)
* **A difficult individual input converges.** For \(m=3145727\) and \(z=(2^{m+7}-271)/81\), the exact first time to 1 is 27,198,220. Seven steps reach \(2^m-1\), followed by \(m\) odd steps to \(3^m-1\). The remaining 24,052,486 steps include 12,029,702 odd departures. Two independent integer evaluations agreed, using blocks of 320 and 383 steps and different decoding methods. The 383-step replay proves firstness by bounding each completed block away from 1 and checking the tail one step at a time. A standalone Lean replay for the exact-hit assertion is undergoing a separate, longer kernel check. [First-hit computation](mersenne_parent_replay.py) · [Proof status and reproduction](ReturnDescent.md)
* **Rational exclusions at actual records.** Write \(M_i=3^i/2^{S_i}\), where \(S_i\) is the total shortcut time to the \(i\)-th odd site. At a strict record \(k\), closeness of \(x_k\) to a rational with controlled numerator and denominator modulo \(3^n\) forces a quantitatively large gain over some suffix of length at most \(n\). The bound is polynomial in the denominator and the requested gain. The natural periodized denominator from the complete prefix grows at least exponentially along successive records on a fixed orbit. This explains why that proposed witness falls outside the polynomial range. [Explicit inequalities, both primes and remaining escape obligation](ReturnOrbit.md)

## A candidate operator-theoretic advance

For Béhani's classical Bergman quotient transfer \(A\), the weighted one-step row norms separate the individual ordinary vertices. Functional calculus then recovers the coordinate projections from the single operator. The resulting von Neumann algebra \(W^*(A)\) consists precisely of the operators acting separately on each ordinary component. Mori's work established related irreducibility criteria for unweighted operators and branch-generated algebras. [Béhani, Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.03203) · [Mori, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08084) · [Mori, 2025, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05713v2)

One explicit arithmetic partial isometry \(V\) then gives a sharp alternative. For every \(k\), the distance of \(VA^k\) from \(W^*(A)\) plus compact operators is zero under universal convergence and is exactly \(\|A^k\|\) if a second component exists. A strict upper bound at one depth would settle the conjecture. The arithmetic row dichotomy, finite propagation controls and coordinate separation have Lean proofs. The argument identifying the full Hilbert-space algebra and operator distance is written mathematics. The strict upper bound remains open. [Construction, proof and verification boundaries](BergmanRelative.md)

## Evidence that bears on the remaining steps

The sharp height formulas rule out selecting a uniformly smaller pair at clock 14. For \(b=1093+3^{14}t\), even allowing every shorter common clock gives a least base \((16384b-313)/81\). Any descent proof through these pairs must also use later dynamics or another comparison. At the other end, finite inspection through \(x=119304647\) found 5,585,854 qualifying synchronized triples, all of whose 28-step endpoints reached 1 within 381 further steps. The required statement concerns arbitrarily large stages. [Exact lower bound](FreePairHeight.lean) · [Finite computation](synchronous_basin_replay.mjs)

The operator test similarly distinguishes infinite from finite observation. Convergent points can have arbitrarily late physical mergers and almost maximizing finite row norms. Approximating the target with uniformly bounded graph propagation has the full error even on these known convergent examples. A proof through the distance estimate has to control the strong closure at every fixed ordinary coordinate. [Finite controls and scope](BergmanRelative.md)

A new PR 201 checkpoint landed during this pass. For a Mersenne peak of exponent \(m\), it classifies every meeting with a smaller start inside the cone \(2i+\max(i,l)<m\), where \(i,l\) are the two postpeak clocks. All 183 of our fixed-source returns have equal postpeak clocks \(h\le22640\) with \(3h<m\). For 179 of them the earlier exponent lies outside the original canonical residue class. The representative theorem transfers those meetings to a smaller canonical parent. The fresh source also identifies infinitely many exponents with no earlier visitor in its short window. This gives a reason to seek longer-time mergers. Its literal finite replay matched the published data for every exponent through 14.
