# Integral orbit moments and simultaneous real and profinite records

**Collatz remains open.** The strongest elementary result here keeps one ordinary positive endpoint fixed and preserves the exact unit increment at every step. At any sufficiently late pair of supplied clocks its inverse basin has the full integral lattice of polynomial moments. The result extends to mixed moments at a prescribed, arbitrarily long list of internal cuts, with exact separate clocks between every two. It pinpoints why an integral determinant bound using these coordinates cannot supply additional prime factors.

Here \(C_k(y)\) is the smallest total cost of an admissible inverse word ending at a ternary-unit source. In the first three rows all paths are genuine positive ordinary paths and \(3\nmid y\). Each integer combination in a lattice may use signed coefficients. A quadratic polynomial proves that turning such a combination into a positive witness at a new, smaller input is a genuinely stronger step. The sparse-correction row isolates the role of the exact integer carry even when profinite congruences, the native valuations, long true finite words, the common fixed starting state, real growth and eventual injectivity all agree.
