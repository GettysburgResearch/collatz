## What changed in the attempted closing argument

Making inverse fan-out linear does not make a bounded-radius selector complete.
The new obstruction permits unbounded within-return odd runs and all section
witnesses in its box. On the other hand, it lies inside a corridor handled
immediately by a different existing proper rank. The next substantive step is
therefore a globally well-founded switching/merging rule whose guards cover every
required source. A favorable next word for a newly chosen CRT source cannot be
imposed on an arbitrary already fixed source. That universal step remains open.
