# Carries and the real/2-adic transfer attempt

Read [REAL_ADIC_TRANSFER_ATTEMPT.md](REAL_ADIC_TRANSFER_ATTEMPT.md).

The direct q-analog construction retains ordinary binary carries in an
exact correction series. Its finite-state and polynomiality obligations
remain open. The companion forced real itinerary gives an explicit
real Bernstein value `-3/2`, and an odd-denominator variant `-4/3`,
whose corresponding 2-adic limits are different. It tests a specific
intermediate transfer assertion in the primary literature.

The itinerary is not a positive ordinary Collatz orbit. No rationality
claim about its 2-adic limit or counterexample to the cited paper's final
Theorem 1 is asserted. Eight finite recurrence theorems are checked in
Lean; the infinite arguments and source assessment are separately
reviewed written mathematics.
