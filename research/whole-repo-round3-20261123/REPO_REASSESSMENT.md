## 4. The mathematical outcome of stepping back

The useful connection is the complete ordered carry. It links all three
current closing attacks while preserving their different obligations:

- For an actual past window, its quotient and source quotient remove
  within-box collisions exactly. Cross-box alignment remains to be bounded.
- For the complementary word, its residue determines a translated
  triadic numerator progression. Its small representative remains to be
  constructed within the same candidate's own height.
- For a cycle word, its zero equation correlates the two block carries.
  The resulting conditioned arithmetic law remains to be controlled.

These are concrete calculations, not evidence that the three missing
statements are interchangeable. The cycle attack cannot assume an
escaping product, and the actual-law argument cannot replace one orbit
by the uniform law on all words.

The next experiment or theorem should preserve this complete data and
target the remaining correlation or construction. Increasing a finite
census, adding another assumed closing lemma, or transferring a dimension
statement to an unproved moving-law upper does not discharge it.
