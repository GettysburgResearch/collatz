# Q-8501 — Construct one forever-defined ordinary residual in the deterministic refund map

**Claim ID:** `Q-8501`  
**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Dependencies:** `D-8501`, `L-8502`, `T-8501`  
**Full-objective role:** a positive solution is an unconditional Collatz counterexample

## Exact target

Find one explicit finite tuple

\[
(t_0,i_0,i_1,z_0),
\qquad
16\mid t_0,
\quad t_0\ge3744,
\quad z_0\ge1,
\]

such that the deterministic low-block/top-block decoder of `T-8501` is defined for every future step.

No future type word is input. At each step the current ordinary residual must itself satisfy

\[
\frac{2^{11(t+33)}}{64}
\mid
3^{7(t+1)}z+\theta_{t;i,j}+\omega'_j,
\]

and the resulting six-bit quotient must be one of the four allowed cells. The next type and next residual are then uniquely determined.

## Positive completion certificate

A valid submission must contain:

1. the explicit integer `z_0` and types `i_0,i_1`;
2. a finite ordinary rule or inductive invariant proving the decoder never fails;
3. exact connector and physical tower replay;
4. proof of positivity at every intermediate state;
5. the unboundedness conclusion `z_n>=2^n z_0`;
6. an independent verifier beginning only from the finite tuple and rule.

Then `T-8501/(7)` gives one explicit positive ordinary Collatz counterexample.

## Negative completion target

Alternatively, prove that every finite ordinary initial tuple eventually fails the decoder. Such a theorem would close the entire linear-height refund escape class.

## Current narrowing

- `L-8502` removes the preloaded-inverse objection: every new inverse block is generated by one exact base-`3^112` carry update.
- `T-8501` removes free future word choice: the next type is uniquely decoded from the current residual.
- `T-8501` also proves growth automatically; only infinite definedness remains.
- `T-8502` shows the completion set has Hausdorff dimension zero.
- `T-8503` excludes every eventually periodic type tail of minimal period at most `58`.

The remaining object is therefore one genuinely nonperiodic, unbounded-state, ordinary safety orbit—not a compatible inverse-limit directive.
