# Q-8501 — Construct one forever-defined ordinary complement quotient

**Claim ID:** `Q-8501`  
**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Dependencies:** `L-8502`--`L-8504`, `T-8504`, `T-8505`  
**Full-objective role:** a positive solution is an unconditional Collatz counterexample

## Exact target

Find one explicit finite tuple

\[
(t_0,i_0,k_0),
\qquad
16\mid t_0,
\quad t_0\ge3744,
\quad k_0\ge256,
\]

such that the deterministic complement-counter map of `T-8504` is defined for every future step.

At state `(t,i,k)`, compute

\[
U=b_i(N-c)+Nk.
\]

Its residue modulo `64` must select one next type `j`, and its complete value must satisfy

\[
2^{11(t+33)}\mid U-b_j(2^{11(t+33)}-r').
\]

The quotient is the unique next counter `k'`. No future type word, connector seed, logarithm digit, or inverse-limit residual is supplied as input.

## Equivalent single-integer formulation

`L-8504` shows that the tuple need not be trusted as external metadata. It corresponds to the one physical positive integer

\[
\boxed{
n_0=2^{11(t_0+1)}
\frac{b_{i_0}(M_0-r_0)+M_0k_0}{64}-34.
}
\]

Conversely, for an integer in this marked family,

\[
\nu_2(n_0+34)=11t_0+5+i_0
\]

recovers the height and type, and the remaining odd boundary word recovers `k_0`. A final certificate may therefore consist of:

```text
one written integer n_0,
one finite inductive rule,
one independent verifier that reconstructs the state from n_0.
```

## Positive completion certificate

A valid submission must contain:

1. the explicit integer `n_0` or equivalently `t_0,i_0,k_0`;
2. a finite ordinary rule or inductive invariant proving the partial map never fails;
3. exact connector and physical tower replay;
4. proof of positivity at every intermediate state;
5. the automatic growth conclusion `k_n>=2^n k_0`;
6. reconstruction of the intrinsic valuation/type marker at every boundary;
7. an independent verifier beginning only from the finite integer and rule.

Then `T-8504` gives one explicit positive ordinary Collatz counterexample.

## Necessary arithmetic renewal

Any positive solution must satisfy `T-8505`:

- every boundary word contains a prime at least `5`;
- no such prime survives to the adjacent boundary;
- infinitely many globally new odd primes divide the physical shifts `n_n+34`.

Thus the invariant cannot be a finite-prime multiplicative library, a bounded catalogue of prime-supported templates, or an ultimately periodic fixed-rank schema. It must causally manufacture the exact new prime content required by the moving cylinders.

## Negative completion target

Alternatively, prove that every finite ordinary initial tuple eventually leaves the domain. Such a theorem would close the entire linear-height quotient-refund escape class.
