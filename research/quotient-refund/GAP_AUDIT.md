## Nonconsequences and remaining warnings

### Finite prefixes

Every finite route has positive ordinary representatives. This does not imply that one representative survives every later changing cylinder.

### Prepaid horizons

A positive complete-cylinder quotient beyond `s` future steps does not prove the `(s+1)`-st route is legal. The production rate is positive but is not itself an inductive closure theorem.

### Fixed alphabets

A fixed four-letter type or toll alphabet does not make the state-dependent affine translations, carries, or long residues periodic.

### Residue lassos

A lasso in any fixed modulus remains a completion ghost unless its canonical most-significant lift is proved. The modulus here grows with the physical height.

### Rapid growth

Growth creates capacity but not the required exact low block. Neither `C_next>2^170 C` nor accelerating top refund supplies the next residue.

### Fresh primes

Prime renewal is necessary, not sufficient. It does not place the new primes in the moving dyadic cylinders.

### Zero dimension and period cutoffs

A zero-dimensional set may contain isolated ordinary integers. Failure of the period-58 source bound at period 59 is not evidence for a witness.

### No candidate identifier

No `K-85xx` object exists until one written positive integer is accompanied by an all-time routing invariant and an independent physical replay verifier.
