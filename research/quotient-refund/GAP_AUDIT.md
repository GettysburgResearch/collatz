## Remaining assumptions and nonconsequences

The low-block divisibility condition in `T-8501` has not been proved to hold forever for any finite residual. This is the sole positive mathematical gap after initialization is written explicitly.

### 4. Zero dimension

A zero-dimensional compact set may contain isolated ordinary integers. `T-8502` is a pressure diagnosis, not nonexistence.

### 5. Period cutoff

`T-8503` excludes minimal eventual periods through `58`. Failure of the source theorem's numerical hypothesis at `59` neither constructs nor suggests a rational completion.

### 6. Physical positivity

### 7. No preloaded infinite data

The packet initializes no logarithm digits and no inverse-limit type word. The inverse carry is updated from finite integers. The missing residual seed must also be finite and explicit.
