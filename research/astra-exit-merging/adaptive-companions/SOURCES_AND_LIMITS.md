## Mathematical limitations to retain beside the claims

- A source smaller than an intermediate state is not necessarily smaller than
  the original root. Every success here uses the original-root inequality.
- The three selected companions do not supply seven-eighths successful coverage:
  the seven residue classes lead to MERGE OR H-return, and the latter may fail.
- The hard-return parameter can increase and can leave the guarded language.
  No global rank or total successful selector has been obtained.
- Fixed affine companions have a forced uniform clock difference. This does
  not forbid individual asynchronous meetings or changing companion rules.
- The new direct result covers all earlier entry inputs for a weaker reduction
  factor; it does not supersede every earlier theorem or prove convergence of
  every smaller source. It is a valid induction edge, not a stand-alone proof
  of convergence for its entire infinite family.

Independent review should prioritize the exact words at each seed bridge,
clock bookkeeping through orientation swaps, the 7/8 domain partition,
positivity at the smallest parameters, and the original-root quantifier.
