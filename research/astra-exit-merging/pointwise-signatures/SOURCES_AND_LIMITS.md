## Research boundary

PSS-001 assumes supplied finite convergence paths. PSS-002 assumes a supplied
numerical merger and gives exact order limits rather than assuming every lift
is smaller. PSS-003 has a proper, stated arithmetic family. PSS-004 is a necessary
condition under an actual floor hypothesis; it does not prohibit the unbounded
escape it identifies. Assigning arbitrary new slopes to a point at every step
is outside its fixed-parametrization hypothesis.

No complete universal restart theorem or full Collatz proof was obtained.
