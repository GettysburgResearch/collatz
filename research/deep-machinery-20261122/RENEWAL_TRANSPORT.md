# Renewal tilt and the actual sibling action

**Status.** This is a direct application of affine-boundary and perpetuity
theory to the positive-integer trace. It proves a uniform comparison on that
trace and tests the comparison on an unchanged hypothetical escaping source.
It also proves that the actual joint affine law is singular under the sibling
action. These statements do not establish OPEN-F or OPEN-S, and do not
exclude an escaping orbit or a nontrivial cycle.

The fixed-target tail identity and the tilted trace identity below are prior
work, explicitly attributed. No priority claim is made for the additional
corollaries. The path-product lower bound remains a source-qualified input
from the repository's pinned ordinary construction.

## 1. The exact affine law and the classical imports

Let the independent exponents have law

\[
\mathbb P(e_i=e)=2^{-e},\quad
f_e(x)=\frac{3x+1}{2^e},\quad
A_r=\frac{3^r}{2^{e_1+\cdots+e_r}},\quad
B_r=\frac13\sum_{j=1}^r A_j.
\]

Thus \(f_{e_1}\circ\cdots\circ f_{e_r}(x)=B_r+A_rx\).
The same series has the real and ternary limits
\(R=\lim B_r\in(0,\infty)\) and \(Z=\lim B_r\in\mathbb Z_3\).
Write \(\mathcal N=\mathcal L(R,Z)\) and \(Q(t)=\mathbb P(R>t)\).

### Goldie: hypotheses checked on the actual multiplier

For \(a=3/2^e\),

\[
m(s)=\mathbb E a^s=\frac{3^s}{2^{s+1}-1},\qquad
m(1)=1,\qquad
\mu=m'(1)=\log3-\frac43\log2>0.
\tag{1}
\]

Also \(\mathbb E\log a=\log(3/4)<0\); the additive term \(a/3\)
is positive and bounded; all required moments near exponent one are finite;
and \(f_1,f_2\) have no common fixed point. The additive group generated by
\(\log3-e\log2\) is dense. Thus the log multiplier is **nonarithmetic in
the centered renewal sense**, although it is supported on a translated
lattice. Goldie's affine theorem gives

\[
Q(t)\sim\frac{C}{t},\qquad
C=\frac1{3\mu}.
\tag{2}
\]

The constant uses the pointwise difference
\((aR'+a/3)-aR'=a/3\), whose expectation is \(1/3\); it does not subtract
two infinite expectations. The original reference is Goldie (1991),
Theorem 4.1, DOI
[10.1214/aoap/1177005985](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoap/1177005985).
The theorem statement and constant were checked in
[Alsmeyer's author manuscript, Propositions 4.1–4.2](https://www.uni-muenster.de/SFB878/publications/files/phpIyzbmn2357.pdf),
which explicitly identifies Goldie's theorem numbers. The original Euclid
full text was inaccessible in this pass. The centered/noncentered distinction
is explicit in
[Kevei, Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.07339).

There are consequently constants \(0<m\le M<\infty\) with

\[
m\le xQ(x)\le M\qquad(x\text{ a positive integer}).
\tag{3}
\]

For the finitely many small integers, positivity follows by taking a long
initial run of \(e_i=1\): its real partial sum is \((3/2)^r-1\).

The Cramér tilt is exactly

\[
\mathbb P_*(e=e_0)=\frac3{4^{e_0}},\qquad
\frac{d\mathbb P_*}{d\mathbb P}\bigg|_{\mathcal F_r}=A_r.
\tag{4}
\]

Let \(\sigma\) be the ternary limit law under this tilt, and define its
**density**, rather than its mass, at precision \(r\) by

\[
\sigma_r(q)=3^r\sigma(q+3^r\mathbb Z_3).
\]

### Brofferio: the two laws have different contracting completions

Brofferio's unnumbered main theorem, printed pages 500–501 of
[The Poisson boundary of random rational affinities (2006)](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2006__56_2_499_0.pdf),
identifies the boundary as the product of the completions with negative
drift, under its first-moment condition and nonabelian support. Here only
the primes 2 and 3 contribute to that moment, and \(\mathbb E e<\infty\).
Noncommutativity follows from
\(f_1f_2(x)=(9x+7)/8\) and \(f_2f_1(x)=(9x+5)/8\).

| Law | Real drift | 3-adic drift | 2-adic drift | Boundary |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| \(2^{-e}\) | \(\log3-2\log2<0\) | \(-\log3\) | \(2\log2>0\) | \(\mathbb R\times\mathbb Q_3\), with joint law \(\mathcal N\) |
| \(3/4^e\) | \(\mu>0\) | \(-\log3\) | \((4/3)\log2>0\) | \(\mathbb Q_3\), with law \(\sigma\) |

This supplies a joint boundary, not independence of its coordinates or
quasi-invariance under every group element. Under the tilted law the real
series diverges; a finite real coordinate under that law must not be
silently introduced.

## 2. Prior exact identities, with the original sources retained

For positive odd \(q\), set

\[
H_r(q)=3^r\mathbb P(Z\in q+3^r\mathbb Z_3,\ R>q).
\]

For an actual source \(x\) with \(U^rx=q\), write

\[
\omega_r(x)=\frac{3^r}{2^{\sum_{i<r}v_2(3U^ix+1)}},\qquad
\Lambda_r(x)=\prod_{i<r}\frac{3U^ix}{3U^ix+1}.
\]

The exact formulas are

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_r(q)&=\sum_{U^rx=q}\omega_r(x)Q(x),\\
\sigma_r(q)&=\sum_{U^rx=q}\omega_r(x)^2,\\
\omega_r(x)&=\frac qx\Lambda_r(x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

The first identity, its summable source bound, and its moving-endpoint
obstruction appear in the October 20 primary post
[An exact real/3-adic tail identity at positive odd targets](https://rstation.ai/r/collatz/posts/13).
The second and its all-depth bound are Proposition 11 of the October 25
manuscript
[Ternary Syracuse laws, v2](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/9).
Both are credited as prior calculations, not new results of this pass.

To check the source interface, a compatible word recovers
\(x=(q-B_r)/A_r\). Successive inverse congruences make every predecessor
a positive odd integer with the prescribed exact valuation. The independent
suffix makes \(R>q\) equivalent to \(R'>x\). Word probabilities multiplied
by \(3^r\) are respectively \(A_r\) and \(A_r^2\). The word order is
inverse chronological. The oldest source may be divisible by 3.

## 3. Uniform comparison on the ordinary diagonal

Retain the pinned path-product input: a finite nonperiodic path of odd
ternary units has product at least \(\delta>0\). See
[the previous pass's exact sibling current](../central-step-20261122/CRITICAL_BOUNDARY.md),
Section 6, and its frozen ordinary-harmonic sources. Since at most the
oldest source in (5) can be a nonunit, put
\(\delta'=3\delta/4\). Then for every such actual path

\[
\delta'\le\Lambda_r(x)\le1.
\tag{6}
\]

### Proposition 1: all-depth ordinary-diagonal comparison

For every nonperiodic positive odd target \(q\) and every \(r\ge0\),

\[
\boxed{
\frac m q\,\sigma_r(q)
\ \le\ H_r(q)\ \le\
\frac M{\delta'q}\,\sigma_r(q).}
\tag{7}
\]

Indeed, for each *same actual source word* in both sums,

\[
\frac{\omega_r(x)Q(x)}{\omega_r(x)^2}
=\frac{xQ(x)}{q\Lambda_r(x)}.
\]

Apply (3) and (6), then sum nonnegative terms. No law is replaced by Haar
measure, no source is changed, and no interchange with a limiting ordinary
density is used.

The corresponding likelihood of one compatible prefix, under conditioning
on the real event \(R>q\), relative to the tilted word law, is

\[
L_{q,r}(x)
=\frac{Q(x)}{\omega_r(x)Q(q)}
=\frac{xQ(x)}{qQ(q)\Lambda_r(x)}.
\tag{8}
\]

Thus, uniformly in \(q,r,x\) on these actual words,

\[
\frac mM\le L_{q,r}(x)\le\frac M{m\delta'}.
\tag{9}
\]

This is a pointwise finite-word comparison, stronger on this specified
diagonal than an unconditioned total-variation estimate. It supplies no
comparison between different targets.

## 4. Testing the critical clock on an unchanged escaping source

Suppose, counterfactually, that \(p\) has an escaping ordinary orbit
\(q_k=U^kp\to\infty\). Its actual inverse word of length \(k\) ends at
the original source \(p\), and

\[
\omega_k(p)=\frac{q_k}{p}\Lambda_k(p),\qquad
\Lambda_k(p)\downarrow\Lambda_\infty(p)\ge\delta'>0.
\]

Equations (5) give the actual retained contributions

\[
\frac{H_k(q_k)}{q_k}\ge\frac{\Lambda_k(p)Q(p)}p,
\qquad
\frac{\sigma_k(q_k)}{q_k^2}\ge\frac{\Lambda_k(p)^2}{p^2}.
\tag{10}
\]

Both lower bounds have positive limits. The word likelihood in (8) has the
finite positive limit

\[
\boxed{
L_{q_k,k}(p)\longrightarrow
\frac{pQ(p)}{C\Lambda_\infty(p)}.}
\tag{11}
\]

Consequently Goldie conditioning does not remove the retained source or
contradict its permitted linear and quadratic trace scales.

This check also covers the proposed critical depth
\(r\approx\log q_k/\mu\). If \(r\le k\), the actual source at that
depth is \(x=q_{k-r}\), and the likelihood is exactly (8). If
\(k-r\to\infty\), then \(x\to\infty\) and the path-product tail tends
to one, so this likelihood tends to one. If \(k-r\) stays bounded, each
fixed-offset subsequence has a positive finite limiting likelihood as in
(11). If \(r>k\), the source is an additional predecessor of \(p\);
it is not legitimate to keep calling it \(p\). The first \(k\) exponents
still have likelihood (11).

Meanwhile a single retained tilted word is exponentially small:

\[
\mathbb P_*(w_k)=\frac{\omega_k(p)^2}{3^k}
\le(3/4)^k.
\tag{12}
\]

Its normalized density can therefore remain high while global additive
mixing or central-limit errors exceed its entire mass. The exact
likelihood test, rather than an appeal to typical paths, exhibits this
scale mismatch.

The exponential scales are also consistent. The tilted one-step entropy
is \(h_*=(8/3)\log2-\log3\), so
\(\log3-h_*=2\mu\). At \(r\approx\log q/\mu\), the retained
density scale \(q^2\) is exactly the scale
\(\exp((\log3-h_*)r)\) suggested by that entropy. There is no exponential
entropy discrepancy from which to deduce a contradiction.

For completeness, the stronger fixed-target budgets do not change this
conclusion. Nonperiodicity counts each source at most once, so (5) gives

\[
\sum_r\frac{H_r(q)}q\le\sum_{x>0\ {\rm odd}}\frac{Q(x)}x<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_r\frac{\sigma_r(q)}{q^2}\le\sum_{x>0\ {\rm odd}}x^{-2}.
\tag{13}
\]

The retained term (10) fits both budgets.

## 5. Direct attempt: quasi-invariance under the actual sibling map

Let \(B(x)=64x+21\), acting on the real and ternary coordinates
simultaneously. It has the exact conjugacy and branch relation

\[
1+3B^jx=64^j(1+3x),\qquad
f_e=f_{e+6j}\circ B^j\quad(j\ge1).
\tag{14}
\]

This is a group action, but it need not preserve the boundary measure
class. In fact it does not.

### Proposition 2: full joint coding and singular sibling translates

The joint random pair \((R,Z)\) determines the infinite original exponent
word almost surely. Consequently

\[
\boxed{\mathcal N\perp(B^j)_*\mathcal N\quad(j\ge1).}
\tag{15}
\]

Here is a direct proof of the coding assertion. Let
\(D_r=e_1+\cdots+e_r\), \(B_r=C_r/2^{D_r}\), and let \(R'_r\) be
the independent real suffix. The data

\[
(R,Z,D_r,\lfloor R'_r\rfloor)
\tag{16}
\]

determine the prefix word. Indeed, with \(D_r=d\) and
\(J=\lfloor R'_r\rfloor\), its numerator satisfies

\[
C_r\equiv2^dZ\pmod{3^r},\qquad
\frac{C_r}{3^r}\in
\left(\frac{2^dR}{3^r}-J-1,\frac{2^dR}{3^r}-J\right].
\tag{17}
\]

An interval of this form contains at most one member of a prescribed
translate of \(\mathbb Z\). Hence \(C_r\) is determined. The numerator
formula

\[
C_r=3^{r-1}+3^{r-2}2^{e_r}+\cdots+2^{e_2+\cdots+e_r}
\]

recovers \(e_r\) from \(v_2(C_r-3^{r-1})\), then recovers earlier
exponents recursively; \(D_r\) determines \(e_1\). The case \(r=1\)
is immediate.

The entropy of \(D_r\) is \(O(\log(r+1))\), by comparison with a
geometric distribution of the same mean. The entropy of
\(\lfloor R'_r\rfloor\) is finite uniformly in \(r\): for
\(0<\theta<1\), subadditivity gives

\[
\mathbb ER^\theta\le
\frac1{2^{1+\theta}-1-3^\theta}<\infty,
\]

and comparison with probabilities proportional to
\((1+j)^{-1-\theta/2}\) bounds its discrete entropy. Therefore (16)
implies

\[
H(e_1,\ldots,e_r\mid R,Z)=O(\log(r+1)).
\tag{18}
\]

The invertible affine recursion and the independent suffix, on the other
hand, give the exact chain-rule identity

\[
H(e_1,\ldots,e_r\mid R,Z)
=rH(e_1\mid R,Z).
\]

Thus \(H(e_1\mid R,Z)=0\), and iteration recovers every exponent.

Let \(E_e\) be the measurable full-measure decoding set for first symbol
\(e\). The branch laws \((f_e)_*\mathcal N\) and
\((f_d)_*\mathcal N\) are supported on disjoint decoding sets for
\(e\ne d\). Equation (14) and injectivity of \(f_{e+6j}\) then give
(15). This proves the failure using the actual joint law and its real
coordinate, without imposing an artificial noise model.

On a forward branch the Radon–Nikodym formula is nevertheless exact:

\[
\frac{d(f_e)_*\mathcal N}{d\mathcal N}
=2^e\mathbf1_{E_e}.
\tag{19}
\]

Passing to the sibling action requires an inverse branch on a disjoint
decoding set; (19) cannot be inverted to obtain a positive bounded
sibling derivative.

The tilted ternary law has the analogous a.s. coding property, already
stated in Section 5 of the cited v2 manuscript. A weaker entropy estimate
than the manuscript's bounded-information theorem suffices to check it
directly here. Put \(V_r=B_r/A_r=C_r/3^r\). Reversal of the independent
tilted increments and subadditivity give, for \(0<\theta<1\),

\[
\mathbb E_*V_r^\theta
\le\frac{3^{-\theta}}{1-m(1-\theta)}<\infty,
\]

uniformly in \(r\). The data \((Z,D_r,\lfloor V_r\rfloor)\) determine
\(C_r\), hence the word: its residue modulo \(3^r\) and the unit
interval containing \(C_r/3^r\) are specified. The two auxiliary discrete
entropies are respectively \(O(\log(r+1))\) and \(O(1)\). Therefore
\(H(W_r\mid Z)=O(\log(r+1))\). The invertible ternary recursion gives
\(H(W_r\mid Z)=rH(e_1\mid Z)\), forcing the latter one-symbol entropy
to be zero. This verifies the coding conclusion without assuming the
manuscript's sharper finite-precision bound. Equation (14) now gives

\[
\sigma\perp(B^j)_*\sigma.
\tag{20}
\]

Coding also proves that \(\sigma\) has no atoms: a single decoded
infinite word has probability at most \((3/4)^r\) for every prefix
length \(r\), hence probability zero.

### The special ordinary events are still the missing estimate

Set

\[
E_{q,r}=(q,\infty)\times(q+3^r\mathbb Z_3).
\]

Because \(B^j\) has positive real slope and is a ternary isometry,

\[
B^jE_{q,r}=E_{B^jq,r},\qquad
\frac{\mathcal N(B^jE_{q,r})}{\mathcal N(E_{q,r})}
=\frac{H_r(B^jq)}{H_r(q)}.
\tag{21}
\]

If both ordinary targets are nonperiodic and the denominators are
positive, (7) yields the precise remaining comparison:

\[
\frac{m\delta'}M\frac q{B^jq}
\frac{\sigma_r(B^jq)}{\sigma_r(q)}
\le\frac{H_r(B^jq)}{H_r(q)}
\le
\frac M{m\delta'}\frac q{B^jq}
\frac{\sigma_r(B^jq)}{\sigma_r(q)}.
\tag{22}
\]

The real-coordinate factor is \(q/B^jq\), of order \(64^{-j}\).
There is no omitted compensating radial gain.

For a fixed \(j\), the finite-cylinder ratios

\[
K_r^{(j)}(z)=
\frac{\sigma(B^j(z+3^r\mathbb Z_3))}
     {\sigma(z+3^r\mathbb Z_3)}
\]

are finite-partition likelihoods for \((B^{-j})_*\sigma\) relative to
\(\sigma\). Equation (20) makes their limit zero for
\(\sigma\)-almost every \(z\). This does **not** decide their values on
a prescribed moving ordinary target. Positive integers form a countable
null set, and the high-linear sector required by OPEN-S is not covered by
an almost-everywhere statement.

## 6. What this attempted import does and does not supply

The Goldie/Brofferio route reaches the exact ordinary trace and preserves
its sources. Its strongest comparison here is (7), and its direct
sibling-action test is (15), (21), and (22). A generic conformal or
Poisson-boundary Harnack inequality cannot be imported across that action:
the full actual measures are singular.

The repository's required statement remains a **specific arithmetic**
lower estimate for

\[
\sum_{j\ge1}64^{-j}\frac{G(B^jq)}{G(q)}
\quad\text{when }G(q)\ge\eta q,
\]

or its finite-obstacle version with the stated original weights and
horizons. Neither \(H_r\) nor \(\sigma_r\) has been identified with
\(G\) or the finite obstacle \(V^{[r]}\). Replacing them would be an
additional unjustified step. Ambient singularity does not refute this
restricted arithmetic estimate; the retained-source calculation explains
why a global renewal limit does not prove it either.

One further literature check matters for the proposed clock import:
the published Buraczewski–Collamore–Damek–Zienkiewicz (2016),
[Theorem 2.2, printed page 3696](https://web.math.ku.dk/~jcolla/AOP1059.pdf),
assumes a **non-lattice** log multiplier for its sharp first-passage
central limit theorem. Our log multiplier is translated-lattice. The
published statement therefore does not directly apply as written. This is
distinct from the centered nonarithmetic condition used in Goldie's
renewal theorem. The older arXiv version has different wording and a
different Theorem 2.2; those versions must not be conflated. Even a valid
replacement clock theorem would still have to control the exact diagonal
atom tested in (10)–(12).
