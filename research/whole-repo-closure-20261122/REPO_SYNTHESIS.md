## Why these attacks, after stepping back

The newest analytic work has already resolved the mere existence of a finite
canonical envelope and the parent-tail interchange needed for harmonic Abel
limits. Repeating those constructions would not target the current gap. The
new analytic lane instead compares the weighted inverse operator directly
with ordinary inverse counting and asks what it says about real transported
source pairs.

The constructive work already has a common multiset rank for its enabled
certificate transformations. A more elaborate rank alone does not supply an
enabled move in an unresolved arithmetic case. The new constructive lane
therefore follows the complementary phase of an actual lower-source selector
into its exact carry equation, keeping the supplied tail intact.

The arithmetic route takes PR186's fixed-block quotient task all the way to an
effective decision theorem for each prescribed schema. Positive affine maps
have extra structure: rotating the fixed block list bounds one periodic
point, so a general ineffective recurrence-quotient theorem is unnecessary
for this input class. The new exact certificate system retains the full
cycle equation and distinguishes an actual identity cycle from a rejected
candidate.

## A combined global route, with the two remaining obligations separated

An ordinary positive trajectory either eventually repeats or is injective.
These are separate targets in this pass:

1. **Injective trajectories.** The canonical inverse potential and its actual
   harmonic flow carry every original source. A uniform upper bound that
   defeats the positive retained atom along the moving forward endpoint would
   exclude escape. Finite target rows, fixed-basin decay, finite reciprocal
   moments, and a bounded collision norm in an infinite-mass reference do not
   yet provide that bound.
2. **Periodic trajectories.** The new theorem completely decides every fixed
   linear block schema, with an unconditional alphabet-four exclusion as a
   worked application. Universal cycle exclusion would additionally require
   a proved restriction on the block descriptions of all possible cycles,
   or a different all-word denominator argument.

The alternate constructive route could settle both at once through a total
successful smaller-source merger selection. Its current residual is kept in
the actual original-source coordinates. No density statement or independent
finite witness is substituted for that universal quantifier.

## Evidence levels

The new generic classification and harmonic-flow deductions are written
proofs. The supplied block-schema certificates have a separate exact rational
checker. The worked denominator/gap-path result has an actual Lean replay.
Any new constructive formalization has its own module and audit. The
source-qualified analytic population and finite-envelope hypotheses remain
named; no PDU, GRH, full convergence, or missing dispatcher is installed as a
Lean axiom.
