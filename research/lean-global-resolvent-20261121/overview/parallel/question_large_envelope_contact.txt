CAPTURE ONLY AT LARGE CANONICAL ENVELOPE VALUES

On the positive integers define

  (Pf)(q) = sum_{e>=1, 2^e q=1 (mod3)}
              3*2^(-e) f((2^e q-1)/3),
  g(q) = 1 if 3 does not divide q, and 0 otherwise,
  V_0=g, V_{j+1}=max(g, P V_j), V=sup_j V_j.

The following facts may be used as stated inputs:

1. V is finite at every positive integer and is the least nonnegative
   excessive majorant of g. It satisfies V=max(g,PV).
2. D_j=P^j1 satisfies D_j(q)<=(4j+2)(3/2)^j.
3. For C={positive units q: V(q)<=2}, A={q:V(q)>2}, and Qf=1_A Pf,
   Q^n V(q) decreases to zero at every fixed positive integer q.

For q in A, define a probability transition to each positive unit
x=(2^e q-1)/3 by the weight (3/2^e)*V(x)/V(q). Sum coincident branches
if necessary, and make C absorbing. Let tau be first entrance into C,
including time zero. Every edge is an actual edge of this integer kernel.
The index tau counts inverse steps, not the sum of the exponents e.

For sufficiently large q, set

  ell(q)=log(q+2),
  L(q)=floor((log q-3 log ell(q))/log(3/2)),
  p(q)=Prob_q(tau<L(q)),
  K_0=16/log(3/2).

One has the exact entrance identity and its finite bound

  V(q)*p(q) = sum_{0<=j<L(q)} Q^j(1_C V)(q)
             <= K_0*q/ell(q)^2.

Restrict q to positive odd integers not divisible by three such that,
for the integer d with 2^d<=q<2^(d+1),

  (d+1)(q-2^d)<=70*2^d.

QUESTION

Prove or refute the existence of explicit B,c>0 and q_0 such that
B*c>K_0 and every such q>=q_0 satisfies

  V(q)>=B*q/ell(q)  implies  p(q)>=c/ell(q).

The assertion imposes no capture requirement at states already below
the stated envelope threshold. In particular, failure of a fast-contact
claim on some bounded-envelope auxiliary model would not refute this
conditional statement for the specified integer kernel.

The actual contact set is defined using V, not a finite V_j. Fixed-root
almost-sure entrance alone does not supply the requested estimate.
No ordinary convergence theorem is an assumption of this question.
