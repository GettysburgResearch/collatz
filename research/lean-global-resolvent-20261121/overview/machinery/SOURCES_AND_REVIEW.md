word-changing target return from the fresh joint minimum, possibly by

number of affine correction summands and supplies no fixed-dimensional

No positive return theorem was obtained.

mass kept explicit. The critical iid affine recursion satisfies a
substantial renewal theorem, but its annular asymptotic gives no ordinary
endpoint comparison for the canonical Doob law. No weighted closing
estimate was obtained.

**Compact and entropy rigidity.** The aim was to construct the invariant
law and apply a rigidity theorem to eliminate it. The law from one fixed
ordinary source was constructed. A correlated ergodic zero-entropy model
then demonstrated that the obtained compact constraints do not supply
the needed entropy hypothesis. A full-denominator estimate controls each
contiguous negative-cycle residence, but not the sum of repeatedly
replenished residences. No arithmetic restriction eliminating the
constructed control was obtained.

**Graph criticality.** The aim was an energy inequality contradicting the
terminal run-weighted divergence. The actual clock-layer identity gives
finite-support null functions, allowing the criticality theorem to be
applied. It proves recurrence and the absence of a nonzero global Hardy
weight for the ordinary symmetric form. The canonical directed potential
does not have the missing symmetric supersolution sign. No run-weighted
upper bound was obtained.
