FINITE AFFINE-WORD QUESTION

Status: open research target. A proof or a counterexample is requested.

Let A and p be integers with A>=3, 1<=p<A, and D=2^A-3^p>0.
For each increasing p-tuple

    0<=d_0<d_1<...<d_(p-1)<A,

define

    C(d)=sum_(i=0)^(p-1) 2^(d_i) 3^(p-1-i).

Let N_r(A,p) be the number of these tuples for which C(d) is congruent
to r modulo the full integer D.

PRIMARY QUESTION

Prove that N_0(A,p)<A for every admissible pair (A,p).

A stronger possible route is to prove max_r N_r(A,p)<A. This stronger
statement is also unproved. A counterexample to the stronger statement
would not by itself answer the primary question.

EXACT OPERATOR FORM

On Z/DZ let P0 and P1 be the permutation matrices induced by

    x -> 2^(-1)x,             x -> 2^(-1)(3x+1).

Both 2 and 3 are units modulo D. For the unnormalized matrices,

    [z^p] trace((P0+z P1)^A)=D N_0(A,p).

Thus the primary question is the coefficient bound <A D.

KNOWN DIAGNOSTICS

1. The restriction A>=3 matters: A=2,p=1,D=1 gives N_0=2.
2. When A=2p, two alternating binary words lie in the zero fiber.
3. At A=8,p=5,D=13, the maximum fiber is 7=A-1 and the zero fiber is empty.
4. Fibers are not generally antichains in the coordinatewise order on
   the d_i, even for residues coprime to D. For A=35,p=22,D=2978678759,
   the two tuples

   u=(0,2,3,4,5,8,9,10,11,12,14,16,17,20,22,23,24,25,26,30,31,32),
   v=(1,2,5,6,10,11,12,14,15,16,17,18,19,20,22,24,25,26,29,31,32,34)

   satisfy u_i<=v_i for every i and

   C(u)=2209100811+44D,
   C(v)=2209100811+233D,
   gcd(2209100811,D)=1.

5. Rotation of a word sends its residue r to 3^b r/2 when its first
   bit is b. Both branches fix zero. Rotation balance alone therefore
   does not bound N_0.

The target concerns the exact zero residue at every full D. An estimate
for most moduli, a proper divisor of D, or an exponentially large error
does not establish it. Do not assume independent word digits after
conditioning on their total count p. No application of this question
is needed to attempt its proof.
