# Exponential finite-length convergence of the optimal positionwise price

Fix the shortcut horizon $s\ge1$ and ternary radius $r\ge1$. Use $W=2r+1$, $m=2^s$, $a=\lfloor s\log_3 2\rfloor$, $H=a+1$ and the ordinary tags and long division of [the fixed-horizon theorem](FIXED_HORIZON_POSITION_CERTIFICATES.md). For sufficiently large $L$, let $A_L$ be the largest average odd count among weighted positive sources prime to three with source and true $s$-step endpoint of length $L$, balanced at every position/window pair. The finite linear program has a rational maximum. Testing against its weighted inputs identifies this number as the optimal parity cost in the finite dual LP of additive position/window scores on that length.

There is a constant $A_*=A_*(s,r)>s\log_3 2$ and there are $c,C>0$ depending only on $(s,r)$ such that

$$|A_L-A_*|\le Ce^{-cL}.$$

The price $A_*$ is a computable real. It is the maximum over compatible **infinite leading digit** laws, before imposing a true terminal dyadic residue. Explicit decreasing rational upper bounds $B_J$ from the first $J$ input trits also satisfy $0\le B_J-A_*\le Ce^{-cJ}$. Neither a closed expression nor practically sized rate constants are asserted.

## 1. Leading-digit optimization

An admissible leading string consists of a tag $b$ with $h_b=a$ or $a+1$, and nonzero-leading ternary input digits. Long division by $m$ must emit exactly $h_b$ initial zero digits followed by a nonzero one. Drop those zeros to obtain the leading endpoint stream. This convention uses actual digit paths, including the possible two symbolic representations of a limiting rational real value. For $J>H+r+1$ maximize the mean $h_b$ over probability distributions of admissible length-$J$ prefixes, imposing every window histogram equality whose last needed digit is present in the first $J-H$ positions of both streams, with upper sentinel padding. Call the maximum $B_J$. This is a finite rational LP and the preceding truncation map gives $B_J\ge B_{J+1}$. Weak compactness on the tag times the space of one-sided digit strings shows that $A_*:=\lim B_J$ is the maximum over laws satisfying all positions: extend finite prefixes arbitrarily, take a weak limit of optimizers, and use the clopen finite constraints and tag cost. Conversely any infinite law projects to each LP. The smooth upper law has rational mean $p_0>s\alpha$, so $A_*>s\alpha$.

All sufficiently long ordinary equal-length sources in $A_L$ use these tags. In fact $q=\lfloor n/m\rfloor$ then has either $L-a$ or $L-a-1$ trits, since $3^a<m<3^{a+1}$. Its endpoint in (1) of the fixed-horizon theorem is its quotient trits followed by exactly $h_b$ additional trits. Projecting an actual feasible distribution to its leading digits gives $A_L\le B_L$.

There is $d>0$ such that at every sufficiently large $J$ both clock groups have probability at least $d$ for **every** feasible leading law. For a prefix let $x$ be its represented mantissa (append zeros), $y=3^{h_b}x/m$. Both lie in $[1,3)$ by admissibility. Equality of the leading individual digit means through $J-H-r$ positions gives $|\mathbb E(y-x)|\le C_{s,r}3^{-J}$. On the high-clock tag $y-x$ lies between $v$ and $3v$ for $v=3^{a+1}/m-1>0$; on the low-clock tag $x-y$ lies between $u$ and $3u$ for $u=1-3^a/m>0$. These bounds separate both tag-group masses from zero. Within each group an optimizer may redistribute its labels uniformly conditional on the input string, since its entire leading transducer depends only on the clock $h_b$. Thus one can ensure that each individual tag mass is at least a common $d'>0$.

## 2. A quantitative bridge to the uniform interior

Let $A$ be a re-labelled optimizer on $J$ leading trits and $A^0$ the fixed smooth positive upper string from the preceding theorem using all tags. For a parameter $0<\epsilon<1/2$ set $E=(1-\epsilon)A+\epsilon A^0$ on this finite prefix. Its cost differs from $B_J$ by at most $s\epsilon$. Its tag probabilities belong to a fixed compact subset of the open simplex, independent of $J,\epsilon$. Away from the fixed initial region and the finite unfinished right edge, its physical arcs are all at least $c_0\epsilon$. Let $U$ be the fully uniform constant interior with these same tag probabilities. Its physical arcs are all at least $u_0>0$ uniformly in the probabilities. All augmented layer coordinates are bounded by a fixed constant, since each pending histogram has bounded mass over finitely many future delays.

On each available $K$-layer block the signed pencil connector $D$ from $E$ to $U$ is bounded by one constant $C_0$ independent of $J,\epsilon$ and the block. The canonical-form argument only needs $K$ spare layers on either side; finite leading prefixes suffice. Change $t$ to $t+h$ in $(1-t)E+tU$ by the connector

$$(1-t-h)E+tU+hD.$$

For $t\le1/2$, choose $h=\eta(\epsilon+t)$, shortened to reach $1/2$ exactly when necessary. Take a fixed sufficiently small $\eta>0$ so $1-t-h\ge1/4$ and $\eta C_0<\min(c_0/4,u_0)/2$. Then the physical entries remain nonnegative. Before shortening, $t+\epsilon$ is multiplied by $1+\eta$ at every block. Thus $O_{s,r}(1+\log(1/\epsilon))$ blocks suffice to reach $1/2$. Thereafter $tU$ gives a fixed positive minimum, so finitely many additional steps of fixed small size (shortening the last one) reach $t=1$. This leaves exactly $U$ and preserves the mean of the original mixture of tags. Starting after a fixed leading margin, the whole join fits into $J$ trits, including the required remaining spare buffer, with rational $\epsilon\le C e^{-cJ}$ for suitable $c,C>0$ (choose a negative power of two of order $\exp(-cJ)$).

Continuing with $U$ forever gives a balanced infinite leading law: nonnegative conserved tagged flow over the finite layers extends to one-sided paths by conditional transitions, and the initial, adjoining and subsequent window constraints hold by the port equations. Consequently $A_*\ge B_J-s\epsilon$ and the asserted exponential upper error follows.

## 3. A uniformly bounded true lower cap

The fixed terminal join can be chosen of bounded length uniformly over these tag probabilities. For $s=1$ every stationary law of the lower random maps has full unit support, as established in the one-step theorem. For $s\ge2$ all lower maps are strict $3$-adic contractions. The support of any stationary probability satisfies $K=\bigcup_b F_b(K)$ because every tag has positive probability. The map on nonempty compact subsets given by this finite union contracts the Hausdorff metric by a factor at most $1/3$, so its nonempty compact fixed set is unique. Thus $K$ is common to all the probability vectors being considered. Choose the single finite positive lower transition band using this $K$ as in the remote-trit argument; its cylinder events are clopen. The collection of pairs consisting of a probability vector in the fixed compact simplex subset and one of its stationary measures is compact, by the weak stationarity identity for continuous tests. Every selected cylinder has positive stationary mass on every pair by common support. Its continuous mass therefore has a strictly positive common minimum. The same compactness argument works directly with the full unit support at $s=1$.

The lower interpolation reducing the uniform share geometrically and then removing it on the selected band now takes a fixed number of layers, since its uniform positive mass, the connector norm bound and the final positive band minimum are all uniform in the probability vector. This bounds the total lower cap length by a constant depending only on $(s,r)$, including the physical distance of this selected band from the last digit. For a desired large length $L$, take $J=\lfloor L/2\rfloor$, form the upper join within that prefix, fill unused positions with $U$, and attach the lower cap. The finite rational feasible system admits a rational nonnegative physical flow: the finite optimizer, equal relabelling, smooth upper tag masses and $\epsilon$ all have rational tag vectors. Scaling and path decomposition yield an ordinary family of length exactly $L$ and cost at least $B_J-Ce^{-cJ}\ge A_*-Ce^{-cJ}$. Together with $A_L\le B_L$ this proves the native estimate after adjusting $c$.

For computability, enumerate increasing leading-prefix LPs for upper bounds and rational integer seeds with repeatable middle layers for lower bounds. Any lower receipt extends to arbitrarily large lengths, hence is at most $A_*$. The construction attains values arbitrarily close to $A_*$ with repeated uniform interior layers, and the upper LPs decrease to it. Waiting for the two rational bounds to enter an interval of any prescribed accuracy terminates. This algorithm requires no prior numerical evaluation of the constants in the exponential estimate.
