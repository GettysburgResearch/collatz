## Recent logarithmic approximation results

Chudnovsky's October 2026 preprint states, in Theorem 11.1, that for multiplicatively independent positive rational numbers `a,b`, the irrationality exponent of `log(a)/log(b)` is at most three. Corollary 11.5 gives the corresponding homogeneous two-logarithm lower bound with exponent `2+epsilon`. The source explicitly identifies `QuadraticPi.rationalLogRatio_irrationalityExponent_bounds` and the underlying two-torus interpolation development. The public statement and its quantifiers were read directly. This refresh does **not** claim that our packet has independently compiled that large external proof chain. [Primary October text, Section 11](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28079v1).

The November 23 follow-up states a lower bound of order `H^(-n-epsilon)` for a nonzero integer form in `1,log(a_1),...,log(a_n)`, for fixed multiplicatively independent positive rational arguments. Its constant is ineffective. The case with zero constant term is a corollary; the paper itself notes that the generic lower obstruction there has exponent `n-1`, so one should not call its exponent `n` optimal for that restricted family. The theorem, induction and formal-scope appendix were inspected. It builds on the October supplement and does not give uniform constants for a growing number of arguments or moving rational bases. [Primary November text, Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.2 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.30820v1).

These results can improve specific finite-dimensional approximation estimates. They do not by themselves control the dimension and coefficient heights of an unrestricted Collatz word. The cycle applications on the other branches retain their own explicit external-input qualifications.

## Tilted Syracuse laws: the exact imported statement

The original source of Leen's *Tilted 3-adic Syracuse laws* was inspected, including Theorem C and the parameter conventions. For positive geometric gaps of mean `1/theta`, it states

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{H(X\bmod3^n)}{n\log3}
=\min\left(1,\frac{H(E_1)}{\log3}\right).
$$

This includes the entropy-critical parameter `theta_* = 0.6090897679...`. The proven absolute-continuity interval stated near Theorem B is only `[1/2,0.550024]`, in addition to a separate result at `1/4`. Absolute continuity near the entropy-critical endpoint is not supplied. The [dependent-gap theorem](DEPENDENT_GAP_DIMENSION.md) proves its own different dimension and decoding conclusions; the [inverse-cost spectrum](INVERSE_COST_SPECTRUM.md) and critical component assertion in [adaptive weak marginals](ADAPTIVE_WEAK_MARGINALS.md) explicitly import the iid Shannon-dimension statement. [Original record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23268832).
