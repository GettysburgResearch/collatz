## The new connection

Return to the real interior point z=1 of the same actual parity series:

\[
B_q(z)=\sum_{i\ge0}\frac{z^{A_i}}{3^{i+1}},\qquad
h(q)=2B'_q(1)=\sum_{i\ge0}\frac{g_i}{3^{i+1}}.
\]

The ordinary bound `A_i<=2i+log_2(q)` makes this real derivative
unconditionally available. Its exact transport is especially simple:
`h(Uq)=3h(q)-g(q)`. A triadic denominator therefore clears after a
known number of odd steps. At that point the positive integer scalar
enables a contracting potential, and an explicit finite budget follows.

This replaces the construction target of a whole arithmetic germ by
the arithmetic of one already defined real number. It does not prove
that arithmetic property. [GERM_BRIDGE.md](GERM_BRIDGE.md) states the
equivalence and total candidate verifier; [SCALAR_SEPARATION.md](SCALAR_SEPARATION.md)
states the exact approximation threshold.

The pressure lane uses the same scalar, rather than an independent
comparison law. At an actual attained pressure maximum, the complete
forward binary clocks lie below the powers of three. Their discounted
sum confines the scalar to a narrow real interval. Independently,
rational tripling phases exclude broad families of residual scalar

The ordinary lane tests whether high-scalar contraction can force the
remaining low phases to end. Its explicit family has arbitrarily large
growth between two low-scalar states despite only one intervening
high-scalar state. Every member is a genuine positive ordinary source;
the scalar inequalities use unconditional tail bounds and require no
assumption that the terminal source converges. This blocks that simple
amortization, while leaving richer source-dependent estimates possible.

## The next decisive construction

For an arbitrary fixed positive odd q, construct integers a and r such
that the actual scalar equals `a/3^r`. A sufficient concrete variant
is a certified real approximation meeting the strict precision bound
in R8-S1. Rationality with an unrestricted denominator would not yet
be enough: only the specified denominator families have been excluded.

Alternatively, exploit additional ordinary-source information at the
pressure maximum to rule out its surviving scalar phases. A proof must
amortize low-state growth using more than the mere occurrence of a
contracting high-scalar step; the explicit ordinary return family shows
why. Neither construction is supplied by this packet.
