## Complete the five-return dictionary

The correction algorithm decides the entire balanced affine architecture,
not a bounded sample of words. Its left-unitary property means another
ordering of these same rules cannot rescue a rejected word. The valid
source certificate `17:(2,3,4)` lies outside this architecture although
`85:(8)` is an explicit convergent target. A full transformer therefore
needs rank-changing or terminal rules. Complete recognition is genuine
algorithmic progress, but is not complete successful transport.

## Absorb the remaining five energy

The new kernel has coefficient below `.903` and reaches smaller ordinary
endpoints. Its remainder is still an infinite source domain carrying an
unknown component label. The finite inequality can bound total energy by
that remainder; it cannot bound the remainder by a finite number.

Adding every valid return also does not produce a uniformly contracting
positive kernel. The all-ones extension with `r=9,b=8`, together with
three already legal rows, has incoming column mass above `1.069` on an
infinite explicit ordinary CRT family. This refutes that particular
uniform-column completion, while leaving label-dependent cancellations,
different positive weights, and other selections available.

PR146 transports every possible failed five comparison into a single
covered progression at a larger height. Our renewal bounds covered mass
using smaller endpoints. Composing these statements yields comparisons
between unknown energies, not an absolute estimate. No infinity is
subtracted or suppressed in the proofs.

## Switch between signed-reference descent rules

The negative-template compiler resolves a real concern: its witness can
grow in length while the ordinary parameter `M` decreases. Thus a solver
can switch among its valid templates without losing a common well-founded
rank. However, the eight demonstrated congruence domains are not a cover.
Already `M=2047` lies outside their ternary guards. No claim that this
integer fails the underlying convergence implication follows.

The rank-five Mersenne family has a source reference ending at zero,
rather than the terminal negative cycle required by this grammar. Its
separate slope equations are derived in the note, but no complete
contracting construction is provided. Treating the zero numerator as an
ordinary exact odd valuation would be invalid.

## Force invariant labels to be arithmetically regular

Exact periodicity is rigid, and the new inequalities quantify how small
an approximate representation would need to be. The crucial variable is
the error against the actual invariant label on the specified enlarged
ordinary interval. A Fourier expansion on an initial interval, or a
finite parity cylinder, does not control its periodic extension there.
The current prefix-survivor bound neither identifies this mismatch set
nor reaches the `.84` finite-source threshold.

The conductor factor cannot be discarded: explicit bounded approximate
invariants have a fixed positive oscillation and defect of order `1/r`.
The same finite-quotient principles apply to the positive `3n-1` map,
which has distinct cycles. They therefore cannot by themselves select
the desired ordinary basin for `3n+1`.

## Use ternary mixing to repair the ordinary error estimate

The ternary kernel mixes quickly, but under a nonuniform stationary
weight. A uniform `L^2` density bound is false, by the all-depth proof
TM5. Tao's `L^1` estimate nevertheless gives the source-preserving
bounded-error conversion TM7; this is the positive replacement retained
in this pass. It still needs a sufficiently accurate ordinary
approximation and a pointwise mass argument. No such approximation is
constructed, so the modular calculation does not close Collatz.
