# Exact all-depth rational least-multiple models

Status: exact finite certificates of a deliberately enlarged dynamical system. Not ordinary counterexamples and not a proof of Collatz. This strengthens the finite inverse-screen obstruction in PR200's previous checkpoint by testing actual complete future orbits.

Fix an odd D>1 with 3∤D. On positive rational points u/D with integer u>0, use the 2-adic parity of u/D, equivalently of u. The shortcut map acts on numerators by T_D(u)=u/2 if even, and (3u+D)/2 if odd. At odd points the accelerated map is U_D(u)=(3u+D)/2^v2(3u+D). Since D is coprime to 6, gcd(u,D) is preserved under each branch. In particular all ancestors of a reduced denominator-D point in this lattice have gcd(u,D)=1. More generally every positive rational ancestor with odd denominator of a reduced denominator-D point has exactly that denominator: a factor 3 of a reduced denominator could be canceled in an odd step, so in this broader claim the rational domain must be restricted to denominators prime to BOTH 2 and 3.

For coprime integers A,p with A/p>log_2 3, define D=2^A-3^p and inverse mechanical gaps
e_i=ceil((i+1)A/p)-ceil(iA/p), 0<=i<p.
Build C from C_0=0, C_{i+1}=2^{e_i} C_i+3^i. The affine inverse identity gives the rational cycle candidate s=C_p/D. The executable certificate verifies gcd(C_p,D)=1, exact primitive period p, positivity and minimality of s on the cycle, for each of six models. All arithmetic uses BigInt; the finite exponents for ceiling are computed exactly as small integer products and quotients.

Define b(x)=1 iff the forward orbit reaches this distinguished cycle (NOT iff it fails to reach rational 1). This label is exactly invariant. Put n=2s+1 and q=n/3. The exact certificates show that q is a positive odd rational point of the lattice, n lies in the distinguished basin, q does not, and that EVERY lattice point with positive numerator below or equal to the numerator of q has label 0, while EVERY positive lattice point divisible by 3 with value less than n has label 0. For even numerators these assertions follow by stripping twos; for numerators not coprime to D they follow by gcd invariance. Consequently q is the least positive odd lattice tripling disagreement. The point n is its least bad multiple of three. There is NO shorter multiple in its full basin, even permitting arbitrarily deep ordinary rational paths and all positive rational starts of denominator prime to 6.

The finite certificate proves full future fate for each relevant numerator, rather than imposing a finite clock cutoff: a path is traced until it hits the designated cycle, reaches a cached previously certified fate, or repeats a vertex outside the designated cycle. If a path hits a safety cap the entire check throws and cannot certify the model. Nonprimitive numerators are excluded by the exact gcd invariant, not a heuristic. Each path has odd positive vertices. Determinism justifies all memoized outcomes.

In the first five models the only odd numerators below n in the distinguished basin are its cycle phases (which in particular establishes that s is the minimum of the entire rational basin). The largest model checks the union of all odd numerators <=q and all positive odd multiples of three below n; this suffices for the least-disagreement result but does not certify the minimum of the entire basin in that model.

The six denominators are 5,47,13,1909,1631,84997. Every pair is coprime (all fifteen gcd computations are certified). Hence for ANY set S of at most five primes, there is at least one model whose reduced denominator is coprime to every prime in S. All its rational iterates and the exact least-disagreement pair are S-integral. This refutes a proposed proof architecture using only a fixed set of at most five local integrality tests and the abstract invariant-label least-multiple property, even if that architecture attempts unrestricted inverse-clock descent. It does not refute a proof using every prime, exact ordinary integrality, or facts particular to the basin of 1.

The certificate checked 367640 odd starts in the indicated ranges (including gcd-excluded cases), and 867895 newly traversed odd edges; see the JSON for the exact per-model counters. The six cases are finite and their minima are small rational values. No claim of an unbounded family satisfying full basin minimality is made. They are not counterexamples to Collatz. In particular, the complement of the chosen rational cycle can contain many other rational cycles; it is not asserted to consist entirely of points reaching rational 1. This limitation is essential.

Connection: PR192's least-bad-multiple theorem and its latest inverse-optimality strengthening, PR205's bounded excursion dichotomy, and the exact full-denominator arguments in PR195 are genuine ordinary results. The models only demonstrate that one cannot omit the global arithmetic information while trying to derive a universal contradiction from local minimality. They give a concrete negative test for an inverse-minimum search, not a replacement for the prior ordinary theorem.
