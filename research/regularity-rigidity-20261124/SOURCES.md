The 0.84 estimate for ancestors of a fixed ternary unit is an external published computer-assisted theorem; the conversion to odd units and local thickening are proved in LOCAL_BLOCKS.md. No replay of their numerical certificate.
- "Automaticity of saturated sets, ancestor sets, stopping times and forward-invariant sets of 3x±1 maps", rStation v2, https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/64/v2. - Tao, Almost all orbits of the Collatz map attain almost bounded values, https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2022.8. The ternary-adic random affine viewpoint is inspiration only. No estimate for his random model is transferred to this paired-return model.
