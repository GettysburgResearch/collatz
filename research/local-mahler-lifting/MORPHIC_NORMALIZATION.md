# Erasing output morphisms and the remaining growth boundary

**Proposed corollary.** Suppose a parity word or integer-chart error word is given as $\tau(\sigma^\infty(a))$, where $\sigma$ is prolongable on $a$ and both morphisms may erase letters. Assume the output is infinite. If the finite output-prefix lengths $|\tau\sigma^n(a)|$ have exponential growth, the [exponential-presentation theorem](EXPONENTIAL_MORPHIC.md) still applies. Here the growth is measured in actual emitted letters. In particular any hypothetical divergent morphic positive-rational Collatz itinerary has polynomial output growth of degree $d\ge2$ in **every** such presentation. Its odd frequency $\theta$ would be rational and strictly exceed $\log_3 2$, and
$$
 s_N=\theta N+O(N^{1-1/d}),\qquad
 \log x_N=N(\theta\log3-\log2)+O(N^{1-1/d}).
$$

First consider a nonerasing substitution followed by a nonerasing output morphism $\tau$. For shortcut parity the effective weight of a source letter $b$ is $z_b=2^{|\tau(b)|}/3^{|\tau(b)|_1}$. Summing the finite contributions inside each $\tau(b)$ expresses the inverse parity identity as a rational row in the prefix sums $G_b(z)$ and $1$. For the fixed-affine chart use $z_b=(Q/P)^{|\tau(b)|}$ and the corresponding finite weighted digit sums. Each valuation at the distinguished prime is a positive constant times $|\tau(b)|$, bounded above and below by positive multiples of the all-ones vector. Accordingly its iterated values in a given row have exactly that row's length order, with a positive leading constant on a common power by the same nonnegative component path calculation. The primed layer construction and its contraction estimates are unchanged. Real absolute convergence at the block boundaries follows by sampling the full output prefix; in the parity case the original weights are positive and real summability holds on the hypothetical orbit. Thus the transfer proof covers nonerasing output words.

For the general case put $b\in E_n$ when $\tau\sigma^n(b)$ is nonempty. These subsets evolve by the deterministic Boolean incidence map and hence are eventually periodic. Choose $J,e$ with $E_{J+en}=E_J=:E$ for all $n\ge0$. Put $T=\sigma^e$, $\eta=\tau\sigma^J$, and let $\pi$ delete letters outside $E$. For $b\notin E$ there is no $E$-letter in $T(b)$, while for $b\in E$ there is at least one. Hence $\pi T=T_+\pi$, where $T_+(b)=\pi T(b)$ is nonerasing on $E$. The restriction $\eta_+$ is also nonerasing there. The seed belongs to $E$ because $\tau\sigma^n(a)$ are nested prefixes tending to the infinite output. Consequently $\eta_+ T_+^n(a)=\tau\sigma^{J+en}(a)$ are nested prefixes approaching the output. Also $T_+(a)$ starts with $a$ and has a nonempty suffix, since otherwise the displayed prefixes would be bounded. Actual lengths in this representation are comparable to $|T_+^n(a)|$ by nonerasure on a finite alphabet. Exponential growth, if present on any subsequence, remains on the selected progression by monotonicity of the original prefix lengths. This proves the reduction.

In the remaining case, restrict to the reachable productive alphabet. The incidence matrix has spectral radius one; a larger radius would give exponential growth by an accessible positive component path. On a common power each nonnilpotent diagonal block is the scalar one, as in the [quadratic normal form](../two-prime-growing-blocks/QUADRATIC_MORPHIC.md). Thus all full prefix letter counts, before and after the nonerasing output morphism, are eventually polynomials in the generation index $n$ with rational coefficients. The length polynomial has integer degree $d\ge1$ and is comparable to $n^d$. Linear growth would make the output eventually periodic by the same normal form. For $d\ge2$, the ratio of leading coefficients gives a rational zero or positive frequency $\theta$ of ones, and the full-prefix discrepancy in the count is $O(n^{d-1})$. The gap between adjacent full lengths is also $O(n^{d-1})$, giving the displayed estimate at all $N$. Finally the positive-rational orbitwise reciprocal product in the exponential proof gives $\log x_N=s_N\log3-N\log2+O(1)$. Since a nonperiodic positive rational trajectory escapes bounded sets, the leading coefficient must be nonnegative; rationality of $\theta$ rules out equality with $\log_3 2$.
