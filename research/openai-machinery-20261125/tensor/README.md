## What the proofs actually use

1. **One price system.** For at least two fixed sites, tensors modulo mutual local restriction form a semiring. A spectral point is a monotone normalized semiring homomorphism, so multiplicativity and direct-sum additivity are part of its algebraic specification. Wire distances are log prices of two-site diagonals. To restrict a point to a subset of sites with positive diagonal price e, raise its evaluation to 1/e. Grouping the nth power of a direct sum by type gives n+1 blocks; their binomial weights yield additivity after taking nth roots. This is why arbitrary independently selected scalar gauges on different tasks would not constitute an upstream spectral point. Transporting an index of dimension q costs at most its displacement times log₂q. [Price calculus](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/build/prices.tex)
2. **Closure and simultaneous calibration.** In a fixed ambient format of dimension D, taking an nth tensor power has polynomial coordinates. The span of powers of a set contains the powers of its Zariski closure and has dimension at most binomial(n+D−1,D−1). A closure point's power is a sum with at most that many terms. The logarithmic sum penalty divided by n vanishes. The format and placement are fixed in this argument. The manuscript proves existence of all finite-dimensional flats by ruling out a largest dimension: exclusion of approximate higher-dimensional cube corners, a graph routing/compression branch and a tagged-gate approximation branch yield a contradictory distributed simulation. On flats, convexity handles interior labels, a copying inequality handles shared labels, and support-function approximation extends segment and triangle prices to mean width. For Hilbert-space isometry invariance in the manuscript, homogenized prices on large flats are simultaneously approximately matched on **each prescribed finite family** by a random nearly isometric frame, concentration, and compactness. The stated Lean scope is finite-dimensional Euclidean prices and the flat diagonal law under its convexity hypotheses; the infinite-dimensional extension is outside that scope. [Geometric strategy](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/build/intro.tex) · [Hilbert calibration](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/build/hilbert.tex) · [Lean closure proof](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/lean/OAI/LinearAlgebra/SpectralBooks/SpectralSublevels.lean) · [Lean flat diagonal law](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/lean/OAI/LinearAlgebra/SpectralBooks/FlatLaw.lean)
3. **An independent opposing estimate.** Over a finite field k, assume ω(k)<9/4. For trivalent networks with size m ≤ n^1.05 and e ≥ n exterior bits, a tensor power with d separate copies can then be realized at cost at most d(m h^(−γ)+ e h^η), h=ceil(log₂n), with d ≤ n^o(1). The list-distinctness tensor on 2^w records of 2w bits has n=2w·2^w exterior bits; an independent bound gives cost > t n w^σ for all large w and 1 ≤ t ≤ 2^w. That leaves O(w^η) per exterior bit, contradicting the lower bound. Uniformity in t matters because compression chooses the replication factors. ## What the endpoint tensor remembers

Let A be a finite set of ordinary sources with attached clocks, and f(a) their **actual integer** endpoints. Across every field the two-party tensor
`Σ_a e_a ⊗ e_f(a) = Σ_y v_y ⊗ e_y`
has rank |f(A)|. The nonzero fiber vectors v_y have disjoint support. A local projection onto one chosen representative per attained y and the converse linear map carrying e_y to v_y exhibit mutual restriction with the diagonal of dimension |f(A)|. At two sites distance d apart its calibrated price is exactly d log₂|f(A)| when the batch is nonempty. Relabeling the endpoints by an arbitrary bijection preserves this price; integer height, basin, and smooth core can change.

For the full tensor of a deterministic length-k path with all states exposed, cut between times j−1 and j. Its rank is exactly the number of attained states at time j: prefix vectors for distinct time-j states have disjoint supports because they retain the source, and suffixes begin with distinct labels and are determined by that time-j state. Thus each contiguous cut forgets fiber sizes. This statement makes no claim about arbitrary interleaved cuts. Conversely, for a fixed endpoint and fixed depth, exposing both a valid exact valuation word and its source gives a diagonal of size equal to the number of valid words. This potentially enormous rank also occurs at the endpoint 1 of convergent orbits.

**An exact use of independent blocks.** For a finite source-clock function f, take its one-hot incidence matrix, keeping every source-clock row and one column per attained endpoint. Tag both domains and outputs of two batches, making a block diagonal matrix. Remove only the output tag, retaining distinct rows. Then, over every field,

```
tagged rank = aligned rank + |image(f) ∩ image(g)|.
```

The upstream theorem `OAI.LinearAlgebra.SpectralBooks.rank_fromBlocks_diagonal` gives additivity for independent blocks. The identity minor and the column bound give the rank for each actual endpoint matrix. Inclusion-exclusion proves the formula. A **strict drop is equivalent to an actual common endpoint** of the two batches, at possibly different clocks. Projecting to residues before comparing would change that conclusion. The comparison records each common endpoint once, independently of how many source pairs yield it. If the second batch consists of certified earlier-tower or smaller-base sources, a drop witnesses the merger required by #192. An independent upper estimate forcing a drop for every frontier input is still missing. ## Positive sibling mergers in every smooth-tower row

Write U(n)=(3n+1)/2^v₂(3n+1) for a positive odd n. Set A_h=(4^h−1)/3 and P_h(n)=4^h n+A_h for h≥1. The identity 3P_h(n)+1=4^h(3n+1) proves U(P_h(n))=U(n) as an equality of integers. On the shortcut map T used by the repository (divide by two on even, use (3n+1)/2 on odd), the clocks satisfy T^(2h+1)(P_h(n))=T(n): after the first shortcut step the larger branch is 4^h T(n). This already supplies clocks to the ordinary frontier statement.

Let n=3^r w with positive odd w coprime to 3. Three elementary controls are useful.

* **Necessary resonance for a positive shift.** v₃(A_h)=v₃(h). To see this without a p-adic theorem, factor 4^h−1 first at the 3-free part u of h: (4^u−1)/(4−1)≡u mod 3. Each cubing of a factor x≡1 mod 3 adds exactly one to its 3-valuation since x²+x+1≡3 mod 9. If v₃(h)≠r the two summands in P_h(n) have unequal valuations, so v₃(P_h(n))=min(r,v₃(h))≤r. Its 3-free core therefore exceeds w. Any improving positive sibling needs h≥3^r with v₃(h)=r and v₃(P_h(n))>r+h log₃4.
* **A sufficient residue with an actual lower base.** Fix h=3^r, write B=A_h/3^r, a positive 3-unit, and choose m with 3^m>4^h+B (m=2h suffices). The condition 4^h w≡−B mod 3^m selects exactly one residue, a unit. For every positive odd w in it, b=(4^h w+B)/3^m is a positive odd integer strictly smaller than w; the inequality follows from B≤Bw. Then P_h(3^r w)=3^(r+m)b and the displayed shortcut clocks provide a merger from n to a lower base. No coprimality assumption on b is needed in #192. An extra factor of 3 in b only reduces its smooth core.
* **Surviving the previous exclusions.** For each r≥1 impose also 3^r w≡1 mod 8 by the Chinese remainder theorem. This picks one positive progression modulo 8·3^m, of natural density 1/(8·3^m) among all positive bases for that fixed row. Other unit residues modulo 3^m with the same binary guard exist. Then M=v₂(3^r w+1)=1, so 2^M≤3^r. Also 3^r w≢85 mod 512 since 85≡5 mod 8. These are the exact two previously published exit guards, including the r≥2 guard. Thus infinitely many inputs in each positive row satisfying the previous arithmetic frontier now have certified meetings. The same congruence gives 3n+1≡4 mod 8, so the first odd successor y=(3n+1)/4 is odd, 1 modulo 3 and strictly above w: 3^(r+1)≥9. Thus even its full 2,3-free core exceeds w. The witness uses a larger positive sibling with a smaller base. These inputs need not be counterexamples, and a row still has uncovered bases. ## Capped inverse fibers and the remaining obstruction

For a positive odd 3-unit endpoint y, the inverse at exponent e is x=(2^e y−1)/3. It is an odd 3-unit exactly when 2^e y mod 9 belongs to {4,7}. Since 2 generates the six units modulo 9, there are exactly 2m eligible exponents in [1,6m] for each y. At depth k there are exactly (2m)^k ordinary unit sources with every inverse exponent in that interval: multiply the independent per-vertex counts, and recover the exponents uniquely from a source by forward iteration. This is a fixed-depth statement; the self-loop at 1 illustrates why cross-depth counts need care. Removing the budget permits arbitrarily many predecessors of 1 already at depth one.

A different budget fixes total clock S. For a positive length-k valuation word with prefix sums S_j, put D=Σ_{j=0}^{k−1}3^(k−1−j)2^S_j. An attainable endpoint y must satisfy 2^S y≡D mod 3^k, a unique residue because 2 is invertible. Conversely for any positive odd y in that residue with 2^S y>D, reverse inversion gives the unique positive start a=(2^S y−D)/3^k. Reducing the affine equation modulo 3 at each reverse step forces the required integer division; the intermediate numerators are even minus one and the intermediate values are odd. The positivity of the starting integer forces positivity along the forward word. Consequently the histogram of candidate words over all endpoint residues modulo 3^k sums to binomial(S−1,k−1) for S≥k≥1, and every sufficiently large odd representative of each residue realizes its full count. The mean over unit residues divides this by 2·3^(k−1). A designated bad endpoint is not a random unit residue.

The block identity identifies the missing *arithmetic* quantity exactly. A strict loss requires contact with earlier-tower or smaller-base ordinary sources. Batching independent copies merely multiplies both sides of the identity. A bad orbit could avoid the certified residue in every tested row. The machine and tensor results supply neither height decay nor a lower bound on overlap for this particular orbit.

A concrete forcing target can be tested without assigning an unknown basin label. Fix w and a forward source/clock. Suppose its attained odd endpoint y satisfies y≡2 mod 3 and 2y−1<3w. Then p=(2y−1)/3 is a positive odd integer below w and U(p)=y. More generally for e≥1 and j≥0, the tests 2^e y≡1 mod 3^(j+1) and 2^e y−1<3^(j+1)w imply an explicit comparison source p=3^j b with 0<b<w and U(p)=y. Here y must be positive odd, and the congruence ensures positivity of b because 2^e y≥2. These tests give specific height-and-residue windows in which the rank drop is forced. An independent occupancy estimate for surviving ordinary sources in a chosen union of such windows would enlarge the covered frontier. The rank equality itself supplies no occupancy estimate.
