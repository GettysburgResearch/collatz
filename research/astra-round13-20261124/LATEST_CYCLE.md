# Complete top-component absorption at arbitrary height with gaps one and two

**Status.** Separately reconstructed written results and one exact finite fixture. The new theorem concerns primitive positive rational cycles. The Collatz conjecture remains open. The source caps, the comparison with earlier results, and the new construction are specified below.

## Exact source review

* **PR192, be783656f0e889fe0d5318a2f7a9140099cb94b5**, research/astra-phase-and-cycle-20261124/CYCLE_FACTORS.md, §§1–4. For F=X^p−2^A and the carry polynomial Q, residual means F/gcd(F,Q) over the rationals. Complex conjugate root pairing in a monic rational factor P of F gives reciprocal coefficient symmetry at radius 2^(a/b); hence a nonzero coefficient of degree i requires b|2i. Rotations by (b/gcd(2,b))-th roots of unity preserve its roots. Together with conjugacy of the projected roots, this gives at least that many roots per projected fibre. For even b a split is possible precisely when 2η is square in the cyclotomic field Q(η), since a is odd; the two half-fibres then exhaust the degree. For order h of η this square occurs exactly at v₂(h)=2: the odd-order portion is a square, ±2i are squares in Q(i), while for larger 2-parts squaring would lift a primitive even-order root into the strictly larger field of conductor 2h. For odd or twice-odd h the odd-conductor field excludes both square roots of ±2. The projected-order lcm equals g by the pinned PR185 result used in PR192, so a primitive prime-power word must retain the top projected order. Outside g=4 with even b its entire irreducible top factor survives. This result and its broader general-h factorization predate the parallel prime-power reconstruction in PRIME_POWER_NORM.md; they deserve source credit. The superpolynomial bound concerns the residual polynomial evaluated at 3 and retains the PR185 numerical-gap dependency. No evaluated gcd saving follows. PR192 also gives examples where every nonzero component norm is numerically saturated.
* **PR193, c6cb5e66516a86c33a757154983bbddc822510de**, research/astra-universal-descent-20261124/cycles.md, C1–C5. The cut at a descending ordinary segment obeys y=v−3^s(u−v)/(2^A−3^s); integrality requires the complete divisor, not only a local congruence. C2 and C3 use distinct objects: an ordinary almost-closed segment and an exactly closed rational cycle. C5 is a full-denominator estimate: among P(k)+1 positions around the minimum of a primitive period, two share k shortcut bits if P(k)<A. Their scaled distinct integer numerators are congruent modulo 2^k. Backward bounds are 2^s e and forward bounds (3/2)^t(e+d)−d for e=dm. Subtracting e gives the two alternatives in C5.1 exactly. Its lower-complexity corollary requires an actual scale with the stated complexity slack; neither a small symbolic gcd nor a large polynomial residual supplies such a scale.
* **PR194, b1bf1f151a2da8936f80d229f132d0b7539ba42c**, research/astra-birth-rigidity-20261124/TOP_COMPONENT.md and LATEST_SOURCE_SYNTHESIS.md. Its two distinct coset grids force primitivity by their simultaneous period and annihilate the weighted rows separately at every primitive g-th root. These results overlap our prime-power reconstruction and supersede any priority claim for that classification. PR194 LS2 bounds the actual reduced denominator in this specific grid: an interior of length g/s−1 free of either grid exists because r−1<s, and the scaled displacement from the baseline 37/5 is a positive multiple of 2^(5(g/s−1)) bounded above by 21d. ## New synthesis

**Theorem.** Fix any g with at least two distinct prime divisors. There are primitive positive rational shortcut cycles with gcd(A,p)=g, every accelerated gap in {1,2}, all states arbitrarily high, and positive A−p log₂3 arbitrarily small, in which the entire top polynomial H_g divides the full carry polynomial Q over Z[X]. In particular its entire numerical factor H_g(3) cancels. Their complete reduced denominators nonetheless tend to infinity, growing at least as c_g 2^n/n for block length n in this construction. This is stronger than the split-component zero for the earlier high-floor family, and stronger in floor and gap restrictions than the original mixed-g grid. It gives no prime that must survive in a general word.

**A two-gap mixed seed.** Choose different primes r,s dividing g. For j modulo g let I(j) be the indicator of j=1 modulo g/r, and J(j) that of j=1 modulo g/s. In the j-th eight-bit cell mark the five odd positions
    0, 1+I(j), 3, 4+J(j), 6.
The five gaps are 1+I(j), 2−I(j), 1+J(j), 2−J(j), 2. The seed has p₀=5g and A₀=8g and starts odd. An integer repeat count k>1 would divide both counts, hence g, so its period would translate cells by g/k. Both indicators must be preserved by this translation. Their least common period is lcm(g/r,g/s)=g, impossible.

Let Q₀(Y)=sum 2^(d_i) Y^(p₀−1−i), using the odd-position prefixes d_i. For any root y of H₀(Y)=(2^8)^φ(g) Φ_g(Y^5/2^8), set w=2^8/y^5. After dividing Q₀(y) by y^(p₀−1) and grouping i modulo 5, the row coefficients at index j are respectively 1, 2(1+I(j)), 8, 16(1+J(j)), 64. Their sums against w^j vanish: the full geometric sum is zero and each grid coset sums a complete progression of prime order r or s. Every root of H₀ is simple, so H₀ divides Q₀. Both are integral and H₀ is monic, hence division holds in Z[Y].

**Primitive clocks with a usable positive overshoot.** Write α=log₂3. Fractional parts of jα approach 1/5 from below, so there are upper approximants with denominator 5j, numerator ≡1 mod 5, and positive error tending to zero. Divide numerator and denominator by their gcd. This preserves 5|b₀ and 5∤a₀; the reduced error δ₀=a₀−b₀α remains positive and tends to zero. The reduced denominators tend to infinity by irrationality. For each pair solve b₀ c−a₀ d=1 with 0<d<b₀, and choose t in {1,2,3,4} with t a₀≡3 mod 5. Put (a,b)=t(a₀,b₀)+5(c,d). The two column vectors form a unimodular basis, hence gcd(a,b)=gcd(t,5)=1. Now a≡8 mod 5, b≡0 mod 5 and
    δ=a−bα=(t+5d/b₀)δ₀+5/b₀,     5/b<δ≤9δ₀+5/b₀→0.
Set n=b/5 and S=(a−8)/5. For sufficiently large n, S/(n−1) tends to α in (1,2); there is a word v of n−1 gaps in {1,2} totaling S.

**Lift and cancellation.** Before each seed gap insert the same prefix v. The new counts are A=g(8+5S)=ga and p=5gn=gb, so gcd(A,p)=g. A period must be a multiple of n on the gap index: the nonempty difference set of the lifted word and its n-shift is contained in the single terminal column modulo n, and a period preserves that set. Seed primitivity then forces the whole period. Any smaller shortcut period would yield a smaller gap period by rotating from an odd mark to an odd mark.

Set u=2^S. For the n within-block prefix positions e_i, P(X)=sum 2^(e_i)X^(n−1−i), including e₀=0 and e_(n−1)=S. Grouping each gap by its seed index and within-block index yields exactly
    Q(X)=P(X)u^(5g−1)Q₀(X^n/u).
The identity includes every prefix carry. A top root x satisfies x^(5n)/2^(8+5S) of order g. Then y=x^n/u has y^5/2^8 of order g, hence Q₀(y)=0. The full H_g(X)=(2^a)^φ(g)Φ_g(X^b/2^a) is monic and separable and divides Q in Z[X]. At 3, H_g(3)>0 divides both Q(3) and 2^A−3^p (up to the conventional sign of the latter factorization). Rotation preserves common roots, by 2^gap Q_rot(X)=X Q(X)−X^p+2^A.

**Actual height and denominator.** Here D=2^A−3^p>0. The cyclic carry at each odd boundary is positive and odd; dividing by D, all phases have odd reduced denominator, link by the specified affine equations, and have exactly the asserted valuations. Their positive rational product identity is
    product_i (1+1/(3q_i))=2^(gδ).
Consequently every odd phase exceeds or equals 1/[3(2^(gδ)−1)], tending to infinity. Intervening even shortcut states decrease to the following odd phase; they satisfy the same floor.

Apply DEFECT_COVER.md (1) to the 5g disjoint copies of the literal shortcut prefix v, whose common word contains N=n−1 one bits. There are k=5g distinct boundary states by primitivity; the terminal defects have L=5g one bits and E=8g−5g=3g zero bits. The full-cycle η=1−2^(−gδ) is at least (g log 2)δ/2 for sufficiently small positive δ. Since δ>1/n, the full numerical denominator exceeds a positive constant depending only on g times 2^n/n. No external linear-forms estimate is required. This proves nonintegrality while allowing complete absorption of the designated top component.

**One exact fixture.** With g=6, r=2, s=3, n=572, S=905, the clocks are (A,p)=(27198,17160). The top numerical factor has 9066 bits and divides the full carry; the actual reduced denominator still has 18127 bits. The floor of the actual minimum is 90269. The separate product-only bound certifies a minimum above 10. The standard-library script mixed_top_control.py checks the seed polynomial remainder, expanded clock and primitivity, numerical divisibility, literal gap at every step, and full return. The checks run under optimized Python as well. No new Lean build or repository-wide validation was performed for this review.

The unresolved ordinary question is still simultaneous cancellation across every surviving numerical component of an arbitrary word.
