## The coefficient and the constant

For each finite word the displacements of all distinct partial endpoints differ, since $\alpha$ is irrational. Cutting at its maximum bijectively gives a positive-final-record prefix and a negative-prefix tail. In the two-variable commutative series all powers contributing to $\log P$ have positive displacement, while all powers contributing to $\log Q$ have negative displacement. Positive projection of the unrestricted log therefore gives $\log P$. Its length-$n$ coefficient involves only clocks $s\le\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor$, so setting the clock variable equal to one is legitimate there. The unrestricted coefficient sum is exactly $\binom{\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor}{n}/n$.

Differentiating the normalized identity $1-A=\exp(-B)$ gives the stated P4 with nonnegative original coefficients, hence $a_n\le b_n\ll n^{-3/2}$. Multiplication by $\sqrt n$ leaves kernel $\sqrt{n/(n-k)}$ in the subtracted term. The contribution above $k=n/2$ is $O(n^{-1/2})$, and the middle range from $M$ to $n/2$ is $O(\sum_{k>M}a_k)$. For bounded $k$ the floor phase is exactly $\{n\alpha-k\alpha\}$. The profile $\Phi$ is a uniform sum of bounded Riemann-integrable functions; its integral is $e^{-B(1)}/(h\sigma\sqrt{2\pi})$. The endpoint support inequality follows since the terminal step has gap one and the overshoot is less than $\alpha-1$; rotating the preceding positive-total word realizes every feasible length. Its cyclic class may have fewer rotations than its length, which only improves the stated lower bound.

At $tJ=\frac12\log J+\log\log J+C$, the last window has scale $1/t=o(J)$. The irrational-rotation averages of $\Phi$ are uniform under translation by continuous upper and lower approximation, including points exactly on jumps. The exponentially weighted average follows by summation by parts. The normalized terminal contribution is $2e^C\int\Phi$ and the low half tends to $A(1)$, giving the constant $\log(h\sigma\sqrt{\pi/2})$. Direct numerical evaluation gave $0.18473100923606242$.

## The incomplete final block and real carry

For a reversed complete ladder word the terminal deficit is strictly positive and is **strictly smaller** than every nonempty proper-prefix deficit: reversing a forward proper prefix of negative displacement subtracts that negative number from the total. Every nonempty prefix of every subsequent reversed block has positive deficit. Thus for any observation containing the first complete block, its ending position is the unique minimum nonempty deficit, equivalently the maximum prefix ratio. This holds even when the last observed block is partial. On the restricted event $G$ all blocks meeting the first $n$ gaps have length at most $J=O(\log n)$. Forced decoding can thus continue whenever $J$ gaps remain. Its ending depth is $l>n-J$ and it has $k\gg n/\log n$ forced complete blocks; in particular the uniform concentration hypothesis for $k/l$ applies. The restricted probability of that observed gap word is bounded by the unrestricted product mass of the forced complete prefix.

Every complete reversed block and its observed terminal prefix is critical; each normalized inverse multiplier is at most one. Each piece has length at most $J$ and clock at most $\alpha J$, so its individual normalized real offset is at most $J3^J$. Composing $O(n)$ such pieces gives $0\le C/3^n\ll n J3^J$, of polynomial size in $n$. For one fixed clock $s$ and ternary cell, all carries are congruent to $2^s z$ modulo $3^n$, hence occur at polynomial multiplicity in that real interval. At fixed $n,s$ a carry determines the full gap word by the successive exact 2-adic valuations after subtracting the terminal $3^{n-1}$. There are only $O(n)$ clocks. This is the required spatial multiplicity estimate even when several infinite gap scripts represent the same point. Multiplication by the Gibbs prefix bound gives the Frostman lower dimension. The upper dimension uses only the complete-boundary $Z(s)^k$ count with infinitely many endpoints; taking a strict density threshold below the requested limsup avoids an endpoint assumption.

The exact-limit lower measures have finite mean as $s>H$, and the maximizing parameter is unique because the mean derivative is minus a strictly positive variance. Mean divergence at $H$ follows already by summing $m^{-1/2}$ on the syndetic feasible lengths. In the zero-density construction, the deficits tend to infinity and are sublinear, so each tail has a global minimum nonempty prefix. Successive minima give an infinite reversed-ladder parsing. Positive block-count density would put linearly many bounded length blocks among a subsequence of prefixes, contradicting the sublinear deficit there. The pressure expansion uses Cesàro averaging of the normalized $m^{3/2}$ coefficients against the two integrable kernels. The optimizing shift has square root $(\sqrt\pi\overline\Phi/q)\rho+o(\rho)$; substitution gives the negative quadratic M2 coefficient with exactly one power of $\pi$.

For M3 the normalized F1 edge law has expected odd block length at most $1/\delta$ by truncation. A stationary two-sided edge process with those source/target marginals gives finite conditional backward mean length $L$ almost surely by Birkhoff. Backward contraction puts its sample point in $E_\rho$ whenever $L\le1/\rho$. Markov's inequality and the analytic measurability of the coded sets justify the asserted occupancy probabilities. The dimension conclusions address limiting temporal occupation and allow rare record subsequences elsewhere.

## Finite checks

I computed P2's integer recurrence through length 250, checking divisibility by $n$ at each step. The exact floor was $(3^n).\mathrm{bit\_length}()-1$. A separate finite-clock dynamic program counted ladder words directly through length 70, with agreement throughout. Its partial state of clock $s$ after $j<n$ gaps retains $s>\lfloor\alpha j\rfloor$ and enough remaining clock to complete at $\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor$. Feasible support agreed through length 250.

The complete words of lengths at most 6 form an 11-letter library. Across all ordered triples I checked 19,239 observed prefixes, decoding the unique minimal positive deficit by exact comparison of $2^{S_j}/3^j$ without floating point. Every forced block agreed with the supplied concatenation and the remainder had fewer than 6 gaps. At observed depths at most 10 the sampled words gave 430 distinct triples $(n,s,C)$, each with a unique word. One ternary cell had three different gap words, so this control also exercises a real overlap. These checks touch finite instances only; the asymptotics, stationarity and dimension statements above are mathematical proofs.
