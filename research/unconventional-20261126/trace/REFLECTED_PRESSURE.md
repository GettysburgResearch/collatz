# Reflection preserves the full ternary pressure and moves its growing cylinder to a positive root

**Status.** Elementary exact control. Let \(Jf(z)=f(-z)\) on \(C(\mathbb Z_3)\). For any geometric bias \(0<p<1\), conjugate O15's inverse operator by \(J\). Writing \(p_e=p(1-p)^{e-1}\), the result is
\[
 (J B_p J f)(z)=\sum_{e\ge1}3p_e\,
 \mathbf1_{2^e z\equiv-1\pmod3}\ f\big((2^e z+1)/3\big).
\]
The random affine maps in the opposite direction are \((3z-1)/2^e\). Reflection preserves Haar measure, the transfer norms of all powers, spectra, and the inverse-process entropy statements after relabeling the state. Thus the entire family has exactly O15's pressure profile. See [O15](../operator/EXACT_BERGMAN_PRESSURE.md).

At the original bias \(p=1/2\), the reflected finite densities are \(\rho_k(-a)\). The inverse exponent-one branch fixes the positive integer \(a=1\) in the reflected system and contributes \((3/2)^k\) there. The positive one-period harmonic head is therefore at least \((3/2)^k\), from its first term. In the original system this growing branch fixes \(-1\). The reflected positive odd map is \((3x-1)/2^{v_2(3x-1)}\). Its positive fixed point has lap weight \(3/2>1\); the weighted positive first-hit damping and cycle-lap hypotheses of [the charge formula](DIRICHLET_CHARGE.md) do not transfer. Consequently even the full pressure profile plus the isometrically transported stationary-process statements cannot by themselves give the original system's positive-representative harmonic upper bound.
