# Independent review of the ordinary rational-diagonal exit

**Reviewed source.** The renewal lane's `RATIONAL_DIAGONAL.md`, SHA-256 `3b823138448df4579e404f8d145fdbde35ce2f0416cf6352d80b2994a447be90`, and PR210's signed-rigidity proof at `592328fecf8ea2cf41736294856d95f890a1ba98`, §4. All three conclusions hold under the stated single ordinary nonrepeating-ray hypothesis.

**D1.** Unbounded first-ladder lengths give a convergent sequence of starting record phases with no finite upcrossing. The cylinder test must include the terminal odd bit, as the author does. Otherwise bounded odd length gives bounded native duration by the first-ladder identity. Every centered native omega word therefore has a periodic right tail under the proposed confinement, since the offset to the preceding record is bounded and can be fixed on a subsequence. The projection to one-sided tails is compact and the shift is onto. For purely periodic tails the entire preimage is unique by comparison through a common period. Continuity of the inverse and compactness give a uniform finite predecessor memory. The finite factor graph then has indegree one and positive outdegree everywhere, so is a union of cycles. Eventual periodicity of the original sequence follows because all other words of the required fixed length occur only finitely often. The exact dyadic source congruence forces a repeated ordinary source. A periodic odd dyadic phase is a nonzero rational. A negative lap has strictly positive affine carry and multiplier bigger than one, so the sign conclusion follows.

**D2.** The finite-clock bound to a later record lets one fix its offset as well as its ternary cluster point. At backward depth $j$ a critical suffix has clock strictly less than $j\log_2 3$, so the diagonal extraction yields one infinite script satisfying every finite congruence at that point. The full proof of PR210 R1 indeed says *each* tail numerator is on the same negative cycle when numerator and denominator are odd, hence *every* admissible script is purely periodic from the first mark. Eventual periodicity alone would be insufficient for the shift argument. Expanding the odd gaps yields the required purely periodic left native tail, preserving periodicity under the fixed offset. The same finite-subshift rigidity works after reversal.

**D3.** Projection of the joint cluster set onto its dyadic coordinate is onto $K_2$ by compactness of the ternary coordinate. If all pairs were on the indicated rational diagonal, R1 makes every dyadic point thereby obtained negative periodic. D1 rules this out without a bounded-gap assumption. The finite-neighborhood consequence follows by separating a point outside the given finite list in the product topology. This does not force either separate coordinate irrational unless the other hypotheses in D1 or D2 apply.

**Comparison.** PR210 R2 handles a finite list of rational ternary points under bounded gaps. D2 excludes an arbitrary rational dyadic-unit ternary cluster set under that gap condition. D3 removes it for the specific joint rational diagonal. Neither result excludes different rational local centers or proves arithmetic compact confinement.
