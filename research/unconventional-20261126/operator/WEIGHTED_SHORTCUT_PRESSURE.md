# Exact weighted shortcut pressure

**Corollary of O13 and O15.** Write \(Uf(x)=f(2x)\) and \(Jf(x)={\bf1}_{x=2\bmod3}f((2x-1)/3)\) on \(C(\mathbb Z_3)\). For \(a,b>0\),
$$
                       r(aU+bJ)=\max(a+b/3,\ b).
$$
Indeed the Haar average gives \(r\ge a+b/3>a\) and the all-odd path at \(-1\) gives \(r\ge b\). For \(t>a\), the positive induced operator \((tI-aU)^{-1}bJ\) is \(b/[3(t-a)]\) times the biased operator \(B_p\) in O15 at \(p=1-a/t\). Its radius is \(\max\{b/[3(t-a)],b/t\}\) by O15. It is strictly below one for every \(t>\max(a+b/3,b)\). The positive-resolvent comparison proved in O13 (with \(2\) replaced by \(a\)) therefore gives the matching upper bound. These radii concern the positive operator on ternary continuous functions; transfer to a different Hilbert or Banach operator requires its own norm or essential-spectrum argument.
