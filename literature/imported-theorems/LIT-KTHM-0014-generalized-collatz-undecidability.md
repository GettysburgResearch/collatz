# LIT-KTHM-0014 — undecidability belongs to generalized Collatz systems

**Sources:** [@Conway1972], [@KurtzSimon2007]
**Inspection:** Conway bibliography confirmed in later primary sources; Kurtz–Simon official abstract
**Proof status:** BLACK BOX

## Imported result

Building on Conway's generalized iteration constructions, Kurtz and Simon prove that a natural generalized Collatz totality problem is `Π^0_2`-complete.

## Scope

The theorem concerns a family of generalized piecewise-affine maps supplied as input. It establishes a strong undecidability classification for that generalized decision problem.

## Native relevance

It justifies caution about expecting one universal algorithm to decide arbitrary Collatz-like rewrite systems and provides context for expressive generalized maps.

## Non-applications

It does **not** prove that the single standard `3x+1` conjecture is undecidable, independent of a formal system, or computationally universal in the same parameterized sense. Generalized-map undecidability cannot be used as evidence for either convergence or divergence of the ordinary map.
