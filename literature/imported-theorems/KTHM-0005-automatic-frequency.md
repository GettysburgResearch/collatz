# KTHM-0005 — Existing letter frequencies of automatic sequences are rational

**Source context:** standard finite-kernel / DFAO theory; compare Allouche–Shallit. [@AlloucheShallit2003]  
**Proof status:** complete finite-state proof  
**Maps to:** repairs the frequency obstruction in `CLAUDE/T-0003`

## Statement

Let `(a_n)_{n≥0}` be a `k`-automatic sequence over a finite alphabet. Fix a letter `b`. If the natural frequency

\[
\delta_b=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\#\{0\le n<N:a_n=b\}
\]

exists, then `δ_b` is rational.

## Proof

Represent the sequence by a deterministic finite automaton with output that reads base-`k` expansions, allowing leading zeros. Let its state set have size `r`, let `A_d` be the `0`–`1` transition matrix for digit `d`, let `u` select the initial state, and let `h` be the indicator column of states outputting `b`.

Averaging over all `k^m` words of length `m` gives

\[
\frac1{k^m}\#\{0\le n<k^m:a_n=b\}
=uP^m h,
\qquad
P=\frac1k\sum_{d=0}^{k-1}A_d.
\]

The matrix `P` has rational entries and is stochastic. The assumed natural frequency forces the scalar sequence `uP^m h` to converge to `δ_b`.

Because powers of a stochastic matrix are bounded, eigenvalue `1` has no nontrivial Jordan block. The spectral projection `E` onto the `1`-eigenspace is a polynomial in `P` with rational coefficients: apply Bézout to the coprime factors `x-1` and the remaining factor of the minimal polynomial. All eigencomponents of modulus less than one vanish; convergence of the scalar sequence eliminates the contributions of any other unit-modulus eigenvalues. Hence

\[
\delta_b=uEh.
\]

The vectors `u,h` and matrix `E` are rational, so `δ_b∈Q`. ∎

## Why this is not Cobham's theorem

Cobham concerns recognition of the same set in two multiplicatively independent bases. The theorem above uses one automaton and one existing frequency. Cobham is not needed for this conclusion. [@Cobham1969]

## Scope limitations

- The frequency must exist.
- The schedule itself must actually be generated by a fixed finite automaton in an integer base.
- Nonautomaticity does not imply Sturmianity or prove any carry-closure property.
