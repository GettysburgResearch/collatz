# LIT-KTHM-0011 — Everett's almost-every descent theorem

**Sources:** [@Everett1976], [@Everett1977]
**Inspection:** full Los Alamos report; journal metadata
**Proof status:** BLACK BOX with scope summary

## Statement

For the shortcut Collatz function used by Everett, the set of positive integers whose trajectory has an iterate below its starting value has natural density one.

Everett's Theorem 1 also gives the finite parity-vector/residue bijection imported separately in `LIT-KTHM-0001`.

## Native relevance

This is classical evidence that typical finite prefixes descend. It supplies historical context for repository discussions of parity statistics, stopping times, and the rarity of supercritical excursions.

## Non-applications

The theorem does not prove convergence of every orbit, exclude an exceptional divergent integer, establish independence of successive parity bits along one infinite orbit, or obstruct a deliberately constructed collision grammar.
