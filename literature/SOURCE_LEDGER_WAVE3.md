# Source ledger — literature audit wave 3

Only sources actually located during wave 3 appear here. “Inspected” distinguishes metadata/abstract inspection from theorem-level inspection.

## S28 — Bernstein and Lagarias, `3x+1` conjugacy

**Record:** Daniel J. Bernstein and Jeffrey C. Lagarias, *The 3x+1 Conjugacy Map*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 48 (1996), no. 6, 1154–1169. DOI `10.4153/CJM-1996-060-x`.

**Inspected:** publisher abstract and bibliographic record; theorem content cross-checked against the existing literature entry and the native parity-map convention.

**Located content:** the `3x+1` map on `Z_2` is conjugate to the `2`-adic shift by an isometric homeomorphism; reductions modulo `2^n` are permutations.

**Native use:** `LIT-KTHM-0028`; `FOUNDRY/T-9601`.

## S29 — Anashin, `p`-adic automata

**Record:** Vladimir Anashin, *Automata finiteness criterion in terms of van der Put series of automata functions*, p-Adic Numbers, Ultrametric Analysis and Applications 4 (2012), no. 2, 151–160; arXiv `1112.5089`.

**Inspected:** author abstract/arXiv metadata and later publisher summary of the finite-state criterion.

**Located content:** digit transducers induce `1`-Lipschitz functions on `Z_p`; finite-state realizability is characterized by the reduced van der Put coefficients.

**Native use:** Foundry operator taxonomy and finite-state search interfaces. No rationality or ordinary-integer conclusion is imported.

## S30 — Mahler, `p`-adic Hermite--Lindemann

**Record:** Kurt Mahler, *Ein Beweis der Transzendenz der P-adischen Exponentialfunktion*, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 169 (1932), 61–66. Reprint DOI `10.4171/DMS/8/19`.

**Inspected:** EMS reprint metadata/abstract; historical theorem identification independently confirmed in Kunrui Yu's survey of `p`-adic logarithmic forms.

**Located content:** the `p`-adic Hermite--Lindemann theorem: within the convergence disk, the `p`-adic exponential of a nonzero algebraic argument is transcendental. Equivalently, a nonzero `p`-adic logarithm of an algebraic number in the exponential image is transcendental.

**Native use:** `LIT-KTHM-0030`, identifying the PR #3 moving-bulk limit as a transcendental `2`-adic logarithm.

## S31 — Mahler, `p`-adic Gelfond--Schneider

**Record:** Kurt Mahler, *Über transzendente P-adische Zahlen*, Compositio Mathematica 2 (1935), 259–275.

**Inspected:** full article, especially the stated Hauptsatz on the final page.

**Located content:** for suitable algebraic `p`-adic numbers, the quotient of their `p`-adic logarithms is rational or transcendental.

**Native use:** broader completion-height and logarithmic-form context. It is not needed for the elementary limit formula in `LIT-KTHM-0030`.

## S32 — Mauldin and Urbański, countable conformal IFS

**Record:** R. Daniel Mauldin and Mariusz Urbański, *Dimensions and Measures in Infinite Iterated Function Systems*, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society s3-73 (1996), no. 1, 105–154. DOI `10.1112/plms/s3-73.1.105`.

**Inspected:** publisher abstract and bibliographic record.

**Located content:** dimension and measure theory for countable conformal iterated-function systems via pressure.

**Native use:** conceptual framework for `LIT-KTHM-0031`. The H-ghost residue recurrence and dimension proof are supplied self-contained because direct transfer of a real conformal theorem to the ultrametric setting must not be assumed.

## S33 — Abram and Lagarias, `p`-adic path sets

**Record:** William C. Abram and Jeffrey C. Lagarias, *p-adic path set fractals and arithmetic*, Journal of Fractal Geometry 1 (2014), 45–81. DOI `10.4171/JFG/2`.

**Inspected:** source metadata and abstract-level description.

**Located content:** automaton-described subsets of `Z_p`, arithmetic operations, and computable Hausdorff dimensions.

**Native use:** finite-alphabet neighboring framework for H ghosts, active stack cylinders, and regular survivor sets. The H system is countable-alphabet, so this is partial overlap rather than a direct theorem.

## S34 — Lyons, Pemantle, and Peres, size-biased spines

**Record:** Russell Lyons, Robin Pemantle, and Yuval Peres, *Conceptual Proofs of L Log L Criteria for Mean Behavior of Branching Processes*, Annals of Probability 23 (1995), no. 3, 1125–1138. DOI `10.1214/aop/1176988176`.

**Inspected:** author/arXiv abstract, bibliographic metadata, and later descriptions of the size-biased measure.

**Located content:** comparison of the ordinary branching law with a size-biased law; conceptual `L log L` criteria.

**Native use:** terminology and next-tool suggestions for PR #3's exact critical-particle spine.

## S35 — Hardy and Harris, many-to-one spine method

## S36 — Ridout, `p`-adic Roth theorem

## S37 — Adamczewski and Bugeaud, symmetric real/`p`-adic expansions

## S38 — Mosquera and Shmerkin, sparse exceptional Fourier frequencies

**Record:** Carolina A. Mosquera and Pablo S. Shmerkin, *Self-similar measures: asymptotic bounds for the dimension and Fourier decay of smooth images*, Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae Mathematica 43 (2018), 823–834. DOI `10.5186/aasfm.2018.4350`.

**Inspected:** publisher abstract and metadata.

**Located content:** quantitative power Fourier decay outside a sparse set for homogeneous real self-similar measures.

**Native use:** methodology/positioning for `ADEL/T-9307`–`T-9312`; hypotheses do not directly cover the fixed nonarchimedean moving-character cusp.

## S39 — Krieger, embedding into a mixing topological Markov chain

**Record:** Wolfgang Krieger, *On the subsystems of topological Markov chains*, Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems 2 (1982), no. 2, 195–202. DOI `10.1017/S0143385700001516`.

**Inspected:** publisher abstract and bibliographic record.

**Located content:** a lower-entropy expansive Cantor system embeds into a mixing topological Markov chain when its least-period point counts do not exceed those of the target. For subshifts this is the classical entropy-plus-periodic-point embedding criterion.

**Native use:** `LIT-KTHM-0033`; a potential route from PR #3's bit surplus to an actual symbolic router after the arithmetic stage relation is encoded as a stationary mixing SFT.

## S40 — MacDonald, zero-error coding through sliding block codes

**Record:** Sophie MacDonald, *Encoding subshifts through sliding block codes*, Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems 44 (2024), no. 4, 1091–1115. DOI `10.1017/S0143385723000325`; arXiv `2210.08150`.

**Inspected:** publisher abstract and arXiv metadata.

**Located content:** necessary and sufficient conditions for a lower-entropy subshift to embed into a mixing SFT while remaining injective through a prescribed surjective sliding block code; explicitly framed in the spirit of zero-error information theory.

**Native use:** the correct stronger model when a PR #3 router must preserve both a hidden connector state and a visible residue/output projection.

## Inspection cautions

- No source above proves that a completion point selected by an infinite Collatz directive is an ordinary integer.
- Real self-similar Fourier theorems are not silently transferred to `Z_2` or to the triangular cusp.
- General IFS dimension theory is used as context; the H-ghost theorem has a local proof.
- The Foundry contraction corollary does not classify which fixed points are rational or integral.
- Entropy surplus alone does not invoke Krieger's theorem: the native connector system must first be a stationary mixing SFT and satisfy periodic-point/observation conditions.
