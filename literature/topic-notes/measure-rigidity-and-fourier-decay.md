# Measure rigidity and Fourier decay: exact near misses

## Fixed self-similar measures

Li–Sahlsten prove Rajchman decay for every self-similar measure of a fixed real IFS when two contraction logarithms have irrational ratio, with logarithmic rates under Diophantine hypotheses. Solomyak proves generic power decay for parameterized fixed real self-similar measures outside a zero-dimensional exceptional set.

## Repository triangular array

For each depth `K`, the repository has a different finite measure supported on coded residues modulo `64^K`. Its Fourier product contains modular inverses of `81`, and the application needs bounds uniform in a frequency interval growing with `K`.

That is not presently a fixed real IFS measure. Direct application would require a theorem embedding the whole triangular array into one stationary random walk/renewal model with uniform constants.

## Rigidity results

Rudolph classifies positive-entropy measures jointly invariant under multiplicatively independent maps. Shmerkin and Wu control dimensions/intersections of real invariant sets. These results do not answer whether one ordinary integer belongs to a 2-adic coded set.

## Actionable research questions

1. Can the Fourier product be represented as a stopped random walk with increments independent of `K` after rescaling?
2. Can one prove a uniform renewal theorem for the backward `81` orbit modulo `64^K`?
3. Can a hypothetical integer survivor generate a nontrivial invariant empirical measure with positive entropy? Without this, Rudolph does not engage.
4. Is there a transference principle between the finite-group measures and a real self-similar measure on a solenoid?

Until one of these bridges is proved, cite this literature as methodology and hypothesis diagnostics, not theorem support.
