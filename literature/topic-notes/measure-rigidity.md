# Measure rigidity and `×p/×q` dynamics

## Located theory

Furstenberg introduced foundational disjointness and `×2/×3` questions. Rudolph proved a measure-rigidity result for measures invariant under multiplicatively independent maps with positive entropy. Shmerkin and Wu proved sharp dimension/intersection results for invariant sets and related self-similar structures. [@Furstenberg1967; @Rudolph1990; @Shmerkin2019; @Wu2019]

## Why this does not yet settle a repository question

`CLAUDE/Q-0005` concerns one explicit ordinary-integer point, or the intersection of a particular `2`-adic coded set with `Z`. The located results concern invariant probability measures, entropy, Hausdorff dimension, or intersections of closed invariant sets in real tori/lines.

No current repository reduction establishes all of the following:

1. a real or compact dynamical system matching the published theorem;
2. simultaneous invariance under the required maps;
3. a nontrivial invariant measure of the required entropy;
4. an implication from the measure/dimension conclusion to exclusion or existence of one integer point.

## Verdict

The literature is a research compass, not a theorem dependency. Any statement that these results “say something about `V∞∩Z`” must name a new reduction first.
