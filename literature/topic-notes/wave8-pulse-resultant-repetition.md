# Wave 8 topic note — from pulse resultants to an effective repetition bound

PR #53 `L-8201` makes every fixed pulse support finite by eliminating each dyadic pulse-height variable. The remaining infinite direction is repetition length.

## Recommended theorem ladder

### 1. Freeze one baseline and support

Write the exact divisibility condition after inserting the resultant caps. Every pulse height now lies in a finite set.

### 2. Normalize repetition

For each surviving finite pulse vector, express the numerator/denominator condition as one equation whose variable is the repetition number `r`. Required output:

```text
fixed number of exponential terms;
fixed algebraic/rational coefficients;
explicit bases;
all proper subsums classified;
exact positivity and primitive replay conditions.
```

### 3. Split degeneracies

- A nondegenerate fixed S-unit equation is eligible for finiteness theorems.
- A two-term equation is eligible for explicit real and p-adic logarithmic-form bounds.
- A zero resultant/proper subsum should be promoted to its own resonant-family claim; it may be constructive.

### 4. Make the bound effective

Use global logarithmic bounds to obtain a large initial cap, then apply de Weger's real and p-adic lattice-reduction strategy to reduce it to a computable range.

### 5. Test independent-exponential gcd

If the normalized condition forces a common divisor comparable to the full denominator in two multiplicatively independent exponential sequences, compare it with the Bugeaud–Corvaja–Zannier subexponential gcd theorem.

### 6. Replay every survivor

A divisor hit is not a cycle. Reconstruct the positive integer and replay every exact accelerated valuation.

## Why this is preferable to another raw search

The native resultants already remove every pulse-size continuum. Number-theoretic reduction should now attack the only remaining unbounded parameter rather than enlarge finite pulse scans.
