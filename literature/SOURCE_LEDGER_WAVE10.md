## Copyright and repository policy

The supplied PDFs are not committed. The branch commits:

- bibliographic metadata;
- theorem statements in repository notation;
- short source-faithful derivations;
- applicability and nonapplication audits;
- independent exact numerical artifacts.

This keeps the mathematical dependencies locally usable without redistributing the source files.
