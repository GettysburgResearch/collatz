## Executive synthesis

The repository is now a portfolio of interacting programs: collision codes and negative-return graphs; the `81/64` survivor and EQ program; automated rewrite termination; affine ping-pong/cycle/heteroclinic/fuel searches; regular forward-invariant sanctuaries; and conditioned rare-event/compressed-cycle ideas.

Their common bottleneck is an **infinite ordinary-integer realization theorem**. The project can manufacture and verify large finite objects. A successful grammar, graph, automaton, or compressed word must still contain one finite positive integer and remain valid forever.

The literature is most useful when it separates standard infrastructure from arithmetic realization:

- finite graph potentials, regular transductions, safety kernels, affine monoids, exact cycle equations, and finite-state large-deviation bounds are reusable infrastructure;
- ordinary-integer realization of a compatible `2`-adic path, exact Collatz cylinder coverage, and indefinitely regenerative arithmetic remain native open work;
- stationary real self-similar, invariant-measure, and generic-parameter theorems do not automatically solve triangular finite-group or individual-orbit problems.
