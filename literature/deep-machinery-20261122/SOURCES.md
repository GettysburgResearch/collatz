# Sources and reading ladder

**57 distinct works, checked through 22 November 2026.** This is a short reading index; [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) contains the complete aliases, versions, primary/alternative URLs, theorem locations, evidence scopes, and repository-specific caveats.

The three specialist lists and rigidity chapter contain 58 citation records. Two papers recur across chapters, giving 56 main unique works. Vojta's separately consulted 1999 exposition is listed as one additional work. Errata and corrected versions are attached to their original work.

## Read in this order

1. **Understand the exact missing implication:** read [the problem interfaces](01_problem_interfaces.md), then A01 (Tao), A02 (Krasikov–Lagarias), and AR15/BL96 (Bernstein–Lagarias). Keep ordinary sources, completion points, and almost-all quantifiers separate.
2. **A concrete arithmetic payoff:** AR01 (Matveev), then AR08 (Corvaja–Zannier). The proposed tasks are a faster band-entry clock and classification of one fixed full-denominator cycle family. Neither gives the missing universal return drift.
3. **The sharp analytic target:** A04's versioned predecessor bound, followed by the OPEN-FLOOR deduction in [the analytic chapter](02_analysis_and_entropy.md). A03–A05 are recent source-qualified results; their formal builds were not independently replayed.
4. **Retain each original source while transporting mass:** D03, D01, D02, then D04. Read the assumptions before applying renewal, drift, minorization, or tower conclusions.
5. **Build certificates that tolerate numeric growth:** D09, D11, D12, then D15. Well-founded obligations, transition closure, and exact guards matter more than a small finite state picture.
6. **Try structure only after its input is proved:** AR07, AR05, AR13 and A09. Fixed dimension, algebraicity, finite Mahler closure, and independence are substantial hypotheses.
7. **Expert long shots:** F67/R01, R90/R02, EL21/R03, BQ14/R05, P14/R08. First construct the actual commuting action, irreducible representation, or legal analytic family.
8. **Read safeguards before importing a slogan:** D07 (absorption is assumed), A11–A12 (direct algebraic mismatches), KS07/R12 (generalized undecidability), and the explicit lead-only entries.

## Evidence labels

“Full-text statement read” means the relevant statement and hypotheses were inspected, not that the entire proof was independently audited. “Primary indexed statement” records full statement text retrieved from the original source's index when its file was unavailable. “Author exposition” and “later primary restatement” identify indirect theorem access explicitly. “Primary abstract statement” supports only the statement actually exposed there. “Lead researcher: statement/abstract inspected” preserves the rigidity chapter's narrower recorded scope. Entries labelled as leads supply no untranscribed numerical or algorithmic premise.

Every repository application and stop condition is the review team's analysis unless a chapter says otherwise. The new deductions are not attributed to the source authors.
