## Purpose

This packet verifies the complete finite remainder of `T-9602` after its centered-contraction and largest-neutral-gap reductions.

Centering the accelerated Collatz word at the trivial fixed point `1` makes every valuation `2` disappear from the defect numerator. The theorem classifies all words with exactly eight remaining valuations and reduces them to seven exceptional-letter types and finitely many neutral-gap rows. This experiment performs every final exact divisor test.

It is a cycle-exclusion computation. It does not produce a positive cycle or an infinite divergent orbit.

All high-valuation magnitudes are covered through the theorem's monotone lowering argument. Tied largest gaps can duplicate a cyclic word but cannot omit one.

## Independent implementations

`run.py` uses recursive weak compositions, reconstructs the contraction and cutoff tables, and computes each centered numerator as both `C-D` and the sparse centered sum.

`verify.py` uses a separate separator-position stars-and-bars generator, reconstructs all finite rows independently, and aborts on any divisor hit.

Both programs use exact Python integers only.
