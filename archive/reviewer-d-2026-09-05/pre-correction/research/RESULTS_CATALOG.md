# Reviewed results catalog beyond the resident spine

This catalog is organized by mathematics, not by PR number. It covers significant surviving exact-SHA-reviewed work outside the initial eight resident records. “Source-pinned” means the proof remains on the cited commit; it is not yet a local integrated packet. Mixed branch verdicts are reported claim-by-claim rather than promoted wholesale.

The frozen review wave covered 42 of 45 source PRs. PRs #67, #68, and #69 were unreviewed in that wave and are not presented here as reviewed results.

## Centered/adelic ordinary sections and weighted EQ

**Strongest surviving results.** The all-depth weighted-EQ chain and the centered `64→81` ordinary-section machinery—including the centered-power equivalence, canonical-cylinder stabilization interface, exact appended blocks, recurrence cone, Thue–Morse exclusion, and finite-state/morphic transfer—passed exact-SHA review with fixes. A separate review PR independently reconstructed the `9309–9312` EQ chain. The fixed-precision PDR kernel was also verified as a 2-adic ghost graph rather than an ordinary witness.

**Boundary.** The depth-46 minimum artifact was not independently fully replayed as a global-minimality computation; scalar EQ does not imply ordinary extraction; residue-only lassos are not positive trajectories.

**Residency.** Source-pinned, except the factor-complexity repair that uses three precisely pinned dependencies.

**Relation.** Supplies representation and recurrence barriers for the ordinary-extraction program and the factor-complexity packet.

**Next step.** Import the ordinary-section dependencies locally, then prove all nontrivial centered itineraries have infinitely many nonzero canonical blocks or decide the least-root sequence.
