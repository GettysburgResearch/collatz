## 1. Exact Current State in Repo

  - $D(X) \ge c X^{0.84}$ for arbitrarily large $X$ (via a 17-comparison chain, irrational rotation invariance, and Krasikov–Lagarias predecessor counts), and
  - $D(X) > X^{0.164}$ for all sufficiently large $X$ (via Wu–Wang linear forms).

- **PR #145** proves that the reciprocal sum over $n$ whose $(2n, 2n+1)$ orbits never meet within $\le 1$ shortcut step is finite iff Collatz holds, with an elementary criterion given an $o(X^\gamma)$ upper bound for $\gamma = \log 2 / \log(64/3) \approx 0.2265$.

## 2. Verified Computational Diagnosis (Why Pure $2$-Adic Sieving Fails Alone)

Running `algebraic_coalescence_sieve.py` gives exact counts of residue classes mod $2^M$ where $T^{k_1}(n) = T^{k_2}(3n)$ holds **identically** for all $n \equiv r \pmod{2^M}$:
- **First exact algebraic coalescence of $(n, 3n)$:** $M = 5$, $r \equiv 23 \pmod{32}$:
  $$T^5(32m + 23) = 27m + 20 = T^5(3(32m + 23)).$$
  (Notice $23 \equiv 7 \pmod 8$, aligning directly with PR #146's Lean theorem $x_{\min} \not\equiv 7 \pmod 8$, while proving *unconditional* coalescence without needing minimality!)
- **Asymptotic bottleneck:** At $M = 13$ ($4096$ odd classes), only $855$ ($20.87\%$) coalesce algebraically, leaving $3241$ survivors (box-dimension estimate $\approx 0.97185 > 0.84$).
- By contrast, the **minimality-based stopping-time sieve** ($T^k(n) < n$ for $n = 2^M m + r$) already eliminates **$91.04\%$** of odd residue classes by $M = 13$ ($367 / 4096$ survivors).

## 3. Proposed Proof Strategy (Two-Pronged Attack)

### Prong A — Immediate PR-Ready Extension of PR #146 in Lean 4
Extend $x_{\min} \not\equiv 7 \pmod 8$ by combining:
1. **Unconditional algebraic coalescence identities** for $(n, 3n)$ mod $2^M$ (starting with $23 \pmod{32}$, $39, 55 \pmod{64}$, etc., all auto-checkable by `omega`/`ring` in Lean 4).
2. **Pullback/minimality reductions** for $x_{\min}$: whenever $3x$ (or $x$) drops to an odd $y < x$ whose pair $(y, 3y)$ shares a common descendant with $(x, 3x)$ (e.g., via $T^2(4y+1) = 2T(y)$), $x$ cannot be the least failed odd input $x_{\min}$.

### Prong B — Asynchronous Coupled Syracuse Random Walk (Path to $D(X) \ll X^{0.84 - \varepsilon}$)
To obtain an unconditional power-saving upper bound $D(X) \ll X^{0.84 - \varepsilon}$:
1. **Ratio/Difference Cocycle:** Start with odd $n$ and $3n$. After one shortcut step, $T(n) = (3n+1)/2$ and $T(3n) = (9n+1)/2$, so $T(3n) - 3 T(n) = -1$ and $T(3n)/T(n) = 3 - \frac{2}{3n+1}$.
2. **Coupled 2-Orbit Syracuse Map:** Track the pair $(\text{Syr}^{j_1}(n), \text{Syr}^{j_2}(3n))$ using **asynchronous clock advancement** (always advancing whichever coordinate is currently larger in archimedean norm!).
3. **Key Open Lemma (`OPEN_OBLIGATION: R1-COUPLE`):** Adapt Tao's (2019) $3$-adic Syracuse random walk (which bounds $\mathbb{P}(\text{Syr}^k(n) \equiv a \pmod{3^m})$ via characteristic functions on $\mathbb{Z}/3^m\mathbb{Z}$) to the **joint distribution** of $(\text{Syr}^{j_1}(n) - \text{Syr}^{j_2}(3n)) \pmod{3^m}$ on the synchronized-magnitude stopping time $|\log \text{Syr}^{j_1}(n) - \log \text{Syr}^{j_2}(3n)| \le \log 3$.
   - *Why this is the right target:* Single-orbit Tao mixing only gives logarithmic-density $o(X)$ exceptions because of the slow $1/(\log X)^c$ error in $3$-adic equidistribution. To reach a **power saving** $X^{-\delta}$ ($\delta > 0.16$) in natural density, one must replace the $L^2$ Fourier bound on $\mathbb{Z}/3^m\mathbb{Z}$ with a **Bourgain–Gamburd / Lindenstrauss–Varjú-type spectral gap** on the affine group $\text{Aff}(\mathbb{Z}/3^m\mathbb{Z})$ generated by $x \mapsto 3x + 1$ and $x \mapsto x/2$.
   - *Falsifiable Milestone:* First prove a power-saving bound on the *finite-horizon* non-coalescence count $D_H(X)$ for $H = c \log X$ under a $3$-adic spectral-gap hypothesis, and verify numerically whether the empirical survival exponent for $(n, 3n)$ at $H = 2 \log_2 X$ is strictly below $0.84$.
