## What would resolve the project?

A proof-side resolution requires a complete proof that every positive integer
reaches the trivial cycle under the chosen standard Collatz formulation,
together with verified equivalence for any alternate formulation used.

A disproof-side resolution requires one of:

- an explicit positive integer whose orbit is proved to avoid the trivial cycle
  forever;
- a rigorously proved nontrivial positive cycle;
- another object accompanied by a complete proof that it is equivalent to a
  Collatz counterexample.

## Current high-level concentration

The current research record has repeatedly encountered two global boundaries.

### Ordinary-integer realization

Many constructions provide compatible finite prefixes, residue cylinders,
symbolic paths, or `2`-adic objects. The missing step is often one ordinary
positive integer that realizes all depths.

Current forms of the target include:

- bounded or stabilizing least representatives;
- eventual zero transported blocks;
- an explicit all-time legal initial state;
- a seed-preserving invariant;
- a proof that the entire architecture has no ordinary survivor.

### Full-denominator cycle closure

Many cycle constructions provide near-integrality, proper-factor divisibility,
or large finite compatibility.

A complete finite cycle certificate requires:

- the entire denominator condition;
- positivity;
- integrality;
- exact valuation or branch replay;
- nontriviality.

These concentrations are not a restriction on new approaches.
