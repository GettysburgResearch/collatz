Integer states are exact. Plotted logs are approximate. Every experiment has a finite, visible budget.

Resource budgets

Up to 20,000 displayed steps; 8,192 bits including hidden raw peaks; 15 seconds per job; two jobs at once. Families: at most 512 sources and one million combined steps. Words: 512 bits. Inverse view: at most 128 nodes from the UI.

Trace retention also stops after 1.5 million decimal digits. Every limited result remains explicitly incomplete.
