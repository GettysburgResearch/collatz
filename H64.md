## Theorems in the H-frame (`experiments/h64_theory.py` — ALL PASS)

**Theorem 1 (H-rigidity, three lines).** Every valid H-word gives
`A_S = (81^S A_0 − 17C)/64^S` with `0 ≤ 17C ≤ 81^S`, hence
`(81/64)^S (A_0−1) ≤ A_S ≤ (81/64)^S A_0`. An expanding identity
`H^{S(k)}(N(k)) = N(k+Δ)` on an exp-poly family forces
`(81/64)^{S(k)} → 64^{mΔ}`, so `S(k)` is eventually constant, and
coefficient matching gives `81^S = 64^{S+mΔ}` — impossible. **No
hypothesis on S(k) at all**: the subsystem freezes density at 4/6, so
the crash-and-climb pathologies of the T-frame dichotomy cannot occur.

unique factorization alone.

{0,1}}` is homeomorphic to `{0,1}^ℕ`: each ε-sequence is realized by

ε-words correspond to the rational fixed points `A = 17C_w/(81^p−64^p)`,

only integer valid cycles are the trivial digits 0, 1** (the H-frame

**Theorem 3 (automaticity/Cobham obstruction).** The regeneration
residues satisfy: `r_{m,m'} mod 64^j` is periodic in m with exact period

rules out Walnut-style automatic-sequence proofs.
